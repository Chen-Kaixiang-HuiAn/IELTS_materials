# Agree / Disagree（同意 / 不同意）

> 题型标志：`To what extent do you agree or disagree?` / `Do you agree or disagree?`
>
> 这类题的本质：**你必须亮明立场**，可以完全同意、完全不同意、或"部分同意"。部分同意最容易拿高分，但也最难写稳——既要承认对方合理，最后又要回到自己的立场，不能变成墙头草。
>
> 下面用 Part 1 主线程的同一套"路程"走一遍：**破题 → 推因果链（我怎么想的）→ 试水英文 → 升级润色 → 完整范文**。

---

## 代表真题（教育类）

> **The values that we learn from our parents and family have a greater influence on our future success than any knowledge and skills we learned at home. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement?**
>
> 我们从父母和家庭中学到的价值观，对未来成功的影响大于在学校学到的任何知识和技能。你在多大程度上同意或不同意？

---

## ① 破题：题目到底在问什么

- **题型**：Agree / Disagree。题干是一个比较级论断（家庭价值观 > 学校知识技能），问你多大程度上同意。
- **核心词展开**：
  - `values from parents/family` —— 价值观、性格、职业道德、为人处世
  - `knowledge and skills at school` —— 专业知识、数字素养、就业硬技能
  - `greater influence on future success` —— 成功到底由什么决定？这是一个因果判断题
- **表面争论**：家庭和学校，谁更塑造一个人的成功？
- **深层**：成功是"软实力（品格/关系）"主导，还是"硬技能（知识/就业力）"主导？二者是否其实缺一不可？

**先写一句话立场（第一反应最诚实）**：

> I disagree with the claim that family values outweigh school knowledge. While家庭价值观确实塑造品格，但正规教育提供的专业知识和数字素养，在现代就业市场里是硬通货，缺了它光有品格走不远。

注意：这里我选了**不同意**（家庭价值观并不"大于"学校知识）。选不同意比选"部分同意"更干脆——因为题目用的是 `greater than` 这种绝对比较，直接拆"二者无法简单比大小"最有力。

---

## ② 推因果链：论点不能像口号

模板：`前提 → 机制（为什么导致结果）→ 结果 → 推到底`

**下面用"自言自语"还原我怎么一步步把每条链想出来。**

**因果链 A：家庭价值观塑造品格，但品格≠成功全部**

> 我怎么想的：
>
> 题目说家庭价值观影响大。这没错——父母教你怎么做人、是不是勤劳、有没有诚信，这些是底层操作系统。
>
> 但"影响大"不等于"比学校知识影响更大"。为什么？因为现代社会评价成功，很大一部分看你能不能找到好工作、创造收入。
>
> 好工作看什么？看专业能力和数字素养，这些恰恰主要在学校里学。一个品格再好但完全没受过专业训练的人，在今天的职场会很吃亏。
>
> 推到底：所以家庭价值观是"地基"，但学校知识是"上层建筑"；光有地基、没楼，住不了人。二者不是谁大于谁，而是分工不同。

链条：**家庭价值观塑品格 → 但现代成功高度依赖就业力 → 就业力主要靠学校专业训练 → 故二者不可比大小，应并列**

**因果链 B：没有学校硬技能，品格优势会被现实抵消**

> 我怎么想的：
>
> 反过来想：如果一个人只有家庭教的品格，没有学校知识，会怎样？
>
> 前沿科学、医疗、工程这类岗位，门槛就是跨学科专业知识。你再诚信、再坚韧，不会做研究也进不去。
>
> 而且现在 AI 和自动化在吃掉低技能岗位。没有数字素养的人，容易被挤到失业或低薪岗。
>
> 推到底：在数字时代，数字素养是"入场券"。没有这张券，家庭价值观再好也只能在场外鼓掌。

链条：**缺学校硬技能 → 难进高门槛行业 + 易被自动化取代 → 就业力塌陷 → 品格优势被现实抵消**

## ③ 试水英文

**Intro（破题+立场）**：
> Some people think family values are more important than school knowledge for success. I don't agree. Family values are good but school knowledge is also important. Both matter.

**Body 1（家庭价值观是地基，但不是成功全部）**：
> Family values teach people how to be a good person. Parents teach children to work hard and be honest. These things are very important for life. Children who see their parents work hard will also work hard. Families that talk and care about others help children make friends and work with people. So family values are like the base of a person. But being a good person is not enough for success.

**Body 2（没有学校硬技能，品格优势会被现实抵消）**：
> School teaches science and computer skills. Without these skills, people can't get good jobs. In the modern world, many jobs need professional knowledge. Also, computers are everywhere so digital skills matter. If you don't have digital skills, you may lose job because of AI. For example, in science research, you need to know many subjects. A person with only good character but no training cannot do this job. So both are important, but school knowledge is also needed.

**Conclusion**：
> In conclusion, family values and school knowledge are both important for success. Family values build character, and school knowledge gives skills for jobs. A person needs both to do well.

**初稿问题诊断**（先认账，下一步④改）：
- 全是简单句，GRA 低；
- `good / important` 反复横跳，LR 弱；
- 因果关系没展开，只有 "because / so" 硬连接；
- 立场句 "Both matter / So both are important" 没体现 "拆比较级" 的逻辑，像和稀泥；
- 没有把 "数字时代入场券 / AI 淘汰低技能" 这层推到底。

## ④ 升级润色

**Intro 逐句改**：
- 普通：Some people think family values are more important than school knowledge for success. I don't agree.
- 升：`Some people claim that the foundational values instilled by parents and family members contribute more to future achievements than any expertise acquired through formal education. However, I disagree with their opinion.`（`claim` 替 think；`foundational values instilled` 把"家庭价值观"升级为可论证短语；`However, I disagree` 复合句亮立场）

**Body 1 逐句改**：
- 普通：Family values teach people how to be a good person. Parents teach children to work hard and be honest.
- 升：`It is admitted that core values learned from parents and family not only serve as a guiding compass for life but also act as a catalyst for long-term success, primarily through their influence on character development and decision-making.`（先认账 family values 有用，再用 `not only... but also` 复合句落地）
- 普通：Children who see their parents work hard will also work hard.
- 升：`Specifically, children who witness their parents' dedication and perseverance are more likely to adopt these traits themselves.`（`witness / dedication / perseverance` 精准词替口语）
- 普通：But being a good person is not enough for success.
- 升：`While the principles learned at home shape paths to success, technical skills and knowledge gained through education are also essential for individuals' accomplishments.`（`While...` 让步亮出"二者并列、不可比大小"的立场）

**Body 2 逐句改**：
- 普通：School teaches science and computer skills. Without these skills, people can't get good jobs.
- 升：`On the other hand, the cutting-edge knowledge and digital literacy acquired at school enhance personal capabilities and serve as critical factors in achieving long-term success in a rapidly evolving job market.`（`cutting-edge knowledge and digital literacy` 替 "science and computer skills"；`serve as critical factors` 升级）
- 普通：Without these skills, people can't get good jobs.
- 升：`Without this professional knowledge, individuals may struggle to secure a decent job in the competitive labor market even if they have strong decision-making skills and work ethics.`（补 "即便有品格也难就业" 的限定，因果更密）
- 普通：For example, in science research, you need to know many subjects.
- 升：`This is especially true in fields like frontier science, where research often involves multiple academic disciplines.`（落地具体领域）
- 普通：If you don't have digital skills, you may lose job because of AI.
- 升：`They may find themselves relegated to unemployment or low-skill jobs that are increasingly susceptible to automation due to artificial intelligence.`（推到底：AI 淘汰低技能，数字素养是入场券）

**Conclusion 逐句改**：
- 普通：Family values and school knowledge are both important for success.
- 升：`In conclusion, both family values and formal education are essential for future success.`（`essential` 替 important，去重复）
- 普通：A person needs both to do well.
- 升：`Together, these elements create a solid foundation for personal growth and professional attainment in an ever-changing world.`（收尾落到 "ever-changing world"，呼应数字时代）

把上面升后句子拼起来，就是下方⑤完整范文。

## ⑤ 完整范文
Some people claim that the foundational values instilled by parents and family members contribute more to future achievements than any expertise acquired through formal education. However, I disagree with their opinion. While the principles learned at home shape paths to success, technical skills and knowledge gained through education are also essential for individuals' accomplishments.

一些人声称，父母和家庭成员灌输的基础价值观比通过正规教育获得的知识和技能对未来成就的贡献更大。然而，我不同意这种观点。虽然在家学到的原则塑造通往成功的路径，但通过教育获得的技术技能和知识对个人成就同样至关重要。

It is admitted that core values learned from parents and family not only serve as a guiding compass for life but also act as a catalyst for long-term success, primarily through their influence on character development and decision-making. Strong family values foster resilience and a positive work ethic, which in turn enhances students' interpersonal skills. Specifically, children who witness their parents' dedication and perseverance are more likely to adopt these traits themselves. Additionally, families that prioritize communication and empathy nurture individuals who excel in building relationships, essential for effective networking and collaboration in professional settings later in life. This foundation creates a support system that can significantly propel one's career. In addition to awareness of collaboration, the moral principles instilled at home guide ethical decision-making. Individuals raised with a strong sense of integrity, resilience, and positive work ethic are often perceived as trustworthy, so they tend to have greater opportunities in both personal and career arenas.

毋庸置疑，父母及家庭所传承的核心价值观，不仅是引领人生航向的指南针，更是驱动长远成功的催化剂，其影响力主要体现在对个性塑造与决策能力提升方面。坚实的家庭价值观如同沃土，滋养出孩子的坚韧不拔与积极向上的职业道德，这种精神气质反过来又极大地促进了他们的人际交往能力。具体而言，孩子在亲眼见证父母无私奉献与坚持不懈的过程中，更容易内化并传承这些宝贵品质。此外，那些注重沟通与同理心培养的家庭，能够孕育出卓越的人际关系构建能力，这对于职业生涯中的高效网络构建与团队协作至关重要。这样的基础架构起一个强有力的支持网络，为个人职业发展提供了显著的助力。除了对合作重要性的深刻理解，家庭所灌输的道德观念还指引着个体做出合乎伦理的决策。那些秉持强烈诚信原则的人，因其可靠性而广受信赖，无论是在个人生活还是职业生涯中，都能赢得更多的机遇与尊重。

On the other hand, the cutting-edge knowledge and digital literacy acquired at school enhance personal capabilities and serve as critical factors in achieving long-term success in a rapidly evolving job market, particularly in today's digital age. Firstly, many competency-based jobs require candidates to have a strong foundation in interdisciplinary courses. Without this professional knowledge, individuals may struggle to secure a decent job in the competitive labor market even if they have strong decision-making skills and work ethics. This is especially true in fields like frontier science, where research often involves multiple academic disciplines. Secondly, digital literacy, which is not always obtained at home, is increasingly essential for employability and innovation. Hence, individuals who lack the ability to create and evaluate information on digital platforms are at a significant disadvantage. They may find themselves relegated to unemployment or low-skill jobs that are increasingly susceptible to automation due to artificial intelligence.

另一方面，从学校汲取的前沿知识与数字素养，不仅能够大幅度提升个人的综合能力，更是长远成功之路上的核心驱动力，尤其是在当下这个日新月异的数字时代。首先，众多以能力为导向的职位往往对求职者提出严苛要求，需其具备坚实的跨学科知识底蕴。倘若缺乏这一专业基石，即便个体拥有出众的决策力与高尚的职业道德，也可能在竞争白热化的职场中难以觅得一份理想的工作机会。这一现象在前沿科学等领域尤为凸显，因为这些领域的研究往往横跨多个学科领域，对知识的广度与深度有着极高的要求。其次，数字素养，这一非家庭环境所能轻易培养的技能，对于提升就业竞争力与激发创新思维的作用日益凸显。那些未能掌握在数字平台上创建、分析并评估信息能力的人，将明显处于不利地位。他们可能会遭遇失业的困境，或被迫从事那些日益受到自动化与人工智能技术冲击的低技能工作，面临着职业发展的重大挑战。

In conclusion, both family values and formal education are essential for future success. While core values instill resilience and ethical decision-making, specificized knowledge and digital literacy equip individuals for competitive job markets. Together, these elements create a solid foundation for personal growth and professional attainment in an ever-changing world.

总而言之，家庭价值观与正规教育对于未来的成功而言，均是不可或缺的基石。家庭核心价值观不仅赋予了个人坚韧不拔的精神与做出伦理决策的能力，而正规教育所提供的专业知识与数字素养，则让个体在竞争激烈的就业市场中占据了先机，做好了充分准备。这些要素相辅相成，共同为个人的成长与职业成就铺设了一条坚实的道路，在日新月异、瞬息万变的世界中显得尤为关键与重要。

## ⑥ 同题型变体示例
为看清 Agree/Disagree 在不同话题怎么换皮不换骨，再贴一篇环境类完整范文。破题思路完全一致：**先承认对方合理，再反转到自己立场，用因果链推到底**（快时尚 → 过度生产 → 污染+废弃物 → 生态足迹扩大；让步段承认"消费自由"，反转指出"不可持续的消费模式必须被约束"）。

---

**Some people say that the fashion industry is bad for the environment as it encourages consumers to by all the time. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

**有人说时尚行业对环境有害，因为它鼓励消费者不断购买。你在多大程度上同意或不同意这一观点？**

The fashion industry has been increasingly criticized for its negative environmental impacts because it often encourages overconsumption and wastefulness through practices like fast fashion. Although the industry may not directly enforce overconsumption, I agree that it is highly detrimental to the environment due to its unsustainable practices and significant ecological footprint.

时尚行业因其对环境的负面影响而受到越来越多的批评，因为它通过快时尚等方式鼓励过度消费和浪费。虽然该行业并未直接强制消费者过度消费，但我同意其对环境的高度破坏性，这是由于其不可持续的运作方式和显著的生态足迹所致。

It cannot be denied that the fashion industry creates opportunities for frequent purchases by introducing new trends and affordable products, thereby influencing consumer behavior. However, the decision to overconsume ultimately depends on individual choices. While the industry provides options, it does not mandate excessive consumption. Instead, it offers a spectrum of choices, including many brands and designers that focus on creating durable pieces designed to last for years rather than disposable items in order to cater to varying consumer values and priorities. For example, those who prioritize sustainability or minimalism can engage with brands that align with their principles. Consequently, critics of the overconsumption argument emphasize personal accountability, arguing that not all shoppers are influenced by marketing tactics or the allure of fast fashion.

不可否认的是，时尚行业通过推出新趋势和实惠的产品，为频繁购买创造了条件，从而影响了消费者的行为。然而，过度消费的决定最终取决于个人选择。尽管时尚行业提供了各种选择，但它并未强制要求消费者过度消费。相反，它提供了一个广泛的选择范围，其中包括许多专注于制造耐用产品的品牌和设计师，这些产品旨在使用多年，而不是作为一次性商品，以迎合不同消费者的价值观和优先事项。例如，那些优先考虑可持续性或极简主义的人可以选择与其原则相符的品牌。因此，批评过度消费论点的人强调个人责任，认为并非所有消费者都会受到市场营销策略或快时尚吸引力的影响。

Nevertheless, the environmental impact of the fashion industry remains severe due to its manufacturing practices and disposable culture, which directly contribute to environmental degradation. To begin with, the production of textiles demands extensive water usage and pesticides. This not only depletes local water resources but also disrupts ecosystems, linking production methods to widespread ecological harm. Additionally, the use of synthetic fibers and chemical-intensive dyeing processes releases pollutants into rivers and, thus, contaminate water sources. This, in turn, adversely affects aquatic life and endangers nearby communities that rely on these resources for survival. Even worse, fast fashion amplifies these problems by encouraging unsustainable consumer behavior, particularly as brands release new collections multiple times a year, fostering a culture of frequent purchases and disposability. This leads to the generation of vast amounts of textile waste, much of which is non-biodegradable. These materials persist in landfills or require incineration that result in long-term environmental damage.

尽管如此，由于其生产方式和一次性文化，时尚行业对环境的影响仍然十分严重，这直接导致了环境退化。首先，纺织品的生产需要大量的水和农药。这不仅耗尽了当地的水资源，还破坏了生态系统，使生产方式与广泛的生态破坏联系在一起。此外，合成纤维的使用以及化学密集型的染色过程会向河流中排放污染物，从而污染水源。这反过来对水生生物产生不利影响，并危及依赖这些资源生存的周边社区。更糟糕的是，快时尚通过频繁发布新系列，鼓励了不可持续的消费行为，尤其是随着品牌一年多次推出新款，进一步助长了频繁购买和一次性使用的文化。这导致了大量纺织废弃物的产生，其中许多是不可降解的。这些材料要么长期存在于垃圾填埋场中，要么需要焚烧处理，从而造成长期的环境破坏。

In conclusion, while the fashion industry offers diverse options that accommodate varying consumer preferences, its manufacturing processes and the rise of fast fashion have led to significant environmental challenges from resource depletion to textile waste. These issues highlight the need for systemic reforms and more conscious consumer choices to reduce the industry's ecological impact.

综上所述，尽管时尚行业提供了多样化的选择以满足不同的消费者偏好，其生产过程和快时尚的兴起却导致了从资源耗竭到纺织废弃物等一系列严重的环境问题。这些问题凸显了系统性改革的必要性，以及消费者在减少该行业生态影响方面做出更加自觉选择的需求。

## 本题型要点回顾

- 立场句必须"承认+转折"二合一，别光说 I disagree。
- 因果链要推到"就业/社会/最坏情况"这一层，别停在"it is important"。
- 让步段结尾一定要转回自己立场，这是 Agree/Disagree 的得分命门。
