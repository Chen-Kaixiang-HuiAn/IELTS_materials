# Discuss Both Views + Opinion（讨论双方观点并给出自己的看法）

> 题型标志：`Discuss both views and give your opinion.`
>
> 这类题给你**两个对立观点**，要求你两边都讨论，最后必须给**自己的观点**。最容易犯的错是写成"一边一段然后没立场"，或者立场段太弱。
>
> 下面用 Part 1 主线程的同一套"路程"走一遍：**破题 → 推因果链（我怎么想的）→ 试水英文 → 升级润色 → 完整范文**。

---

## 代表真题（教育类）

> **Some people believe that studying at university or college is the best route to a successful career, while others believe that it is better to get a job straight after school. Discuss both views and give your opinion.**
>
> 有些人认为，上大学是通往成功职业生涯的最佳途径，而另一些人则认为，直接在中学毕业后参加工作更好。讨论双方观点并给出你的自己的观点。

---

## ① 破题：题目到底在问什么

- **题型**：Discuss Both Views + Opinion。题干给出两个对立路径（高等教育 vs 直接就业），要求两边都讨论 + 亮明自己立场。
- **核心词展开**：
  - `studying at university` —— 专业知识、跨学科训练、人脉、文凭门槛
  - `get a job straight after school` —— 实践经验、经济独立、早起步、行业积累
  - `best route to a successful career` —— 什么是"成功"？是收入、稳定性，还是 fulfillment？
- **表面争论**：成才靠"读书"还是靠"早入行"？
- **深层**：知识资本和经验资本，哪个在今天的就业市场更硬？二者是否其实互补？

**先写一句话立场（第一反应最诚实）**：

> I think university matters more, because today's jobs need specialized knowledge and digital skills that you mostly get at school, but starting work early also has real value like experience and independence.

注意：这里我选了**倾向高等教育**的立场。选"一边倒但承认对方合理"比"完全中立"更稳——Discuss 题最怕写成没立场的墙头草。

---

## ② 推因果链：论点不能像口号

模板：`前提 → 机制（为什么导致结果）→ 结果 → 推到底`

**下面用"自言自语"还原我怎么一步步把每条链想出来。**

**因果链 A：高等教育的价值（支持方）**

> 我怎么想的：
>
> 题目说大学是成功最佳路径。这有道理——现代好工作大多要专业知识。
>
> 为什么？因为医学、工程、科研这些岗位，门槛就是跨学科专业能力。你不在大学里系统学，进不去。
>
> 而且大学不只是教知识，还给你人脉和多元视角，这些对解决复杂职场问题很重要。
>
> 推到底：所以高等教育提供的是"硬门槛 + 软实力"，是长期职业成功的基石。

链条：**高等教育 → 专业知识+跨学科基础 → 进入高门槛行业 + 人脉视野 → 长期竞争力**

**因果链 B：直接就业的价值与局限（反对方）**

> 我怎么想的：
>
> 反过来，直接工作也有道理——能早点赚钱、经济独立，还能在行业里早积累。
>
> 但问题是：在信息时代，数字素养成了就业入场券。没受过高等教育的人，缺乏在数字平台创造和评估信息的能力。
>
> 更糟的是，很多低技能岗位正被 AI 自动化取代。没有专业训练，容易被挤到这些脆弱岗位。
>
> 推到底：直接工作的"经验优势"被"技能短板"抵消——越早入行若没知识底子，长期反而吃亏。

链条：**直接就业 → 经验+经济独立 → 但缺数字素养+专业知识 → 易被自动化取代 → 经验优势被抵消**

---

## ③ 试水英文

**Intro（破题+立场）**：
> Some people think university is the best way to a good career. Others think it is better to work right after school. I think university is more important.

**Body 1（支持高等教育）**：
> University gives people knowledge and skills for success. Many jobs need special training that you can only get at school. Also, university helps people meet others and learn different ideas. Without a degree, young people may only get low-level jobs.

**Body 2（承认直接就业的价值，但反驳）**：
> Some say working early gives experience and money. That is true for some people. But without higher education, it is hard to do well in today's job market. Many jobs need computer and digital skills. If you don't have these, you may lose your job because of AI.

**Conclusion**：
> In conclusion, both ways have value, but university is more important for long-term success because it gives knowledge and skills.

**初稿问题诊断**（先认账，下一步④改）：
- 立场句 "I think university is more important" 太裸，没"承认对方再转折"；
- Body 2 只半句话带过对方（experience and money），随即全段反驳，对方观点讨论不足；
- 因果只用 "because / so" 硬连，没推到底；
- 全是简单句，GRA 低；`good / important` 反复横跳，LR 弱。

## ④ 升级润色

**Intro 逐句改**：
- 普通：Some people think university is the best way to a good career. Others think it is better to work right after school. I think university is more important.
- 升：`Some argue that higher education equips individuals with essential skills for long-term success, while others believe entering the workforce immediately provides practical experience and an early start. In my view, tertiary education is vital, as it offers professional expertise and ensures better opportunities in today's competitive job market.`（`argue / equip` 替 think/gives；`while` 并置双方；`In my view` 亮立场）

**Body 1 逐句改**：
- 普通：University gives people knowledge and skills for success.
- 升：`It cannot be denied that pursuing tertiary education is essential for success in today's digital age.`（`It cannot be denied that` 形式主语句，比 "University gives" 正式）
- 普通：Many jobs need special training that you can only get at school.
- 升：`Many competency-based positions require a strong foundation in interdisciplinary studies, which is vital for staying competitive in fields such as frontier science, where research often spans multiple academic disciplines.`（落地具体领域）
- 普通：Without a degree, young people may only get low-level jobs.
- 升：`Thus, without a university degree or vocational training, young people risk being confined to entry-level roles with limited opportunities for career advancement.`（`risk being confined to` 精准）

**Body 2 逐句改**：
- 普通：Some say working early gives experience and money. That is true for some people.
- 升：`It is understandable why some argue that university subjects are outdated and that entering the workforce immediately after school helps young people gain work experience and achieve economic independence.`（`It is understandable why some argue` 先退后进）
- 普通：But without higher education, it is hard to do well in today's job market.
- 升：`However, bypassing higher education in the information era can significantly hinder the young generation's ability to thrive in a competitive job market.`（`bypassing / hinder / thrive` 升级）
- 普通：If you don't have digital skills, you may lose job because of AI.
- 升：`Those lacking the skills to create and evaluate information on digital platforms face limited opportunities for well-paying jobs and are more likely to end up in manual labor roles, such as construction or packing, which are increasingly vulnerable to replacement by artificial intelligence-driven automation.`（推到底：AI 淘汰低技能）

**Conclusion 逐句改**：
- 普通：In conclusion, both ways have value, but university is more important for long-term success.
- 升：`In conclusion, while gaining practical experience and financial independence by entering the workforce early may appeal to some, the indispensable role of tertiary education in equipping individuals with professional knowledge, critical skills, and adaptability cannot be overstated.`（`while` 让步复现；`cannot be overstated` 强调）

把上面升后句子拼起来，就是下方⑤完整范文。

---

## ⑤ 完整范文
Some argue that higher education equips individuals with essential skills for long-term success, while others believe entering the workforce immediately provides practical experience and an early start. In my view, tertiary education is vital, as it offers professional expertise and ensures better opportunities in today's competitive job market.

一些人认为，高等教育能够为个人提供长期成功所需的必要技能，而另一些人则认为，立即进入职场能够带来实践经验和更早的职业起步。在我看来，高等教育至关重要，因为它提供了专业知识并确保在当今竞争激烈的就业市场中获得更好的机会。

It cannot be denied that pursuing tertiary education is essential for success in today's digital age. Tertiary education provides cutting-edge knowledge and specialized skills crucial for achieving enduring success in a rapidly evolving job market. Many competency-based positions require a strong foundation in interdisciplinary studies, which is vital for staying competitive in fields such as frontier science, where research often spans multiple academic disciplines. Thus, without a university degree or vocational training, young people risk being confined to entry-level roles with limited opportunities for career advancement. Beyond professional knowledge, tertiary education fosters intellectual and social development by enhancing analytical reasoning and innovation skills. It offers invaluable opportunities for networking and exposure to diverse perspectives, all of which are indispensable for addressing complex challenges in the workplace and are key to both personal and professional growth.

不可否认，在当今数字化时代，接受高等教育对于成功至关重要。高等教育提供了前沿知识和专业技能，这对于在快速变化的就业市场中取得持久成功至关重要。许多基于能力的职位需要扎实的跨学科基础，这在前沿科学等领域尤为重要，因为这些领域的研究通常跨越多个学术学科。因此，没有大学学历或职业培训的年轻人可能面临被限制在初级职位上的风险，从而失去更好职业发展的机会。除了专业知识之外，高等教育还促进智力和社交能力的发展，通过提升分析推理和创新能力为个人赋能。它还提供了宝贵的机会让学生建立人脉并接触多样化的视角，而这些都对于解决职场中的复杂挑战以及个人和职业成长至关重要。

It is understandable why some argue that university subjects are outdated and that entering the workforce immediately after school helps young people gain work experience and achieve economic independence, especially for those who prefer a more hands-on approach to building their careers. However, bypassing higher education in the information era can significantly hinder the young generation's ability to thrive in a competitive job market. As the world increasingly transitions into a digitally driven society, digital literacy has become a crucial factor for employability and innovation, so those lacking the skills to create and evaluate information on digital platforms face limited opportunities for well-paying jobs and are more likely to end up in manual labor roles, such as construction or packing, which are increasingly vulnerable to replacement by artificial intelligence-driven automation. Without university qualifications, individuals may struggle to secure desirable employment in today's highly competitive labor market.

可以理解，有些人认为大学课程过时，直接在中学毕业后进入职场能帮助年轻人获得工作经验并实现经济独立，尤其是对于那些更倾向于通过实践方式发展职业的人。然而，在信息时代放弃高等教育可能会严重削弱年轻一代在竞争激烈的就业市场中脱颖而出的能力。随着世界逐渐向数字化社会转型，数字素养已成为就业能力和创新的重要因素。因此，那些缺乏在数字平台上创建和评估信息技能的人，将面临难以获得高薪工作的局面，并可能更容易从事如建筑或包装等体力劳动岗位，而这些岗位正越来越容易被人工智能驱动的自动化取代。没有大学学历的人可能会发现自己难以在当今高度竞争的劳动力市场中获得理想的就业机会。

In conclusion, while gaining practical experience and financial independence by entering the workforce early may appeal to some, the indispensable role of tertiary education in equipping individuals with professional knowledge, critical skills, and adaptability cannot be overstated. Therefore, I believe that, in today's digital era, higher education remains the cornerstone of long-term career success.

综上所述，虽然通过早早进入职场来获得实践经验和经济独立可能对一些人有吸引力，但高等教育在为个人提供专业知识、关键技能以及适应能力方面的不可或缺的作用无法忽视。因此，我认为在当今数字时代，高等教育仍然是实现长期职业成功的基石。

## ⑥ 同题型变体示例
为看清 Discuss Both Views 在不同话题怎么换皮不换骨，再贴一篇环境类完整范文。破题思路完全一致：**两边都先承认合理，再在立场段收口到自己倾向，用因果链推到底**（动物利用 vs 动物保护：承认动物贡献人类进步，反转指出需以伦理和替代方案平衡）。

---

**Some people think it is acceptable to use animals in any way the benefits of human beings. Others think it is wrong to exploit animals for human purposes. Discuss both views and give your opinion.**

**有些人认为以任何方式利用动物为人类谋利是可以接受的，而另一些人则认为为了人类目的剥削动物是不对的。请讨论双方的观点并表达你的看法。**

Some argue that animals should be manipulated in whatever manner to profit humans, while others advocate for their protection. In my opinion, although animals have undeniably contributed to human progress, it is crucial to balance their use with ethical and environmental responsibility by developing alternatives to traditional practices.

一些人认为应该以任何方式利用动物来为人类谋利，而另一些人则主张保护动物。在我看来，尽管动物无疑为人类进步做出了贡献，但必须通过开发传统做法的替代方案，在利用动物与道德及环境责任之间取得平衡。

It is understandable why many regard animals as resources for human advancement. Throughout history, animals have served as sources of food, labour, and medical research subjects, all of which have improved living standards and extended human lifespans. For instance, vaccine development has long relied on animal testing to ensure drug safety before human trials. Without such contributions, modern medicine would have progressed far more slowly, and countless lives might have been lost to preventable diseases.

可以理解为何许多人将动物视为人类进步的资源。纵观历史，动物一直作为食物、劳力和医学研究对象的来源，所有这些都有助于提高生活水准并延长人类寿命。例如，疫苗研发长期依赖动物实验，以确保药物在人体试验前的安全性。如果没有这些贡献，现代医学的发展会缓慢得多，无数生命可能死于可预防的疾病。

However, exploiting animals without restraint undermines ecological stability and raises serious ethical concerns. Intensive factory farming generates massive greenhouse-gas emissions and accelerates biodiversity loss, while inhumane testing procedures cause widespread suffering that increasingly conflicts with public morality. More importantly, technological progress now offers viable alternatives: lab-grown meat, cellular agriculture, and organ-on-chip models can replace many traditional uses of animals. Therefore, a balanced approach—using animals only where no substitute exists, under strict regulation—best serves both human interest and planetary health.

然而，无节制地剥削动物会破坏生态稳定，并引发严重的道德关切。集约化工厂养殖产生大量温室气体排放并加速生物多样性丧失，而不人道的实验程序造成的广泛痛苦也日益与公共道德相冲突。更重要的是，技术进步如今提供了可行的替代方案：人造肉、细胞农业和器官芯片模型可以取代动物的许多传统用途。因此，一种平衡的方法——仅在无替代方案时于严格监管下利用动物——最能兼顾人类利益与地球健康。

In conclusion, while animals have legitimately supported human development, unchecked exploitation is neither ethical nor sustainable. Striking a balance through regulation and innovation represents the most responsible path forward.

总之，尽管动物确实支撑了人类发展，但无节制的剥削既不道德也不可持续。通过监管与创新取得平衡，才是向前迈进最负责任的路径。

## 本题型要点回顾

- 双方观点都要**实质性展开**，不能半句话带过就转反驳（否则 TA 会被判 under-addressed）。
- 立场句必须"承认+转折"二合一，用 `It is understandable why some argue... However...` 先退后进。
- 因果链要推到"就业/社会/最坏情况"这一层，别停在 "it is important"。
- 结论段用 `while` 复现让步、`cannot be overstated` 收口立场，首尾咬合。
