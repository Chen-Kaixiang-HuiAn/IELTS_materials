# Two-part Question（两问式 / 复合问题）

> 题型标志：题目抛**两个问题**，如 `What are the reasons? Is it positive or negative?` 或 `Is this good? What are the consequences?`
>
> 这类题**两个问题都要答**，且顺序通常对应两段主体。最容易漏掉第二个问题，或两段之间没逻辑衔接。
>
> 下面用 Part 1 主线程的同一套"路程"走一遍：**破题 → 推因果链（我怎么想的）→ 试水英文 → 升级润色 → 完整范文**。

---

## 代表真题（社会类）

> **Nowadays, more and more people want to get things (services, information, tasks) done instantly. Why is this? Do you think it is a positive or negative development?**
>
> 如今，越来越多的人希望立即完成各种事务（服务、信息、任务）。为什么会这样？你认为这是一种积极还是消极的发展？

---

## ① 破题：题目到底在问什么

- **题型**：Two-part Question。题干两个问题：**(1) 为什么人们想要即时满足？(2) 这是积极还是消极？**
- **核心词展开**：
  - `want things done instantly` —— 即时满足、效率崇拜、耐心下降
  - `Why` —— 必须给原因（技术 + 社会压力两条线）
  - `positive or negative` —— 必须给明确取向
- **表面争论**：即时满足是进步还是浮躁？
- **深层**：技术训练出的"速度预期"如何反噬人的耐心与心理健康？

**先写一句话立场（第一反应最诚实）**：

> People want instant results because technology made them expect speed, and because competition pushes them to save time. I think it is mostly negative because it causes stress.

注意：这里我选了**总体消极**。两问都要答——第一段专答原因，第二段答"消极+为何"。

---

## ② 推因果链：论点不能像口号

模板：`前提 → 机制（为什么导致结果）→ 结果 → 推到底`

**下面用"自言自语"还原我怎么一步步把每条链想出来。**

**因果链 A：为什么想要即时（原因段）**

> 我怎么想的：
>
> 第一个问题：为什么？先看技术。AI、软件几秒出结果，让人习惯了"什么都得快"。
>
> 再看社会：工作机会少、晋升竞争激烈（尤其东北亚），年轻人被迫加班内卷。
>
> 推到底：技术训练出速度预期 + 竞争逼出效率焦虑 → 人失去耐心、渴望即时满足。两条线合起来回答"why"。

链条：**技术提速(AI秒回) → 速度预期内化；社会竞争(内卷加班) → 效率焦虑 → 失去耐心、要即时**

**因果链 B：为什么总体消极（第二问）**

> 我怎么想的：
>
> 第二个问题：积极还是消极？先承认有点好处——省时间、推动创新。
>
> 但长期代价大：为跟上即时预期，人长期承压，倦怠、抑郁风险升高。
>
> 推到底：即时满足 → 持续压力 → 心理疾病+社会危机 → 总体消极。好处被代价盖过。

链条：**即时满足 → 省时+创新(利) vs 持续压力→心理疾病(弊) → 代价压倒收益 → 总体消极**

## ③ 试水英文

**Intro（破题+立场）**：
> More people want instant results. This is because of technology and competition. I think it is negative.

**Body 1（回答 why）**：
> Technology makes things fast. AI can do tasks in seconds, so people expect speed everywhere. Also, competition for jobs is high, so young people work overtime and lose patience.

**Body 2（回答 positive or negative）**：
> This trend has some good points, like saving time and pushing innovation. But it causes stress and mental health problems. So it is negative overall.

**Conclusion**：
> In conclusion, technology and competition cause instant desire, and it is mostly negative because of stress.

**初稿问题诊断**（先认账，下一步④改）：
- 两问都答了，但原因段只写"技术+竞争"没推到底；
- 第二问 "negative overall" 裸判断，没显性权衡；
- 因果硬连，简单句多，GRA/LR 弱；
- "bad / good / fast" 反复横跳。

## ④ 升级润色

**Intro 逐句改**：
- 普通：More people want instant results. This is because of technology and competition. I think it is negative.
- 升：`In today's fast-paced world, the desire for instant gratification, whether through services or task completion, has become increasingly prevalent. This shift is driven by advancements in technology, as well as by some societal factors. In my opinion, despite some positive effects, the growing preference for immediate results is a negative phenomenon in general.`（`instant gratification` 替 instant results；`driven by` 替 because of；`despite` 留余地）

**Body 1（why）逐句改**：
- 普通：Technology makes things fast. AI can do tasks in seconds.
- 升：`Scientific breakthroughs achieved in frontier areas and the latest technological innovations facilitate productivity improvements and enable people to process tedious tasks and multitask simultaneously. For example, software programs powered by artificial intelligence, like ChatGPT, can find and evaluate information for complex missions in seconds.`（`facilitate productivity / multitask` 升级；ChatGPT 落地）
- 普通：Competition for jobs is high, so young people work overtime and lose patience.
- 升：`Due to shortages of decent job opportunities in undeveloped nations, excessive competition for career advancement aggravates involution among the younger generation, especially in Northeast Asian countries, causing them to force themselves to work overtime. Under such conditions, they lose patience and desire immediate gratification of their needs and wants.`（`involution / aggravates` 精准）

**Body 2（positive or negative）逐句改**：
- 普通：This trend has some good points, like saving time and pushing innovation.
- 升：`Admittedly, faster access to services and information allows individuals to save time and focus on other priorities, and the demand for speed has propelled innovation in various industries.`（`Admittedly` 先认账；`propelled innovation` 升级）
- 普通：But it causes stress and mental health problems.
- 升：`However, getting things done immediately leads to long-term exposure to mental stress and life imbalance for the parties involved, which not only negatively affects the morale and productivity of employees due to constant tiredness but is also detrimental to workers' physical and mental health.`（`detrimental to` 推到底）
- 普通：So it is negative overall.
- 升：`Employees who feel pressured to keep up with rapidly changing expectations experience a higher risk of depressive disorders, suicidal ideation, anxiety, and hypertension, and such negative influences on individuals contribute to grave social crises.`（`depressive disorders / grave social crises` 把代价推到社会层）

**Conclusion 逐句改**：
- 普通：In conclusion, technology and competition cause instant desire, and it is mostly negative because of stress.
- 升：`In conclusion, the growing desire for instant gratification stems from rapid technological advancements and increasing societal competition. Although this trend enhances convenience and productivity, the constant pressure to meet instant expectations also fosters stress and harms mental health. Balancing efficiency with sustainability is vital for fostering long-term individual and societal well-being.`（`stems from` 回扣原因；`Balancing... is vital` 收口）

把上面升后句子拼起来，就是下方⑤完整范文。

## ⑤ 完整范文
In today's fast-paced world, the desire for instant gratification, whether through services or task completion, has become increasingly prevalent. This shift is driven by advancements in technology, as well as by some societal factors. In my opinion, despite some positive effects, the growing preference for immediate results is a negative phenomenon in general.

在当今快节奏的世界里，无论是服务还是任务的完成，人们对即时完成的渴望变得越来越普遍。这种转变由技术进步以及一些社会因素推动。在我看来，尽管这种趋势有一定的积极影响，但对即时结果的日益偏好总体而言是一种负面的现象。

It is believed that technological advancements and increased competition drive an enlarging number of people to want instant completion of tasks or services. That is, scientific breakthroughs achieved in frontier areas and the latest technological innovations facilitate productivity improvements and enable people to process tedious tasks and multitask simultaneously. For example, software programs powered by artificial intelligence, like ChatGPT, can find and evaluate information for complex missions in seconds. These innovations have conditioned people to expect speed and efficiency in all aspects of life. Furthermore, the growing pressure to be efficient and productive reinforces the expectation for instant feedback. Due to shortages of decent job opportunities in undeveloped nations, excessive competition for career advancement aggravates involution among the younger generation, especially in Northeast Asian countries, causing them to force themselves to work overtime. Under such conditions, they lose patience and desire immediate gratification of their needs and wants.

人们普遍认为，技术进步和竞争加剧推动了越来越多的人希望任务或服务能够即时完成。也就是说，前沿领域的科学突破和最新技术创新促进了生产力的提升，使人们能够高效处理繁琐任务并同时执行多任务。例如，由人工智能驱动的程序，如 ChatGPT，可以在几秒钟内完成复杂任务的信息查找和评估。这些创新让人们习惯于在生活的各个方面都期待速度与效率。此外，效率和生产力的日益增长的压力进一步强化了对即时反馈的需求。由于不发达国家合适工作机会的短缺，职业晋升的过度竞争加剧了年轻一代之间的内卷，尤其是在东北亚国家，这导致他们不得不加班工作。在这样的环境下，他们变得缺乏耐心，并渴望立即满足自身的需求和愿望。

While this trend has its benefits, it also brings significant drawbacks. Admittedly, faster access to services and information allows individuals to save time and focus on other priorities, and the demand for speed has propelled innovation in various industries. Businesses that cater to this trend often experience growth, contributing to overall economic progress and simplifying daily life. For instance, on-demand healthcare services enable quicker diagnosis, which potentially saves patients time and provides timely treatment. However, getting things done immediately leads to long-term exposure to mental stress and life imbalance for the parties involved, which not only negatively affects the morale and productivity of employees due to constant tiredness but is also detrimental to workers' physical and mental health. Employees who feel pressured to keep up with rapidly changing expectations experience a higher risk of depressive disorders, suicidal ideation, anxiety, and hypertension, and such negative influences on individuals contribute to grave social crises.

尽管这种趋势有其好处，但它也带来了显著的缺陷。诚然，更快地获取服务和信息使人们能够节省时间并专注于其他优先事项，且对速度的需求推动了各行业的创新。迎合这一趋势的企业往往会实现增长，从而促进整体经济进步并简化日常生活。例如，按需医疗服务能够更快地进行诊断，从而节省患者的时间并提供及时的治疗。然而，即时完成事务会导致相关方长期承受心理压力和生活失衡，这不仅因持续的疲劳而对员工的士气和生产力产生负面影响，还会损害工人的身体和心理健康。为了跟上快速变化的期望，员工感到压力倍增，面临更高的抑郁症、自杀念头、焦虑和高血压等风险，这些对个体的负面影响进一步引发了严重的社会危机。

In conclusion, the growing desire for instant gratification stems from rapid technological advancements and increasing societal competition. Although this trend enhances convenience and productivity, the constant pressure to meet instant expectations also fosters stress and harms mental health. Balancing efficiency with sustainability is vital for fostering long-term individual and societal well-being.

总之，对即时满足的日益增长的渴望源于快速的技术进步和日益激烈的社会竞争。尽管这种趋势提高了便利性和生产力，但为了满足即时期望而持续承受的压力也加剧了压力并损害心理健康。在效率与可持续性之间取得平衡，对于促进个人与社会的长期福祉至关重要。

## ⑥ 同题型变体示例
为看清 Two-part Question 在不同话题怎么换皮不换骨，再贴一篇全球化类完整范文。破题思路完全一致：**第一段答原因、第二段答取向，两问都用因果链推到底**（旅游业扩张：原因=外汇+就业；取向=积极但需管理）。

---

**An increasing number of developing countries are expanding their tourism industry. What are the reasons for this trend? Do you think it is a positive or negative development?**

**越来越多的发展中国家正在扩大其旅游业。造成这一趋势的原因是什么？你认为这是一种积极还是消极的发展？**

In recent years, more developing nations have turned to tourism as a major pillar of their economic development. Several reasons contribute to growing reliance on tourism, and while the expansion of the tourism industry carries certain risks, I believe it is a positive development in general.

近年来，更多发展中国家已将旅游业作为其经济发展的主要支柱。若干原因导致了各国对旅游业的日益依赖，尽管旅游业扩张带来一定风险，但我认为总体上这是一种积极的发展。

The primary driver is economic. Tourism generates foreign exchange without the heavy infrastructure required by manufacturing, and creates jobs for local residents in hospitality, transport, and guided services. For many low-income countries, this revenue stream is less volatile than commodity exports and helps reduce trade deficits. A secondary reason is strategic: governments promote tourism to showcase national identity and attract foreign investment, using cultural heritage and natural scenery as soft-power assets.

主要驱动力是经济性的。旅游业在不需制造业那样 heavy 基础设施的情况下创造外汇，并为当地居民在接待、交通和导游服务中创造就业。对许多低收入国家而言，这一收入流比商品出口更稳定，并有助于减少贸易逆差。次要原因是战略性的：政府推广旅游业以展示国家身份并吸引外资，将文化遗产和自然景观作为软实力资产。

Regarding its nature, the expansion brings clear benefits but also real costs. On the positive side, tourism diversifies fragile economies and funds the conservation of parks and historic sites through entrance fees. On the negative side, overcrowding degrades local environments and inflates living costs for residents, while cultural commodification can erode authentic traditions. With proper regulation—visitor caps, revenue sharing with communities, and environmental standards—these downsides can be contained.

就其性质而言，扩张带来明显收益也有真实代价。积极面，旅游使脆弱的经济多元化，并通过门票收入资助公园与历史遗址保护。消极面，过度拥挤破坏当地环境、推高居民生活成本，而文化商品化会侵蚀真实传统。借助恰当监管——游客上限、社区收入分成、环境标准——这些负面可被控制。

In conclusion, developing countries expand tourism for sound economic and strategic reasons. Although it poses environmental and cultural risks, managed properly it remains a positive force for development.

总之，发展中国家出于合理的经济和战略原因扩张旅游业。尽管它带来环境与文化的母风险，但若管理得当，仍是发展的积极力量。

## 本题型要点回顾

- 两个问题**都要答**，且主体段顺序对应两问（先因后果 / 先现象后后果）。
- 原因段用"技术线 + 社会线"双因展开，别只写一句 "because technology"。
- 第二问必须给明确取向，用 `Admittedly... However...` 做显性权衡，别只列现象不下结论。
- 结论段回扣第一问的"原因"（stems from），让两问在结尾重新咬合。
