# Part 1 · 大作文主线题走通：从 0 到 7.5（按"路程"走一遍）

> 主线题：**Some people think that the government should make university education free for all students. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**
>
> 本文件把原来拆成 10 步的内容，按一条**连贯的路程**重排：
>
> **① 思维拉伸**（中文把因果链推深）→ **② PEEL 落地成段**（把逻辑落成英文段落）→ **③ 英文升级**（合并复合句 / 精准动词 / 概念词 / so what）→ **④ 考场复用库**（让步+结尾+完整范文，背进 Appendix）。
>
> 学习方式：跟着每个阶段往下读，每个阶段都先给"初稿"，再给"升级稿"，对照看差距在哪里。初稿丑很正常，高分是改出来的。

---

## 题目与初步立场

> Some people think that the government should make university education free for all students.
>
> To what extent do you agree or disagree?
>
> （有些人认为政府应该让所有学生免费上大学。你在多大程度上同意或不同意？）

**为什么选这道题**：① 教育类最高频，人人有话讲；② 有辩证空间，适合练"明确立场 + 深度让步"；③ 涉及政府/财政/公平/质量，能覆盖很多话题的论证思路。

**先写一句话立场**（第一反应最诚实）：
> I only partly agree with the idea of making university education completely free. While removing tuition fees would help disadvantaged students access higher education, a blanket free-tuition policy would place an unsustainable burden on public finances and could reduce educational quality.

# 阶段一 · 思维拉伸（先把中文逻辑想透）

> 目标：不急着写英文。用中文把每个论点推成 **3–4 层因果链**，做到"结论之下有机制、机制之下有结果、结果之下有最坏/最好情况"。

## 1.1 破题：题目到底在问什么

- **题型**：Agree / Disagree（必须立场明确，可承认对方合理但最后回自己立场）。
- **核心词展开**：government（职责/财政有限）｜ university education（高教/技能/社会流动）｜ free for all（公平/全覆盖是否该有针对性）。
- **表面争论**：大学该不该免费？**深层**：公共财政怎么分？公平与效率怎么平衡？

**常见立场与风险**：

| 立场 | 风险 |
|------|------|
| 完全同意 | 易写空、缺反驳，显得思考单一 |
| 完全不同意 | 易显冷漠，忽视教育公平价值 |
| 部分同意（推荐） | 最难写但最易高分：既认免费价值，又指"全民免费"的问题 |

**示范立场**：
> I only partly agree with the idea of making university education completely free. While removing tuition fees would help disadvantaged students access higher education, a blanket free-tuition policy would place an unsustainable burden on public finances and could reduce educational quality.

---

## 1.2 推因果链：论点不能像口号（我是怎么一步步想出来的）

模板：`前提（事实）→ 机制（为什么导致这结果）→ 结果（对学生/社会/政府影响）→ 最坏/最好情况（推到底）`

**下面用"自言自语"的方式，还原我怎么一步步把每条链想出来**——你写自己题时，也照这个"追问下一层"的办法推。

**因果链 A：免费教育促进公平**

> 我怎么想的：
>
> 题目说"免费"。免费对谁影响最大？——付不起钱的人。
>
> 为什么？因为学费是现金支出，穷人手里没现金。所以第一环：贫困家庭付不起学费（这是"免费"政策的直接反面）。
>
> 那付不起会怎样？现实里只有两条路：要么借钱上（负债），要么不上（放弃）。这是学生的两个真实反应。
>
> 再往下：如果很多有才华的穷学生因为没钱放弃，社会损失什么？——人才没被培养出来，而"穷人靠读书变富"的通道（社会流动）被堵死。
>
> 推到底：长期看，穷的更穷、富的更富，贫富差距固化。

链条：**贫困家庭付不起学费 → 要么负债要么放弃高教 → 社会流动下降、人才被浪费 → 长期贫富差距固化**

**因果链 B：全民免费加重财政**

> 我怎么想的：
>
> "免费"意味着谁出钱？原本学生交的学费，现在政府替学生出。所以政府要承担原本由学生家庭承担的全部学费。
>
> 政府钱从哪来？税收，而税收总额有限。把一大块税收拿去补学费，别的领域就少了。
>
> 哪些领域会被挤？医疗、基础教育、基建——这些是民生基本盘。
>
> 推到底：如果基本盘被砍，整体社会福利反而下降，等于"为了教育伤了别的公平"。

链条：**大学成本高 → 全民免费=政府承担所有学费 → 公共预算被大量占用 → 医疗/基础教育/基建资金减少 → 整体社会福利反而降**

**因果链 C：财政依赖影响质量**

> 我怎么想的：
>
> 大学如果完全靠政府拨款，它的收入来源就只剩一个。没有学费收入，也没有社会捐赠、企业合作等多元收入。
>
> 钱少了，学校会省在哪里？设施更新慢、薪资没竞争力、留不住好老师。
>
> 师资和设施差了，会怎样？国际排名和竞争力下降。
>
> 推到底：毕业生质量跟着下滑，最后损害的是学生自己和国家利益——这跟"免费为了学生好"的初衷反而矛盾。

链条：**大学依赖政府拨款 → 经费受限、难更新设施吸引师资 → 国际竞争力降 → 毕业生质量滑、损及学生国家利益**

**写成"主题句 + 支撑句"示范（Body 1 让步）**：
- 主题句：It is undeniable that tuition fees can prevent talented but disadvantaged students from attending university.
- 支撑1：For many low-income families, the cost of several years of tuition and living expenses is simply unaffordable.
- 支撑2：As a result, some capable students either accumulate substantial debt or abandon higher education altogether.
- 支撑3：Over time, this pattern limits social mobility and means that valuable talent is wasted simply because of family background.

## 1.3 写中文段：逻辑说清，再想英文

> 开头：大学教育常被视为向上流动的重要途径，因此有人认为政府应取消所有学费。虽然免费教育确实能促进公平，但我只部分同意，因为全民免费会给公共财政带来巨大压力，并可能影响教育质量。
>
> Body1（让步）：不可否认，学费确实会阻碍一些贫困家庭的有才华学生。对许多低收入家庭，几年学费加生活费根本负担不起。因此，一些有能力的学生要么背负沉重债务，要么干脆放弃上大学。长此以往，社会流动性受限，宝贵人才仅因家庭背景被浪费。
>
> Body2（主论·财政）：然而，为所有人提供免费大学教育需要庞大公共支出。高教成本高昂，若政府承担所有学费，必从其他领域削减预算。这意味着医疗、基础教育或基建可能获得更少资金。最终，全民福利整体水平反而可能下降。
>
> Body3（主论·质量+方案）：此外，完全依赖政府拨款的大学可能资源不足。缺乏竞争和多元收入会削弱学校改善设施、吸引优秀师资的动力。结果是大学国际竞争力下降，毕业生质量受影响。因此更好的做法是按需补助或基于收入的助学贷款，而非一刀切免费。
>
> 结尾：总之，虽然取消学费能帮弱势学生，但全民免费大学既不财政可持续，也不是公共资金最公平的用法。政府应把资源集中在真正有需要的学生身上，而非让所有人受益。

# 阶段二 · PEEL 落地成段（把逻辑落成英文）

> PEEL = **P**oint（主题句）· **E**xplain（解释）· **E**xample/Elaborate（举例/展开）· **L**ink（回扣立场/段旨）。
>
> 本阶段先搭骨架，再把中文段直译成英文"堆"出来——不追求一步到位。

## 2.1 搭骨架（五段式）

| 段落 | 功能 | 内容 |
|------|------|------|
| Intro | 破题+立场 | 改写题目，亮"部分同意"，预告两 body 方向 |
| Body 1 | 让步 | 免费大学好处：促进公平、减轻债务 |
| Body 2 | 主论1·财政 | 全民免费=不可持续财政负担 |
| Body 3 | 主论2·质量+方案 | 质量下滑 + 替代方案（按需补助/助学贷款） |
| Conclusion | 收束 | 重申部分同意，给最终判断 |

> 7.5 不一定要 5 段，4 段也行；5 段只为把"让步"和"主论"拆清。

**骨架示范**：
- Intro：University education is often seen as a route to social mobility, and some argue that governments should remove tuition fees entirely. While free higher education has clear benefits for equality, I only partly agree with this view, because making it universal would strain public finances and could lower standards.
- Body1：It is undeniable that tuition fees create barriers for students from low-income families...
- Body2：However, providing free university education for everyone would require enormous public spending...
- Body3：Furthermore, universities that depend entirely on state funding may become under-resourced...
- Conclusion：In conclusion, while removing fees would help disadvantaged students, universal free university education is neither financially sustainable nor the fairest use of public money...

## 2.2 直译版（先把中文一句句翻过去，不准查词典）

> Intro：University education is often seen as an important way to move up. So some people think the government should cancel all tuition fees. Although free education can really promote fairness, I only partly agree, because free education for everyone will bring huge pressure to public finance and may affect education quality.
>
> Body1：It cannot be denied that tuition fees really stop some talented students from poor families. For many low-income families, several years of tuition and living costs are unaffordable. So some capable students either carry heavy debt or give up going to university. For a long time, social mobility will be limited, and valuable talent is wasted just because of family background.
>
> Body2：However, providing free university education for everyone needs huge public spending. Higher education is very expensive. If the government pays tuition for all students, it must cut budgets from other areas. This means healthcare, basic education, or infrastructure may get less money. Finally, the overall level of public welfare may drop.
>
> Body3：Besides, universities that only depend on government money may lack resources. Without competition and diverse income sources, schools have less motivation to improve facilities and attract good teachers. The result is that universities' international competitiveness falls, and graduate quality is affected. Therefore, a better way is to provide need-based subsidies or income-based student loans, not free education for all.
>
> Conclusion：In conclusion, although canceling tuition fees can help disadvantaged students, free university for everyone is not financially sustainable and is not the fairest use of public money. The government should focus resources on students who really need help, not let everyone benefit.

**直译版的问题（先认账，下一步改）**：表达口语化（way to move up / very expensive）；衔接单调（So/Besides/Therefore 反复）；中式结构；几乎无分词/定从/被动。

# 阶段三 · 英文升级（从"说得对"到"说得好"）

> 语法正确只到 6.0–6.5。冲 7.5 要：① 词汇精准（用对词不用大词）② 同义替换 ③ 句式多样（分词/定从/被动/形式主语）④ 衔接自然（衔接词是逻辑信号）。

## 3.1 改语法：先灭硬伤

逐句查常见硬伤：
1. 主谓一致：government 单数 → it/its（❌ they deserve it → ✅ it would widen access）
2. 时态：大作文主要一般现在时，仅举过去例用过去时
3. 冠词：✅ important for society（❌ the society）
4. 单复数：✅ Universities give students chances
5. 从句双主语：✅ Students who cannot afford tuition often give up（❌ ..., they often give up）
6. 介词：✅ reduce the burden **on** public finance（❌ of）

**Intro 逐句改**：
- University education is often seen as a pathway to social mobility.
- Consequently, some argue that governments should abolish tuition fees entirely.
- While removing tuition fees would undoubtedly promote equality, I only partly agree with this proposal, because universal free higher education would place an unsustainable burden on public finances and could undermine educational standards.

> 建议用**朗读法**：读不顺的地方往往就是语法/衔接问题。

## 3.2 升级语言

| 普通 | 升级 |
|------|------|
| help poor students | enable disadvantaged students to access higher education |
| very expensive | financially demanding / costly |
| give up | abandon higher education altogether |
| bad for quality | undermine educational standards |
| need a lot of money | require enormous public expenditure |
| get less money | see their budgets squeezed |

**句式升级示范**：
原：Some capable students either carry heavy debt or give up going to university.
升：Faced with prohibitive costs, some capable students are forced either to take on substantial debt or to abandon higher education altogether.
（技巧：Faced with... 分词开头；prohibitive 替 very expensive；are forced to 被动；either...or... 对称）

**Body2/Body3 升级示范**：
- 普通：Higher education is very expensive. If the government pays tuition for all students, it must cut budgets from other areas.
- 升级：Since higher education is financially demanding, governments that abolished fees would have to divert funds from other essential services, such as healthcare, primary education, and infrastructure.
- 普通：Without competition, schools have less motivation to improve facilities.
- 升级：Without competition and diverse income streams, institutions have less incentive to invest in modern facilities, research, and high-quality teaching staff.

# 阶段四 · 考场复用库（让步+结尾+完整范文）

> 这一步的产物，就是考场上能直接复用的"库"。相关真实句库见 `../Appendix/A-大作文通用句型骨架库.md`。

## 4.1 让步段：承认合理，立刻回立场

结构：主题句承认 → 1–2 句展开 → However/That said/Nevertheless 回立场。
> It is undeniable that tuition fees can act as a barrier to talented students from disadvantaged backgrounds. For many low-income families, the combined cost of tuition and living expenses is prohibitive, and some capable students are forced either to take on substantial debt or to abandon higher education altogether. **However**, a blanket policy of free tuition for all students is not the most efficient way to address this problem, because it would also subsidise those who can afford to pay.

## 4.2 结尾段：重申+概括+推进一层（别复读开头）

> In conclusion, while removing tuition fees would undoubtedly widen access for disadvantaged students, making university free for everyone is neither financially sustainable nor the fairest use of public resources. A more effective approach would be to provide targeted financial support, such as means-tested grants and income-contingent loans, so that limited government funds reach those who need them most.

**框架复用**：
- 让步：It is undeniable that ____________. For example, ____________. However, ____________.
- 结尾：In conclusion, while ____________, ____________. A more effective approach would be to ____________.

## 4.3 完整 7.5 范文（全文标注）

> University education is widely regarded as a pathway to better career prospects and social mobility, and some argue that governments should remove tuition fees to make it accessible to all. While free higher education has obvious appeal, I only partly agree with this view, because a blanket policy would strain public finances and could reduce educational quality.

【标注】widely regarded as / pathway to... / remove tuition fees / While... has obvious appeal 让步开头 / only partly agree 明确立场 / a blanket policy 精准批"一刀切" / strain public finances.

> It is undeniable that tuition fees can act as a barrier to talented students from disadvantaged backgrounds. For many low-income families, the combined cost of tuition and living expenses is simply prohibitive, meaning that some capable students are forced either to take on substantial debt or to abandon higher education altogether. Over time, this pattern limits social mobility and means that valuable potential is wasted merely because of family background.

【标注】It is undeniable that... 经典让步 / act as a barrier to / disadvantaged backgrounds / the combined cost... / prohibitive / meaning that... 分词表结果 / either...or... 对称 / merely because of family background 推到底.

> However, providing free university education for everyone would require enormous public expenditure. Since higher education is financially demanding, governments that abolished fees would have to divert funds from other essential services, such as healthcare, primary education, and infrastructure. As a result, the overall quality of public services could decline, leaving society worse off despite the apparent benefit of free tuition.

【标注】However 转折 / providing... 动名词主语 / enormous public expenditure / financially demanding / divert funds from... / As a result / leaving society worse off 分词结果 / despite... 回扣让步.

> Furthermore, universities that depend entirely on state funding may become under-resourced. Without competition and diverse income streams, institutions have less incentive to invest in modern facilities, research, and high-quality teaching staff. This can gradually undermine their international competitiveness and, ultimately, the value of the degrees they award. A more sensible policy, therefore, would be to offer means-tested grants or income-contingent loans, which target assistance at those who genuinely need it.

【标注】Furthermore 递进 / depend entirely on state funding / under-resourced / diverse income streams / undermine... competitiveness / A more sensible policy, therefore, would be to... / means-tested grants / income-contingent loans 专业术语 / target assistance at those who genuinely need it 回扣公平.

> In conclusion, while removing tuition fees would help disadvantaged students access higher education, universal free university education is neither financially sustainable nor the fairest use of public money. Governments should therefore provide targeted financial support to those in need, rather than extending the same subsidy to every student regardless of their ability to pay.

【标注】重申换表达 / neither... nor... 平行 / rather than extending the same subsidy to every student regardless of their ability to pay 精准收尾 / 给明确建议，结尾有推进.
