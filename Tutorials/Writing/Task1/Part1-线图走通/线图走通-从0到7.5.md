# Part 1 · 小作文线图走通：从 0 到 7.5（按"路程"走一遍）

> 主线题（来源文件 `Writing-examples/Writing-2024-11-02.md`，题目与范文已完整贴在下方）：
>
> **The chart below shows the average monthly contract cost for four different mobile phones in a European country from January to September 2002, measured in euros.**
>
> ![2024-11-02](../../../../Writing-examples/Task1_img/2024-11-02.png)

本文件把线图写作按真实考场操作重排，小作文**没有 PEEL**，就三步加一遍打磨：

> **① 首段改写**（把题干改写成英文，不发挥）→ **② Overview**（写主要趋势，不写具体数字）→ **③ 主体段**（列数字 + 分析主要不同）→ **④ 改写润色实词替换**（把列出来的句子升级，再拼成范文）。
>
> 核心原则：**有什么写什么**。图里有什么数据，就先写一句出来；再对这一句做句式改写、润色、实词替换。

---

# 阶段一 · 读图与分组（先读图，再动笔）

> 目标：30 秒内搞清楚对象/时间/单位/极值/关系；用"分组"取代逐条罗列；用一句话概括全局（Overview 雏形）。

## 1.1 看数据：读图五要素

| 要素 | 问题 |
|------|------|
| 对象 | 几条线？代表什么？ |
| 时间 | 起止？连续还是离散年份？ |
| 单位 | 百分比/人数/金额？ |
| 范围 | 最大/最小、起点/终点？ |
| 关系 | 谁高谁低、谁升谁降、有无交叉反超？ |

**主线题扫描**：
- 对象：4 品牌 Domo / Lex / Sim TX / Alpha
- 时间：2002 年 1–9 月；单位：月合约费（欧元）
- 关键数据：Domo ~€15→7月峰~€25→9月~€23（多数时间最高）；Lex ~€12 先降后升→9月~€18；Alpha ~€10 前低后高→9月~€25（反超最高）；Sim TX ~€10 波动→6月短高→9月~€7（最低）
- 关键发现：三升一降；Alpha 从最低反超最高；Domo 多数最高但 9 月被超。

## 1.2 分组：别逐条罗列

原则：① 按趋势 ② 按量级 ③ 按关系 ④ 不要每条线单独成段。
**推荐方案 A（按趋势）**：上升组 Domo/Lex/Alpha；波动下降组 Sim TX。
段落安排：Intro（改写）→ Overview（主要趋势）→ Body1（上升组）→ Body2（下降组）。

# 阶段二 · 首段改写 + Overview（主要趋势）

## 2.1 首段就是改写（不发挥）

小作文首段**只做一件事**：把题干用英文重述一遍，换词、不换信息。不要加自己的判断。
> The graph illustrates the changes in the typical price of a contract per month for Domo, Lex, Sim TX, and Alpha cellphones in a particular European country in the period from January to September 2002.

改写要点：shows→illustrates；average monthly contract cost→typical price of a contract per month；列出品牌名；in a European country→in a particular European country；from...to...→in the period from...。

## 2.2 Overview 写主要趋势（不写具体数字）

没有 Overview 最高只能 5.0–5.5。Overview 写：**整体趋势 + 极值/排名 + 主要对比**；**不写具体数字**。
> Overall, Domo maintained the highest average monthly cost for most of the period, while Alpha, which started as the cheapest, saw the sharpest rise and overtook the others by September. In contrast, Sim TX ended the period at the lowest point.

位置：放 Intro 之后最稳（Intro → Overview → Body1 → Body2）。

# 阶段三 · 主体段：列数字 + 分析主要不同

> 原则：**有什么写什么**。图里能看到的每一个关键数据点，先写一句平实的话；不同数据点之间，用"谁高谁低 / 谁反超 / 谁交叉"连成"主要不同"的分析。写完所有该写的句子，下一步再统一升级。

## 3.1 列数字：每个关键点写一句

按方案 A，先把上升组三品牌的关键节点各写一句（平实版，不追求高级）：
> Domo's monthly cost stood at about €15 in January and rose to around €25 in July, then dipped to €23 in September.
>
> Lex's cost began at €12, dropped to €10 in February, then rose to €18 by September.
>
> Alpha started lowest at about €10, and by September it became the highest at €25.
>
> Sim TX started near €10, rose a bit to just below €13 in June, then fell to the lowest at about €7 in September.

## 3.2 分析主要不同：谁高谁低、谁反超

光列数字不够，还要把"不同"说出来——这是 CC 和 TA 的得分点：
> Among the three risers, Domo stayed the highest for most of the period, but Alpha, which began the cheapest, overtook it by September.
>
> Sim TX was the only one to fall overall, ending far below the others.

# 阶段四 · 改写润色实词替换（把列出来的句子升级）

> 上一步已经把所有"该写的数据"写全了。这一步只做加工：句式改写（合并/分词/定从）、润色（趋势动词精准）、实词替换（同义换名词动词避免重复）。加工完，拼起来就是范文。

## 4.1 句式改写（4 种）

1. **分词短语表伴随/结果**：Alpha's contract price remained the lowest..., surging to become the highest at €25 in September.
2. **定语从句补信息**：Domo's monthly cost stood at approximately €15 in January, which was roughly €3 above Lex's figure.
3. **独立主格链**：Sim TX's price sharply declined, falling below those of Alpha in July and arriving at the lowest monthly cost of approximately €7 in September.
4. **介词短语多重比较**：Sim TX recorded the second-largest monthly cost in June, at almost €1 and €9 above Lex's and Alpha's respectively.

## 4.2 润色：趋势动词精准化

| 平实 | 润色 |
|------|------|
| rose | climbed / surged / soared |
| fell | dropped / declined / plunged |
| went up and down | fluctuated |
| was the highest | maintained the greatest... / peaked at |

## 4.3 实词替换：避免重复

- increase→rise / grow / climb / surge
- decrease→fall / drop / decline / plunge
- cost→price / expenditure / figure
- mobile phone→cellphone / handset / device

**升级示范**（把 3.1 的平实句加工）：
> As seen, the expenditures for Domo, Lex, and Alpha showed general upward trends, despite having some fluctuations. In January, Domo's monthly cost stood at approximately €15 and gradually rose in the succeeding months. By July, it had peaked at around €25 before it slightly dipped to €23 in September. Similarly, Lex's monthly payment plan began at €12 but fell slightly to €10 in February. However, it then experienced a gradual increase, reaching €18 by September. In contrast, Alpha's contract price remained the lowest during the first half of 2002, but it surged and became the highest at €25 in September.

# 阶段五 · 完整范文与复习

## 5.1 完整 7.5 范文（全文标注）

> The graph illustrates the changes in the typical price of a contract per month for Domo, Lex, Sim TX, and Alpha cellphones in a particular European country in the period from January to September 2002.

【标注】illustrates 替 shows；typical price of a contract per month 改写；列出四品牌；in the period from... 改写时间。

> As seen, the expenditures for Domo, Lex, and Alpha showed general upward trends, despite having some fluctuations. In January, Domo's monthly cost stood at approximately €15 and gradually rose in the succeeding months. By July, it had peaked at around €25 before it slightly dipped to €23 in September. Similarly, Lex's monthly payment plan began at €12 but fell slightly to €10 in February. However, it then experienced a gradual increase, reaching €18 by September. In contrast, Alpha's contract price remained the lowest during the first half of 2002, but it surged and became the highest at €25 in September.

【标注】As seen 总→具；stood at... peaked at... dipped to 完整数据链；Similarly 组内；In contrast 引 Alpha；surged 强动词；became the highest 排名变化。

> On the other hand, Sim TX followed a different pattern. Starting at around €10 in January, its costs dropped by €1 before February, then rose slightly to just below €13 in June, with the second-largest monthly cost recorded (almost 1 and 9 euros more than Lex's and Alpha's, respectively). Despite that, after June, Sim TX's price sharply declined, falling below those of Alpha in July and arriving at the lowest monthly cost of approximately €7 in September.

【标注】On the other hand 组间过渡；Starting at... 分词开头；with the second-largest... recorded 独立主格；respectively 多重比较；Despite that 转折；falling below... and arriving at... 分词链。

> Overall, Domo maintained the greatest average monthly cost for most of the period, except in September. Meanwhile, Alpha, which had the lowest contract rate in the early months, saw a sharp upswing in its data toward the end.

【标注】maintained the greatest... 极值概括；except in September 准确处理；Meanwhile 并置；which... 定从补前期；saw a sharp upswing 趋势概括。

## 5.2 范文也不是满分（可提升空间）

1. Overview 只提 Domo/Alpha，可补 Lex/Sim TX 整体走向；2. "almost 1 and 9 euros more than... respectively" 较挤，可拆 "about €1 above Lex's and €9 above Alpha's"；3. Overview 可前置让阅卷人更快抓全局。

## 5.3 复习方法

1. 抄一遍体会趋势动词与衔接；2. 合上凭记忆重写；3. 对比找 3 个差距最大的句子；4. 用同一条路程，换一道线图再走一遍。
