# 表格（Table）

> 表格是 Task 1 里最"吓人"的图表——数据密密麻麻、行列交叉。但处理方法和线图/柱状图一致：**按变化方向或极值分组，抓重点不抓全部**。
>
> 表格没有图形暗示趋势，全靠你自己读出行列之间的规律。高分做法是**先给全图一个分组框架**，再往里填关键数字。
>
> 下面用 Part 1 主线程的同一套小作文流程走一遍：**首段改写 → Overview（主要趋势）→ 主体段（按趋势分组列数字 + 分析主要不同）→ 改写润色（实词替换 / 句式升级）**。

---

## 代表真题（五国税收占 GDP 比）

> **The table below gives information about the amount of tax collected (as % of GDP) in five countries for four years between 1975 and 2005.**
> **Summarise the information by selecting and reporting main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-03-29](../../../Writing-examples/Task1_img/2025-03-29.png)

---

## ① 首段改写（有什么数据，先写一句出来）

题目说"五个国家 1975–2005 四个年份的税收占 GDP 比"。改写时把 `gives information about` 升级，点名五国：

> 初稿改写：`The table shows tax as a percentage of GDP in five countries from 1975 to 2005.`
> 升级改写：`The table illustrates changes in the percentage of GDP generated as tax in Sweden, the USA, Korea, Japan, and Turkey from 1975 to 2005.`

**要点**：把 `table shows` 换成 `table illustrates changes in`；把 `five countries` 具体点名；用 `generated as tax` 把"税收占 GDP"这个关系说完整。

---

## ② Overview

看完全表，先想"最大的几件事是什么"：
- 五国税收占比**总体都上升**。
- Sweden 一直最高（且涨幅最猛，到 70.1%）；USA 几乎没动（25%–27% 波动）。

> Overview 初稿：`Overall, all countries' tax went up a bit, and Sweden was always the highest.`
> Overview 升级：`Overall, all five countries experienced an upturn in their tax-to-GDP ratios over the given period, with Sweden consistently having the highest percentage. However, the data in the USA saw only a marginal rise.`

**要点**：Overview 不写一串数字，只写"总体升 + 谁一直最高 + 谁几乎不动"这三层关系。

---

## ③ 主体段

**Body 1（上升/稳定组）**：

列数字试水句：
> Sweden went from 46% in 1975 to 51% in 1995, then jumped to 70.1% in 2005. Japan was only 15% in 1975 but more than doubled to 32.1% in 2005. The USA stayed between 25% and 27%.

分析不同试水句：
> Sweden and Japan both rose a lot, but Sweden rose the most. The USA was almost flat the whole time.

中文思路：这一组国家都在升（或基本持平）。重点是 Sweden 涨幅最猛、Japan 翻倍、USA 几乎不动——把"幅度差异"点出来，比平铺五个数字有用。

**Body 2（波动组）**：

列数字试水句：
> Korea went from 15.1% to 27.3%, with a small drop in 1995. Turkey fell first from 16.4% to 15%, then jumped to 27.4% in 2005.

分析不同试水句：
> Korea and Turkey did not rise smoothly. Turkey first fell then rose, and ended at almost the same level as Korea and the USA.

中文思路：波动组的关键是"不是一路涨"——Turkey 先降后升，Korea 中间有小回落。结尾点出它们最终和 USA 数字接近，把孤立数字连成关系。

---

## ④ 改写润色

**Body 1 列数字句升级**：
- 普通：Sweden went from 46% in 1975 to 51% in 1995, then jumped to 70.1% in 2005.
- 升：`In Sweden, the proportion stood at 46% in 1975, rose slightly to 51% in 1995, and then surged to a peak of 70.1% in 2005, the most significant increase among the five countries.`（`stood at / surged to a peak` 替 went/jumped；同位语补"最大涨幅"）
- 普通：Japan was only 15% in 1975 but more than doubled to 32.1% in 2005.
- 升：`Japan, which had the lowest figure in 1975 (15%), more than doubled its tax ratio to 32.1%, ranking second, in 2005.`（`which` 定从补背景；`more than doubled` 倍数表达；`ranking second` 补排名）
- 普通：The USA stayed between 25% and 27%.
- 升：`Meanwhile, the USA maintained a relatively stable tax share which fluctuated between 25% and 27% over the decades.`（`maintained a relatively stable` 替 stayed；`fluctuated between` 精准）

**Body 2 列数字句升级**：
- 普通：Korea went from 15.1% to 27.3%, with a small drop in 1995.
- 升：`In Korea, a significant increase was recorded, rising from 15.1% in 1975 to 27.3% in 2005, despite a slight decline in 1995.`（`despite a slight decline` 标出中间波动）
- 普通：Turkey fell first from 16.4% to 15%, then jumped to 27.4% in 2005.
- 升：`Similarly, Turkey's ratio initially fell from 16.4% in 1975 to 15% in 1985 before rebounding sharply to 27.4% in 2005, aligning with the final figures for both Korea and the USA.`（`rebounding sharply` 替 jumped；`aligning with` 点出最终并列关系）

把升后句子拼起来，就是下方⑤完整范文主体段。

---

## ⑤ 完整范文
The table illustrates changes in the percentage of GDP generated as tax in Sweden, the USA, Korea, Japan, and Turkey from 1975 to 2005.

As seen, Sweden, the USA, and Japan witnessed an upward trend in their tax revenue as a share of GDP. In Sweden, the proportion stood at 46% in 1975, rose slightly to 51% in 1995, and then surged to a peak of 70.1% in 2005, the most significant increase among the five countries. Japan, which had the lowest figure in 1975 (15%), more than doubled its tax ratio to 32.1%, ranking second, in 2005. Meanwhile, the USA maintained a relatively stable tax share which fluctuated between 25% and 27% over the decades.

In contrast, the tax-to-GDP ratios in Korea and Turkey showed greater fluctuation. In Korea, a significant increase was recorded, rising from 15.1% in 1975 to 27.3% in 2005, despite a slight decline in 1995. Similarly, Turkey's ratio initially fell from 16.4% in 1975 to 15% in 1985 before rebounding sharply to 27.4% in 2005, aligning with the final figures for both Korea and the USA.

Overall, all five countries experienced an upturn in their tax-to-GDP ratios over the given period, with Sweden consistently having the highest percentage. However, the data in the USA saw only a marginal rise.

## ⑥ 同类型变体（双维度表格 · 场馆收支）

这篇也是表格，但有"收入项 + 支出项 + 利润"多个维度。看它怎么处理**多列数据的取舍**。

**The table below shows the income and expenditure of Harckley Hall, a public venue available for hire in the UK, over a period of three years.**
![2025-05-24](../../../Writing-examples/Task1_img/2025-05-24.png)

The table illustrates changes in income from room hire, the café, and funding from both the local council and other sources of Harckley Hall, a public venue for hire in the UK, over a three-year period, with profit calculated as the difference between total income and expenditure.

As seen, Harckley Hall generated a total income of £83,500 in Year 1, and that figure rose slightly to £85,000 in Year 2 before dipping marginally to £84,000 in Year 3. Meanwhile, expenditure increased steadily from £56,000 in Year 1 to £62,000 in Year 3, with this upward trend in spending accompanied by a corresponding decline in profit from £27,500 to £22,000.

Among income contributors, the largest one each year was room hire, which brought in around 34,000 to 35,000 pounds. Café income also showed minor fluctuations but contributed the least (£3,000 to £4,000), while funding from the local council remained constant at £22,000 for the first two years before decreasing slightly to £21,000 in Year 3. In contrast, funding from other sources increased steadily, rising from £24,000 to £27,000 over the same period.

## 表格要点回顾

- 首段改写：点名行列对象 + 时间范围 + 单位；用 `illustrates changes in` 而非 `shows`。
- Overview：写"总体方向 + 谁一直最高/最低 + 有无异常平稳者"，不堆数字。
- 主体段：**先分组再填数**。按趋势稳定性（上升组 / 波动组）或按极值分组；每组内列起点—中点—终点，用 `stood at / surged to a peak / more than doubled` 让数字会说话。
- 润色：`went up` → `surged to a peak`；`went down` → `dipped marginally`；善用 `despite / aligning with / with... having`。
- 多维度表：先抓"总额/净差"主线，再展开分项，别被列数吓住。
