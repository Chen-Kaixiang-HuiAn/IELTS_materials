# 饼图（Pie Chart）

> 饼图展示的是**比例 / 占比**关系。它不考"绝对值大小"，考的是**份额多少、谁最大谁最小、占比怎么分布**。
>
> 饼图最容易写成"国家 × 类别"的格子清单。高分的做法是**以类别为纲、以国家为目**——把占比按能源/群体重新分组，而不是逐个国家平铺。
>
> 下面用 Part 1 主线程的同一套小作文流程走一遍：**首段改写 → Overview（主要占比结构）→ 主体段（按类别列比例 + 点出极值与镜像对比）→ 改写润色（实词替换 / 句式升级）**。

---

## 代表真题（四国电力来源占比）

> **The charts below show the percentages of electricity produced from different sources in four countries in 2008.**
> **Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2026-07-11](../../../Writing-examples/Task1_img/2026-07-11.png)

---

## ① 首段改写（有什么数据，先写一句出来）

题目说"四个国家 2008 年不同电力来源的百分比"。改写时把模糊的 `different sources` 具体化为三类能源：

> 初稿改写：`The charts show the percentage of electricity from different sources in four countries in 2008.`
> 升级改写：`The pie charts compare the proportions of electricity generated from nuclear power, fossil fuels and hydropower in Sweden, Vietnam, Morocco and India in 2008.`

**要点**：把 `charts show` 换成 `pie charts compare the proportions of`；把 `different sources` 具体点名为 `nuclear power, fossil fuels and hydropower`——信息量比原题高，是加分改写。

---

## ② Overview

看完全图，先想"最大的结构是什么"：
- 三个能源的领跑者各不相同：核能 → 瑞典；化石燃料 → 越南、印度；水电 → 摩洛哥。
- 这是一张"每个国家靠不同能源"的图，Overview 用一句话分述三国主导能源最清楚。

> Overview 初稿：`Overall, different countries used different main energy sources.`
> Overview 升级：`Overall, the dominant source of electricity varied markedly among the four countries: fossil fuels led in Vietnam and India, hydropower was the main source in Morocco, and nuclear power accounted for the largest share in Sweden.`

**要点**：Overview 不写数字，只写"主导能源因国而异"这个**结构关系**，并用冒号分述三个领跑者。

---

## ③ 主体段

**Body 1（核能 + 化石燃料，合并段）**：

列数字试水句：
> Nuclear power was the biggest in Sweden, at 50%. India and Morocco only had 5% and 2%. Vietnam had none. Fossil fuels were the biggest in Vietnam (75%) and India (65%), but Sweden only had 15%.

分析不同试水句：
> Sweden led in nuclear but was last in fossil fuels. Vietnam was the opposite: first in fossil fuels but zero in nuclear. The two countries swap places between the two energy types.

中文思路：把核能和化石燃料压进同一段，是因为瑞典和越南在两种能源上**此消彼长**——瑞典核能第一、化石垫底；越南化石第一、核能为零。同一段里直接对照，比跨段比较更省力。

**Body 2（水电）**：

列数字试水句：
> Hydropower was important in all four countries. Morocco used it most (55%), then Sweden (35%), India (30%) and Vietnam (25%).

分析不同试水句：
> Unlike the other two energy types, hydropower mattered everywhere, but Morocco depended on it the most.

中文思路：水电是唯一的"共性"维度——四国都用得不少，但程度不同。按降序串成一条链（55→35→30→25），读者一眼能排序。

---

## ④ 改写润色

**Body 1 列数字句升级**：
- 普通：Nuclear power was the biggest in Sweden, at 50%.
- 升：`As shown, nuclear power was the largest source of electricity in Sweden, supplying half of the country's total.`（`supplying half` 把 50% 换成文字，避免百分号堆砌）
- 普通：India and Morocco only had 5% and 2%.
- 升：`This was far higher than the corresponding proportions in India and Morocco, where nuclear energy made up only 5% and 2% respectively.`（`the corresponding proportions` + `where` 定从把两国压进一句）
- 普通：Vietnam had none.
- 升：`Vietnam, by contrast, produced no electricity from nuclear power.`（0% 不写 "0%"，用 `produced no electricity` 更像英语）
- 普通：Fossil fuels were the biggest in Vietnam (75%) and India (65%), but Sweden only had 15%.
- 升：`Fossil fuels, however, were the dominant source in Vietnam and India. In the former, three quarters of electricity came from this source, the highest proportion among the four countries. India showed a similar pattern (65%), while Sweden recorded the lowest share (15%).`（`In the former` / `this source` 双回指避重复）

**Body 2 列数字句升级**：
- 普通：Hydropower was important in all four countries.
- 升：`Regarding hydropower, it made a substantial contribution in all four countries, although its importance varied considerably.`（`Regarding` 开启新维度；`although` 预告差异）

把升后句子拼起来，就是下方⑤完整范文主体段。

---

## ⑤ 完整范文
The pie charts compare the proportions of electricity generated from nuclear power, fossil fuels and hydropower in Sweden, Vietnam, Morocco and India in 2008.

As shown, nuclear power was the largest source of electricity in Sweden, supplying half of the country's total. This was far higher than the corresponding proportions in India and Morocco, where nuclear energy made up only 5% and 2% respectively. Vietnam, by contrast, produced no electricity from nuclear power. Fossil fuels, however, were the dominant source in Vietnam and India. In the former, three quarters of electricity came from this source, the highest proportion among the four countries. India showed a similar pattern (65%), while Sweden recorded the lowest share (15%).

Regarding hydropower, it made a substantial contribution in all four countries, although its importance varied considerably. Morocco relied most heavily on this source, with hydropower providing 55% of its total electricity. In Sweden, 35% of electricity also came from hydropower, while the figures for India and Vietnam were slightly lower, at 30% and 25% respectively.

Overall, the dominant source of electricity varied markedly among the four countries: fossil fuels led in Vietnam and India, hydropower was the main source in Morocco, and nuclear power accounted for the largest share in Sweden.

## ⑥ 同类型变体（双饼图对比 · 上学方式）

这篇也是饼图，但讲"两个年份的饼图对比"：看它怎么处理**同群体在两年的份额迁移**。

**The charts below show the percentages of students travelling to school by different modes of transport in 2010 and 2015.**
![2025-12-06](../../../Writing-examples/Task1_img/2025-12-06.png)

The pie charts compare the proportions of students who travelled to school by bus, bicycle, car, and on foot in 2010 and 2015.

As illustrated, in 2010, walking was the most common mode of transport, accounting for 42% of all students, followed by taking the bus at 30%. Cycling and being driven by car represented smaller shares, at 18% and 10% respectively. In 2015, however, the pattern shifted noticeably: the proportion of students taking the bus rose to 45%, becoming the leading mode, while walking dropped to 28%. Car use doubled from 10% to 20%, whereas the share of cyclists fell slightly from 18% to 7%.

Overall, bus travel gained the largest share and replaced walking as the dominant mode, while car use increased and cycling declined over the five-year period.

---

## 饼图要点回顾

- 首段改写：把模糊的 `different sources` 具体化为各类别；用 `compare the proportions of` 而非 `show`。
- Overview：写"主导类别因对象而异"的结构关系，冒号分述各领跑者，不写数字。
- 主体段：**以类别为纲、以对象为目**。可把"此消彼长"的两类压进同一段直接对照；共性维度单独成段按降序串链。
- 润色：`was the biggest` → `was the largest source`；`had none` → `produced no electricity`；善用 `As shown / by contrast / Regarding / respectively`。
- 双饼图：同一群体两年对比，重点写"份额迁移"（谁升谁降、谁换位）。
