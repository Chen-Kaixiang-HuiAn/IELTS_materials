# 线图（Line Graph）

> 线图是 Task 1 最基础也最高频的图表。它考的是**随时间的变化趋势**：上升 / 下降 / 波动 / 稳定，以及多条线之间的**交叉与对比**。
>
> 下面用 Part 1 主线程的同一套小作文流程走一遍：**首段改写 → Overview（主要趋势）→ 主体段（列数字 + 分析主要不同）→ 改写润色（实词替换 / 句式升级）**。

---

## 代表真题（多线图 · 4 个手机品牌月度合约价）

> **The chart below shows the average monthly contract cost for four different mobile phones (cell phones) in a European country from January to September 2002, measured in euros.**
> **Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2024-11-02](../../../Writing-examples/Task1_img/2024-11-02.png)

---

## ① 首段改写（有什么数据，先写一句出来）

题目说"四个手机品牌 2002 年 1-9 月月度合约价（欧元）"。改写时**只换词、不发挥**：

> 初稿改写：`The chart shows the monthly contract price of four mobile phones from January to September 2002.`
> 升级改写：`The graph illustrates the changes in the typical price of a contract per month for Domo, Lex, Sim TX, and Alpha cellphones in a particular European country in the period from January to September 2002.`

**要点**：把 `chart shows` 换成 `graph illustrates the changes in`；把 `four mobile phones` 具体点名四个品牌；补 `in a particular European country` 把题目里的地点坐实。

---

## ② Overview

看完全图，先想"最大的几件事是什么"：
- 三条线（Domo / Lex / Alpha）总体上升，一条（Sim TX）先升后暴跌。
- Domo 大部分时间最贵；Alpha 从最低冲到最高；Sim TX 最后最便宜。

> Overview 初稿：`Overall, most phones became more expensive, but one became cheaper.`
> Overview 升级：`Overall, Domo maintained the greatest average monthly cost for most of the period, except in September. Meanwhile, Alpha, which had the lowest contract rate in the early months, saw a sharp upswing in its data toward the end.`

**要点**：Overview 不写数字，只写"谁一直最高 / 谁逆势 / 谁最后最低"这种**关系**。

---

## ③ 主体段

**Body 1（三条上升线）**：

列数字试水句：
> Domo's price was about 15 euros in January and went up to 25 in July, then went down a bit to 23 in September.
> Lex started at 12 euros in January, dropped to 10 in February, then went up slowly to 18 in September.
> Alpha was the lowest at first, about 10 euros, but went up to 25 in September and became the highest.

分析不同试水句：
> Domo went up slowly and was the most expensive most of the time. Lex first went down then went up. Alpha was different because it started low but ended high.

中文思路：三条线都升，但节奏不同——Domo 稳升、Lex 先降后升、Alpha 逆袭。先把数字句写出来，再用分析句点出"谁一直最高 / 谁逆势"。

**Body 2（Sim TX 异常线）**：

列数字试水句：
> Sim TX was about 10 euros in January and went up to 13 in June, which was the second highest. But after June it went down fast and was only 7 euros in September, the lowest.

分析不同试水句：
> Sim TX was the only line that went up first and then dropped a lot. The other three lines all went up, so Sim TX was the opposite.

中文思路：Sim TX 是唯一"先升后断崖"的线，和其他三条反向。这句分析要突出"反向"，是 Overview 之外最关键的对比点。

---

## ④ 改写润色

**Body 1 列数字句升级**：
- 普通：Domo's price was about 15 euros in January and went up to 25 in July, then went down a bit to 23 in September.
- 升：`In January, Domo's monthly cost stood at approximately €15 and gradually rose in the succeeding months. By July, it had peaked at around €25 before it slightly dipped to €23 in September.`（`stood at / peaked at / dipped` 替 went up/down；过去完成时体现时序）
- 普通：Lex started at 12 euros in January, dropped to 10 in February, then went up slowly to 18 in September.
- 升：`Lex's monthly payment plan began at €12 but fell slightly to €10 in February. However, it then experienced a gradual increase, reaching €18 by September.`
- 普通：Alpha was the lowest at first, about 10 euros, but went up to 25 in September and became the highest.
- 升：`In contrast, Alpha's contract price remained the lowest during the first half of 2002, but it surged and became the highest at €25 in September.`（`In contrast` 衔接；`surged` 替 went up fast）

**Body 1 分析句升级**：
- 普通：Domo went up slowly and was the most expensive most of the time.
- 升：`Domo maintained the greatest average monthly cost for most of the period.`（名词化 `maintained the greatest... cost` 替口语）
- 普通：Alpha was different because it started low but ended high.
- 升：`Alpha, which had the lowest contract rate in the early months, saw a sharp upswing in its data toward the end.`（`which` 定从；`sharp upswing` 替 went up fast）

**Body 2 列数字句升级**：
- 普通：Sim TX was about 10 euros in January and went up to 13 in June, which was the second highest. But after June it went down fast and was only 7 euros in September, the lowest.
- 升：`Starting at around €10 in January, its costs dropped by €1 before February, then rose slightly to just below €13 in June, with the second-largest monthly cost recorded. Despite that, after June, Sim TX's price sharply declined, falling below those of Alpha in July and arriving at the lowest monthly cost of approximately €7 in September.`（分词开头 `Starting at`；`sharply declined / falling below` 替 went down fast）

**Body 2 分析句升级**：
- 普通：Sim TX was the only line that went up first and then dropped a lot. The other three lines all went up, so Sim TX was the opposite.
- 升：`Sim TX followed a different pattern.` + `On the other hand, Sim TX followed a different pattern...`（用 `followed a different pattern` 一句话点出反向，简洁有力）

把升后句子拼起来，就是下方⑤完整范文主体段。

---

## ⑤ 完整范文
The graph illustrates the changes in the typical price of a contract per month for Domo, Lex, Sim TX, and Alpha cellphones in a particular European country in the period from January to September 2002.

As seen, the expenditures for Domo, Lex, and Alpha showed general upward trends, despite having some fluctuations. In January, Domo's monthly cost stood at approximately €15 and gradually rose in the succeeding months. By July, it had peaked at around €25 before it slightly dipped to €23 in September. Similarly, Lex's monthly payment plan began at €12 but fell slightly to €10 in February. However, it then experienced a gradual increase, reaching €18 by September. In contrast, Alpha's contract price remained the lowest during the first half of 2002, but it surged and became the highest at €25 in September.

On the other hand, Sim TX followed a different pattern. Starting at around €10 in January, its costs dropped by €1 before February, then rose slightly to just below €13 in June, with the second-largest monthly cost recorded (almost 1 and 9 euros more than Lex's and Alpha's, respectively). Despite that, after June, Sim TX's price sharply declined, falling below those of Alpha in July and arriving at the lowest monthly cost of approximately €7 in September.

Overall, Domo maintained the greatest average monthly cost for most of the period, except in September. Meanwhile, Alpha, which had the lowest contract rate in the early months, saw a sharp upswing in its data toward the end.

## ⑥ 同类型变体（单线多对象 · 旅游咨询方式）

这篇也是线图，但讲的是"三种咨询方式 6 个月变化"，看它怎么处理"多对象同图、有升有降"。


**The graph below shows the number of enquiries received by the tourist information office in one city over a six-month period in 2011.**
![2024-11-16](../../../Writing-examples/Task1_img/2024-11-16.png)

The graph illustrates changes in the volume of inquiries handled by a tourist information office in a particular city through in-person visits, letters/emails, and telephone calls during the first half of 2011.

As seen, enquiries made by telephone and in person both increased considerably in general. Notably, the former started at 900 in January, accounting for the highest number among the three methods in that month, and it experienced slight fluctuations in the first three months before rising steadily to 1,600 in June. Meanwhile, despite the latter being by far the lowest (around 400), the figure for it grew steadily each month, surpassing that for letter or email enquiries and telephone enquiries in March and April, respectively. By June, in-person enquiries reached approximately 1,900, the highest volume recorded.

On the other hand, queries made by letter/email registered at around 750 in January and decreased gradually throughout the six months, dropping to just under 400 by May and stabilizing onwards. This method became the least preferred after March, coinciding with the rising popularity of in-person and telephone communication.

Overall, questions made in person and by telephone surged over the period, while those made by letter or email saw a steady decline. Among the three methods, in-person enquiries experienced the most rapid growth, eventually becoming the most common means of communication.

## 线图要点回顾

- 首段改写：点名所有对象 + 时间 + 单位，别只说 "the chart shows"。
- Overview：写"总体升/降 + 谁最高最低 + 有无逆势线"，不写数字。
- 主体段：每条线先列关键数字，再分析它和别的线的不同（交叉点、谁超过谁）。
- 润色：`rose` → `showed an upward trend`；`was highest` → `peaked at`；善用 `Similarly / In contrast / Despite that`。
