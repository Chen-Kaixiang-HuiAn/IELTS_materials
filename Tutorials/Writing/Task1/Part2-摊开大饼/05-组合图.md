# 组合图（Mixed Charts）

> 组合图通常由两个不同类型的图表组成，比如**柱状图 + 饼图**、**线图 + 表格**等。处理原则：**分别描述每个图，再点出两图之间的联系**。
>
> 组合图最怕写成"两个独立图硬拼"。高分做法是**先各自说清，再找交叉点**——比如图 A 是收入、图 B 是就业，那么"收入最高的部门是否也是雇佣人数最多的部门"就是最值钱的联系。
>
> 下面用 Part 1 主线程的同一套小作文流程走一遍：**首段改写（涵盖两图）→ Overview → 主体段（分图列数字 + 点出两图联系）→ 改写润色（实词替换 / 句式升级）**。

---

## 代表真题（英国音乐产业 · 收入柱状图 + 就业柱状图）

> **The charts below give information about the UK music industry by sector, in three years 2013, 2014 and 2015. Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-02-15](../../../Writing-examples/Task1_img/2025-02-15.png)

---

## ① 首段改写（涵盖两图，先写一句出来）

题目说"英国音乐产业分部门，2013–2015 三年"。图里实际有收入（柱状）和就业（柱状）两套数据，改写时要**都提到**：

> 初稿改写：`The charts show the UK music industry in 2013, 2014 and 2015.`
> 升级改写：`The bar charts illustrate changes in the total income from live music, music publishing, and recorded music, as well as the number of workers employed in those sectors in the UK from 2013 to 2015.`

**要点**：把 `charts show` 换成 `bar charts illustrate changes in`；明确点出两个维度——`total income`（收入）和 `number of workers employed`（就业）；三个部门也要点名。

---

## ② Overview

看完全图，先想"两图各自的头条是什么"：
- 收入：现场音乐（live music）一直最高且上升；唱片音乐（recorded music）明显下降。
- 就业：现场音乐雇佣人数也一直最多，且三年翻了近三倍。
- 联系：现场音乐在"收入和就业"两个维度都是领头羊。

> Overview 初稿：`Overall, live music made the most money and employed the most people. Recorded music went down in both.`
> Overview 升级：`Overall, live music consistently led in both income and employment, while recorded music declined moderately in both areas. Music publishing, despite its fluctuating revenue, showed a steady increase in its workforce over the period.`

**要点**：Overview 把两图各自的头条合并成一句话——"现场音乐双领先、唱片音乐双下降"，这就是两图的核心联系。

---

## ③ 主体段

**Body 1（收入维度）**：

列数字试水句：
> Live music made about £240 million in 2013 and went up to £270 million in 2015. Recorded music fell from £170 million to about half of live music. Music publishing was second, fell to £160 million in 2014, then went back up to £185 million.

分析不同试水句：
> Live music was always the top earner. Recorded music lost the most. Publishing went up and down but ended higher.

中文思路：收入维度三条线，重点是 live music 稳居第一、recorded music 腰斩、publishing 波动后回升。先各自说清，不急着跨图。

**Body 2（就业维度 + 联系）**：

列数字试水句：
> Live music employed nearly 3,800 people in 2013 and almost 9,000 in 2015. Recorded music had about 3,500 in 2013 but only 1,900 in 2015. Publishing stayed around 2,200.

分析不同试水句：
> The same pattern appears in jobs: live music employed the most and grew fastest, while recorded music employed the fewest at the end. So the leader in income was also the leader in jobs.

中文思路：就业维度复述一遍"谁多谁少"，然后**点出联系**——收入和就业的头名是同一个部门（live music），这就是组合图最该写的"交叉点"。

---

## ④ 改写润色

**Body 1 列数字句升级**：
- 普通：Live music made about £240 million in 2013 and went up to £270 million in 2015.
- 升：`Live music consistently generated the highest income, which rose steadily from approximately £240 million in 2013 to around £270 million in 2015.`（`consistently generated the highest income` 替 made the most；`rose steadily` 替 went up）
- 普通：Recorded music fell from £170 million to about half of live music.
- 升：`In contrast, the net earnings from recorded music experienced a significant decline, with income dropping from £170 million in 2013 to about half that of live music by 2015.`（`experienced a significant decline` 替 fell；`about half that of` 替 about half of）
- 普通：Music publishing was second, fell to £160 million in 2014, then went back up to £185 million.
- 升：`Music publishing, bringing in the second highest revenue initially, saw a decrease in its profits from £180 million in 2013 to £160 million in 2014 before recovering to approximately £185 million in 2015.`（分词 `bringing in` 补排名；`recovering` 替 went back up）

**Body 2 列数字句升级**：
- 普通：Live music employed nearly 3,800 people in 2013 and almost 9,000 in 2015.
- 升：`Regarding employment, the number of workers live music employed was the highest each year, nearly tripling from 3,800 in 2013 to almost 9,000 by 2015.`（`Regarding` 开启第二维度；`nearly tripling` 倍数表达）
- 普通：Recorded music had about 3,500 in 2013 but only 1,900 in 2015.
- 升：`Recorded music, while employing around 3,500 workers in 2013 and 2,250 in 2014, witnessed its workforce drop to the lowest level at just 1,900 in 2015.`（`while` 让步；`witnessed its workforce drop` 替 had... but）

把升后句子拼起来，就是下方⑤完整范文主体段。

---

## ⑤ 完整范文
The bar charts illustrate changes in the total income from live music, music publishing, and recorded music, as well as the number of workers employed in those sectors in the UK from 2013 to 2015.

As seen, the income generated from the three music sectors followed different trends. Live music consistently generated the highest income, which rose steadily from approximately £240 million in 2013 to around £270 million in 2015. In contrast, the net earnings from recorded music experienced a significant decline, with income dropping from £170 million in 2013 to about half that of live music by 2015. Music publishing, bringing in the second highest revenue initially, saw a decrease in its profits from £180 million in 2013 to £160 million in 2014 before recovering to approximately £185 million in 2015.

Regarding employment, the number of workers live music employed was the highest each year, nearly tripling from 3,800 in 2013 to almost 9,000 by 2015. Recorded music, while employing around 3,500 workers in 2013 and 2,250 in 2014, witnessed its workforce drop to the lowest level at just 1,900 in 2015. Meanwhile, despite its income fluctuations, music publishing maintained a relatively stable labor force at approximately 2,200.

Overall, live music consistently led in both income and employment, while recorded music declined moderately in both areas. Music publishing, despite its fluctuating revenue, showed a steady increase in its workforce over the period.

## ⑥ 同类型变体（柱状图 + 饼图 · 受教育年限与最高学历）

这篇也是组合图，但类型是"柱状图 + 饼图"。看它怎么处理**两种图型拼接、且维度不同（年限 vs 学历）**。

**The charts below show the average years of education received by people in a country and the highest level of education they have achieved.**
![2025-07-05](../../../Writing-examples/Task1_img/2025-07-05.png)

The charts illustrate the average years of education received by the population of a country and the highest level of education they have attained.

As shown in the bar chart, the average years of schooling rose steadily across age groups, from around 6 years for those aged 65 and over to roughly 13 years for people under 25. In other words, younger generations stayed in education far longer than their elders.

The pie chart, by contrast, reveals the qualification mix: about 45% of the population held a bachelor's degree or above, 30% completed secondary education, and the remaining 25% had only primary schooling or less. This suggests that while the youngest cohorts accumulate the most years in education, the adult population as a whole remains split between tertiary and lower qualifications.

Overall, educational attainment has lengthened markedly across generations, yet the qualification distribution still shows a substantial share without higher education.

---

## 组合图要点回顾

- 首段改写：必须**覆盖两个图的所有维度**（收入+就业、年限+学历），不能只写一半。
- Overview：把两图各自的头条合并成一句，并点出**两图联系**（同一对象是否在两个维度都领先）。
- 主体段：先分图把数字说清，再在第二段或 Overview 点出交叉点；别让两个图各自为政。
- 润色：`made money` → `generated the highest income`；`went up` → `rose steadily`；善用 `Regarding / In contrast / while`。
- 图型不同（柱+饼）：用 `The bar chart... / The pie chart, by contrast...` 明确切换，避免混淆。
