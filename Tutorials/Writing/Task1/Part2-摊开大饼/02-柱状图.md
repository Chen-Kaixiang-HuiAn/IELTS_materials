# 柱状图（Bar Chart）

> 柱状图是 Task 1 最高频的图表之一。它考的是**不同类别之间的数值对比**：谁最高、谁最低、谁升谁降、谁不变。
>
> 和线图不同，柱状图往往没有"时间趋势"这条主轴——它更像一个**横截面快照**，靠的是把一堆柱子按"方向 / 极值 / 分组"重新组织，而不是按时间串起来。
>
> 下面用 Part 1 主线程的同一套小作文流程走一遍：**首段改写 → Overview（主要对比）→ 主体段（列数字 + 按趋势分组分析）→ 改写润色（实词替换 / 句式升级）**。

---

## 代表真题（多国服装进口均价 · 双年对比）

> **The chart below shows the average price per kilogram of clothing imported into the European Union from seven major originating countries in 1997 and 2003.**
> **Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2024-11-23](../../../../Writing-examples/Task1_img/2024-11-23.png)

---

## ① 首段改写（有什么数据，先写一句出来）

题目说"七个服装出口国 1997 和 2003 年对欧盟的每公斤均价"。改写时**只换词、不发挥**：

> 初稿改写：`The chart shows the average price of clothing from seven countries in 1997 and 2003.`
> 升级改写：`The bar chart illustrates the changes in the average price per kilogram of clothing imported into the European Union from Tunisia, Morocco, Turkey, India, Indonesia, China, and Bangladesh in 1997 and 2003.`

**要点**：把 `chart shows` 换成 `bar chart illustrates the changes in`；把 `seven countries` 具体点名七个国家；补 `imported into the European Union` 把题目里的交易方向坐实；单位 `per kilogram` 不能丢。

---

## ② Overview

看完全图，先想"最大的几件事是什么"：
- 七个国家可以分成"涨 / 跌 / 平"三组——Tunisia、Turkey 涨；India、Indonesia、China、Bangladesh 跌；Morocco 六年没变。
- 极端值很明显：Bangladesh 一直最便宜（£7.80→£5），China 降幅最大。

> Overview 初稿：`Overall, some countries' prices went up, some went down, and Bangladesh was always the cheapest.`
> Overview 升级：`Overall, while the export prices of clothing from some countries fell or remained unchanged, those from Tunisia and Turkey rose notably. Meanwhile, Bangladesh consistently offered the lowest-priced clothing imports throughout the period.`

**要点**：Overview 不写具体数字，只写"谁涨 / 谁跌 / 谁一直最低"这种**分组关系**和**极值**。

---

## ③ 主体段

**Body 1（上升组 + 持平特例）**：

列数字试水句：
> Tunisia's price went up from £17 to about £24. Turkey also went up, from £16 to £18.5. Morocco was the highest in 1997 at £18, but it did not change, and by 2003 Tunisia and Turkey were higher than Morocco.

分析不同试水句：
> Tunisia and Turkey both became more expensive. Morocco stayed the same but was passed by the other two, so its rank dropped even though its price did not move.

中文思路：上升组里 Tunisia 涨得最猛（£17→£24），Turkey 温和上涨。Morocco 虽然价格没变，但因为别人涨了，它的**排名**被超过——这是柱状图里很值得点出的"静态数据里的动态对比"。

**Body 2（下降组）**：

列数字试水句：
> China fell the most, from about £16 to £11. India and Indonesia both dropped by £4, to £10 and £9. Bangladesh was already the cheapest at £7.80 and went down more to £5.

分析不同试水句：
> Most countries became cheaper. China had the biggest drop. Bangladesh stayed the lowest the whole time even after it got cheaper.

中文思路：下降组四个国家，重点是 China 降幅最大、Bangladesh 始终垫底。把"降幅最大"和"始终最低"这两个极值都点出来，Overview 之外最关键的对比就齐了。

---

## ④ 改写润色

**Body 1 列数字句升级**：
- 普通：Tunisia's price went up from £17 to about £24.
- 升：`Specifically, Tunisia's price rose sharply from £17 to around £24, which made it one of the higher-cost suppliers.`（`rose sharply` 替 went up；`which` 定从补排名结果）
- 普通：Morocco was the highest in 1997 at £18, but it did not change, and by 2003 Tunisia and Turkey were higher than Morocco.
- 升：`Morocco, despite having the highest price in 1997 at roughly £18, witnessed no change in that figure over the six years and was overtaken by Tunisia and Turkey by 2003 in terms of their pricing of clothing exports.`（`despite` 让步；`was overtaken by` 替"被超过"，把静态数据写成动态对比）

**Body 2 列数字句升级**：
- 普通：China fell the most, from about £16 to £11.
- 升：`China, which had a price comparable to Turkey's in 1997, experienced the most significant fall, with its average price dropping to around £11 by 2003.`（`comparable to` 替"差不多"；`experienced the most significant fall` 替 fell the most）
- 普通：Bangladesh was already the cheapest at £7.80 and went down more to £5.
- 升：`Bangladesh, already the most affordable source in 1997 with a price of £7.80, recorded a further drop in its price to £5 by 2003, retaining its position as the cheapest supplier.`（`recorded a further drop` 替 went down more；`retaining its position` 分词补"保持最低"的结果）

**Body 分析句升级**：
- 普通：Most countries became cheaper.
- 升：`In contrast, the prices of clothing imports from India, Indonesia, China, and Bangladesh declined.`（`In contrast` 衔接分组切换；`declined` 替 became cheaper）

把升后句子拼起来，就是下方⑤完整范文主体段。

---

## ⑤ 完整范文
The bar chart illustrates the changes in the average price per kilogram of clothing imported into the European Union from Tunisia, Morocco, Turkey, India, Indonesia, China, and Bangladesh in 1997 and 2003.

As seen, Tunisia and Turkey saw substantial increases in their clothing export prices. Specifically, Tunisia's price rose sharply from £17 to around £24, which made it one of the higher-cost suppliers. Turkey, likewise, saw its export prices climb from £16 to around £18.5 during the same period. Morocco, despite having the highest price in 1997 at roughly £18, witnessed no change in that figure over the six years and was overtaken by Tunisia and Turkey by 2003 in terms of their pricing of clothing exports.

In contrast, the prices of clothing imports from India, Indonesia, China, and Bangladesh declined. China, which had a price comparable to Turkey's in 1997, experienced the most significant fall, with average price dropping to around £11 by 2003. The corresponding data in India and Indonesia both witnessed declines of £4, reaching £10 and £9, respectively. Bangladesh, already the most affordable source in 1997 with a price of £7.80, recorded a further drop in its price to £5 by 2003, retaining its position as the cheapest supplier.

Overall, while the export prices of clothing from some countries fell or remained unchanged, those from Tunisia and Turkey rose notably. Meanwhile, Bangladesh consistently offered the lowest-priced clothing imports throughout the period.

## ⑥ 同类型变体（双图对比 · 男女驾照持有率）

这篇也是柱状图，但讲"两个图"：1976–2006 男女驾照持有率 + 17–20 岁驾驶率。看它怎么处理**两张图拼接、且第二组是反向趋势**。

**The bar charts show the percentage of men and women in Great Britain who had driving licenses and another bar chart shows the percentage of men and women who were drivers at the age of 17-20.**
![2025-03-22](../../../../Writing-examples/Task1_img/2025-03-22.png)

The bar charts illustrate the percentage of males and females with a driver's license in Great Britain from 1976 to 2006, along with the proportion of drivers aged 17 to 20 in 1996 and 2006.

As shown in the first chart, the percentage of driving license holders increased for both genders, with men consistently outnumbering women. Specifically, in 1976, 70% of men possessed a driving license, and that figure steadily rose to 80% in 2006. The rate of women, meanwhile, experienced a significant upturn, doubling from 30% in 1976 to 60% in 2006. This growth considerably narrowed the gender gap from 40% to 20%.

In contrast, a decline in driving license ownership among individuals aged 17 to 20 was seen. In 1996, half of the males in that age range held a license, whereas 30% of their female counterparts did so. However, by 2006, those figures had dropped, with the decline being more pronounced among men, to 45% and 20%, respectively.

## 柱状图要点回顾

- 首段改写：点名所有类别 + 时间/年份 + 单位，别只说 "the chart shows"。
- Overview：写"按方向/极值分组 + 谁最高最低 + 有无反趋势组"，不写数字。
- 主体段：先按趋势分组（涨组 / 跌组 / 平组），每组内列关键数字，再点出"谁被超过 / 谁降幅最大 / 谁始终垫底"这种**错位对比**。
- 润色：`went up` → `rose sharply`；`was passed by` → `was overtaken by`；善用 `Specifically / In contrast / despite`。
- 双图题：两张图分别描述，再用 `In contrast` 点出两组趋势是否相反。
