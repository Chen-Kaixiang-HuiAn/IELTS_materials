# 流程图（Process Diagram）

> 流程图描述的是一个**过程或制作步骤**。它不考趋势也不考对比，考的是**阶段划分 + 被动语态 + 顺序连接词（first / then / after which）**。
>
> 流程图最容易写成"步骤清单"。高分做法是**先分段（原材料→成型→固化→完工），再用被动语态串起顺序**，别用一堆 "and then" 硬连。
>
> 下面用 Part 1 主线程的同一套小作文流程走一遍：**首段改写 → Overview（分几阶段）→ 主体段（列步骤 + 分析主要不同）→ 改写润色（实词替换 / 句式升级）**。

---

## 代表真题（混凝土帆布储库制作）

> **The diagram below shows the stages in making a storage area from a material called concrete canvas.**
> **Summarise the information by selecting and reporting main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

## ① 首段改写（有什么数据，先写一句出来）

题目说"用混凝土帆布做储库的步骤"。改写时把 `shows the stages` 升级，点出材料特性：

> 初稿改写：`The diagram shows how to make a storage area with concrete canvas.`
> 升级改写：`The diagram illustrates the process of constructing a storage area using concrete canvas, a material that contains concrete and solidifies upon contact with water.`

**要点**：把 `diagram shows` 换成 `diagram illustrates the process of`；补 `a material that... solidifies upon contact with water` 这句材料特性，是图里关键背景，写进首段更专业。

---

## ② Overview

看完全图，先想"分几个阶段"：
- 阶段一：材料制作（三层结构）。
- 阶段二：运输与铺开。
- 阶段三：充气 + 喷水固化。
- 阶段四：静置硬化、完工。

> Overview 初稿：`Overall, there are four main steps: making the material, laying it, blowing it up, and letting it dry.`
> Overview 升级：`Overall, the process consists of four main stages: producing the composite material, transporting and unfolding it on site, inflating the structure, and allowing it to harden into a rigid storage dome.`

**要点**：Overview 不写每步细节，只列"四个阶段 + 每阶段一句话定性"，给阅卷人一张导航图。

---

## ③ 主体段

**Body 1（材料制作 + 运输铺开）**：

列步骤试水句：
> First, the canvas is made by putting three layers together: a top fibrous layer, dry concrete in the middle, and a waterproof backing. Then it is packed and sent by truck. After that, it is unfolded on the ground and fixed with pegs.

分析不同试水句：
> The first part is about preparing the material and getting it to the site. The three-layer structure is the key to why it can harden later.

中文思路：前两步是"准备 + 到场"。重点是三层结构（这是后面遇水固化的前提），以及"铺开+地钉固定"这个动作。

**Body 2（充气固化 + 完工）**：

列步骤试水句：
> Next, air is pumped in while the canvas covers a big ball, making it into a dome. Then water is sprayed on to start hardening. It is left for 20 to 24 hours until hard. After that, it is ready to use.

分析不同试水句：
> The last part is the change from soft to hard: air gives it shape, water makes it solid, and time finishes the job.

中文思路：后两步是"成型 + 固化"。关键是"充气给形状、喷水给硬度、静置完成"这个因果链，用 `after which / once` 串起来。

---

## ④ 改写润色

**Body 1 列步骤句升级**：
- 普通：First, the canvas is made by putting three layers together.
- 升：`Initially, the concrete canvas is produced by adhering three layers: a fibrous top layer for hydration, a dry concrete mix in the middle, and a waterproof PVC backing.`（`Initially` 替 First；`adhering` 替 putting together；点名三层）
- 普通：Then it is packed and sent by truck. After that, it is unfolded on the ground and fixed with pegs.
- 升：`Afterwards, the material is packaged and transported by a truck to its designated location, where the canvas is unwrapped and carefully unfolded across a prepared ground surface before being secured with pegs.`（`Afterwards` 替 Then；`designated location` 替 site；`secured with pegs` 精准）

**Body 2 列步骤句升级**：
- 普通：Next, air is pumped in while the canvas covers a big ball, making it into a dome.
- 升：`Subsequently, air is pumped into the structure while the canvas is slung over a large inflatable ball, inflating it into a dome-like shape.`（`Subsequently` 替 Next；`slung over` 替 covers；`inflating it into` 分词补结果）
- 普通：Then water is sprayed on to start hardening. It is left for 20 to 24 hours until hard.
- 升：`After which water is sprayed over the surface to initiate the hydration process necessary for hardening. The canvas is then left to set for approximately 20 to 24 hours until the material solidifies into a rigid, durable form.`（`After which` 衔接；`initiate the hydration process` 替 start hardening；`solidifies into a rigid form` 替 until hard）

把升后句子拼起来，就是下方⑤完整范文主体段。

---

## ⑤ 完整范文
The diagram illustrates the process of constructing a storage area using concrete canvas, a material that contains concrete and solidifies upon contact with water.

Initially, the concrete canvas is produced by adhering three layers: a fibrous top layer for hydration, a dry concrete mix in the middle, and a waterproof PVC backing. Afterwards, the material is packaged and transported by a truck to its designated location, where the canvas is unwrapped and carefully unfolded across a prepared ground surface before being secured with pegs.

Subsequently, air is pumped into the structure while the canvas is slung over a large inflatable ball, inflating it into a dome-like shape, after which water is sprayed over the surface to initiate the hydration process necessary for hardening. The canvas is then left to set for approximately 20 to 24 hours until the material solidifies into a rigid, durable form. Once fully hardened, the solidified dome-like structure, which primarily serves as a storage facility, is ready for use.

## ⑥ 同类型变体（果酱制作）

这篇也是流程图，但是"做食物"。看它怎么处理**配方类流程（有具体用量、加热、冷却）**。

**The diagram shows how to make jam from the fruit.**
![2025-04-05](../../../../Writing-examples/Task1_img/2025-04-05.png)

The diagram illustrates the step-by-step process of how fruit jam is produced.

In the first stage, a batch of 4 kilograms of fruit is washed under running tap water. Once cleaned, the fruit is pureed thoroughly with a masher. The mashed fruit is then transferred into a cooking pot, where it is combined with 1/4 cup of sugar, two tablespoons of peanuts, and a cup of water, and afterwards the mixture is heated at a high temperature for 10 minutes.

In the following stage, two additional cups of sugar and four cups of water are added to the pot, after which the mixture is boiled again over high heat for half an hour to allow the ingredients to blend and thicken into jam.

In the last stage, the finished jam is poured into separate containers, and then these containers are sealed with lids and left to rest for one hour. When it is sufficiently cooled, the jam is ready for consumption.

## 流程图要点回顾

- 首段改写：点出"过程 / 阶段"；如材料特殊（遇水固化、需加热），把特性写进首段。
- Overview：列"几个阶段 + 每阶段定性"，不写步骤细节。
- 主体段：**按阶段分段**，每段内用被动语态 + 顺序词（Initially / Afterwards / Subsequently / After which / Once）串步骤。
- 润色：`put together` → `adhering`；`sent by truck` → `transported by a truck`；`until hard` → `solidifies into a rigid form`；善用 `initiate / set / durable`。
- 配方类：用量（kg/cup/tablespoon）和时长（minutes/hours）是关键词，照写实写。
