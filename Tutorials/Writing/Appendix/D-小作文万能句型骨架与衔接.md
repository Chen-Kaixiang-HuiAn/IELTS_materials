# Appendix D · 小作文万能句型骨架与衔接（提炼 + 中文 · 海量版）

> 本文件是**提炼的句型骨架库**，取材自三部分：① `Writing-examples/` 范文 ② 外部雅思语料（IELTS Writing Analytics / LandingPrep / Rizz Education 等）③ 原创整理。
>
> 每条格式：`骨架模板` → 中文说明 →（可选）简短示例（含中文）。
>
> 用法：看图后，先判断写哪类图，再挑对应骨架，把横线换成图里的真实数据即可。

---

## 一、开头段改写骨架（open：只换词，不发挥）

**万能骨架 1**
- `The [chart/graph/table/diagram] illustrates [what] in [entities] over [period].` → 图表展示了[某对象]在[时间段]内[某数据]的情况。
- 示例：`The line graph illustrates the monthly prices of four smartphone brands over a year.`（线图展示了四个手机品牌一年内的月度价格。）

**万能骨架 2（对比类）**
- `The bar chart compares [A] with [B] in terms of [category].` → 柱图就[某维度]比较了 A 与 B。

**万能骨架 3（构成类）**
- `The pie chart shows the distribution of [categories] in [whole].` → 饼图展示了[整体]中[各分类]的分布。

**万能骨架 4（表格类）**
- `The table presents the [what] across [number] [entities] in [year].` → 表格呈现了[年][若干对象]的[某数据]。

**动词替换（挑一个换掉 illustrates，附中文）**
- `shows` —— 显示
- `compares` —— 比较
- `demonstrates` —— 证明、展示
- `depicts` —— 描绘
- `provides information about` —— 提供……的信息
- `gives data on` —— 给出……的数据
- `reveals` —— 揭示
- `displays` —— 陈列
- `indicates` —— 表明
- `portrays` —— 刻画

**名词替换（挑一个换掉 what，附中文）**
- `changes in` —— ……的变化
- `the proportion of` —— ……的比例
- `the number of` —— ……的数量
- `the amount of` —— ……的总量
- `the share of` —— ……的份额
- `the distribution of` —— ……的分布

**避雷**：别老写 *the graph shows*（考官视作 low sophistication），换上面任一动词。

**补充开头句型（不同结构，增加多样性）**
- `What the [chart] reveals is [a clear pattern / stark contrast] among [entities].` → 该图揭示的是[对象]间[明显模式/鲜明对比]。
- `The data, sourced from [来源], chart[s] [what] for [entities] between [years].` → 数据来自[来源]，描绘了[年]间[对象]的[数据]。
- `A closer look at the [figure] shows how [entity] [behaviour] relative to its peers.` → 细看该图可见[对象]相对于同类[表现]。
- `Spanning [period], the [graph] tracks [what] across [number] [entities].` → 该图跨越[时段]，追踪了[若干对象]的[数据]。

**万能骨架 5（流程图）**
- `The diagram outlines the process by which [产品] is [produced/made], from [起点] to [终点].` → 该图概述了[产品]从[起点]到[终点]的[生产]过程。

**万能骨架 6（地图）**
- `The two maps contrast [地点] in [年份1] and [年份2], highlighting [主要变化].` → 两幅地图对比了[地点]在[年份1]与[年份2]的变化，突出了[主要变化]。

**万能骨架 7（多对象并列）**
- `The supplied [chart] gives a breakdown of [what] for [entities] during [period].` → 所给[图表]给出了[时段]内[对象]的[数据]细分。

**万能骨架 8（含单位说明）**
- `Units are measured in [单位] unless otherwise specified.` → 除特别说明外，单位均为[单位]。（表格/复杂图常加一句）

## 二、Overview 骨架（主要趋势，不写具体数字）

**万能骨架**
- `Overall, [entity A] remained the highest / most popular, while [entity B] saw the sharpest rise and overtook [C] by [time].` → 总体来看，A 始终最高/最普遍，而 B 涨幅最大，并在[时间]超过 C。

**要点（中文）**
① 覆盖所有主要分组，不要漏掉 ② 只写趋势/排名，**不写具体数字** ③ 放在 Intro 之后最稳，单独成段 ④ 高分可省略结尾段，只要 Overview 强。

**常见 Overview 套路**
- 最高/最低：`X accounted for the largest share, whereas Y was the smallest.`（X 占比最大，Y 最小。）
- 总体涨/跌：`There was a general upward / downward trend in [category].`（某类呈总体上升/下降趋势。）
- 最突出变化：`The most striking feature was the surge in [X].`（最显著的特征是 X 的激增。）
- 双图综合：`Overall, both visuals show [共性], with [图1特征] and [图2特征].`（两图都显示……，其中……）

**Overview 补充骨架**
- 排名式：`Ranked from highest to lowest, the order was [A > B > C > D].`（从高到低排序为……）
- 交叉式：`Notably, [A] and [B] changed places by the end of the period.`（值得注意的是，A 与 B 在期末互换了位置。）
- 稳定式：`Apart from [异常项], all categories remained relatively stable.`（除……外，各类均相对稳定。）
- 不动式：`[A] was the only category to buck the trend, [变动].`（A 是唯一逆势的类别，……）

**Overview 额外结构（不同写法）**
- `It is immediately apparent that [entity] dominated the landscape throughout.` → 一眼可见[对象]始终占主导地位。
- `The standout trend is the [direction] of [X], set against the [opposite direction] of [Y].` → 最突出的趋势是 X 的[方向]，与 Y 的[反方向]形成对照。
- `If one pattern defines the period, it is the [trend] of [X].` → 若说有个模式能定义这一时期，那就是 X 的[趋势]。
- `A tale of two trajectories emerges: [X] [up], while [Y] [down].` → 呈现出两条轨迹：X 升、Y 降。

- ## 三、主体段数据描述骨架（起点 → 峰值 → 终点链）

**三件套骨架**
- `[Entity] stood at [X] in [year], rose steadily, peaking at [Y] before dipping to [Z].` → [对象]在[年]为 X，稳步上升，在 Y 达到峰值，随后跌至 Z。

**单一趋势骨架**
- `[Entity] witnessed a [sharp / steady / slight] [rise / decline] from [A] to [B] over [period].` → [对象]在[时段]内经历了[大幅/平稳/小幅]的[上升/下降]，从 A 到 B。

**比较骨架**
- `In contrast, [entity] ...`（相反，……）/ `Similarly, [entity] ...`（类似地，……）/ `[X] was twice as high as [Y].`（X 是 Y 的两倍。）

**并列数字骨架**
- `[A] accounted for X%, compared with [B]'s Y% and [C]'s Z%.` → A 占 X%，相比之下 B 为 Y%、C 为 Z%。

**占比骨架**
- `Roughly a third of [subject] went to [category].`（约三分之一的……归于……）/ `a clear majority`（明显多数）/ `a small minority`（少数）/ `marginally higher`（略高）

**补充主体骨架**
- 波动式：`[X] fluctuated wildly between [A] and [B] before settling at [C].`（X 在 A 与 B 间剧烈波动，最终稳定在 C。）
- 渐进式：`A gradual upward trajectory was observed in [X], from [A] to [B].`（X 呈渐进上升趋势，从 A 到 B。）
- 骤变式：`A sudden spike occurred in [year], pushing [X] to [value].`（某年骤升，将 X 推至某值。）
- 收敛式：`The gap between [A] and [B] narrowed / widened over time.`（A 与 B 的差距随时间缩小/扩大。）
- 居中式：`[X] hovered around [value] for most of the period.`（X 在大部分时间徘徊于某值附近。）
- 逆势式：`Defying the overall trend, [X] [反向变动].`（逆整体趋势，X……）

**主体段额外结构（不同写法）**
- `Commencing at [X] in [year], [entity] embarked on a [direction] path, concluding at [Y].` → 从[年]的 X 起步，[对象]走上[方向]轨迹，终至 Y。
- `A modest beginning of [X] gave way to a pronounced [direction], with [entity] finishing at [Y].` → 以 X 的 modest 起点，让位于明显的[方向]，终值 Y。
- `The lion's share of [metric] came from [category], at [X]%, dwarfing [other] at [Y]%.` → [指标]的绝大部分来自[类别]，达 X%，远超[其他]的 Y%。
- `Not to be overlooked is [minor entity], whose [behaviour] [data].` → 不容忽视的是[次要对象]，其[表现][数据]。

## 四、各图表类型专属骨架

- **线图 / 柱图**：`[Entity] showed a general upward/downward trend, standing at [X] in [year] and reaching [Y] by [year].`（总体升降趋势 + 起止点。）
- **饼图 / 占比**：`[Category] accounted for the largest share at [X]%, while [category] made up only [Y]%.`（最大占比 + 最小占比。）
- **表格**：`[Entity] had the highest [X] at [Y], in contrast to [entity] which recorded the lowest at [Z].`（最高值对象 vs 最低值对象。）/`[X] ranked highest in [metric], while [Y] was at the bottom.`（X 居首，Y 垫底。）
- **组合图**：先写一句总体 Overview，再分段写每个子图（线/柱/饼各一句）。
- **地图**：`[Area A] expanded / was replaced by [Area B]; the most noticeable change was [detail].`（A 扩张/被 B 取代；最明显变化是……）/`The residential area was relocated.`（住宅区被迁走。）
- **流程图**：`The process consists of [N] main stages, beginning with [stage] and ending with [final product].`（流程含 N 个阶段，始于……终于……）/`Initially, [raw] is [action]. It is then [action].`（起初……被……，接着……）
- **F 系列（无题干图）**：先判断图类型（表/饼/组合），再按上述对应骨架写；无题干时自己补一句 `The figure presents [what].` 作开头。

## 五、比较与对比骨架（Band 8 结构）

**1. While / Whereas 句首对比（最自然）**
- `While [A] + [verb+data], [B] + [verb+data].` → 当 A……时，B 却……。
- 示例：`While car ownership in Country A rose steadily, Country B saw a gradual decline.`（A 国汽车保有量稳步升，B 国渐降。）

**2. In contrast / By contrast 句首强对比**
- `In contrast, [Subject] + [verb+data].`
- 示例：`In contrast, Korea recorded a sharp fall to just 12,000.`（相反，韩国骤降至仅 1.2 万。）

**3. In comparison with X, which + 从句（嵌入对比，更高级）**
- `In comparison with [A], which [verb+data], [B] + [verb+data].`
- 示例：`In comparison with Japan, where factories rose to 120,000, Korea saw a sharp decline.`（与日本[工厂升至12万]相比，韩国锐减。）

**4. 句末对比（简洁可读）**
- `[A] + [verb+data], whereas/while [B] + [verb+data].`
- 示例：`Footwear spending rose sharply, while food expenditure remained stable.`（鞋类支出猛增，食品支出稳定。）

**5. 比例对比**
- `As [A] increases, [B] decreases.`（A 升则 B 降，常用于线图反向。）/ `[X] was almost double that of [Y].`（X 几乎是 Y 的两倍。）/`A pronounced gap emerged between [X] and [Y].`（X 与 Y 间出现明显缺口。）/`The figures converged towards the end.`（期末数据趋同。）

**6. 程度对比**
- `[X] far exceeded [Y].`（X 远超 Y）/ `[X] was by far the highest.`（X 迄今为止最高。）/`[X] was significantly higher than [Y].`（X 显著高于 Y。）

**7. 排名对比（Band 8）**
- `[X] ranked first / second / last among [group], at [value].`（X 在……中排名第一/垫底，为……）

**8. 倍数对比**
- `[X] was threefold / four times as [high/large] as [Y].`（X 是 Y 的三倍/四倍。）/`[X] tripled, whereas [Y] halved.`（X 增至三倍，Y 减半。）

**9. 反向对称**
- `While [A] climbed, [B] experienced a parallel decline.`（A 升的同时，B 同步下降。）

**10. 相似收敛**
- `Both [A] and [B] converged at around [value] by [time].`（A 与 B 在……时均收敛于约……）

**比较额外结构（不同写法）**
- `[X] and [Y] tell opposite stories: the former [up], the latter [down].` → X 与 Y 讲着相反的故事：前者升，后者降。
- `The disparity between [X] and [Y] is stark, with a gap of [value].` → X 与 Y 的差距悬殊，差值达[值]。
- `Nowhere is the contrast more evident than in [category], where [X] [data] against [Y]'s [data].` → 差距在[类别]最为明显，X[数据]对 Y[数据]。
- `Put side by side, [X] and [Y] reveal a [similarity / divergence].` → 并置来看，X 与 Y 呈现出[相似/分歧]。

- ## 六、趋势描述骨架（方向 / 程度 / 速度）

**方向 + 动词（主语用）**
- 上升：`rise / climb / grow / go up / surge / soar / rocket / leap / shoot up`
- 下降：`fall / drop / decline / go down / plummet / plunge / slump / tumble / nosedive`
- 稳定：`remain stable / stay constant / plateau / hover around / level off / stagnate`
- 波动：`fluctuate / vary`

**方向 + 名词（with 'in'）**
- 上升：`an increase / a rise / a growth / a surge`
- 下降：`a decrease / a fall / a drop / a decline / a plunge`
- 波动：`a fluctuation / variation`

**程度副词（附中文，勿与自带幅度动词叠加）**
- 大幅：`dramatically / sharply / significantly / substantially / steeply / precipitously`（剧烈地/锐利地/显著地）
- 中幅：`steadily / noticeably / considerably / moderately / markedly`（平稳地/明显地）
- 小幅：`slightly / marginally / gradually / mildly`（轻微地/逐渐地）
- 突然：`abruptly / suddenly / rapidly`（突然/迅速）
- 不规则：`irregularly / inconsistently`（不规则地）

**速度 / 时间表达（避错）**
- 正确：`Over a 10-year period, it increased by 5%.`（十年间增长 5%）/`From 2010 to 2020, it rose by 10%.`（2010–2020 上升 10%）/`In 2015, it fell.`（2015 年下降。）
- 错误：`In 10 years, it increased by 5%`（缺 over）/ `From 2010 to 2020, it rise 10%`（时态错）/ `In the year of 2015, it fall`（冗余+时态错）
- `by` 表变化量（rose by 10%），`to` 表终值（rose to 50%）。

**幅度动词 + 名词化对照（写 overview 用）**
- 小幅升：`edged up` / `a slight uptick`
- 中幅升：`climbed` / `a moderate rise`
- 大幅升：`soared` / `a dramatic surge`
- 小幅降：`slipped` / `a marginal dip`
- 中幅降：`declined` / `a steady fall`
- 大幅降：`plummeted` / `a precipitous drop`
- 稳定：`held steady` / `stability`
- 波动：`oscillated` / `volatility`

## 七、衔接词库（按功能，附中文）

- **总→分**：Overall（总体）/ In general（一般而言）/ With regard to（关于）/ As for（至于）/ As seen（如所见）
- **相似**：Similarly（类似地）/ Likewise（同样地）/ In the same vein（同理）
- **对比**：In contrast（相反）/ However（然而）/ On the other hand（另一方面）/ Conversely（反之）/ By comparison（相比之下）/ Whereas（而）
- **时间推进**：In [year]（在……年）/ By [year]（到……年）/ Over the period（在此期间）/ From [year] onwards（从……年起）/ Throughout the period（整个期间）
- **结果**：resulting in（导致）/ leading to（通向）/ leaving（留下）/ making（使得）
- **极值/排名**：the highest（最高）/ the lowest（最低）/ the most noticeable（最显著的）/ by far（迄今为止最）/ the most striking（最惊人的）

## 八、高级语法结构（Band 8+，Task 1 适用）

**分词短语开头（合并两句）**
- `Having reached a peak in 2015, unemployment subsequently declined.`（2015 达峰后，失业随之下降。）→ 把 "after it reached" 变 participle，显语法多样性。

**被动语态（流程/地图必备）**
- `the raw materials are transported` / `the residential area was relocated` / `impurities are removed` / `the mixture is heated`（流程/地图无明确施动者时用被动，避免硬找主语。）

**定语从句浓缩**
- `Countries which have shorter school hours report similar attainment.` → 省略为 `Countries with shorter hours...`

**嵌入式对比（见第五节 3）**
- `In comparison with X, which..., Y...` 比平铺两句更高级。

## 九、Task 1 高频错误清单（中文）

1. 缺 Overview（最高只能 5.0–5.5）。
2. Overview 写了具体数字（应只写趋势/排名）。
3. 动词幅度与副词重复（如 *hugely surge*）。
4. 单位/对象写错（把 percentages 写成 numbers，或漏写单位）。
5. 比较对象不清晰（没说清谁比谁高/低）。
6. 时态错（过去数据用一般现在时；ongoing 趋势用现在时）。
7. `account for` 误接时长（应接比例/数量，时长用 *lasted*）。
8. 滥用 *the graph shows*（换 illustrate/demonstrate 等）。
9. 罗列所有数字（应挑最重要 2–3 个，分组写）。
10. 用缩写/口语（it's / don't → it is / do not；went up → rose）。
