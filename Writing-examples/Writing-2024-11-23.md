> **图表类型标注**：柱状图（多国服装进口均价）

**The chart below shows the average price per kilogram of clothing imported into the European Union from seven major originating countries in 1997 and 2003.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2024-11-23](./Task1_img/2024-11-23.png)

The bar chart illustrates the changes in the average price per kilogram of clothing imported into the European Union from Tunisia, Morocco, Turkey, India, Indonesia, China, and Bangladesh in 1997 and 2003.

As seen, Tunisia and Turkey saw substantial increases in their clothing export prices. Specifically, Tunisia's price rose sharply from £17 to around £24, which made it one of the higher-cost suppliers. Turkey, likewise, saw its export prices climb from £16 to around £18.5 during the same period. Morocco, despite having the highest price in 1997 at roughly £18, witnessed no change in that figure over the six years and was overtaken by Tunisia and Turkey by 2003 in terms of their pricing of clothing exports.

In contrast, the prices of clothing imports from India, Indonesia, China, and Bangladesh declined. China, which had a price comparable to Turkey's in 1997, experienced the most significant fall, with average price dropping to around £11 by 2003. The corresponding data in India and Indonesia both witnessed declines of £4, reaching £10 and £9, respectively. Bangladesh, already the most affordable source in 1997 with a price of £7.80, recorded a further drop in its price to £5 by 2003, retaining its position as the cheapest supplier.

Overall, while the export prices of clothing from some countries fell or remained unchanged, those from Tunisia and Turkey rose notably. Meanwhile, Bangladesh consistently offered the lowest-priced clothing imports throughout the period.

**2. 让步状语+转折描述**
> *"Morocco, **despite having the highest price in 1997** at roughly £18, **witnessed no change** in that figure over the six years..."*

**技巧**：despite 引导的让步结构先强调 Morocco 1997年的最高价格，主句再转折描述其六年未变。这种"高起点+零变化"的反差，突出了 Morocco 的特殊性，为后文的 "was overtaken" 做铺垫。

**4. 对比结构+综合概括**

**技巧**：用 while 引导对比从句，将"下降/不变"与"上升"并列呈现，those from 避免重复。这种"总体对比+具体突出"的结构，在概述段精准概括了数据的核心特征。

**有些人认为，上大学是通往成功职业生涯的最佳途径，而另一些人则认为，直接在中学毕业后参加工作更好。讨论双方观点并给出自己的观点。**

Some argue that higher education equips individuals with essential skills for long-term success, while others believe entering the workforce immediately provides practical experience and an early start. It cannot be denied that pursuing tertiary education is essential for success in today's digital age. Tertiary education provides cutting-edge knowledge and specialized skills crucial for achieving enduring success in a rapidly evolving job market. 不可否认，在当今数字化时代，接受高等教育对于成功至关重要。高等教育提供了前沿知识和专业技能，这对于在快速变化的就业市场中取得持久成功至关重要。许多基于能力的职位需要扎实的跨学科基础，这在前沿科学等领域尤为重要，因为这些领域的研究通常跨越多个学术学科。因此，没有大学学历或职业培训的年轻人可能面临被限制在初级职位上的风险，从而失去更好职业发展的机会。除了专业知识之外，高等教育还促进智力和社交能力的发展，通过提优质析推理和创新能力为个人赋能。它还提供了宝贵的机会让学生建立人脉并接触多样化的视角，而这些都对于解决职场中的复杂挑战以及个人和职业成长至关重要。

As the world increasingly transitions into a digitally driven society, digital literacy has become a crucial factor for employability and innovation, so those lacking the skills to create and evaluate information on digital platforms face limited opportunities for well-paying jobs and are more likely to end up in manual labor roles, such as construction or packing, which are increasingly vulnerable to replacement by artificial intelligence-driven automation. #### ④ 可迁移句型骨架（直接套用）（题型：Discuss）

**骨架 1 — 先退后进亮立场（Intro）**
> Although ______ deserves a central place because ______, I disagree with the view that ______.

**骨架 2 — 分类论证起手（Body）**
> From the perspective of ______, doing X helps ______ and ______. Meanwhile, X also offers ______ that go beyond ______.

**骨架 3 — 抽象→具体例证（Body）**
> For example, a course on ______ may show how ______ have harmed ______, as illustrated by ______.

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

| 衔接类型 | 原文表达 | 功能说明 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **让步承认** | `It is understandable why some argue...` | 先承认对方观点的合理性，为后文反驳铺垫 |
| **强烈转折** | `However, bypassing higher education...` | 从承认转向反驳，标志论证方向的重大转换 |
| **因果推导** | `Thus, without a university degree...` | 推导缺乏学历的后果，强化论证逻辑 |
| **递进扩展** | `Beyond professional knowledge...` | 在 Body 1 内部从专业知识推进到智力发展，丰富论证层次 |

> **衔接亮点**：作者的衔接手段功能明确。"It is understandable why some argue..." 是典型的让步衔接，既展示了批判性思维，又为后文的反驳创造了空间。"Thus" 是精妙的因果衔接，将前文的专业知识要求推导为具体的职业风险。段落之间的 "However" 清晰地标示了视角转换，使文章结构一目了然。

| 词汇/短语 | 中文含义 | 词性/用法 | 替代普通表达 |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **equips** | 使具备；装备（能力） | 动词 | prepares / provides |
| **indispensable** | 不可或缺的 | 形容词 | essential / necessary |
| **cornerstone** | 基石；根本 | 名词 | foundation / basis |
| **hinder** | 阻碍；妨碍 | 动词 | block / obstruct |
| **vulnerable to** | 易受…冲击的 | 形容词短语 | easily affected by |
| **thrive** | 蓬勃发展；茁壮成长 | 动词 | succeed / flourish |

**1. 形式主语+被动语态**
> *"**It cannot be denied that** pursuing tertiary education is essential for success in today's digital age."*

**技巧**：用 It cannot be denied that 引导的形式主语句，强调高等教育的必要性。这种结构比 "Tertiary education is essential" 更正式，且 cannot be denied 的否定强调增强了论证的权威性。

**2. 条件状语从句+结果推导**
> *"**Thus, without a university degree or vocational training**, young people **risk being confined to** entry-level roles with limited opportunities for career advancement."*

**技巧**：用 without 引导的含蓄条件句，强调缺乏学历的风险。risk being confined to 精准描述了被困在初级职位的可能性，with limited opportunities 补充说明后果。这种"条件+风险+后果"的结构，使论证更具紧迫感。

**3. 让步状语从句+转折反驳**
> *"**While gaining practical experience and financial independence by entering the workforce early may appeal to some**, the indispensable role of tertiary education... **cannot be overstated**."*

**技巧**：用 While 引导让步从句，先承认早期工作的吸引力，主句再转折强调高等教育的不可或缺性。cannot be overstated 是精妙的表达，意为"再强调也不为过"，比 "is very important" 更具强调效果。

**4. 因果链式推导**
> *"...those lacking the skills to create and evaluate information on digital platforms face limited opportunities for well-paying jobs and are more likely to end up in manual labor roles, such as construction or packing, **which are increasingly vulnerable to replacement by artificial intelligence-driven automation**."*

**技巧**：用 lacking 现在分词短语描述缺乏技能的人群，face 和 are more likely to end up 描述连锁后果，which 引导的定语从句将逻辑推至最坏结果（被AI自动化取代）。这种"多因→连锁后果→最坏结果"的推导，使论证具有强烈的警示效果。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
