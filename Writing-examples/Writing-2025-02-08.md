The bar chart illustrates changes in visitor numbers at British Museum, History Museum, National Museum, and Science Museum between June and December. 
 
As seen, the British Museum and the History Museum experienced a similar pattern in visitor trends, with the latter consistently attracting a higher number of museumgoers. In June and July, the British Museum welcomed just over 400,000 visitors. However, attendance surged dramatically to 600,000 in August, after which it declined progressively to 260,000 in December. A comparable trend was observed at the History Museum, with around 410,000 visitors recorded in June and July. After peaking in August (nearly 720,000), attendance steadily decreased, ending at 450,000, the highest figure among those for the four museums.

In contrast, the National Museum recorded the lowest number of guests throughout the period. Meanwhile, the Science Museum documented the highest attendance at first, drawing nearly 460,000 visitors in June. However, that number steadily declined over the next four months, arriving at 300,000 in December despite a spike to 500,000 in September.

Overall, the British and History Museums followed a similar pattern, experiencing a peak in the quantity of guests they welcomed in August and a constant drop thereafter, while the corresponding statistics on the National Museum was relatively stable.

**1. 伴随比较句**
> *"The British Museum and the History Museum experienced a similar pattern in visitor trends, **with the latter consistently attracting a higher number of museumgoers**."*

**技巧**：主句陈述总体趋势，"with + 名词 + 现在分词"的独立主格结构伴随比较两个对象的大小关系。这种句式将"趋势相似"和"数值差异"两个信息压缩在一个句子中，信息密度极高。

**2. 时间链式句**
> *"In June and July, the British Museum welcomed just over 400,000 visitors. However, attendance **surged dramatically** to 600,000 in August, after which it **declined progressively** to 260,000 in December."*

**技巧**：用"However"标记转折（从平稳到激增），"after which"引导非限制性定语从句连接峰值后的下降趋势。三个句子通过时间推进和衔接词串联，形成了完整的"上升-峰值-下降"叙事弧线。

**3. 让步异常句**
> *"However, that number steadily declined over the next four months, arriving at 300,000 in December **despite a spike to 500,000 in September**."*

**技巧**：主句描述总体下降趋势，"despite + 名词短语"标注中间的异常峰值。这种"总体趋势+局部异常"的句式是处理波动数据的经典写法，既忠实于数据又体现了观察的细致。

**4. 综合概述句**
> *"Overall, the British and History Museums followed a similar pattern, experiencing a peak in the quantity of guests they welcomed in August and a constant drop thereafter, while the corresponding statistics on the National Museum was relatively stable."*

**技巧**：概述段用"while"将两组对比对象并置，"experiencing"和"a constant drop thereafter"用分词和时间副词压缩了复杂的时间变化。"corresponding statistics"的指代用法避免了重复"visitor numbers"，体现了词汇的多样性。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：社会与政府类 · Discuss Both Views

**Some people think rapid population growth in cities only benefits people who live there while others believe that there are disadvantages. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.**

**一些人认为城市人口的快速增长仅惠及当地居民，而另一些人则认为这一趋势带来了诸多弊端。讨论这两种观点，并给出你自己的看法。**

Some argue that the explosion of urban populations brings numerous benefits for city residents. However, others believe this trend leads to significant drawbacks. In my view, while urban expansion may cause some challenges, its overall advantages for locals, newcomers, and society as a whole far outweigh the drawbacks.

有些人认为，城市人口的快速增长为居民带来了诸多好处。然而，另一些人则认为，这一趋势会导致显著的负面影响。在我看来，尽管城市扩张可能带来一定挑战，但其对当地居民、新来者乃至整个社会的总体益处远远超过其弊端。

On the one hand, a significant increase in the population in cities undeniably benefits local residents by fostering economic prosperity and improving daily convenience. In most cases, this increase is driven by young migrants from rural areas who bring energy and adaptability to the labor market. Their presence expands the talent pool, providing businesses access to a diverse and skilled workforce that aligns with evolving industry demands. Additionally, a high concentration of young workers stimulates the tertiary sector. For example, many young people prefer independent living, which drives their demand for housing and household appliances, thereby boosting growth in the real estate and retail industries. Even those who struggle to find stable employment due to limited qualifications often take up service-oriented jobs, such as food delivery and courier services. These roles not only provide livelihoods for migrants but also enhance urban convenience, particularly in some Northeast Asian countries where such services are in high demand.

一方面，城市人口的快速增长无疑促进了当地经济繁荣，并提高了人们的日常便利性。在大多数情况下，人口增长主要由来自农村地区的年轻移民推动，他们为劳动力市场带来了活力和适应性。这些年轻人的涌入扩大了人才库，使企业能够获得多元化且技能匹配的劳动力，以满足不断变化的行业需求。此外，高度集中的年轻劳动力还刺激了第三产业的发展。例如，许多年轻人倾向于独立生活，这推动了住房和家用电器的需求，进而促进了房地产和零售行业的发展。即使是那些由于资质有限而难以找到稳定工作的人，也往往从事餐饮配送、快递等服务行业。这些工作不仅为移民提供了生计，还提升了城市的便利性，尤其是在一些东北亚国家，这类服务需求极高。

On the other hand, a growing number of residents in metropolises places significant strain on local authorities, particularly in aspects of public expenditure and equitable resource allocation. That is, governments must spend substantial amounts to develop critical infrastructure, as a growing population, often accompanied by higher birth rates, necessitates the construction of more schools and medical facilities. This heightened demand can impose a heavy financial burden on the national economy. Aside from the extra burden on authorities, local individuals and newcomers from lower-income backgrounds or those who struggle in a highly competitive society may find it difficult to access opportunities for upward mobility due to limited resources, especially in developing countries. The widening gap in social status and the unequal distribution of wealth can lead to rising social tensions and diminished morale among newcomers. Worse still, the concentration of resources among a privileged few can exacerbate inequality that would fuel social unrest and intensify the risk of national instability.

另一方面，城市人口的快速增长给地方政府带来了巨大压力，尤其是在公共支出和公平资源分配方面。政府必须投入大量预算以扩建关键基础设施，因为人口增长（通常伴随较高的出生率）要求建设更多的学校和医疗设施。这种额外的需求可能会给国家经济带来沉重的财政负担。除了对政府的额外压力外，低收入背景的新来者或在高度竞争社会中挣扎的个人，可能因资源有限而难以获得向上流动的机会，尤其是在发展中国家。社会地位的差距扩大以及财富分配的不均衡可能导致社会紧张局势上升，并削弱新来者的士气。更糟糕的是，资源过度集中于少数特权阶层可能加剧不平等，进一步引发社会动荡，并增加国家不稳定的风险。

In conclusion, although the population increase stimulates economic prosperity and enhances living convenience by strengthening the labor market and boosting local industries, it also puts pressure on government expenditures and exacerbates social inequality. However, in my view, the overall benefits to individuals and society counterbalance the challenges, making the population boom in cities a net positive development.

总之，尽管人口增长通过增强劳动力市场和促进地方产业发展，推动了经济繁荣并提升了生活便利性，但它同时也加重了政府财政负担，并加剧了社会不平等。然而，在我看来，其对个人和社会的整体益处远远超过所带来的挑战，使得城市扩张成为一种总体上积极的发展趋势。

| **因果推导** | `This is because... As a result...` | 解释原因并推导结果 |
| **递进加剧** | `Even worse... Worse still...` | 将弊端论证层层升级 |
| **例证衔接** | `For example...` | 用具体例子支撑论点 |

> **衔接亮点**：本文在益处段中使用了"In most cases""Additionally""Even those who..."等衔接，使论证从一般到特殊、从主要到补充，层次分明。特别是"Even those who struggle to find stable employment"这一让步式补充，使益处论证更加全面——即使找不到稳定工作的人也能为城市做出贡献。

| 词汇/短语 | 中文含义 | 词性/用法 | 替代普通表达 |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **fostering** | 促进；培育 | 动词 | helping |
| **tertiary sector** | 第三产业；服务业 | 名词短语 | service industry |
| **places significant strain on** | 给…带来沉重压力 | 动词短语 | makes things hard for |
| **equitable resource allocation** | 公平的资源分配 | 名词短语 | fair sharing of resources |
| **upward mobility** | 向上流动（阶层跃升） | 名词短语 | moving up in society |
| **counterbalance** | 抵消；平衡 | 动词 | balance out |

**1. 因果链式长句**
> *"Their presence expands the talent pool, providing businesses access to a diverse and skilled workforce that aligns with evolving industry demands."*

**技巧**：主句陈述直接效果（扩大人才库），现在分词短语"providing..."说明连带效果，定语从句"that aligns with..."进一步限定 workforce 的特征。三个层次层层推进，从"有什么"到"带来什么"再到"什么样的"，信息密度极高。

**2. 让步补充句**
> *"**Even those who struggle to find stable employment** due to limited qualifications often take up service-oriented jobs, such as food delivery and courier services."*

**技巧**：使用"Even those who..."结构，将论证范围扩展到最不利的群体，使益处论证更加全面。这种"即使...也..."的句式能够堵住逻辑漏洞，体现论证的严密性。

**3. 递进后果句**
> *"**Worse still**, the concentration of resources among a privileged few can exacerbate inequality that would fuel social unrest and intensify the risk of national instability."*

**技巧**："Worse still"将论证推向最坏后果，"that would fuel..."和"intensify..."两个动词短语层层递进，从"社会动荡"到"国家不稳定"，体现了归谬式推演的论证力度。

**4. 综合总结句**
> *"In conclusion, although the population increase stimulates economic prosperity and enhances living convenience by strengthening the labor market and boosting local industries, it also puts pressure on government expenditures and exacerbates social inequality."*

**技巧**：结尾句用"although"引导的让步从句完整概括了两种观点的核心内容，然后用主句表明自己的立场。"by strengthening... and boosting..."用介词短语说明益处的机制，使总结既有广度又有深度。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 题干第一种观点的关键限定词是 "only benefits people who live there"，而 Body 1 只论证"有好处"，未回应"是否仅惠及本地居民"这一层；开头段虽提到 newcomers，正文却没接住，TA 上有缺口。
- 结论段用 `counterbalance the challenges`（势均力敌地抵消）与开头段的 `far outweigh the drawbacks`（远超）力度不符，前后立场强度不一致，宜统一为 outweigh。
- 弊端段末尾从"资源集中于少数人"直推 "fuel social unrest and intensify the risk of national instability"，跨度偏大且无任何例证，属于论断偏重；可降级表述或补一个具体情境。
