The graphs illustrate the changes in the proportions of people using public transportation and private cars in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, and Adelaide from 2000 to 2010. 

As seen, Melbourne, Perth, and Adelaide saw relatively stable figures for public transport usage at around 18%, 10%, and 5%, respectively. In contrast, Sydney and Brisbane experienced noticeable growths. Specifically, in 2000, approximately 20% of Sydney’s population were taking public transport, the highest proportion at that time, and that figure rose gradually to about 24% in 2010; the corresponding data in Brisbane, in comparison, started at around 15%, and it climbed sharply over the decade, reaching about 27% and surpassing those in Melbourne and Sydney in 2010.

The remaining three cities experienced more moderate and fluctuating patterns, with Sydney’s figure rising from 20% in 2000 to a peak of around 42% in 2006 before dropping to 30% in 2010.

Overall, public transport use either remained steady or climbed slightly in most cities, while private vehicles usage continued to dominate and rose further during the decade, particularly in Adelaide.

**Some people think that it is better to use scientific techniques to grow food, while others think that we should grow food naturally. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.**

**有些人认为，利用科学技术种植粮食更好，而另一些人则认为我们应当以自然的方式进行耕作。讨论这两种观点，并给出你自己的看法。**

Some people argue that scientific approaches are essential for feeding the growing global population, whereas others believe that food should be cultivated using natural and traditional methods. My view is that both perspectives are justified, and a balanced integration of science and nature offers a sustainable path forward.

一些人认为，科学方法对于养活不断增长的全球人口至关重要，而另一些人则主张粮食应以自然和传统方式种植。依我之见，这两种观点都有其合理之处，而科学与自然的平衡融合才是可持续的发展道路。

On the one hand, scientific methods have revolutionized agriculture, enabling farmers to produce more food with fewer resources. Technologies such as hydroponics and vertical farming make it possible to grow crops on barren land or in urban environments, addressing the contradiction between land scarcity and population growth. For example, due to geopolitical conflicts and climate change, Israel faces limited land resources and prefers not to develop agricultural zones near territorial boundaries. Advanced farming techniques have made it possible for the country to cultivate drought-tolerant, land-efficient crops and employ precision irrigation systems with higher yields. These innovations not only strengthen food security under climate challenges but also reduce dependence on unsafe frontier land use in Israel.

一方面，科学方法已经彻底改变了农业，使农民能够以更少的资源生产更多的粮食。像水培和垂直农场这样的技术使得人们能够在贫瘠土地或城市环境中种植作物，从而缓解土地稀缺与人口增长之间的矛盾。**为例，由于**和气候变化，该国土地资源有限，并且倾向于避免在边境附近开发农业区。先进的农业技术使得种植耐旱、高效利用土地的作物成为可能，同时精确灌溉系统也带来了更高的产量。这些创新不仅在气候挑战下增强了粮食安全，还减少了**对边境不安全土地利用的依赖。

On the other hand, advocates of natural farming argue that reliance on modern agricultural materials, including synthetic fertilizers and growth hormones, may pose risks to the environment and human health. In some regions, especially those with infertile soil, modern agriculture may require excessive use of fertilizers and pesticides to secure the food supply, leading to soil degradation, water pollution, and loss of biodiversity. For example, chemical residues can contaminate soil and water sources and, even worse, result in the disappearance of native species. In contrast, natural farming practices such as crop rotation, composting, and organic pest control help preserve soil fertility and maintain ecological balance. By sustaining the natural ecosystem, they also yield food with higher nutritional value and authenticity, aligning with the increasing consumer demand for healthier and safer produce.

另一方面，自然农业的倡导者认为，过度依赖现代农业材料，包括合成肥料和生长激素，可能对环境和人类健康构成威胁。在一些地区，尤其是土壤贫瘠的地方，现代农业可能需要大量使用化肥和农药以保障粮食供给，从而导致土壤退化、水体污染和生物多样性丧失。例如，化学残留物可能污染土壤和水源，甚至更严重地造成本地物种的消失。相比之下，自然农业实践，如轮作、堆肥和有机病虫害防治，有助于保持土壤肥力和维持生态平衡。通过维持自然生态系统，这些方法还能够生产出营养价值更高、更加纯正的食物，以满足消费者对健康和安全农产品日益增长的需求。

In my view, the debate should not be framed as a binary opposition between science and nature but rather as an opportunity to integrate the strengths of both. For instance, precision agriculture that uses satellite data and sensors to optimize water and fertilizer use demonstrates how technology can support sustainable farming without compromising natural integrity. Governments should therefore promote agroecological science, which combines technological innovation with respect for natural processes.

在我看来，这场争论不应被视为科学与自然的对立，而应被看作是整合两者优势的契机。例如，利用卫星数据和传感器来优化水和肥料使用的精准农业，就展示了科技如何在不破坏自然完整性的前提下支持可持续耕作。因此，各国政府应当推动农业生态学的发展，这一领域将技术创新与对自然规律的尊重相结合。

In conclusion, while science enhances efficiency and food security, natural farming safeguards health and the environment. Integrating modern technology with ecological wisdom can create a sustainable agricultural system that meets human needs while preserving the planet for future generations.

总之，科学提升了农业效率和粮食安全，而自然农业守护了人类健康与生态环境。将现代科技与生态智慧相融合，能够建立一种既满足人类需求、又保护地球以惠及后代的可持续农业体系。

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

| 衔接类型 | 原文表达 | 功能说明 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **观点引入** | `On the one hand... On the other hand...` | 清晰划分双方立场 |
| **因果衔接** | `enabling farmers to...` | 说明科学方法的结果 |
| **例证衔接** | `For example...` | 用具体案例支撑论点 |
| **对比转折** | `In contrast...` | 在自然农业论内部引出对比 |
| **递进补充** | `even worse...` | 升级环境风险的严重性 |
| **总结衔接** | `In conclusion...` | 收束全文，重申立场 |

**1. 多重修饰+结果说明**
> *"Technologies such as hydroponics and vertical farming **make it possible to** grow crops on barren land or in urban environments, **addressing the contradiction between** land scarcity and population growth"*

**技巧**：用"such as"举例说明技术类型，"make it possible to..."说明技术效果，"addressing..."现在分词短语说明技术解决的问题。"contradiction between... and..."的表述精准概括了核心矛盾。

**2. 案例+因果链**
> *"due to geopolitical conflicts and climate change, Israel **faces limited land resources** and **prefers not to** develop agricultural zones near territorial boundaries. Advanced farming techniques **have made it possible for** the country to cultivate drought-tolerant, land-efficient crops and employ precision irrigation systems **with higher yields**"*

**技巧**：用"due to"引导原因，"faces... and prefers not to..."并列两个困境，"have made it possible for..."说明技术解决方案，"with higher yields"补充结果。因果链完整而清晰。

**3. 升级式危害描述**
> *"chemical residues **can contaminate** soil and water sources and, **even worse**, **result in** the disappearance of native species"*

**技巧**：用"can contaminate"描述直接危害，"even worse"升级严重性，"result in"引出更深层后果。"even worse"的插入使危害描述层层递进。

**4. 平衡立场句**
> *"**Integrating modern technology with ecological wisdom can create** a sustainable agricultural system **that meets** human needs **while preserving** the planet for future generations"*

**技巧**：用"Integrating... with..."动名词短语作主语，"can create"说明结果，"that meets... while preserving..."定语从句并列两个功能（满足需求+保护地球）。总结句全面而有力，体现了平衡立场。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 例子与论点错位：Body 1 的论点落在 hydroponics 与 vertical farming（城市、贫瘠地种植），但紧接的以色列例子讲的是耐旱作物与精准灌溉，属另一类技术。要么把论点改写为"技术让不宜耕作之地也能产粮"，要么换一个真正的垂直农业案例。
