The graph illustrates changes in the proportion of employees working in the service, manufacturing, agriculture, and mining sectors in Australia between 1900 and 2000. 

As shown, the highest proportion of Australian workers were employed in the service industry throughout the century. In 1900, around half of the workforce was engaged in that sector, and the figure increased gradually to 60% in 1940. It then remained stable until 1960, after which it rose once again, eventually reaching 70% by 2000. By contrast, mining accounted for only a small fraction of jobs, with its workforce proportion dropping from nearly 7% to just 1%. 

The proportions of workers in manufacturing and agriculture also decreased overall, with the data in both sectors showing brief growths in the first half of the century. In 1900, manufacturing employed about one-fifth of workers, and although that share rose modestly to just under 30% in 1920, it subsequently declined steadily to around 10% in 2000. Similarly, agriculture initially employed nearly 10% of the workforce, and that figure grew slightly to 15% (1940) before declining steadily to about 8% (2000). 

Overall, while the tertiary sector expanded to become overwhelmingly dominant, the shares of manufacturing and agriculture in employing people diminished gradually, and mining remained a marginal employer throughout the period.

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：文化与传统类 · Agree/Disagree

**The best way to learn a culture is to learn its language. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

**学习一种文化的最好方式是学习其语言。你在多大程度上同意或不同意？**

Some claim that acquiring a language is the most effective way to understand the culture of the people who speak it. Although learning a language provides valuable cultural insights, I disagree that it is the best dimension of cultural learning.

有人认为，掌握一门语言是理解讲这种语言的人所处文化的最有效途径。尽管学习语言确实能够提供宝贵的文化洞察，我不同意它是文化学习的最佳维度。

It is believed that learning a language is necessary for acquiring a culture because of its role as a precise communication tool. Many traditional proverbs or cyber vocabularies in a language usually cannot be fully rendered in another language as their imagery, historical connotations, or moral lessons are deeply tied to the original words. Without a solid command of the language, relying on translations frequently results in the loss of the core meaning of cultural expressions, and such exchanges inevitably become limited or even cause misunderstanding. For example, the Chinese word pronounced yuanfen cannot be translated into English accurately; alternatives such as "destiny" or "serendipity" only partially convey its meaning. Similarly, some young people prefer to compliment others on significant performance with the internet slang 666; however, the same expression signifies the devil in Western countries. Therefore, language learning enables individuals to engage directly with cultural bearers, ensuring that cultural meanings are transmitted with accuracy and authenticity.

人们认为学习语言对于获取文化是必要的，因为语言是精确的交流工具。许多语言中的传统谚语或网络词汇通常无法完全用另一种语言表达，因为它们的意象、历史内涵或道德教训深深植根于原词之中。如果没有扎实的语言能力，仅依靠翻译往往会丢失文化表达的核心含义，而这种交流不可避免地会受限，甚至导致误解。例如，中文词语“缘分”无法被准确地翻译成英语；像“destiny”（命运）或“serendipity”（机缘巧合）这样的替代词仅能部分传达其意义。同样，一些年轻人喜欢用网络用语“666”来夸奖他人的出色表现；然而，在西方国家，同样的表达却意味着“魔鬼”。因此，语言学习使个体能够直接与文化承载者互动，确保文化意义能够准确、真实地传递。

Nevertheless, while language offers valuable insights, it cannot be regarded as the best way to learn a culture. This is because a society's traditions and values extend far beyond linguistic expressions and are embodied in practices that words alone cannot fully capture. The practice of Chinese calligraphy, for example, deeply tied to philosophy and aesthetics, conveys ideals of harmony and self-cultivation that cannot be understood by studying the Chinese language alone. Likewise, traditional festivals such as the Spring Festival embodies family reunion, ancestor worship, and seasonal rituals, which are best appreciated through participation rather than verbal or written description. Even when Chinese words attempt to capture these traditions, the sensory experiences of food, the symbolism of red decorations, or the emotional depth of family gatherings transcend linguistic boundaries. Thus, while language is part of culture, it

cannot fully express other cultural forms, and genuine cultural learning requires direct engagement with practices and traditions.

然而，尽管语言提供了宝贵的洞察，它不能被视为学习文化的最佳方式。这是因为一个社会的传统和价值观远远超越语言表达，它们体现在仅凭文字无法完全理解的实践中。例如，中国书法的实践深受哲学与美学影响，它所传达的和谐与自我修养的理念，仅通过学习中文是无法完全理解的。同样，像春节这样的传统节日体现了家庭团聚、祭祖和季节性仪式，这些最佳的理解方式是通过参与而非语言描述来感受。即使中文词汇试图捕捉这些传统，食物的感官体验、红色装饰的象征意义或家庭聚会的情感深度都超越了语言的界限。因此，尽管语言是文化的一部分，但它无法完全表达其他文化形式，而真正的文化学习需要直接参与实践和传统。

In conclusion, mastering a language is indispensable for grasping cultural nuances and avoiding misinterpretation, yet it represents only one dimension of cultural learning. To fully appreciate a society, learners should also immerse themselves in its customs, rituals, and shared experiences, which communicate meanings that words alone cannot convey.

总之，掌握一门语言对于理解文化细微差别和避免误解是不可或缺的，但它仅代表文化学习的一个维度。要全面理解一个社会，学习者还应沉浸于其风俗、仪式和共享体验中，因为这些传达的意义是单靠文字无法表达的。

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

| 衔接类型 | 原文表达 | 功能说明 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **观点引入** | `It is believed that...` | 引出正方观点，语气客观 |
| **因果衔接** | `because of its role as...` | 解释语言学习必要性的原因 |
| **条件推演** | `Without... relying on translations frequently results in...` | 用条件句推演缺乏语言的后果 |
| **例证衔接** | `For example... Similarly...` | 用具体案例支撑论点 |
| **转折反驳** | `Nevertheless...` | 从让步转向反驳 |
| **递进补充** | `Likewise... Even when...` | 在反驳段内添加新论点 |
| **总结衔接** | `Thus...` | 从论证推导到结论 |

> **衔接亮点**：衔接手段层次分明——"It is believed that→because of→Without→Therefore"在让步段内部构建完整因果链，"Nevertheless→Likewise→Even when→Thus"在反驳段内部推进论证深度。

**1. 因果链式长句**
> *"Many traditional proverbs or cyber vocabularies in a language usually **cannot be fully rendered** in another language **as** their imagery, historical connotations, or moral lessons **are deeply tied to** the original words"*

**技巧**：用 "cannot be fully rendered" 指出翻译局限，再用 "as" 引导原因状语从句解释为何无法翻译。"imagery, historical connotations, or moral lessons"三个并列名词短语全面概括了语言的文化内涵。

**2. 条件-后果推演**
> *"**Without** a solid command of the language, relying on translations **frequently results in** the loss of the core meaning of cultural expressions, and such exchanges **inevitably become** limited or even cause misunderstanding"*

**技巧**：用 "Without..." 引导条件，"frequently results in" 指出直接后果，"inevitably become" 强调必然性。"or even cause misunderstanding"用 "even" 升级后果的严重性。

**3. 分词短语+例证**
> *"The practice of Chinese calligraphy, **for example**, **deeply tied to philosophy and aesthetics**, conveys ideals of harmony and self-cultivation **that cannot be understood by studying the Chinese language alone**"*

**技巧**：用 "for example" 引入例证，"deeply tied to philosophy and aesthetics" 过去分词短语作插入语补充背景，"that cannot be understood..." 定语从句强调仅靠语言无法理解的局限性。

**4. 让步-递进复合句**
> *"**Even when** Chinese words attempt to capture these traditions, the sensory experiences of food, the symbolism of red decorations, or the emotional depth of family gatherings **transcend linguistic boundaries**"*

**技巧**：用 "Even when" 引导让步从句，承认语言试图捕捉传统的努力，再用主句指出感官体验、象征意义和情感深度超越语言界限。"transcend linguistic boundaries"的表述极具学术性。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 反驳段被硬生生断成两截（`...while language is part of culture, it` 与 `cannot fully express...` 之间多出一个空行），需合并为完整段落；同段 `traditional festivals such as the Spring Festival embodies` 主谓不一致，应为 `embody`。
- "666" 这个例子说服力偏弱：中西差异源于数字符号的巧合，并非"学了语言就能化解"的翻译难题，反而不太支持"必须学语言"的论点；换成"上火""江湖"一类文化负载词会更贴题。
- 让步段末 `Therefore, language learning enables individuals to engage directly with cultural bearers` 与前文"翻译会丢失原意"之间缺一步推导（从"译不准"到"能与本族人直接交流"），可补一句过渡。
- 全部例证（缘分、666、书法、春节）均取自中国文化，视角单一；补一个非中文语境的例子（如日本的茶道、西班牙的 sobremesa）可提升论证普适性。
