**1. 尺寸+特征描述**
> *"Kitchen A **measures** 3 metres in length and 2.5 metres in width. Its size is **relatively small and compact**, **with** cupboards **taking up much of** the space"*

**技巧**：用"measures"精确描述尺寸，"relatively small and compact"概括空间特征，"with + 名词 + taking up"独立主格结构补充空间使用情况。一句完成"尺寸→特征→空间利用"的三重信息。

**2. 空间定位连锁**
> *"A frozen area **is located right after** the entrance, **while** the washroom (WC) **is placed on** the left-hand side **next to** another frozen section"*

**技巧**：用"is located right after"描述第一个设施位置，"while"并置第二个设施，"next to"描述相对位置。空间描述紧凑而清晰。

**3. 对比+差异标注**
> *"**By contrast**, Kitchen B **is significantly longer at** 5 metres, **providing** a much more open layout... **Unlike Kitchen A**, Kitchen B **features** a large central island with seating for food preparation and dining"*

**技巧**：用"By contrast"标志对比，"providing"现在分词说明结果，"Unlike Kitchen A"标注关键差异，"features"引出 Kitchen B 的独特设施。对比层次分明，差异突出。

**4. 总结段综合对比**
> *"**while** both kitchens **contain** essential features such as cupboards, a frozen storage area, and a facility room, Kitchen B **is more spacious**, **includes** a central dining area, and **has** a more systematic arrangement of working spaces"*

**技巧**：用"while"引导让步从句，承认共同设施，再并列三个差异（更宽敞、中央用餐区、系统化安排）。总结句对比鲜明，覆盖了核心差异。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：教育类 · Discuss Both Views

**Some people think that it is more effective for students to study in a group while others believe that it is better for them to study alone. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.**

**有些人认为学生以小组形式学习更有效，而另一些人则认为独自学习更好。讨论这两种观点并给出你自己的意见。**

Some individuals believe that studying in a group is an effective method for learners; however, others argue that learning individually is more productive. Both views are justified, and I believe that the two methods of studying should be combined according to the specific characteristics of certain curricula.

一些人认为，小组学习是学生的一种有效学习方式；然而，也有人认为独立学习效率更高。两种观点都有其合理性，我认为应根据不同课程的具体特点，将这两种学习方式结合起来。

On the one hand, studying in a group has several advantages in students' educational process. Firstly, group learning encourages the exchange of diverse ideas. When students collaborate, they are exposed to a wider range of perspectives, which can help them identify gaps in their knowledge and refine their understanding. This is particularly beneficial in subjects that require discussion, such as the social sciences, where analysing multiple viewpoints helps students form critical and balanced arguments. Secondly, group study creates a supportive learning environment at school. This atmosphere, created by group work, often encourages students to stay focused and committed. In such an environment, students who struggle with certain concepts can receive immediate clarification from peers, reducing frustration and accelerating their progress.

一方面，小组学习在学生的学习过程中具有多重优势。首先，小组学习能够促进多元观点的交流。当学生合作时，他们会接触到更广泛的视角，这有助于他们发现自身知识的不足并深化理解。这种方式在需要讨论的科目中尤为有益，例如社会科学，通过分析多种观点，学生能够形成更具批判性和更平衡的论证。其次，小组学习能够营造一种支持性的学校学习氛围。由小组合作所带来的这种氛围，往往能够促使学生保持专注并积极投入。在这样的环境中，在某些概念上遇到困难的学生可以从同伴那里立即得到解释，从而减少挫败感，并加快学习进度。

On the other hand, independent study allows learners to work at their own pace and tailor their approach to their individual needs. Every student has unique strengths and learning styles, and studying alone gives them the freedom to focus on areas they find challenging without the pressure of keeping up with others. This personalised approach leads to deeper concentration and more efficient use of time. More importantly, independent study fosters self-discipline in the cultivation of scientific literacy, a crucial skill not only for academic success but also for future careers, in which individuals are often expected to manage their workload autonomously. When equipped with self-discipline, students can maintain sustained attention in a solitary setting to read dense materials or solve complex academic problems without being distracted by external temptations.

另一方面，独立学习使学生能够按照自己的节奏学习，并根据个人需求调整学习方式。每个学生都有独特的优势和学习风格，而独自学习让他们能够在没有跟上他人步伐压力的情况下，专注于自己感到困难的部分。这种个性化的方式能够带来更深层次的专注和更高效的时间利用。更重要的是，独立学习有助于培养在科学素养形成过程中所需的自律能力，而这种能力不仅对学业成功至关重要，对未来职业生涯同样重要，因为在许多职业中，个人往往被期望能够自主管理工作任务。当具备自律能力时，学生便能够在独处的环境中保持持续专注，阅读高难度材料或解决复杂的学术问题，而不会被外界诱惑所干扰。

In my view, although group study offers valuable benefits in terms of interaction and motivation, it is independent learning that ultimately plays a more decisive role instudents' academic development. Group discussions can enhance understanding, but true mastery usually comes from personal effort and focused practice. For this reason, the most effective approach is often a combination of both methods.

在我看来，尽管小组学习在互动和激励方面具有宝贵的优势，但真正对学生学业发展起决定性作用的仍然是独立学习。小组讨论可以增强理解，但真正的掌握通常源于个人的努力与专注的练习。因此，最有效的方式往往是将两者结合起来。

In conclusion, group study broadens perspectives and strengthens motivation, while independent learning enables deeper focus, personalised progress, and stronger self-discipline. Since each method contributes differently to academic development, combining collaborative activities with individual practice offers students a balanced and effective way to achieve meaningful learning outcomes.

总之，小组学习能够开阔视野、增强学习动力，而独立学习则使学生获得更深层的专注、更个性化的进步以及更强的自律性。由于两种方式对学业发展都有不同的贡献，将协作学习活动与个人练习相结合，能够为学生提供一种平衡且高效的途径，以实现有意义的学习成果。

### 精析

#### ① 结构分析（精简）

本题属于 **Discuss Both Views** 类题型。此类题型的核心策略是：双方观点各一段分析，最后给出自己的综合立场。

本文采用 **"平衡-立场"** 四段式结构：

- **Paragraph 2 — Body 1（小组学习论）**
  - 主题句：`studying in a group has several advantages in students' educational process`

- **Paragraph 3 — Body 2（独立学习论）**

- **Paragraph 4 — Conclusion（结尾段）**

- **CC**：衔接手段功能分明——"Firstly→Secondly"在小组学习论内部推进，"More importantly→When equipped with"在独立学习论内部升级。"This is particularly beneficial in"的强调使论证更加精准。
- **LR**：见模块⑤词汇表，含 `productive`、`exchange of diverse ideas` 等精准词
- **GRA**：见模块⑤句式亮点

#### ④ 可迁移句型骨架（直接套用）（题型：Discuss）

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

| 衔接类型 | 原文表达 | 功能说明 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **观点引入** | `On the one hand... On the other hand...` | 清晰划分双方立场 |
| **列举递进** | `Firstly... Secondly...` | 在小组学习论内部分层展开 |
| **因果衔接** | `which can help them...` | 说明小组学习的具体益处 |
| **强调衔接** | `This is particularly beneficial in...` | 强调特定科目的适用性 |
| **递进升级** | `More importantly...` | 在独立学习论中升级论点重要性 |
| **条件衔接** | `When equipped with...` | 说明自律能力的作用条件 |
| **总结衔接** | `In conclusion...` | 收束全文，重申立场 |

> **衔接亮点**：衔接手段功能分明——"Firstly→Secondly"在小组学习论内部推进，"More importantly→When equipped with"在独立学习论内部升级。"This is particularly beneficial in"的强调使论证更加精准。

**1. 因果链式长句**
> *"When students collaborate, they **are exposed to** a wider range of perspectives, **which can help them** identify gaps in their knowledge and refine their understanding"*

**技巧**：用"When..."引导条件，"are exposed to"描述结果，"which can help them..."定语从句说明进一步影响。"identify gaps"和"refine their understanding"两个动词短语精准描述了认知提升的过程。

**2. 强调+例证**
> *"**This is particularly beneficial in** subjects that require discussion, **such as** the social sciences, **where** analysing multiple viewpoints helps students form critical and balanced arguments"*

**技巧**：用"This is particularly beneficial in"强调适用领域，"such as"举例说明，"where"引导定语从句描述具体机制。"critical and balanced arguments"的表述精准概括了社会科学学习的核心目标。

**3. 递进+重要性升级**
> *"**More importantly**, independent study **fosters self-discipline in** the cultivation of scientific literacy, **a crucial skill not only for** academic success **but also for** future careers, **in which** individuals are often expected to manage their workload autonomously"*

**技巧**：用"More importantly"升级论点重要性，"fosters self-discipline in..."说明培养内容，"a crucial skill not only for... but also for..."同位语结构说明技能的双重价值，"in which"引导定语从句描述职业场景。句式层次极为丰富。

**4. 平衡立场句**
> *"**combining collaborative activities with individual practice offers** students a balanced and effective way to achieve meaningful learning outcomes"*

**技巧**：用"combining... with..."动名词短语作主语，"offers..."说明效果，"a balanced and effective way"概括结合方式的优势。总结句简洁有力，体现了平衡立场。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 立场存在摇摆：开头承诺"两种方式应结合"，第四段却改口 `it is independent learning that ultimately plays a more decisive role`，随后又退回"结合"。三处口径不一，TA 会失分。建议全篇统一为"以独立学习为主、以小组讨论为辅"，一次讲清主次。
- 开头说要"根据具体课程特点"结合，正文却只在小组段顺带提了 social sciences，独立学习该匹配哪类课程（如数理推演、语言背诵）始终没写，承诺未兑现。
- `fosters self-discipline in the cultivation of scientific literacy` 里的 scientific literacy 与本段"自律、专注、抗干扰"的主线无关，属概念错接，删去后句子反而更清晰。另有笔误 `role instudents'` 缺一个空格。
