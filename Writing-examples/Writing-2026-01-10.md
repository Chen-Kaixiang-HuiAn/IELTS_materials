## Task 1

> **图表类型标注**：饼图（四机场乘机目的）

**The charts below give information about the purposes of passengers flying from four different airports in the UK in 2016.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2026-01-10](./Task1_img/2026-01-10.png)

The four pie charts illustrate the proportions of passengers travelling to visit friends, for holidays, for business, and for other reasons from Gatwick, Heathrow, Luton, and Stansted airports in the UK in 2016.

As shown, Gatwick and Heathrow airports were both patronized by the largest percentages of passengers on holiday trips. At Gatwick, 54% of passengers departed for holidays, far exceeding the share of those visiting friends (30%), while only 2% of passengers flew for other reasons. A similar but more balanced pattern was evident at Heathrow, where holidays were the purpose of travel of 37% of passengers, closely followed by visiting friends (33%). Heathrow stood out for business travel, which represented 26%, the highest rate in the four airports.

By contrast, Luton and Stansted showed a same profile. At both airports, visiting friends was the dominant purpose, accounting for 50% at Luton and 48% at Stansted. Holiday travel motivated the same percentage at each airport, with 36% recorded, while business trips were relatively uncommon, with the proportion of travelers on such trips registering at 13% and 14% respectively. As at Gatwick, only 2% of passengers at these two airports travelled for other reasons.

Overall, Gatwick and Heathrow were used primarily by holidaymakers, whereas visiting friends was the leading reason for travel from Luton and Stansted. However, the proportion of passengers flying for other purposes was negligible across all four airports.

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：媒体与广告类 · Two-Part Question

**Nowadays, some consumers are much less influenced by advertising. What do you think are the reasons for this? Do you think this is a positive or negative development?**

**现在，许多消费者更少受到广告的影响。你认为这一现象的原因是什么？你认为这是一个消极还是积极的发展？**

In contemporary society, an increasing number of consumers are far less influenced by advertising than in the past. There are several reasons for this decline in the effectiveness of commercials, and even though this trend presents challenges for businesses, it is largely a positive development.

在当代社会，越来越多的消费者相比过去更少受到广告的影响。广告效果的下降有多方面原因，尽管这一趋势给企业带来了挑战，但总体而言，它在很大程度上是一种积极的发展。

As observed, stereotyped advertising practices and exaggerated product claims reduce the power of advertising over consumers. Many advertising companies design homogeneous and uncreative commercials featuring celebrities to disseminate information. Such advertisements may persuade some young people to buy the products that their idols promote, but they cannot distract the wider public from daily trivialities, and most consumers are unlikely to purchase the merchandise if it is not an absolute necessity. Even worse, the widespread use of exaggerated or misleading claims in advertising has fundamentally eroded public trust in commercial messages. For example, Xiaomi, a high-tech company in China, has been deeply embroiled in a marketing crisis in which it used large-sized characters to attract consumers' attention but small print to present disclaimers. When consumers repeatedly encounter promotions that overstate product performance, conceal limitations, or manipulate images, they gradually develop a defensive attitude toward advertising.

显而易见，模式化的广告手法以及夸大的产品宣传削弱了广告对消费者的影响力。许多广告公司设计同质化、缺乏创意的名人代言广告来传播信息。这类广告或许能说服一些年轻人购买偶像代言的产品，但它们无法让更广泛的公众从日常琐事中分心，而且如果商品并非生活必需品，大多数消费者并不太可能购买。更糟糕的是，广告中夸大或误导性内容的泛滥，从根本上削弱了公众对商业信息的信任。例如，高科技公司小*深陷营销危机，其在广告中用醒目的大号字体吸引消费者注意，却用小字体标注免责条款。当消费者反复接触到夸大产品性能、掩盖局限性或操纵图片的宣传时，他们会逐渐对广告形成一种防御性心理。

Although people becoming less influenced by advertising has some negative consequences, the overall impact remains beneficial. Small businesses, which often depend on advertising to build visibility, may find it harder to compete, and advertising plays a role in informing the public about new products and services, so its declining influence could slow the diffusion of innovation. Nevertheless, people may cultivate more reasonable consumption habits if advertisements do not affect them too much. For example, some luxury brands, such as Gucci, have adopted marketing strategies that convey the idea that buying their products represents high social status, persuading consumers to associate certain brands with superior quality, which leads to impulse buying and distorted consumption concepts. By contrast, the reduced impact of advertising allows people to focus on basic necessities, which eases their financial burdens by minimizing impulsive purchases. When they are less influenced, they may resist competing over material possessions and manage their limited finances more effectively.

尽管人们不再容易受到广告影响也带来了一些消极后果，但总体影响仍然是积极的。依赖广告建立知名度的小企业可能会更难参与竞争，而且广告在向公众介绍新产品和新服务方面发挥着作用，因此其影响力下降可能会减缓创新的传播。然而，如果广告对人们的影响减弱，人们可能会培养出更加理性的消费习惯。例如，一些奢侈品牌（如古驰）采用传递“购买其产品象征较高社会地位”的营销策略，诱导消费者将某些品牌与“更高品质”联系起来，从而导致冲动消费和扭曲的消费观。相比之下，广告影响力的降低使人们能够更加关注基本生活必需品，通过减少冲动性购买来减轻经济负担。当他们不再轻易受到广告左右时，便更有可能避免物质攀比，并更有效地管理有限的财务资源。

In conclusion, the diminished influence of advertising on modern consumers can be attributed to the uncreative nature of many commercials and the public's growing skepticism toward exaggerated or false claims. Ultimately, despite some drawbacks, this trend encourages more rational spending habits and helps individuals focus on essential needs.

总而言之，现代消费者对广告影响力的减弱，可以归因于许多广告缺乏创意以及公众对夸大或虚假宣传日益增强的怀疑态度。总体来看，尽管存在一些弊端，这一趋势有助于促进更理性的消费方式，并帮助人们专注于真正的基本需求。

| **立场预告** | `even though this trend presents challenges for businesses, it is largely a positive development` | 开头段即预告利弊并存但总体积极 |
| **因果衔接** | `As observed, stereotyped advertising practices... reduce the power of advertising` | 用 As observed 引出原因分析 |
| **递进衔接** | `Even worse, the widespread use of exaggerated...` | 用 Even worse 递进强调更严重的问题 |
| **例证衔接** | `For example, Xiaomi...` | 用具体例子支撑论点 |
| **让步衔接** | `Although people becoming less influenced... has some negative consequences...` | 先让步再转折 |
| **对比衔接** | `By contrast, the reduced impact of advertising allows people to focus on basic necessities` | 用 By contrast 形成正反对比 |
| **总结衔接** | `In conclusion... Ultimately...` | 收束全文并升华 |

> **衔接亮点**：本文衔接手段丰富且功能明确。"Even worse"的使用使原因分析层层递进，"By contrast"使评价段的对比论证更加鲜明，"Ultimately"的总结使全文收束有力。

**1. 让步转折复合句**
> *"Such advertisements may persuade some young people to buy the products that their idols promote, **but** they cannot distract the wider public from daily trivialities, and most consumers are unlikely to purchase the merchandise if it is not an absolute necessity."*

**技巧**：用 but 转折连接两个对比分句，再用 and 补充第三个条件分句（if），一句之内完成"部分有效→整体无效→条件限制"的三层论证。

**2. 时间状语+结果推演句**
> *"**When** consumers repeatedly encounter promotions that overstate product performance, conceal limitations, or manipulate images, they gradually develop a defensive attitude toward advertising."*

**技巧**：用 When 引导时间状语从句（内含 that 定语从句修饰 promotions），主句用 gradually develop 描述渐进式心理变化，因果机制清晰。

**3. 定语从句嵌套句**
> *"For example, some luxury brands, such as Gucci, have adopted marketing strategies that convey the idea that buying their products represents high social status, persuading consumers to associate certain brands with superior quality, which leads to impulse buying and distorted consumption concepts."*

**技巧**：用 that 引导定语从句修饰 strategies，再用 that 引导同位语从句说明 idea 的内容，最后用 which 引导非限制性定语从句总结后果，句式层层嵌套却逻辑清晰。

**4. 因果总结句**
> *"In conclusion, the diminished influence of advertising on modern consumers **can be attributed to** the uncreative nature of many commercials and the public's growing skepticism toward exaggerated or false claims."*

**技巧**：用 can be attributed to 的被动语态结构总结原因，既客观又正式，是 Task 2 总结段的经典句式。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- Body 2 的让步部分（小企业难以竞争、创新传播放缓）仅一句带过、无任何支撑，与后文充分展开的 Gucci 对比明显失衡，可补半句机制说明，或干脆压缩成从句以突出主论。
- `they cannot distract the wider public from daily trivialities` 表意别扭——"把公众从琐事中分散注意力"本是广告的正面功能，此处却用作否定，改为 `fail to capture the attention of the wider public` 更直接。
- Gucci 例子证明的是"广告本身有害"，而段落主张的是"广告影响力下降是好事"，两者之间靠 By contrast 硬转，中间缺一步"少受广告影响即少受此类操纵"的过渡推理。
