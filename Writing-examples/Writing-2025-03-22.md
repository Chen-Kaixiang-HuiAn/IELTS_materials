## Task 1

> **图表类型标注**：柱状图（双图对比（男女驾照持有率 + 17-20岁驾驶率））

**The bar charts show the percentage of men and women in Great Britain who had driving licenses and another bar chart shows the percentage of men and women who were drivers at the age of 17-20.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-03-22](./Task1_img/2025-03-22.png)

The bar charts illustrate the percentage of males and females with a driver’s license in Great Britain from 1976 to 2006, along with the proportion of drivers aged 17 to 20 in 1996 and 2006. 

As shown in the first chart, the percentage of driving license holders increased for both genders, with men consistently outnumbering women. Specifically, in 1976, 70% of men possessed a driving license, and that figure steadily rose to 80% in 2006. The rate of women, meanwhile, experienced a significant upturn, doubling from 30% in 1976 to 60% in 2006. This growth considerably narrowed the gender gap from 40% to 20%.

In contrast, a decline in driving license ownership among individuals aged 17 to 20 was seen. In 1996, half of the males in that age range held a license, whereas 30% of their female counterparts did so. However, by 2006, those figures had dropped, with the decline being more pronounced among men, to 45% and 20%, respectively.

Overall, while the proportion of men and women with driving licenses grew over time, with men maintaining a higher percentage, the trend was reversed among young drivers, whose corresponding rates fell over the given period.

**1. 伴随比较句**
> *"the percentage of driving license holders increased for both genders, **with men consistently outnumbering women**."*

**技巧**：主句陈述总体趋势，"with + 名词 + 现在分词"的独立主格结构伴随比较两个群体的大小关系。这种句式将"总体增长"和"性别差异"两个信息压缩在一个句子中，信息密度极高。

**2. 倍数+效果复合句**
> *"The rate of women, meanwhile, experienced a significant upturn, **doubling from 30% in 1976 to 60% in 2006**. **This growth considerably narrowed the gender gap** from 40% to 20%."*

**技巧**：第一句用现在分词"doubling"概括变化幅度；第二句用"This growth"指代前文，"narrowed the gender gap"提炼数据背后的关系。这种"数据→关系"的递进使描述更加深入。

**3. 转折对比句**
> *"**In contrast**, a decline in driving license ownership among individuals aged 17 to 20 was seen."*

**技巧**："In contrast"明确标记与第一个图表的相反趋势，使读者立即意识到数据方向的转变。被动语态"was seen"客观陈述现象，避免了主观判断。

**4. 综合概述句**
> *"Overall, while the proportion of men and women with driving licenses grew over time, with men maintaining a higher percentage, **the trend was reversed among young drivers**, whose corresponding rates fell over the given period."*

**技巧**：概述段用"while"引导让步从句描述总体趋势，主句用"the trend was reversed"点明年轻群体的相反趋势。"whose"引导的非限制性定语从句补充说明年轻驾驶员的具体变化，使概述既有总体又有局部。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：教育类 · Problem-Solution

**Some students dislike learning math. What are the reasons, and what solutions can help students enjoy learning math?**

**一些学生讨厌学数学。这个现象的原因是什么？什么方法能帮学生喜欢学数学？**

Mathematics is often regarded as a challenging subject, and many students struggle to find it enjoyable. This aversion can be attributed to factors such as abstract mathematics concepts and lack of visual applications. However, through interactive learning techniques and encouraging exploratory learning, students can develop a more positive attitude toward mathematics.

数学通常被认为是一门具有挑战性的学科，许多学生很难从中找到乐趣。这种排斥心理主要源于数学概念的抽象性以及缺乏直观的应用。然而，通过互动式学习技术和鼓励探索式学习，学生可以逐渐培养对数学的积极态度。

As observed, the abstract nature of mathematics and the lack of engaging teaching techniques are key reasons why many students struggle with and dislike the subject. Unlike history or literature, which rely on narratives and storytelling, mathematics requires conceptual thinking and the ability to visualize abstract relationships. This can make it difficult for young learners to transition from counting objects to understanding negative numbers or algebraic expressions, all of which lead to a frustrating experience. Aside from abstraction, mathematics is often taught in a rigid and repetitive manner that relies heavily on drills and worksheets, with little emphasis on interactive or exploratory learning. In contrast to science and literature, where students can conduct experiments or analyze stories, mathematics is frequently presented as a series of isolated problems having little connection to real life. Without seeing its practical applications, students may lose interest and perceive mathematics as a subject to pass, overlooking its value in imparting essential skills.

数学的抽象性以及缺乏吸引力的教学方法是许多学生不喜欢数学和学习困难的主要原因。与依赖叙述和故事情节的历史或文学不同，数学需要概念性思维和抽象关系的可视化能力。这使得年轻学习者在从数数到理解负数或代数表达式的过程中面临较大的挑战，进而导致挫败感。除了抽象性，数学教学往往以刻板和重复的方式进行，依赖大量练习和作业，而缺少互动和探索性的学习方式。相比科学和文学，学生可以通过实验或故事分析来加深理解，而数学通常被呈现为一系列彼此孤立的问题，缺乏与现实生活的联系。由于看不到其实际应用，学生可能会失去兴趣，将数学视为一门仅仅需要通过考试的科目，而忽略了它在培养重要技能方面的价值。

With those in mind, making abstract mathematics more concrete and encouraging exploratory learning can help students engage more effectively with mathematics. Specifically, one approach is to use visual aids and manipulatives to bridge the gap between abstract concepts and student understanding. Tools like number lines, geometric models, algebra tiles, and interactive graphs make complex ideas more tangible and easier to grasp. This hands-on approach enhances problem-solving skills and makes mathematics more meaningful. Another approach is shifting from rote memorization to inquiry-based learning, with deeper comprehension being promoted. In place of merely providing formulas, students can experiment with shapes to discover mathematical principles on their own. When students actively explore concepts, instead of passively memorizing rules, they build a stronger foundation and develop a greater appreciation for mathematics, making it an engaging and rewarding subject rather than just a series of abstract calculations.

考虑到这些因素，使抽象数学更加具体化，并鼓励探索性学习可以帮助学生更有效地理解数学。具体而言，其中一种方法是使用视觉工具和操作性教具，以弥合抽象概念与学生理解之间的差距。例如，数轴、几何模型、代数方块和交互式图表等工具可以使复杂的数学概念更加直观和易于掌握。这种动手实践的方法不仅提高了解决问题的能力，还使数学学习更加有意义。另一种方法是从死记硬背转向探究式学习，以促进更深入的理解。与其直接向学生提供公式，不如让他们通过操作几何图形来自主探索数学原理。当学生主动探索概念，而不是被动记忆规则时，他们能够建立更扎实的基础，并对数学产生更大的兴趣，从而使其成为一门富有吸引力且富有成就感的学科，而不仅仅是抽象计算的集合。

In conclusion, students' aversion to mathematics is largely due to its abstract nature and to unengaging teaching methods. By using visual aids, manipulatives, and inquiry-based learning, educators can bridge this gap so that mathematics becomes more accessible and relevant.

总之，学生对数学的排斥主要源于其抽象性以及缺乏吸引力的教学方法。通过使用视觉教具、操作性工具以及探究式学习，教育者可以弥合这一差距，使数学变得更加易懂和相关，从而帮助学生培养对数学的兴趣和理解。

> **衔接亮点**：本文在原因段中使用了"Unlike history or literature, which rely on narratives and storytelling, mathematics requires conceptual thinking"的对比衔接，通过与文学和历史的对比突出数学的特殊性。"all of which lead to"用关系代词将前文的多个因素与结果关联，使因果逻辑更加紧密。

| 词汇/短语 | 中文含义 | 词性/用法 | 替代普通表达 |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **aversion** | 排斥；厌恶 | 名词（排斥） | dislike |
| **abstract nature** | 抽象性（本质） | 名词短语（抽象性） | being abstract |
| **conceptual thinking** | 概念性思维 | 名词短语（概念性思维） | thinking about ideas |
| **visualize abstract relationships** | 将抽象关系可视化 | 动词短语（可视化抽象关系） | see abstract ideas |
| **rote memorization** | 死记硬背 | 名词短语（死记硬背） | memorizing without understanding |
| **inquiry-based learning** | 探究式学习 | 名词短语（探究式学习） | learning by exploring |

**1. 对比分析句**
> *"**Unlike history or literature**, which rely on narratives and storytelling, **mathematics requires conceptual thinking** and the ability to visualize abstract relationships."*

**技巧**：用"Unlike"引导对比对象，"which"引导的非限制性定语从句描述文学和历史的特点，主句指出数学的不同要求。这种"对比→特点→要求"的句式使数学的特殊性一目了然。

**2. 因果推导句**
> *"This can make it difficult for young learners to transition from counting objects to understanding negative numbers or algebraic expressions, **all of which lead to** a frustrating experience."*

**技巧**：主句陈述困难，"all of which lead to"将前文的多个学习难点与"挫败感"关联。"all of which"指代前文的"counting objects""negative numbers""algebraic expressions"，使因果逻辑涵盖全面。

**3. 条件+效果句**
> *"**When students actively explore concepts**, instead of passively memorizing rules, they build a stronger foundation and develop a greater appreciation for mathematics, making it an engaging and rewarding subject rather than just a series of abstract calculations."*

**技巧**："When"引导条件从句，"instead of"插入对比，主句陈述双重效果（建立基础+培养兴趣），现在分词短语"making..."推导最终结果。"rather than"将"engaging and rewarding subject"与"abstract calculations"对比，突出解决方案的价值。

**4. 综合总结句**
> *"By using visual aids, manipulatives, and inquiry-based learning, educators can bridge this gap so that mathematics becomes more accessible and relevant."*

**技巧**：结尾句用"By + 动名词"提出解决方案，"so that"引导目的状语从句说明预期效果。"accessible and relevant"两个形容词并列，分别从"易懂性"和"相关性"两个维度说明解决方案的目标，使结尾既有行动又有愿景。
