## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：全球化类 · Discuss Both Views

**Some people believe that cheap and fast transportation is required for successful international business while others believe good technology is more important. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.**

**一些人认为，低成本且高效率的交通运输是国际商业成功所必需的，而另一些人则认为先进技术更加重要。讨论双方观点并给出你自己的看法。**

Some people argue that affordable and efficient transportation is the key factor behind successful international business, whereas others believe advanced technology plays a more significant role. Although efficient transportation remains essential for global commerce, I believe technological development is ultimately more important.

一些人认为，价格低廉且高效的交通运输是国际商业成功的关键因素，而另一些人则认为先进技术发挥着更重要的作用。尽管高效运输对全球贸易仍然至关重要，但我认为技术发展最终更加重要。

On the one hand, affordable and rapid transportation is undeniably crucial for international business operations. Firstly, efficient transport systems reduce delivery time and operational costs, allowing companies to transport goods, particularly fresh food and pharmaceutical products, quickly between countries in order to maintain quality. For example, air freight and high-speed shipping networks enable multinational supermarkets to import fresh produce from other countries on a daily basis. Without reliable transportation, such businesses would struggle to survive in highly competitive global markets. Secondly, affordable transportation promotes international trade by making imported goods accessible to consumers at reasonable prices. If transport expenses become excessively high, companies may be forced to raise product prices, thereby reducing demand and limiting international commerce. The rise of low-cost container shipping, for instance, has significantly expanded global trade over recent decades by enabling manufacturers to export products efficiently to distant markets.

一方面，价格合理且快速的交通运输无疑对国际商业运营至关重要。首先，高效的运输系统能够减少配送时间和运营成本，使企业能够迅速在国家之间运输商品，尤其是新鲜食品和药品，从而保证产品质量。例如，航空货运和高速航运网络使跨国超市能够每天从其他国家进口新鲜农产品。如果没有可靠的运输体系，这类企业将难以在竞争激烈的全球市场中生存。其次，低成本运输通过让消费者能够以合理价格购买进口商品，从而促进国际贸易。如果运输成本过高，企业可能被迫提高产品价格，进而降低需求并限制国际贸易的发展。例如，低成本集装箱航运的兴起，在过去几十年里极大地推动了全球贸易的发展，因为它使制造商能够高效地向遥远市场出口产品。

On the other hand, supporters of technology argue that it has a far broader and more transformative influence on international business success. Modern technology enables companies to communicate instantly with suppliers and employees worldwide. Through translation software and video conferencing, businesses can manage international operations efficiently without constant physical travel, which not only saves time and money but also accelerates decision-making processes. Another significant point is that technological innovation continuously improves transportation itself, demonstrating that technology functions as the driving force behind many aspects of international business development. For example, satellite navigation systems, automated logistics networks and advanced food preservation technology have made shipping faster, cheaper and more reliable. Thanks to developments in preservation technology, a large proportion of cherries grown in Chile can now be exported to China despite the enormous geographical distance between the two countries. This trade has greatly promoted Chile's agricultural industry and strengthened economic cooperation between the two nations.

另一方面，技术支持者认为，技术对国际商业成功具有更广泛且更具变革性的影响。现代技术使企业能够与全球各地的供应商和员工进行即时沟通。通过翻译软件和视频会议，企业能够高效管理国际业务，而无需频繁进行跨国出行，这不仅节省了时间和资金，还加快了决策过程。另一个重要观点是，技术创新不断改善交通运输本身，这表明技术是推动国际商业多个领域发展的核心动力。例如，卫星导航系统、自动化物流网络以及先进的食品保鲜技术，使运输变得更快速、更廉价且更加可靠。得益于保鲜技术的发展，大量智利生产的樱桃如今能够出口到中国，尽管两国之间相距遥远。这种贸易极大促进了智利农业的发展，并加强了两国之间的经济合作。

In conclusion, although affordable and efficient transportation provides the essential infrastructure for global trade by reducing delivery costs and expanding international markets, technology plays a more decisive role because it enhances communication, improves logistics systems and drives innovations that make international business increasingly efficient and interconnected.

总之，尽管价格合理且高效的交通运输通过降低配送成本和拓展国际市场，为全球贸易提供了基础设施，但技术发挥着更为决定性的作用，因为它能够加强沟通、改善物流系统，并推动创新，使国际商业变得更加高效且联系更加紧密。

**1. 让步转折复合句**
> *"**Although** efficient transportation remains essential for global commerce, **I believe** technological development is ultimately more important."*

**技巧**：用 Although 引导让步从句，先承认运输的重要性，再在主句中用 I believe 明确支持技术，使立场更加审慎有力。

**2. 因果链推演句**
> *"**If** transport expenses become excessively high, companies may be forced to raise product prices, **thereby reducing** demand and limiting international commerce."*

**技巧**：用 If 引导条件从句，主句用 may be forced to 描述可能的后果，thereby reducing 现在分词短语引出连锁反应，因果链条完整。

**3. 多重效果句**
> *"Through translation software and video conferencing, businesses can manage international operations efficiently without constant physical travel, **which not only saves** time and money **but also accelerates** decision-making processes."*

**技巧**：用 Through 说明方式，which 引导非限制性定语从句，not only... but also... 连接双重效果，信息密度极高。

**4. 例证支撑句**
> *"**Thanks to** developments in preservation technology, a large proportion of cherries grown in Chile can now be exported to China **despite** the enormous geographical distance between the two countries."*

**技巧**：用 Thanks to 说明技术支撑，despite 引导让步状语强调距离障碍，使智利樱桃的例子极具说服力。

**5. 综合总结句**
> *"**In conclusion, although** affordable and efficient transportation provides the essential infrastructure for global trade by reducing delivery costs and expanding international markets, technology **plays a more decisive role because** it enhances communication, improves logistics systems and drives innovations that make international business increasingly efficient and interconnected."*

**技巧**：用 although 引导让步从句，先承认运输的基础设施价值，再主句用 plays a more decisive role because... 明确支持技术并给出三个理由，总结全面有力。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 技术段全程以 `supporters of technology argue that...` 转述他人观点，作者本人的判断只在开头段和结论段露面；Discuss 类题型宜在段内补一句表态（如 `This second position strikes me as the more compelling one, because...`），让立场贯穿正文而非只挂两头。
- 两段的信号词不统一：运输段用 `Firstly / Secondly`，技术段却换成 `Another significant point is that`；另外运输段两条论据都配了例证（air freight、container shipping），技术段第一条"即时沟通"却只罗列了工具、没有落地例子，与智利樱桃那条的具体程度落差较大。

**仿写任务**：用「先退后进亮立场」骨架写一句：先承认『低成本且快速的运输是国际贸易的基础』，再点明你为何认为技术更具决定性。
