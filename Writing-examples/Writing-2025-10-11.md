The graphs illustrate changes in hospital bed availability per 1,000 people and the proportion of patients who waited either within or more than 30 days for elective surgery in the UK, Australia, Canada, Sweden, and Mexico in 2010, 2014, and 2018. 

As seen, Sweden and Mexico showed rising numbers of hospital beds per thousand people. To be specific, the figure for Sweden increased from 3.7 to 4.5 beds, surpassing those of Australia and Canada and becoming the highest, while that for Mexico grew from 2.0 to about 2.8 beds, despite remaining the lowest. In contrast, the UK, Australia, and Canada experienced declines in the bed availability of their hospitals. Canada had the most beds in 2010, with 5 beds per 1,000 recorded, followed by Australia (4.6 beds), but the data fell to 4.2 and 4.0 beds, respectively, while the UK saw a sharper drop from 4.2 beds to the same level as that in Mexico. 

Regarding waiting times, Australia and Sweden consistently had the largest and similar shares of patients treated within 30 days, and the corresponding statistics rose from 65% to 82%. Mexico also improved, with the proportion of patients receiving treatment within a month increasing from 45% to 60%. Consequently, the rate of patients waiting over 30 days in those three nations fell sharply, with Sweden recording the lowest level at 18% in 2018. However, the UK and Canada saw declines in timely treatment, from 62% and 55% to about 40%, correspondingly.

Overall, Sweden and Mexico expanded hospital capacity and reduced waiting times, while Australia maintained strong surgical efficiency despite fewer beds.

In my opinion, it is reasonable to expect the government to support children's primary artistic education to safeguard national artistic identity. Without state involvement, children from less privileged families would be deprived of opportunities that can profoundly shape their intellectual growth. However, advanced art education that extends beyond basic needs or national interests should be financed by individuals or private institutions.

在我看来，政府支持儿童的基础艺术教育是合理的，这不仅能维护国家的艺术身份，也能保障公平的教育机会。若缺乏政府介入，来自经济条件较差家庭的孩子将失去能够深刻塑造其智力与人格发展的机会。然而，那些超出基本需求或国家利益范围的高阶艺术教育，应由个人或私人机构资助。

**1. 多重结果+目的说明**
> *"Creative education **not only nurtures** imagination and emotional intelligence **but also enhances** cognitive abilities"*

**技巧**：用"not only... but also..."并列三个积极效果（想象力、情感智力、认知能力），再用"If... it can help..."条件句说明政府投入的目的。句式层次丰富，论证全面。

**2. 目的状语+结果推导**
> *"public funding for the arts is a strategic investment **that preserves cultural heritage, ensuring that** a nation's unique identity and traditions are passed on to future generations"*

**技巧**：用"that preserves..."定语从句说明投资的性质，"ensuring that..."现在分词短语说明投资的目的。"strategic investment"的表述将艺术资助提升到国家战略层面。

**3. 条件-后果推演**
> *"**Without state involvement**, children from less privileged families **would be deprived of** opportunities that can profoundly shape their intellectual growth"*

**技巧**：用"Without..."引导条件，"would be deprived of"指出后果，"that can profoundly shape..."定语从句说明机会的重要性。这种"缺失→剥夺→影响"的推演使政府资助的必要性极具说服力。

**4. 分层立场句**
> *"**while** public funding **is essential to** preserve cultural heritage and ensure equal access to basic arts education, **higher levels of** artistic training **should remain the responsibility of** families and private organizations"*

**技巧**：用"while"引导让步从句，承认公共资助对基础教育的必要性，再用主句指出高阶培训应由私人承担。这种"基础公共+高阶私人"的分层立场使论证更加 nuanced 和实用。
