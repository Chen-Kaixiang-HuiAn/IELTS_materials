> **图表类型标注**：地图（动物园变迁图）

**The maps below show changes that have taken place at a zoo in Australia between 1960 and now.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-09-13](./Task1_img/2025-09-13.png)

The maps illustrate how an Australian zoo has been transformed from 1960 to the present. 

In 1960, the zoo was divided by a main road running from the northeast to the southwest. To the west were two enclosures: an Australian animal area, with an African animal area directly to its north. On the eastern side of the zoo stood a gift shop, with picnic areas further east and a chimpanzee enclosure just to the north. At the northern edge near the harbour, a bird area and a seal show were connected to the main road. 

At present, the main road remains, but a new entrance gate has been added in the south, along with a ferry terminal in the north. The animal enclosures have been reorganized: the African animal section has been relocated to the central area of the zoo, while its original site has been converted into a wild animal zone; the bird and chimpanzee enclosures have been removed. Meanwhile, the gift shop has been expanded, and although the seal show continues to operate, it has been moved closer to the centre. Furthermore, a cable car now runs between the seal show and the picnic area, linking them directly to the northern entrance.

Overall, the zoo has been redesigned to improve accessibility and visitor experience, with the addition of two entrances, a ferry service, and a cable car, alongside a reorganization of animal enclosures.

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：全球化类 · Advantages/Disadvantages

**Although modern technology has made international communication very easy, many business people still travel long distances for business meetings. Do the advantages of face-to-face meetings outweigh the disadvantages?**

**尽管现代技术已经使国际交流变得非常容易，仍然有许多商务人士长途旅行参加国际商务会议。面对面开会的好处大于坏处吗？**

In the digital era, advanced communication technologies have significantly simplified international interaction, yet many professionals continue to travel abroad for business meetings. While such trips may involve considerable costs and inconveniences, I contend that the benefits of face-to-face meetings far outweigh their drawbacks.

数字时代，先进的通信技术显著简化了国际交流，但许多专业人士仍然继续出国参加商务会议。尽管此类出行可能涉及相当可观的费用和不便，但我认为面对面的会议带来的益处远远超过其弊端。

Admittedly, frequent international business travel is burdensome for both institutional budgets and employees' physical well-being when compared with online meetings, which usually require only a modest investment in technical support. Modern communication technologies now provide efficient and high-quality remote experiences by offering instant connectivity across the globe, thereby eliminating geographical barriers and reducing the necessity of physical attendance at international meetings. From an operational standpoint, however, long-haul flights, visa applications, and hotel accommodations are prohibitively expensive, so regular in-person attendance can impose a heavy financial strain on companies, especially small and medium-sized enterprises with limited resources. Nor should the human dimension be overlooked: extended journeys are not only time-consuming and physically exhausting but also disruptive to personal life and work-life balance. As a result, diminished productivity, heightened stress levels, and chronic health issues arising from long-distance travel undermine both individual efficiency and the long-term sustainability of organizations.

诚然，与通常只需少量技术支持投入的线上会议相比，频繁的国际商务旅行无论对机构预算还是员工身体健康而言都十分沉重。现代通信技术如今能够提供高效且高质量的远程体验，凭借即时的全球连接消除地理障碍，从而减少了国际会议中实体出席的必要性。然而，从运营角度看，长途飞行、签证申请以及酒店住宿费用高昂，因而定期的亲自出席会给企业带来沉重的经济负担，尤其是对资源有限的中小型企业而言。此外，人性层面同样不可忽视：漫长的旅程不仅耗时费力、令人身心疲惫，而且还会干扰个人生活与工作平衡。其结果是，生产力下降、压力水平上升以及由长途旅行引发的慢性健康问题，都会削弱个人效率并损害组织的长期可持续性。

Nevertheless, in practice, in-person interactions bring substantial advantages that no digital tool can fully replicate. Specifically, they not only foster stronger interpersonal relationships but also allow nonverbal cues, such as facial expressions and body language, to play a pivotal role in building trust and genuine human connection. This connection is particularly indispensable in high-stakes negotiations, where mutual confidence often determines the success or failure of a deal. Furthermore, physical meetings create spontaneous opportunities for informal conversations during breaks or meals, from which deeper collaboration and long-term partnerships frequently emerge: connections rarely cultivated within the rigid structure of virtual platforms. Beyond the meeting room, business trips also deliver indirect benefits. Professionals gain first-hand exposure to local markets, cultural practices, and consumer behaviors, thereby developing more context-sensitive and effective business strategies. For instance, a manager visiting a foreign branch may identify operational inefficiencies or market opportunities that would remain invisible through remote communication alone.

然而，在实践中，面对面的交流带来了任何数字工具都无法完全替代的巨大优势。具体而言，它们不仅能促进更强的人际关系，还能让非语言线索（如面部表情和肢体语言）在建立信任与真实的人际联结中发挥关键作用。这种联结在高风险谈判中尤为不可或缺，因为相互信任往往决定了一笔交易的成败。此外，实体会议能够在休息或用餐时创造自发的非正式交流机会，从而孕育更深层次的合作与长期伙伴关系--这些联系在虚拟平台的僵化结构中很少能培养出来。会议之外，商务旅行还带来间接收益。专业人士能够亲身接触当地市场、文化习俗和消费者行为，从而制定更具情境敏感性和更有效的商业战略。例如，一位经理在访问海外分支时，可能会发现仅凭远程沟通无法察觉的运营低效或市场机遇。

In conclusion, although business travel entails financial and physical costs, the irreplaceable human connection and practical insights it generates make it more beneficial than disadvantageous. Therefore, face-to-face meetings are likely to remain an indispensable component of international business despite rapid advances in communication technologies.

总之，尽管商务旅行包含经济和身体上的成本，但它所带来的不可替代的人际联系和实践洞见，使其利大于弊。因此，面对面的会议很可能在通信技术迅速发展的背景下，仍然是国际商务中不可或缺的组成部分。

**骨架 2 — 劣势对照**
> However, a notable disadvantage is ______, which may ______.

**骨架 3 — 平衡立场**
> On balance, the benefits outweigh the drawbacks provided that ______.

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

| 衔接类型 | 原文表达 | 功能说明 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **让步引入** | `Admittedly...` | 承认弊端存在 |
| **对比衔接** | `when compared with...` | 与线上会议对比 |
| **转折对比** | `From an operational standpoint, however...` | 从理论转向实际运营角度 |
| **强调衔接** | `Nor should the human dimension be overlooked...` | 用倒装强调身心健康维度 |
| **因果衔接** | `As a result...` | 从原因推导到结果 |
| **转折反驳** | `Nevertheless...` | 从弊端转向优点 |
| **递进补充** | `Furthermore... Beyond the meeting room...` | 在优点段内添加新论点 |
| **总结衔接** | `In conclusion...` | 收束全文，重申立场 |

> **衔接亮点**：衔接手段功能分明——"Admittedly→however→Nor should...→As a result"在弊端段内部构建完整论证链，"Nevertheless→Specifically→Furthermore→Beyond"在优点段内部从核心优势推进到间接收益。"Nor should the human dimension be overlooked"的倒装句式使论证获得修辞强调。

| 词汇/短语 | 中文含义 | 词性/用法 | 替代普通表达 |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **simplified** | 使……变得简便 | 动词 | made easier |
| **burdensome** | 繁重的、令人吃力的 | 形容词 | heavy |

**1. 倒装强调句**
> *"**Nor should the human dimension be overlooked**: extended journeys are not only time-consuming and physically exhausting but also disruptive to personal life and work-life balance"*

**技巧**：用 "Nor should... be overlooked" 倒装结构强调身心健康维度不容忽视，冒号后展开具体说明。"not only... but also..."并列两个负面结果。这种倒装句式使论证获得额外的修辞力度。

**2. 多重结果推导**
> *"As a result, diminished productivity, heightened stress levels, and chronic health issues arising from long-distance travel undermine both individual efficiency and the long-term sustainability of organizations"*

**技巧**：用 "As a result" 引出结果，"diminished productivity, heightened stress levels, and chronic health issues"三个并列名词短语概括负面影响，"arising from"分词短语说明原因，"undermine both... and..."指出最终损害。句式层次极为丰富。

**3. 定语从句+结果说明**
> *"physical meetings create spontaneous opportunities for informal conversations during breaks or meals, **from which** deeper collaboration and long-term partnerships frequently emerge: connections rarely cultivated within the rigid structure of virtual platforms"*

**技巧**：用 "from which" 引导定语从句说明非正式交流的成果，冒号后进一步解释这些联系在虚拟平台中很少能培养出来。"rarely cultivated within..."的被动结构强化了面对面交流的独特价值。

**4. 条件-发现句**
> *"a manager visiting a foreign branch **may identify** operational inefficiencies or market opportunities **that would remain invisible through remote communication alone**"*

**技巧**：用 "may identify" 描述可能发现的问题，"that would remain invisible..."定语从句强调这些问题在远程沟通中无法察觉。"through remote communication alone"的表述精准限定了对比范围。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 让步段第二句（`Modern communication technologies now provide efficient and high-quality remote experiences...`）整句在夸线上会议的好处，与本段主题句"出差沉重"错位，随后又靠 `however` 硬拗回来；建议把这句压缩成半句附在对比里，或直接删去。
- 优点段只有 `a manager visiting a foreign branch` 一个例子，且仍停留在假设层面（may identify）；换成一个具体行业场景（如制造业买家实地验厂）会更实在。
- 全文只在"要么出差、要么线上"之间二选一，未触及现实中最常见的折中方案（关键节点面谈＋日常线上）；结论处补一句混合模式的判断，立场会更成熟。
