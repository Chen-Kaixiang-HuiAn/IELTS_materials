**The graph shows the percentage of different age groups of cinema visitors in a particular country from 1978 to 2008.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2024-12-28](./Task1_img/2024-12-28.png)

The graph illustrates the percentage of moviegoers across four age groups, namely 14-24, 25-34, 35-49, and older 50, in a certain country during the 30-year period from 1978 to 2008.

As seen, younger age groups (14-24 and 25-34) consistently showed higher participation rate in cinema visits compared to older age groups, with the 14-24 age group leading throughout the period. In 1978, this age group recorded the largest percentage of cinema-goers (90%), followed by the 25-34 group (around 80%). In 1988, the figures for both groups experienced declines to approximately 70% and 61%, respectively. However, from 1988 onwards, attendance rate among these groups rebounded and peaked in 1998 before gradually decreasing to around 80% and 65% by 2008.

Meanwhile, cinema attendance among the 35-49 and 50+ age groups was significantly lower. Specifically, roughly 60% and 40% of those groups, respectively, went to the movies in 1978, but their proportions slid by about 20% in 1988. However, their attendance levels had returned to their initial values by 1998. After that, the data on the 35-49 age group experienced a steady downturn, ending at 50% in 2008, while that on the 50+ group witnessed the opposite trend, increasing to match that on the 35-49 group in 2008, although it consistently remained the lowest.

Overall, while younger age groups dominated cinema attendance throughout the period, the statistics on older age groups saw a gradual upward trajectory, narrowing the gap during the 30 years.

> **话题与题型标注**：环境类 · Discuss Both Views

**Some people think it is acceptable to use animals in any way the benefits of human beings. Others think it is wrong to exploit animals for human purposes. Discuss both views and give your opinion.**

**有些人认为以任何方式利用动物为人类谋利是可以接受的，而另一些人则认为为了人类目的剥削动物是不对的。请讨论双方的观点并表达你的看法。**

Some argue that animals should be manipulated in whatever manner to profit humans, while others advocate for their protection. In my opinion, although animals have undeniably contributed to human progress, it is crucial to balance their use with ethical and environmental responsibility by developing alternatives to traditional practices.

有些人主张可以以任何方式操控动物来造福人类，而另一些人则倡导保护动物。在我看来，尽管动物无疑为人类的进步做出了贡献，但通过开发传统实践的替代方案，平衡动物的使用与伦理和环境责任是至关重要的。

On the one hand, proponents of using animals for human benefit argue that animals have historically played a crucial role in advancing human civilization. To begin with, animals have served as an essential source of sustenance and materials throughout history and will likely continue to do so in the future. Their employment as test subjects has also been vital for the advancement of science. For example, the practice of testing drugs and treatments on animals has achieved life-changing medical breakthroughs and had profound effects on public health by identifying potential risks and improving efficacy. Additionally, animals, which are inextricably integrated into cultural traditions, contribute to cultural enrichment and education through their roles in entertainment and traditional practices, so utilizing animals can create cascading advantages that extend across multiple domains of human life. For instance, zoos and circuses not only offer recreational opportunities but also foster public awareness of wildlife, thus indirectly supporting conservation efforts.

一方面，支持为人类利益使用动物的人认为，动物在推动人类文明发展方面历来发挥了关键作用。首先，动物在历史上一直是基本的生存资源和材料来源，并可能在未来继续如此。它们作为实验对象的应用对于科学的进步也至关重要。例如，药物和治疗的动物测试实践实现了改变生命的医学突破，并通过识别潜在风险和提高疗效对公共健康产生了深远影响。此外，动物与文化传统密不可分，通过其在娱乐和传统实践中的角色，为文化充实和教育做出了贡献，因此利用动物可以在多个领域创造连环优势。例如，动物园和马戏团不仅提供娱乐机会，还增强了公众对野生动物的认识，从而间接支持了保护工作。

On the other hand, exploiting animals for human benefit is not acceptable due to far-reaching ethical and environmental repercussions. That is, since animals are sentient beings capable of experiencing pain and suffering, subjecting them to inhumane practices, such as factory farming or animal testing directly, results in significant ethical dilemmas. The physical suffering endured by animals in such conditions not only raises moral questions but also underscores the necessity of exploring alternatives. In contrast, safeguarding animals ensures the long-term sustainability of ecosystems since the overexploitation of animals often triggers a chain reaction of ecological imbalances. When animals are overused or removed from their natural habitats, ecosystems are disrupted, which leads to severe consequences for biodiversity and environmental stability. For example, the decline of pollinators like bees due to human activities, including but not limited to pesticide usage and habitat destruction, adversely affects plant reproduction, which is critical for food production and maintaining ecological harmony.

另一方面，由于深远的伦理和环境影响，为人类利益剥削动物是不可接受的。也就是说，动物是有感知的生物，能够感受到痛苦和折磨，将它们置于不人道的实践中，例如工厂化养殖或动物测试，会直接导致重大的伦理困境。在这种条件下，动物所遭受的身体痛苦不仅引发了道德问题，还凸显了探索替代方案的必要性。相比之下，保护动物有助于生态系统的长期可持续性，因为过度开发动物通常会引发生态失衡的连锁反应。当动物被过度使用或从其自然栖息地中移除时，生态系统会受到破坏，从而对生物多样性和环境稳定性造成严重后果。例如，由于包括农药使用和栖息地破坏在内的人类活动，蜜蜂等传粉昆虫的数量下降，严重影响了植物繁殖，而植物繁殖对于粮食生产和维持生态和谐至关重要。

In conclusion, the use of animals has undeniably advanced human progress, but it is imperative to prioritize ethical considerations and ecological sustainability to reduce the need for the exploitation of animals. By fostering innovation and adopting humane alternatives, humanity can achieve progress that respects both animal and environmental balance.

总之，动物的使用无疑推动了人类进步，但优先考虑伦理问题和生态可持续性以减少对动物剥削的需求是至关重要的。通过促进创新和采用人道的替代方案，人类可以实现既尊重动物福利又维护环境平衡的进步。

**1. 定语从句+分词短语**
> *"Additionally, animals, **which are inextricably integrated into cultural traditions**, contribute to cultural enrichment and education through their roles in entertainment and traditional practices, **so utilizing animals can create cascading advantages that extend across multiple domains of human life**."*

**技巧**：用 which 引导的非限制性定语从句描述动物与文化传统的关系，so 引导结果从句推导利用动物的连锁优势，that 引导的定语从句进一步说明优势的范围。这种"背景→行为→结果→范围"的结构，使论证层次丰富。

**2. 原因状语从句+结果推导**
> *"**That is, since animals are sentient beings capable of experiencing pain and suffering**, subjecting them to inhumane practices, such as factory farming or animal testing directly, **results in significant ethical dilemmas**."*

**技巧**：用 since 引导原因状语从句，说明动物是有感知的生物，subjecting them to... 动名词短语描述行为，results in 推导结果。such as 举例说明不人道实践，directly 强调直接性。这种"原因→行为→结果"的结构，使因果逻辑清晰。

**3. 时间状语从句+因果链**
> *"**When animals are overused or removed from their natural habitats**, ecosystems are disrupted, **which leads to** severe consequences for biodiversity and environmental stability."*

**技巧**：用 When 引导时间状语从句，描述动物被过度使用的情况，主句描述生态系统破坏，which 引导的定语从句推导对生物多样性和环境稳定性的严重后果。这种"条件→破坏→后果"的链式推导，使因果逻辑严密。

**4. 对比结构+综合概括**
> *"**In conclusion, the use of animals has undeniably advanced human progress, but it is imperative to prioritize** ethical considerations and ecological sustainability **to reduce the need for** the exploitation of animals."*

**技巧**：用 but 转折，先承认动物利用推动了人类进步，再强调优先考虑伦理和可持续性的必要性，to reduce the need for 说明目的。这种"承认→转折→强调→目的"的结构，在结论段既总结了全文，又明确表达了最终立场。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 立场前后不一致：Intro 主张"在利用与伦理之间取得平衡"，Body 2 主题句却直接判定 `exploiting animals for human benefit is not acceptable`，语气比自己的立场绝对得多；把它软化为 `unrestricted exploitation is difficult to justify` 就能与结论的"平衡论"接上。
- 例证选得不利于己方：用马戏团（circuses）论证"利用动物有助于保护意识"很容易被反驳，因为马戏团正是动物福利争议的典型；换成动物园的繁育计划或野生动物纪录片会稳妥得多。
- 衔接词误用：Body 2 里伦理与生态是两条并列的支持理由，中间却用了表对比的 `In contrast`；应改为 `Beyond the ethical dimension` 或 `Moreover`。此外 `factory farming or animal testing directly` 中 directly 位置突兀，宜前移修饰 subjecting。
