> **图表类型标注**：折线图（四国医疗支出）

**The graph below shows the average government spending on health in Belgium, the UK, Iceland, and Italy from 2006 to 2013.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-06-14](./Task1_img/2025-06-14.png)

The graph illustrates changes in the average government expenditure on healthcare in Belgium, the UK, Iceland, and Italy from 2006 to 2013.

As seen, Belgium initially had by far the highest health spending per person at approximately $5,000, which rose steadily to a peak of around $6,000 in 2009. However, that figure declined gradually until 2011 and then dropped sharply to just below $4,000 in 2012. Despite a slight recovery in 2013, it became the second highest average expense at $4,000. 

The other countries, on the other hand, experienced general upward trends in healthcare expenditure. Specifically, the UK began with the lowest outlay in 2006 (around $2,000), but its expenditure increased consistently throughout the period, overtaking that of Iceland after 2012 and reaching nearly $3,500 by 2013. Italy’s corresponding cost remained relatively stable at $3,000 during the first two years before gradually increasing to approximately $4,200, becoming the highest in 2013. Similarly, although the data for Iceland showed a general rise from $2,200 to around $3,000 in 2012, it decreased slightly in the final year, with $2,900 spent.

Overall, while Belgium initially led in healthcare spending per capita, Italy experienced the most notable growth and became the highest spender by 2013, whereas the other two countries maintained moderate and less volatile trends in their expenditure on health.

**1. 复杂趋势句**
> *"As seen, Belgium initially had **by far the highest** health spending per person at approximately $5,000, which rose steadily to a peak of around $6,000 in 2009. **However**, that figure declined gradually until 2011 and then **dropped sharply** to just below $4,000 in 2012."*

**技巧**：用"by far the highest"强调领先地位，"which rose steadily to a peak"描述上升阶段，"However"标记转折，"declined gradually"和"dropped sharply"分别描述两个下降阶段。这种"上升→转折→下降1→下降2"的结构使复杂趋势描述清晰而精确。

**2. 排名变化句**
> *"Specifically, the UK began with the lowest outlay in 2006 (around $2,000), but its expenditure increased consistently throughout the period, **overtaking that of Iceland after 2012** and reaching nearly $3,500 by 2013."*

**技巧**：主句陈述起点最低，"but"标记转折，主句描述持续上升，"overtaking that of Iceland after 2012"标注排名变化的时间点，"reaching nearly $3,500"给出终点数据。这种"起点→变化→排名变化→终点"的结构使信息传递完整而高效。

**3. 稳定+增长句**
> *"Italy's corresponding cost remained relatively stable at $3,000 during the first two years **before gradually increasing** to approximately $4,200, becoming the highest in 2013."*

**技巧**：主句描述稳定期，"before gradually increasing"描述增长期，"becoming the highest"标注最终排名。"corresponding cost"避免了重复"health spending"，体现了词汇的多样性。

**4. 综合概述句**
> *"Overall, while Belgium initially led in healthcare spending per capita, Italy experienced the most notable growth and became the highest spender by 2013, **whereas** the other two countries maintained moderate and less volatile trends in their expenditure on health."*

**技巧**：概述段用"while"将Belgium的初始领先与Italy的后来居上对比，"whereas"将前两者与UK和Iceland的温和趋势对比。"most notable growth"和"less volatile trends"的对比突出了不同国家的变化特征。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：文化与传统类 · Problem-Solution

**In some countries, the number of people visiting art galleries is decreasing. What are the reasons? How can we solve this problem?**

**在一些国家，参观艺术画廊的人数正在减少。造成这一现象的原因是什么？我们又该如何解决这个问题？**

Many countries have witnessed a noticeable decline in the number of visitors to art galleries. This trend can be attributed to several interrelated factors, including the rise of digital media and increasing life burdens; however, some strategies can be implemented to reverse this decline.

许多国家已经明显感受到前往艺术画廊的游客数量在下降。这一趋势可以归因于多个相互关联的因素，包括数字媒体的兴起以及日益沉重的生活负担；然而，一些应对策略可以被采纳，以扭转这一下降趋势。

As observed, the growing accessibility of digitalized artworks through online platforms, along with rising life pressures, contributes to the decline in gallery attendance. Firstly, advanced imaging technologies allow artworks to be displayed online, enabling audiences from diverse backgrounds to access virtual exhibitions without time or geographical limitations. These immersive video exhibitions offer people a variety of options to deepen their understanding of masterpieces without needing to visit multiple physical galleries. In addition to virtual art shows, the increase in daily life stress, especially in an increasingly digital world, has also played a role. As digital literacy becomes a crucial factor for employability, many young people are compelled to devote their time to acquiring professional skills that are less susceptible to automation by artificial intelligence. Even during their leisure time, they often prefer multimedia experiences, such as video games and digital content creation, which may seem more engaging than traditional art displays.

可以观察到，随着线上平台上数字化艺术作品的日益普及，以及生活压力的增加，导致人们前往画廊的意愿下降。首先，先进的成像技术使艺术作品得以在互联网上展示，使来自不同背景的观众可以在没有时间和地域限制的情况下，参观虚拟展览。这些沉浸式的视频展览为人们提供了多种方式，加深对杰作的理解，而无需亲自前往多个实体画廊。除了虚拟艺术展览之外，日常生活压力的增加，尤其是在一个愈发数字化的世界中，也起到了推波助澜的作用。由于数字素养正逐渐成为就业的重要因素，许多年轻人不得不将时间投入到那些不易被人工智能取代的职业技能学习中。即便是在空闲时间，他们也更倾向于选择视频游戏或数字内容创作等多媒体体验，这些往往比传统的艺术展示更具吸引力。

With those factors in mind, attendance at cultural and artistic sites could be encouraged in various ways. To attract more visitors, the government should allocate funding to refurbish cultural facilities by incorporating augmented reality guides and integrating art into the local compulsory education system. This can include immersive installations or hands-on workshops that allow students to create their own artwork. As part of this strategy, regular gallery visits might be required for students and their parents, fostering deeper engagement with the arts. In addition, positioning art galleries as spaces for stress relief and holistic personal growth can further promote visits. Galleries can redefine themselves not only as cultural venues but also as peaceful retreats where individuals gain humanistic insights and inner resolve. By creating calming environments, galleries can attract visitors seeking a break from their hectic routines, and promoting gallery visits as a form of "mental wellness" could appeal to overworked individuals.

考虑到上述因素，可以通过多种方式鼓励人们参观文化艺术场所。为了吸引更多参观者，政府应拨款翻新文化设施，引入增强现实导览功能，并将艺术融入本地义务教育体系。这可以包括沉浸式装置或动手工作坊，让学生亲手创作艺术作品。作为这一策略的一部分，可以规定学生及其家长定期参观画廊，从而加深他们对艺术的参与程度。此外，将艺术画廊定位为舒缓压力和实现全面成长的空间，也有助于促进参观热情。画廊可以重新定义自身，不仅是文化场所，也可以是让人获得人文感悟与内心力量的宁静之地。通过营造平和安宁的环境，画廊能够吸引那些希望从忙碌生活中暂时抽身的访客，并通过将参观画廊宣传为一种“心理健康疗愈”方式，有望吸引那些长期处于高压状态的人群。

In conclusion, the decline in art gallery attendance stems largely from digital alternatives and modern life pressures. By modernizing gallery experiences, embedding art in education, and promoting mental well-being through art spaces, societies can reignite public interest and restore the cultural significance of art galleries.

总之，艺术画廊参观人数的下降主要源于数字化替代选择以及现代生活压力。通过使画廊体验现代化、将艺术纳入教育体系，并倡导通过艺术空间促进心理健康，社会可以重新激发公众的兴趣，恢复艺术画廊的文化价值。

#### ① 结构分析（精简）

本题属于 **Problem-Solution** 类题型。此类题型的核心策略是：先分析问题的原因，再提出解决方案，最后总结。

**1. 因果链式长句**
> *"As digital literacy becomes a crucial factor for employability, many young people are compelled to devote their time to acquiring professional skills that are less susceptible to automation by artificial intelligence."*

**技巧**："As"引导原因状语从句，主句陈述结果，"that"引导的定语从句限定"professional skills"的特征。"less susceptible to automation"精准表达了"不易被自动化取代"的含义。

**2. 条件+效果句**
> *"**When watching action movies or playing battle games**, people often momentarily forget the trivialities of life and the pressures of work or study, **thus achieving a sense of balance**."*

**技巧**："When"引导时间条件从句，主句陈述行为效果，"thus achieving..."用现在分词短语总结最终结果。"momentarily forget"精准表达了"暂时忘记"的含义。

**3. 目的+措施句**
> *"**To attract more visitors**, the government should allocate funding to refurbish cultural facilities by incorporating augmented reality guides and integrating art into the local compulsory education system."*

**技巧**："To attract more visitors"用不定式说明目的，主句提出具体措施，"by incorporating... and integrating..."用两个动名词短语说明措施的具体内容。这种"目的→措施→具体内容"的结构使建议非常实用。

**4. 综合总结句**
> *"By modernizing gallery experiences, embedding art in education, and promoting mental well-being through art spaces, societies can reignite public interest and restore the cultural significance of art galleries."*

**技巧**：结尾句用"By + 三个动名词短语"提出综合方案，主句说明预期效果。"reignite public interest"和"restore the cultural significance"两个动词短语并列，从公众兴趣和文化价值两个维度说明解决方案的目标。
