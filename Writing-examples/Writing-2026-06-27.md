The table illustrates changes in the number of temporary migrant workers in the USA, Australia, New Zealand, and the UK in 2003 and 2006, along with the rate per 1,000 people in each country in 2006.

As shown, in 2003, the USA had 650,000 temporary migrant workers, which was considerably higher than the figures for the other three countries. By 2006, this number had risen substantially to 850,000 (an increase of 200,000), and the country also recorded the highest rate, at 21 workers per 1,000 people. Australia ranked second initially, having 230,000 migrant workers, and also experienced growth, though more modestly, reaching 270,000 in 2006, with 17 workers per 1,000 people.

Regarding the UK and New Zealand, the former saw the second-largest rise and surpassed Australia, with its figure growing from 190,000 to 310,000. Meanwhile, New Zealand had the smallest numbers in both years, despite increasing slightly from only 59,000 to 78,000, with a much lower rate of 4 workers per 1,000 people recorded in 2006.

Overall, all four countries experienced an increase in the number of temporary migrant workers over the period. The USA had by far the largest number of such workers in both years and also the highest rate per 1,000 people in 2006, while New Zealand recorded the lowest figures on both measures.

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：教育类 · Agree/Disagree

**The study of ancient cultures is not a relevant subject in modern education. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

Some people argue that the study of ancient cultures has little relevance to modern education. Although modern education mainly aims to equip students with scientific and technological knowledge, I disagree with the view that ancient cultures are no longer useful in contemporary education.

一些人认为，古代文化研究与现代教育关系不大。虽然现代教育主要旨在让学生掌握科学和技术知识，但我不同意古代文化在当代教育中不再有用这一观点。

Admittedly, modern education must prepare students to gain a foothold in a highly competitive and technology-driven world. Subjects such as computer science, engineering, business, and medicine often appear more practical because they are directly linked to stable employment opportunities in an AI-driven society. If schools allocate excessive time to ancient history or traditional culture at the expense of these practical disciplines, students may lack the technical knowledge required in the modern labour market. This is particularly important today, when young people need digital literacy and subject-specific expertise to evaluate the accuracy of AI-generated content and use new technologies responsibly. From this perspective, ancient cultures should not dominate the curriculum or replace subjects that provide clear career-related benefits, as practical disciplines can enhance students’ competitiveness and prepare them for future careers.

诚然，现代教育必须帮助学生在竞争激烈、由技术驱动的世界中站稳脚跟。计算机科学、工程、商业和医学等学科通常显得更实用，因为它们与人工智能驱动社会中的稳定就业机会直接相关。如果学校把过多时间分配给古代历史或传统文化，而牺牲这些实用学科，学生可能会缺乏现代劳动力市场所需的技术知识。今天这一点尤其重要，因为年轻人需要数字素养和特定学科的专业知识，才能评估人工智能生成内容的准确性，并负责任地使用新技术。从这个角度看，古代文化不应主导课程，也不应取代那些能带来明确职业收益的学科，因为实用学科可以提升学生竞争力，并帮助他们为未来职业做好准备。

However, the above reasons for emphasising science and technology do not necessarily mean that ancient cultures are irrelevant to modern education. From the perspective of cultural inheritance, studying ancient cultures helps preserve cultural identity and promote respect for diversity. In many countries, young people are increasingly exposed to global popular culture, which may weaken their connection with their own traditions. Learning about ancient beliefs, customs, and literature can therefore help them appreciate the uniqueness of their cultural heritage. Meanwhile, ancient cultures also offer lessons that go beyond immediate career prospects. By examining how earlier societies solved problems or made mistakes, students can better understand how the past has shaped the relationship between societies and individuals. This historical awareness can help learners address complex modern dilemmas. For example, a course on the history of public health may show how poor environmental management and irresponsible decision-making have harmed disadvantaged communities, as illustrated by the water crisis in Flint, Michigan.

然而，以上强调科学和技术的理由并不必然意味着古代文化与现代教育无关。从文化传承的角度看，学习古代文化有助于保存文化身份，并促进对多样性的尊重。在许多国家，年轻人越来越多地接触全球流行文化，这可能削弱他们与自身传统的联系。因此，学习古代信仰、习俗和文学，可以帮助他们理解自身文化遗产的独特性。同时，古代文化也提供了超越眼前职业前景的经验。通过考察早期社会如何解决问题或犯下错误，学生可以更好地理解过去如何塑造社会与个体之间的关系。这种历史意识可以帮助学习者处理复杂的现代困境。例如，一门公共卫生史课程可能会展示糟糕的环境管理和不负责任的决策如何伤害弱势群体，弗林特水危机就是一个例子。

In conclusion, while science and technology deserve a central place in modern education because they equip students with career-related skills, ancient cultures remain valuable. They help young people preserve cultural identity, respect different traditions, understand the historical causes of present problems, and make wiser decisions in modern society.

总之，虽然科学和技术理应在现代教育中占据核心位置，因为它们能让学生具备与职业相关的技能，但古代文化仍然有价值。它们帮助年轻人保存文化身份、尊重不同传统、理解当下问题的历史成因，并在现代社会中作出更明智的决定。

#### ① 结构分析（精简）

本题属于 **Agree/Disagree** 类题型。此类题型的核心策略是：明确表明立场，再展开论证（通常先让步理解对方，再反驳立论）。

本文采用 **"让步-反驳-综合"** 四段式结构：

- **Paragraph 1 — Introduction（开头段）**
  - 改写题目（paraphrase）：`Although modern education mainly aims to equip students with scientific and technological knowledge, I disagree with the view that ancient cultures are no longer useful in contemporary education`（替换原题 `The study of ancient cultures is not a relevant subject in modern education`）
  - 表明立场：`I disagree with the view that ancient cultures are no longer useful`（反对"古代文化无关"的命题，认为其仍有价值）
  - 预告框架：先让步承认科技学科的实用性，再反驳论证古代文化价值，最后总结

- **Paragraph 2 — Body 1（让步段 Admittedly）**
  - 主题句：`modern education must prepare students to gain a foothold in a highly competitive and technology-driven world`
  - 论点1：实用学科（CS/工程/商业/医学）与 AI 社会的稳定就业直接相关
  - 反面机制：若学校过度投入古代文化而牺牲实用学科 → 学生缺乏技术知识
  - 当前重要性：年轻人需数字素养与专业知识 → 评估 AI 内容准确性、负责任用技术
  - 结论：古代文化不应主导课程，也不应取代职业相关学科

- **Paragraph 3 — Body 2（主论反驳段 However）**
  - 主题句：`the above reasons for emphasising science and technology do not necessarily mean that ancient cultures are irrelevant to modern education`
  - 论点1（文化传承）：保存文化身份、尊重多样性；全球流行文化削弱传统联系；学习古代信仰/习俗/文学理解遗产独特性
  - 论点2（超越职业）：古代文化提供超越眼前职业的经验；考察早期社会解决问题/犯错 → 理解过去如何塑造社会与个体关系 → 历史意识帮助应对现代困境
  - 例证：公共卫生史课程（弗林特水危机）

- **Paragraph 4 — Conclusion（结尾段）**
  - 重申立场：`while science and technology deserve a central place..., ancient cultures remain valuable`
  - 总结升华：帮助保存文化身份、尊重传统、理解当下问题历史成因、作出更明智决定

> **结构亮点**：本文采用经典"先退后进"结构。开头用 Although 让步承认科技学科的目标合理性，再用 I disagree 明确反对"古代文化无关"的极端命题，立场审慎而有张力。Body 1 充分承认对方合理之处（实用学科的就业价值），Body 2 才系统展开己方论证，并以"文化传承"与"超越职业的经验"两个维度支撑，最后用 In conclusion 综合双方、回归平衡立场。这种"理解对方 → 建设己方"的推进，比一边倒更具说服力。

#### ④ 可迁移句型骨架（直接套用）（题型：Agree/Disagree）

**骨架 1 — 先退后进亮立场（Intro）**
> Although ______ deserves a central place because ______, I disagree with the view that ______.

**骨架 2 — 分类论证起手（Body）**
> From the perspective of ______, doing X helps ______ and ______. Meanwhile, X also offers ______ that go beyond ______.

**骨架 3 — 抽象→具体例证（Body）**
> For example, a course on ______ may show how ______ have harmed ______, as illustrated by ______.

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

| 衔接类型 | 原文表达 | 功能说明 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **立场预告** | `Although modern education mainly aims to..., I disagree with the view that...` | 开头段先退后进，明确反对"古代文化无关" |
| **让步衔接** | `Admittedly, modern education must prepare students to gain a foothold...` | 用 Admittedly 引出对方合理之处 |
| **条件因果** | `If schools allocate excessive time... at the expense of these practical disciplines, students may lack the technical knowledge...` | If 引导反面条件，推导后果 |
| **转折反驳** | `However, the above reasons... do not necessarily mean that ancient cultures are irrelevant...` | However 转向己方主论 |
| **视角展开** | `From the perspective of cultural inheritance...` | 用 From the perspective 引出文化传承维度 |
| **并列递进** | `Meanwhile, ancient cultures also offer lessons that go beyond immediate career prospects.` | Meanwhile 引出第二个维度 |
| **举例支撑** | `For example, a course on the history of public health... as illustrated by the water crisis in Flint, Michigan.` | For example + as illustrated by 双重举例 |
| **总结综合** | `In conclusion, while science and technology deserve a central place..., ancient cultures remain valuable.` | while 让步 + 重申立场 |

> **衔接亮点**："Admittedly → However" 构成全篇论证主轴，先充分承认科技学科的实用性，再用 However 转折展开古代文化价值，张弛有度；"From the perspective of... / Meanwhile..." 在 Body 2 内部分出两个并列维度，结构清晰；"as illustrated by the water crisis in Flint, Michigan" 将抽象"历史意识"落到具体案例，增强可信度。

|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|

**1. 让步转折复合句（开头段）**
> *"**Although** modern education mainly aims to equip students with scientific and technological knowledge, **I disagree with the view that** ancient cultures are no longer useful in contemporary education."*

**技巧**：Although 让步承认科技学科的目标合理性，主句用 I disagree with the view that... 明确反对"古代文化无关"的命题，立场审慎而鲜明，避免一边倒。

**2. 条件因果句（让步段）**
> *"**If** schools allocate excessive time to ancient history or traditional culture **at the expense of** these practical disciplines, students **may lack** the technical knowledge required in the modern labour market."*

**技巧**：If 引导反面条件，at the expense of 精准表达"以……为代价"，主句用 may lack 推导可能后果，论证对方立场的潜在代价，使让步段不止于"承认"更指向"限度"。

**3. 视角展开+并列句（主论段）**
> *"**From the perspective of cultural inheritance**, studying ancient cultures **helps preserve** cultural identity **and promote** respect for diversity."*

**技巧**：From the perspective of 引出论证维度，helps... and promote... 并列两个积极效果，句式紧凑、立场正向，是展开分论点的标准起手式。

**4. 举例支撑句（主论段）**
> *"**For example**, a course on the history of public health may show how poor environmental management and irresponsible decision-making have harmed disadvantaged communities, **as illustrated by** the water crisis in Flint, Michigan."*

**技巧**：For example 引出课程例证，how 引导宾语从句说明机制，as illustrated by 用具体地名案例（弗林特水危机）锚定抽象论述，使"历史意识服务现代"的论点具象可信，是"抽象论点 → 具体案例"的优质写法。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 核心例证与论点错位：题目问的是 ancient cultures，而 Flint 水危机（2014 年）属当代事件，用它支撑"古代文化仍有用"跨度过大；换成古罗马供水与公共卫生、或古代城市的疫病治理，会紧扣得多。
- 让步段结尾 `as practical disciplines can enhance students' competitiveness and prepare them for future careers` 与段首"与稳定就业直接相关"同义反复，属可删的冗余。
- 让步段通篇只有学科名罗列、无一具体例子，而反驳段有具名案例，两段的证据密度明显不对等。

**仿写任务**：用「先退后进」骨架重写：*Some think space exploration is a waste of money.*
