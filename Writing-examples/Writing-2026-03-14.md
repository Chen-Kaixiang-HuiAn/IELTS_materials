**The plans below show an Australian market garden in 1950 and now.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2026-03-14](./Task1_img/2026-03-14.png)

The two plans compare the layout of a market garden in Australia in 1950 with its present-day arrangement.

In 1950, the garden was organized into clearly defined sections aligned along a west-to-east axis, with each section dedicated to specific crops. Onions, potatoes, and tomatoes were grown sequentially from west to east, while lettuce and herbs were sown to the south of the tomatoes. By contrast, the southern section was planted with strawberries, apples, and herbs in succession, with a storage building and a house located in the south-eastern corner.

At present, although the overall structure remains partially recognizable, several key changes have taken place. Onions, potatoes, and strawberries continue to be cultivated in broadly similar locations, albeit on slightly larger plots, with potato production now evidently mechanized. However, the growing of tomatoes, herbs, and apples have been discontinued; in their place, greenhouses and a purpose-built lettuce-growing facility have been developed. Furthermore, beans have been introduced in the south-central area, replacing the previous apple plantation, while the adjacent storage building in the south-east has been upgraded to a warehouse.

Overall, the garden has undergone substantial modernization and slight eastward expansion, characterized by the introduction of greenhouse cultivation and mechanized farming, alongside notable changes in crop selection.

> *"Onions, potatoes, and tomatoes were grown sequentially from west to east, **while** lettuce and herbs were sown to the south of the tomatoes."*

**技巧**：用 while 连接两个并列分句，分别描述不同区域的作物分布，空间对比清晰。

**2. 让步变化句**
> *"**At present, although** the overall structure remains partially recognizable, **several key changes have taken place**."*

**技巧**：用 although 引导让步从句，先承认整体结构的部分延续性，再转折强调关键变化，使描述客观全面。

**3. 替代关系句**
> *"However, the growing of tomatoes, herbs, and apples **have been discontinued**; **in their place**, greenhouses and a purpose-built lettuce-growing facility **have been developed**."*

**技巧**：用分号连接两个分句，have been discontinued 说明停种，in their place 引出替代设施，have been developed 说明新增，替代关系清晰。

**有些人认为，上大学是通往成功职业生涯的最佳途径，而另一些人则认为，直接在中学毕业后参加工作更好。讨论双方观点并给出自己的观点。**

Some argue that higher education equips individuals with essential skills for long-term success, while others believe entering the workforce immediately provides practical experience and an early start. In my view, tertiary education is vital, as it offers professional expertise and ensures better opportunities in today’s competitive job market.

一些人认为，高等教育能够为个人提供长期成功所需的必要技能，而另一些人则认为，立即进入职场能够带来实践经验和更早的职业起步。在我看来，高等教育至关重要，因为它提供了专业知识并确保在当今竞争激烈的就业市场中获得更好的机会。

It  cannot be denied that pursuing tertiary educationis essential for success in today’s digital age. Tertiary education provides cutting-edge knowledge and specialized skills crucial for achieving enduring success in a rapidly evolving job market. Many competency-based positions require a strong foundation in interdisciplinary studies, which is vital for staying competitive in fields such as frontier science, where research often spans multiple academic disciplines. Thus, without a university degree or vocational training, young people risk being confined toentry-level roles with limited opportunities for career advancement. Beyond professional knowledge, tertiary education fosters intellectual and social development by enhancing analytical reasoning and innovation skills. It offers invaluable opportunities for networking and exposure to diverse perspectives, all of which are indispensable for addressing complex challenges in the workplace and are key to both personal and professional growth.

不可否认，在当今数字化时代，接受高等教育对于成功至关重要。高等教育提供了前沿知识和专业技能，这对于在快速变化的就业市场中取得持久成功至关重要。许多基于能力的职位需要扎实的跨学科基础，这在前沿科学等领域尤为重要，因为这些领域的研究通常跨越多个学术学科。因此，没有大学学历或职业培训的年轻人可能面临被限制在初级职位上的风险，从而失去更好职业发展的机会。除了专业知识之外，高等教育还促进智力和社交能力的发展，通过提优质析推理和创新能力为个人赋能。它还提供了宝贵的机会让学生建立人脉并接触多样化的视角，而这些都对于解决职场中的复杂挑战以及个人和职业成长至关重要。

It is understandable why some argue that university subjects are outdated and that entering the workforce immediately after school helps young people gain work experience and achieve economic independence, especially for those who prefer a more hands-on approach to building their careers. However, bypassing higher education in the information era can significantly hinder the young generation's ability to thrive in a competitive job market. As the world increasingly transitions into a digitally driven society, digital literacy has become a crucial factor for employability and innovation, so those lacking the skills to create and evaluate information on digital platforms face limited opportunities for well-paying jobs and are more likely to end up in manual labor roles, such as construction or packing, which are increasingly vulnerable to replacement by artificial intelligence-driven automation. Without university qualifications, individuals may struggle to secure desirable employment in today’s highly competitive labor market.

可以理解，有些人认为大学课程过时，直接在中学毕业后进入职场能帮助年轻人获得工作经验并实现经济独立，尤其是对于那些更倾向于通过实践方式发展职业的人。然而，在信息时代放弃高等教育可能会严重削弱年轻一代在竞争激烈的就业市场中脱颖而出的能力。随着世界逐渐向数字化社会转型，数字素养已成为就业能力和创新的重要因素。因此，那些缺乏在数字平台上创建和评估信息技能的人，将面临难以获得高薪工作的局面，并可能更容易从事如建筑或包装等体力劳动岗位，而这些岗位正越来越容易被人工智能驱动的自动化取代。没有大学学历的人可能会发现自己难以在当今高度竞争的劳动力市场中获得理想的就业机会。

In conclusion, while gaining practical experience and financial independence by entering the workforce early may appeal to some, the indispensable role of tertiary education in equipping individuals with professional knowledge, critical skills, and adaptability cannot be overstated. Therefore, I believe that, in today's digital era, higher education remains the cornerstone of long-term career success.

综上所述，虽然通过早早进入职场来获得实践经验和经济独立可能对一些人有吸引力，但高等教育在为个人提供专业知识、关键技能以及适应能力方面的不可或缺的作用无法忽视。因此，我认为在当今数字时代，高等教育仍然是实现长期职业成功的基石。

**骨架 1 — 先退后进亮立场（Intro）**
> Although ______ deserves a central place because ______, I disagree with the view that ______.

**骨架 2 — 分类论证起手（Body）**
> From the perspective of ______, doing X helps ______ and ______. Meanwhile, X also offers ______ that go beyond ______.

**骨架 3 — 抽象→具体例证（Body）**
> For example, a course on ______ may show how ______ have harmed ______, as illustrated by ______.

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

**1. 强调句**
> *"**It cannot be denied that** pursuing tertiary education is essential for success in today's digital age."*

**技巧**：用 It cannot be denied that 强调句型，使高等教育的重要性不容置疑，语气坚定有力。

**2. 因果推导句**
> *"Thus, **without** a university degree or vocational training, young people **risk being confined to** entry-level roles with limited opportunities for career advancement."*

**技巧**：用 without 引导条件状语，主句用 risk being confined to 描述可能的负面后果，因果推导清晰。

**3. 让步转折复合句**
> *"**It is understandable why** some argue that university subjects are outdated and that entering the workforce immediately after school helps young people gain work experience and achieve economic independence, **especially for those who** prefer a more hands-on approach to building their careers. **However**, bypassing higher education in the information era can significantly hinder the young generation's ability to thrive in a competitive job market."*

**技巧**：用 It is understandable why 承认对方观点的合理性，再用 especially for those who 限定适用范围，最后用 However 转折系统反驳，使论证全面而有力。

**4. 因果链推演句**
> *"As the world increasingly transitions into a digitally driven society, digital literacy has become a crucial factor for employability and innovation, **so** those lacking the skills to create and evaluate information on digital platforms face limited opportunities for well-paying jobs and are more likely to end up in manual labor roles, such as construction or packing, **which are increasingly vulnerable to replacement by** artificial intelligence-driven automation."*

**技巧**：用 As 引导时间状语从句说明背景，so 推导因果关系，which 引导非限制性定语从句补充说明体力劳动岗位的长期风险，因果链条完整而深刻。

**5. 综合总结句**
> *"In conclusion, **while** gaining practical experience and financial independence by entering the workforce early may appeal to some, the indispensable role of tertiary education in equipping individuals with professional knowledge, critical skills, and adaptability **cannot be overstated**."*

**技巧**：用 while 引导让步从句，先承认直接工作的部分吸引力，再主句用 cannot be overstated 强调高等教育的重要性无法言过其实，总结有力。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 题型是 Discuss Both Views，但"直接就业"一方只在 Body 2 开头用一句半带过，随即整段转入反驳，另一方观点呈现明显不足，容易被判为 one-sided。
- 让步句抛出的 `university subjects are outdated` 这一质疑，后文再未回应（只谈数字素养），等于自己提了个问题又搁置；要么删掉，要么补一句针对性反驳。
- 正文有若干粘连/漏空格的排版失误：`It  cannot be denied`（双空格）、`tertiary educationis`、`confined toentry-level roles`，作为范文文本应当校正。
