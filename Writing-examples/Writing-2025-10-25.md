> **图表类型标注**：柱状图（七国城市人口占比）

**The chart below shows the percentage of population in the largest two cities and the rest of cities in seven countries in 2017.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-10-25](./Task1_img/2025-10-25.png)

The bar chart illustrates the proportions of people living in the two largest cities and in other cities in Japan, New Zealand, China, the USA, the UK, France, and Germany in 2017.

As seen, Japan showed the highest level of urban concentration, with around 37% of its population living in the two biggest cities and 40% in other cities. In contrast, New Zealand had the lowest urban figures (approximately 6% residing in the former and about 8% in the latter). China and the USA both had relatively low proportions of their populations in biggest metropolises (nearly 5% and 8%), while the rest of urban population accounted for much higher figures, which registered at 35% and 25%, respectively.

However, a different pattern was witnessed in the other three countries, where a higher or comparable percentage of people lived in the two largest megalopolises. Specifically, in the UK, approximately 35% resided in the two biggest cities, only about 20% in the remaining ones. France had a more balanced distribution, with 30% in the two largest cities and slightly fewer, about 28%, in the rest. Germany followed closely, with equal shares of around 25% in both categories.

Overall, Japan had the highest share of population residing in its two largest cities, whereas China recorded the lowest; in most of the given countries, the rest of the cities together accommodated a larger proportion of the populace, except in the UK, France, and Germany.

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：教育类 · Discuss Both Views

**Some people think that only the best students should be rewarded. Others, however, think that it is more important to reward students who show improvements. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.**

**有些人认为，只有最优秀的学生才应该得到奖励；然而，也有人认为，奖励那些表现出进步的学生更为重要。讨论这两种观点，并给出你的个人看法。**

While some argue that prizes should be reserved for top-performing students, others contend that recognising improvement is a more equitable and effective approach. My view is that both perspectives are justified, as education should empower every learner to progress without neglecting the recognition of outstanding achievement.

有人认为，奖项应仅授予成绩最优异的学生；也有人主张，表彰学生的进步是一种更公平且更有效的做法。我的观点是，这两种观点都有其合理性，因为教育的核心应是激励每一位学习者不断进步，同时不能忽视对卓越成就的认可。

On the one hand, those who support rewarding only students with exceptional academic performance often believe that such a system reinforces the value of excellence. In highly competitive academic contexts, such as scholarship examinations or university admissions, recognising top performers can cultivate a culture of ambition and drive. By honouring high achievers, schools not only acknowledge exceptional effort and talent but also establish benchmarks for others to emulate. For instance, in many East Asian countries, merit-based rewards have historically been tied to students' academic rankings, and this system has arguably contributed to high educational standards and strong student discipline, encouraging learners to pursue excellence. From this standpoint, rewarding the best serves as an incentive for others to push their limits and strive for distinction.

一方面，那些主张只奖励学业成绩突出的学生的人往往认为，这种制度强化了“卓越”的价值。在竞争激烈的学术环境中，例如奖学金申请或大学录取过程中，表彰顶尖学生能够培养一种追求卓越与进取的文化。通过嘉奖优异者，学校不仅是在肯定他们的努力与才能，也在为其他学生树立榜样。例如，在许多东亚国家，基于成绩的奖励长期以来与学生的学业排名挂钩，而这种制度可以说在一定程度上促进了高水平的教育标准和良好的学生纪律。从这一角度来看，奖励最优秀的学生有助于激发他人突破自我、追求卓越。

On the other hand, many educators argue that rewarding improvement is a more inclusive and psychologically constructive practice, as prioritising only the highest achievers may inadvertently discourage those who struggle academically or start from a lower baseline. From a personal growth perspective, when progress is acknowledged, whether it involves higher grades or greater participation, students are more likely to develop intrinsic motivation and a growth mindset. This approach recognises that learning is not a competition against others but a personal journey of development. From an educational psychology perspective, positive reinforcement based on improvement enhances long-term engagement and resilience, qualities that are vital for lifelong learning. By contrast, if rewards are confined to a small elite, average or weaker students may feel marginalised and lose confidence in their ability to succeed.

另一方面，许多教育者认为，奖励进步是一种更具包容性且更有心理建设意义的做法，因为只关注最高成就者可能会无意中打击那些学业上有困难或起点较低的学生。从个人成长的角度来看，当学生的进步得到认可，无论是成绩提升还是课堂参与度提高，他们更有可能发展出内在的学习动力和成长型思维模式。这种做法体现出学习并非与他人竞争，而是一段个人发展的旅程。从教育心理学的角度看，基于进步的积极强化能够增强学生的长期投入感和抗挫力，而这些品质对终身学习至关重要。相反，如果奖励仅限于少数精英，普通或成绩较弱的学生可能会感到被边缘化，从而失去成功的信心。

In my view, rewarding students is a common educational strategy used to motivate learning and sustain academic progress. Rewarding both excellence and improvement aligns closely with the broader educational goal of balancing performance with potential. Such a system fosters both academic achievement and emotional growth, qualities that are indispensable beyond the classroom.

在我看来，奖励学生是一种常见的教育策略，用于激发学习动力并保持学业进步。将卓越与进步同时纳入奖励体系，更符合教育在绩效与潜力之间寻求平衡的总体目标。这样的制度既促进学业成就，也推动情感成长，而这两方面的素质在课堂之外同样不可或缺。

In conclusion, while rewarding top performers recognises excellence, celebrating improvement encourages wider participation and sustained motivation. A balanced reward system that values achievement, perseverance, and progress enables a broader range of students to take ownership of their learning and continue striving toward their full potential.

总之，奖励顶尖学生体现了对卓越的认可，而表彰进步则鼓励更广泛的参与和持续的学习动力。一个兼顾成就、毅力与进步的平衡奖励体系，能够让更多学生积极承担学习责任，并不断努力实现自身的最大潜能。

### 精析

#### ① 结构分析（精简）

本题属于 **Discuss Both Views** 类题型。此类题型的核心策略是：双方观点各一段分析，最后给出自己的综合立场。

本文采用 **"平衡-立场"** 四段式结构：

- **Paragraph 1 — Introduction（开头段）**
  - 改写题目（paraphrase）：`While some argue that prizes should be reserved for top-performing students, others contend that recognising improvement is a more equitable and effective approach`（替换原题 `Some people think that only the best students should be rewarded. Others, however, think that it is more important to reward students who show improvements`）
  - 表明立场：`both perspectives are justified, as education should empower every learner to progress without neglecting the recognition of outstanding achievement`（双方都有道理，需平衡）
  - 预告框架：先讨论奖励顶尖学生的价值，再讨论奖励进步的合理性，最后给出平衡立场

- **Paragraph 2 — Body 1（奖励顶尖学生论）**
  - 主题句：`such a system reinforces the value of excellence`
  - 论点1：在竞争激烈的学术环境中（奖学金考试、大学录取），表彰顶尖学生培养雄心和进取文化
  - 论点2：嘉奖优异者不仅肯定其努力和才能，还为他人树立榜样
  - 举例：东亚国家基于成绩的奖励与学业排名挂钩 → 促进高教育标准和良好纪律
  - 结论：奖励最优秀的学生激励他人突破自我、追求卓越

- **Paragraph 3 — Body 2（奖励进步论）**
  - 主题句：`rewarding improvement is a more inclusive and psychologically constructive practice`
  - 论点1：只关注最高成就者可能无意中打击学业困难或起点较低的学生
  - 论点2（个人成长角度）：进步被认可时（成绩提升或参与度提高），学生更可能发展内在动机和成长型思维 → 学习是个人发展旅程而非与他人竞争

- **Paragraph 4 — Conclusion（结尾段）**
  - 重申立场：`A balanced reward system that values achievement, perseverance, and progress`
  - 总结升华：兼顾成就、毅力与进步的平衡奖励体系 → 让更多学生积极承担学习责任

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

**1. 多重目的+结果**
> *"By honouring high achievers, schools **not only acknowledge** exceptional effort and talent **but also establish benchmarks** for others to emulate"*

**技巧**：用"By honouring..."方式状语引出行为，"not only... but also..."并列两个积极结果（肯定努力+树立榜样）。"for others to emulate"不定式短语说明榜样的目的。

**2. 条件-后果推演**
> *"**as prioritising only the highest achievers may inadvertently discourage** those who struggle academically or start from a lower baseline"*

**技巧**：用"as"引导原因状语从句，"prioritising only..."动名词短语作主语，"may inadvertently discourage"指出潜在危害。"inadvertently"一词精准描述了这种打击是无意的。

**3. 角度切换+机制说明**
> *"**From an educational psychology perspective**, positive reinforcement based on improvement **enhances** long-term engagement and resilience, **qualities that are vital for** lifelong learning"*

**技巧**：用"From... perspective"标志角度切换，"positive reinforcement based on improvement"精确描述机制，"qualities that are vital for..."同位语结构说明这些品质的重要性。

**4. 平衡立场句**
> *"**A balanced reward system that values** achievement, perseverance, and progress **enables** a broader range of students to take ownership of their learning and continue striving toward their full potential"*

**技巧**：用"that values..."定语从句限定平衡奖励体系的特征（成就、毅力、进步），"enables... to take ownership of..."说明效果，"continue striving toward..."补充持续努力的方向。总结句全面而有力。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 第四段（`In my view...`）是全文最弱的一段：首句 "rewarding students is a common educational strategy" 属常识铺垫，未推进论证，导致"自己的观点"停在抽象的"两者兼顾"。可改为说明**如何**兼顾——例如学业奖与进步奖分设、同一学期内并行评选，让立场落地。
- 东亚例子止于 `arguably contributed to high educational standards`，用 arguably 自我软化后就收尾，论断缺支撑。补一句机制（如排名公示如何转化为日常学习投入）会比再加一个国家更有效。
- 第四段与结论段的信息高度重合（都在讲"平衡成就与进步"）。可让第四段专注"怎么做"，结论段专注"为什么值得"，避免读起来像同一段写了两遍。
