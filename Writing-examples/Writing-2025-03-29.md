**The table below gives information about the amount of tax collected (as % of GDP) in five countries for four years between 1975 and 2005.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-03-29](./Task1_img/2025-03-29.png)

The table illustrates changes in the percentage of GDP generated as tax in Sweden, the USA, Korea, Japan, and Turkey from 1975 to 2005.

As seen, Sweden, the USA, and Japan witnessed an upward trend in their tax revenue as a share of GDP. In Sweden, the proportion stood at 46% in 1975, rose slightly to 51% in 1995, and then surged to a peak of 70.1% in 2005, the most significant increase among the data of the nations. Japan, which had the lowest figure in 1975 (15%), more than doubled its tax ratio to 32.1%, ranking second, in 2005. Meanwhile, the USA maintained a relatively stable tax share which fluctuated between 25% and 27% over the decades. 

In contrast, the tax-to-GDP ratios in Korea and Turkey showed greater fluctuation. In Korea, a significant increase was recorded, rising from 15.1% in 1975 to 27.3% in 2005, despite a slight decline in 1995. Similarly, Turkey’s ratio initially fell from 16.4% in 1975 to 15% in 1985 before rebounding sharply to 27.4% in 2005, aligning with the final figures for both Korea and the USA.

Overall, all five countries experienced an upturn in their tax-to-GDP ratios over the given period, with Sweden consistently having the highest percentage. However, the data in the USA saw only a marginal rise.

**1. 时间链式句**
> *"In Sweden, the proportion stood at 46% in 1975, rose slightly to 51% in 1995, and then **surged to a peak** of around 70.1% in 2005, the most significant increase among the data of the nations."*

**技巧**：用三个并列谓语"stood at... rose slightly to... surged to a peak..."按时间顺序描述变化，同位语"the most significant increase"补充说明这一变化的特殊地位。"surged to a peak"比"increased a lot"更加精准和学术化。

> *"Japan, which had the lowest figure in 1975 (15%), **more than doubled** its tax ratio to 32.1%, **ranking second**, in 2005."*

**4. 综合概述句**

**技巧**：概述段先陈述总体趋势，"with"独立主格标注排名，"However"转折指出例外（USA增幅最小）。这种"总体→排名→例外"的结构使概述信息丰富而层次分明。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：文化与传统类 · Agree/Disagree

**Old buildings are of historical importance and should be preserved in cities and countryside. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

**老建筑有历史重要性，应在城市和乡村加以保护。你在多大程度上同意或不同意？**

Some argue that old buildings hold historical importance and should be preserved despite modernization and urban expansion. Although I disagree that all aging structures are historically significant, I believe preserving those with cultural value is essential for identity and the economy, while others should be repurposed to meet modern needs.

有人认为，尽管现代化和城市扩张在持续进行，老建筑仍具有历史重要性，应当予以保护。尽管我不同意所有老化建筑都具有历史意义，但我认为保护那些具有文化价值的建筑对于文化认同和经济发展至关重要，而其他建筑则应被重新利用，以满足现代需求。

Undeniably, some old buildings carry historical significance, but not every aging structure warrants preservation. Many old buildings with cultural heritage import serve as tangible links to the past as they are associated with key historical events or notable figures. Thus, their preservation allows future generations to connect with history, understand societal evolution, and learn valuable lessons from the past. Their value extends beyond their architectural presence, as they embody historical narratives that shape cultural identity. For instance, many immigrant families who settle far from their ancestral homeland find that historic buildings in their countries of origin offer a meaningful way for younger generations to connect with their heritage. Visiting such sites in those regions helps them understand their family's roots, fostering a deeper appreciation of their cultural identity and the historical evolution of their ancestral country.

不可否认，一些老建筑确实承载着历史意义，但并非所有老化建筑都值得保护。许多具有文化遗产价值的老建筑作为过去的实物见证，与关键历史事件或著名人物息息相关。因此，保护这些建筑可以让后代与历史建立联系，理解社会演变，并从过去汲取宝贵经验。它们的价值不仅体现在建筑本身，还在于它们承载的历史叙事，这些叙事塑造了文化认同。例如，许多远离祖籍地的移民家庭发现，原籍国的历史建筑为年轻一代提供了一种有意义的方式，使他们能够与自己的文化遗产建立联系。参观这些地区的历史遗址，有助于他们了解自己的家族根源，培养对文化认同和祖籍国历史演变的更深层次理解。

Beyond their historical importance, preserving significant old buildings also provides economic benefits, so structures lacking cultural or historical value can be repurposed or replaced to meet modern needs. In many cases, maintaining old buildings boosts local economies, particularly through tourism, causing many cities worldwide, such as Beijing, to rely on their historic districts to attract millions of visitors annually in order to generate revenue from local businesses, hotels, and restaurants. Similarly, in rural areas, historical landmarks stimulate local economies by promoting heritage tourism that create jobs and support small businesses.

除了历史重要性之外，保护重要的老建筑还带来了经济利益，因此，缺乏文化或历史价值的建筑可以被重新利用或替换，以满足现代需求。在许多情况下，维护老建筑能够促进地方经济发展，尤其是在旅游业方面。例如，北京等全球多个城市依赖其历史街区，每年吸引数百万游客，从当地企业、酒店和餐馆等行业中创造收入。同样，在农村地区，历史地标通过促进文化遗产旅游来刺激当地经济，创造就业机会，并支持小型企业的发展。

However, preserving old buildings presents challenges. In rapidly expanding urban areas, old buildings without historical significance may occupy valuable space needed for housing, commercial centers, or infrastructure. Additionally, maintenance costs can be substantial if structures are too deteriorated to restore. In such cases, their removal may be necessary to accommodate urban development. Hence, a balanced approach, such as adaptive reuse, where old structures are transformed into museums, offices, or residences, can offer a practical compromise between preservation and modernization.

然而，保护老建筑也面临挑战。在快速扩张的城市地区，缺乏历史意义的老建筑可能占据宝贵的空间，而这些空间本可用于住房、商业中心或基础设施建设。此外，如果建筑结构严重损坏，维护成本可能十分高昂。在这种情况下，拆除这些建筑可能是适应城市发展的必要举措。因此，采用一种平衡的方法，如适应性再利用，将老建筑改造为博物馆、办公空间或住宅，可以在保护与现代化之间提供切实可行的折中方案。

In conclusion, preserving historically meaningful buildings is essential for maintaining cultural identity and supporting economic growth, particularly through tourism. However, since not all old structures warrant conservation, prioritizing the preservation of heritage sites while repurposing or replacing others ensures that modernization and historical conservation can coexist harmoniously for future generations.

总之，保护具有历史意义的建筑对于维护文化认同和支持经济增长（特别是在旅游业方面）至关重要。然而，由于并非所有老建筑都值得保护，优先保护文化遗产地，同时对其他建筑进行再利用或替换，可以确保现代化与历史保护和谐共存，使未来世代受益。

**1. 复杂 nuanced 立场句**
> *"**Although I disagree that all aging structures are historically significant**, I believe preserving those with cultural value is essential for identity and the economy, **while others should be repurposed** to meet modern needs."*

**技巧**：用"Although"引导让步从句否定绝对立场，主句肯定部分价值，"while"引导的并列句提出对另一部分的处理方案。这种"否定→肯定→替代方案"的三段式结构使立场表达非常 nuanced 和成熟。

**2. 因果推导句**
> *"maintaining old buildings boosts local economies, particularly through tourism, **causing many cities worldwide**, such as Beijing, **to rely on their historic districts to attract millions of visitors annually** in order to generate revenue from local businesses, hotels, and restaurants."*

**技巧**：主句陈述效果（促进经济），"causing... to rely on..."推导连锁反应，"in order to"说明最终目的。这种"效果→行为→目的"的链条式写法使经济论证非常具体和有说服力。

**3. 条件+方案句**
> *"**In such cases**, their removal may be necessary to accommodate urban development. **Hence**, a balanced approach, such as adaptive reuse, where old structures are transformed into museums, offices, or residences, can offer a practical compromise between preservation and modernization."*

**技巧**："In such cases"指代前文的严重损坏情况，"Hence"推导解决方案，"where"引导的非限制性定语从句具体说明"adaptive reuse"的操作方式。这种"问题→方案→具体操作"的结构使建议非常实用。

**4. 综合总结句**
> *"In conclusion, preserving historically meaningful buildings is essential for maintaining cultural identity and supporting economic growth, particularly through tourism."*

**技巧**：结尾句先肯定保护的价值，"particularly through tourism"精确限定经济价值的主要来源。"maintaining cultural identity"和"supporting economic growth"两个动名词短语并列，从文化和经济两个维度总结保护的意义。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- Body 1 的 `Many old buildings with cultural heritage import serve as...` 中 `import` 系 `importance` 脱字，属硬伤；同文 Body 2 的 `heritage tourism that create jobs` 主谓不一致，应为 `creates`。
