![2026-03-21](./Task1_img/2026-03-21.png)

The bar chart illustrates how many non-medical employees were working in five departments, namely cleaning, administration, maintenance, catering, and security, in one clinic from 2000 to 2015.

As shown, cleaning and administration departments employed increasing numbers of staff over the given period. Specifically, cleaning had the smallest workforce in the first decade, although it rose from approximately 22 staff in 2000 to around 29 in 2010. This growth then accelerated, and the figure increased further to become the second highest (almost 43) in 2015. Administration was the second largest workforce in the first decade and became the largest by the end of the period, with the number of people it employed rising from 25 to 44.

In contrast, the data on employees of maintenance and catering departments showed minor fluctuations with an overall decrease. Notably, in 2000, maintenance had the largest workforce, but its staff size fell from approximately 45 in 2000 to 40 in 2005, recovered to its initial level in 2010 and then dropped to 35 in 2015. Meanwhile, the workforce for security experienced minimal change overall, hovering between 28 and 30 employees throughout the period.

Overall, staffing levels grew in most departments, particularly in administration and cleaning, while that of maintenance showed a fluctuating trend and eventually declined.

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：文化与传统类 · Discuss Both Views

**Some people believe that historical sites should be completely closed, while others argue that they should be open to the public. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.**

**一些人认为历史遗址应当完全关闭，而另一些人则认为这些遗址应该向公众开放。请讨论双方观点，并给出你自己的看法**

Some argue that historical sites should be entirely closed to visitors; however, others believe that making them accessible to the public is of great importance. My view is that although closure may be conducive to preservation, opening them to the general public plays a vital role in cultural preservation and transmission.

一些人认为历史遗址应完全对游客关闭；然而，另一些人则认为应向公众开放这些遗址具有重要意义。我的观点是，尽管关闭有助于保护，但向公众开放在文化的保存与传承方面发挥着至关重要的作用。

On the one hand, proponents of closing historical sites usually contend, from a heritage protection perspective, that human activity poses a significant threat to their preservation. Continuous foot traffic, physical contact, and environmental pollution can accelerate the deterioration of fragile structures and artefacts. For example, ancient ruins and cave paintings are particularly vulnerable to humidity, temperature fluctuations, and even the carbon dioxide exhaled by visitors. The complex preservation environment, worsened by excessive visitor numbers, makes conserving these valuable sites challenging or even impossible. However, by restricting access, authorities can better protect these irreplaceable cultural treasures and ensure their survival for future generations, which explains why conservation should take precedence over public access.

一方面，支持关闭历史遗址的人通常从文物保护的角度认为，人类活动对其保护构成了重大威胁。持续的人流踩踏、身体接触以及环境污染都会加速脆弱结构和文物的损坏。例如，古代遗址和洞穴壁画对湿度、温度波动，甚至游客呼出的二氧化碳都极为敏感。复杂的保护环境在大量游客的影响下进一步恶化，使这些珍贵遗址的保护变得困难甚至不可能。然而，通过限制进入，相关部门可以更好地保护这些不可替代的文化遗产，并确保它们能够传承给后代，这也解释了为什么保护应优先于公众参观。

On the other hand, advocates for keeping historical sites open argue that such places serve essential cultural and economic functions. From an educational perspective, historical monuments usually carry significant cultural and historical value, serving as tangible links to the past, as they are often associated with key historical events or notable figures. Visiting these sites allows individuals to engage directly with history, fostering a deeper appreciation of their cultural identity and the historical development of their nation, which cannot be achieved through textbooks alone. From a financial perspective, public access often generates revenue through heritage tourism, which can be reinvested in maintenance and conservation efforts. Without this financial support, many historical sites might suffer from neglect or insufficient funding. Therefore, controlled accessibility can both educate the public and contribute to the sustainability of these sites.

另一方面，支持开放历史遗址的人认为，这些地方具有重要的文化和经济功能。从教育角度来看，历史遗迹通常承载着重要的历史与文化价值，是连接过去的有形载体，因为它们往往与重大历史事件或重要人物相关。参观这些遗址可以使人们直接接触历史，从而加深对自身文化身份以及国家历史发展的理解，而这些是仅通过教科书无法实现的。从经济角度来看，公众开放通常可以通过文化旅游带来收入，这些资金可以再投入到维护和保护工作中。如果缺乏这种财政支持，许多历史遗址可能会因资金不足而被忽视或得不到妥善维护。因此，在合理控制的前提下开放既能教育公众，也有助于这些遗址的可持续发展。

In my opinion, completely closing historical sites may protect them physically, but it also deprives society of valuable cultural and educational experiences. Instead, heritage organizations should implement strict management strategies, such as limiting visitor numbers, enforcing guided tours, and using advanced preservation technologies. By implementing this approach, people can appreciate historical sites while minimizing potential damage.

在我看来，完全关闭历史遗址虽然可以在物理上保护它们，但同时也剥夺了社会宝贵的文化和教育体验。相反，文物保护机构应采取严格的管理措施，例如限制游客数量、实行导览制度以及采用先进的保护技术。通过这种方式，人们既可以欣赏历史遗址，又能将潜在的破坏降至最低。

In conclusion, although closing historical sites can help preserve them, keeping them open under careful regulation offers greater educational and economic benefits. A well-managed system can successfully reconcile the need for conservation with the public's right to access cultural heritage.

总之，尽管关闭历史遗址有助于保护它们，但在严格监管下开放能够带来更大的教育和经济收益。一个良好的管理体系可以有效平衡文物保护的需求与公众接触文化遗产的权利。

**1. 因果机制句**
> *"Continuous foot traffic, physical contact, and environmental pollution **can accelerate** the deterioration of fragile structures and artefacts."*

**技巧**：用 can accelerate 说明人类活动与文物损坏之间的因果关系，三个并列主语（foot traffic, physical contact, environmental pollution）使论证全面。

**2. 例证+结论句**
> *"For example, ancient ruins and cave paintings are particularly vulnerable to humidity, temperature fluctuations, and even the carbon dioxide exhaled by visitors. The complex preservation environment, worsened by excessive visitor numbers, makes conserving these valuable sites challenging or even impossible. **However, by restricting access**, authorities can better protect these irreplaceable cultural treasures and ensure their survival for future generations, **which explains why** conservation should take precedence over public access."*

**技巧**：用 For example 引出具体例子，再用 However 转折提出解决方案，最后用 which explains why 引导非限制性定语从句总结关闭方的核心论点，逻辑链条完整。

**3. 多角度论证句**
> *"From an educational perspective, historical monuments usually carry significant cultural and historical value, serving as tangible links to the past, **as** they are often associated with key historical events or notable figures. Visiting these sites allows individuals to engage directly with history, **fostering** a deeper appreciation of their cultural identity and the historical development of their nation, **which cannot be achieved through textbooks alone**."*

**技巧**：用 From an educational perspective 明确角度，as 引导原因从句解释历史遗迹的价值，fostering 现在分词短语说明参观的效果，which 引导非限制性定语从句强调独特性，论证饱满。

**4. 反面论证句**
> *"Without this financial support, many historical sites might suffer from neglect or insufficient funding."*

**技巧**：用 Without 引导条件状语，主句用 might suffer from 描述可能的负面后果，反面论证简洁有力。

**5. 建议效果句**
> *"By implementing this approach, people can appreciate historical sites **while minimizing** potential damage."*

**技巧**：用 By implementing 说明方式，while minimizing 现在分词短语说明同时达成的效果，建议与效果并重。

**6. 平衡总结句**
> *"A well-managed system can successfully **reconcile** the need for conservation with the public's right to access cultural heritage."*

**技巧**：用 reconcile... with... 结构精准表达"平衡"的核心概念，将保护需求与公众权利融于一句，总结升华有力。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- Body 1 末句 `However, by restricting access, authorities can better protect...` 里的 However 用反了——这句是顺着关闭方的论点往下推，并非转折，应改为 Therefore 或 Hence。
- Body 3（自己的观点）与结尾段内容高度重合，都是"严格管理下开放"，结尾几乎在复述上一段；结尾只保留一句立场即可，把篇幅让给论证。
- 关闭方有洞穴壁画、游客呼出二氧化碳这样的具体例证，开放方的"教育价值"却全是抽象表述（tangible links、engage directly with history），两侧例证不对称，补一处具体遗址会更平衡。
