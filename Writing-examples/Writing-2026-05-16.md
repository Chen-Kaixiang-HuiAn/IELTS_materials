## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：教育类 · Agree/Disagree

**Education of young people is highly prioritized in many countries. However, educating adults who cannot write or read is even more important and governments should spend more money on this. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

**许多国家高度重视年轻人的教育。然而，一些人认为，教育不会读写的成年人更加重要，政府应当在这方面投入更多资金。你在多大程度上同意或不同意这一观点？**

Many governments allocate substantial resources to schooling for children and adolescents. Some people argue, however, that greater priority should be given to adults who remain illiterate. Although educating adults who are unable to read and write is undeniably important, I disagree that it deserves more funding than the education of young people.

许多政府为儿童和青少年的教育投入大量资源。然而，一些人认为，应当把更高的优先级给予仍然处于文盲状态的成年人。尽管为无法读写的成年人提供教育无疑十分重要，但我不同意其应获得比青少年教育更多的资金支持。

Admittedly, investing in adult literacy programs can help illiterate individuals escape difficult situations in both employment and everyday life. This is because they often struggle to secure stable jobs and experience serious inconvenience in daily activities. Lacking basic reading and writing skills, many are left unemployed or confined to low-skilled manual labor, such as construction work or factory packaging jobs. Such physically demanding work is usually poorly paid and may also pose considerable risks to health. In addition to difficulties in employment, illiteracy creates barriers in daily life, as these individuals are often unable to understand official procedures or communicate effectively with authorities. This is particularly true for migrant workers in developing countries who, because they cannot read road signs or use smartphone navigation applications, may find it difficult to navigate unfamiliar cities independently or ask others for help when necessary. These challenges become even more severe when they encounter unfair treatment, which may further marginalize them within society.

诚然，投资成人识字项目能够帮助文盲群体摆脱就业和日常生活中的困境。这是因为他们往往难以获得稳定工作，并在日常活动中面临诸多不便。由于缺乏基本的读写能力，许多人失业，或者只能从事低技能体力劳动，例如建筑工作或工厂包装工作。这类高强度劳动通常工资较低，并且可能对健康造成较大风险。更重要的是，文盲现象会在日常生活中造成障碍，因为这些人往往无法理解官方程序，也难以与政府机构有效沟通。对于发展中国家的外来务工人员而言，这种情况尤为明显。由于无法阅读路标或使用手机导航软件，他们可能难以独立在陌生城市中行动，或在需要时向他人寻求帮助。当他们遭遇不公平待遇时，这些困难会进一步加剧，并可能导致他们在社会中被进一步边缘化。

Nevertheless, prioritizing adult literacy education above the schooling of children would be a short-sighted approach. As the world increasingly transitions into a digitally driven society, young people represent the future workforce and leadership of a nation, meaning that the quality of their education is likely to determine a country's long-term competitiveness and stability. If governments divert excessive resources away from schools, the consequences may persist for decades, including weaker innovation and a less skilled population. More importantly, young people are generally more receptive to learning than adults, so investing in education early in life tends to bring greater long-term benefits. Such investment not only prevents illiteracy from emerging in the first place but also exposes young people to diverse perspectives, which are indispensable for addressing complex challenges in the information age and essential for both personal and societal development.

然而，将成人识字教育置于儿童教育之上是一种短视的做法。随着世界日益迈向数字化社会，年轻人代表着国家未来的劳动力和领导力量，这意味着他们所接受教育的质量很可能决定一个国家长期的竞争力和社会稳定。如果政府将过多资源从学校教育中转移出去，其后果可能持续数十年，包括创新能力减弱以及劳动力素质下降。更重要的是，年轻人的学习能力通常强于成年人，因此，在人生早期阶段投资教育往往能够带来更大的长期收益。这种投资不仅能够从源头上防止文盲问题的产生，还能够使年轻人接触多元观点，而这对于应对信息时代中的复杂挑战，以及促进个人与社会的发展，都至关重要。

In conclusion, although adult literacy programs can improve employment opportunities and living conditions for disadvantaged individuals, prioritizing children's education remains more important because it produces greater long-term social benefits, prevents illiteracy at its source, and prepares future generations to address increasingly complex challenges.

总而言之，尽管成人识字项目能够改善弱势群体的就业机会和生活条件，但优先发展儿童教育仍然更加重要，因为它能够带来更长期的社会收益，从根源上减少文盲现象，并帮助未来一代应对日益复杂的挑战。

**1. 让步转折复合句**
> *"**Although** educating adults who are unable to read and write is undeniably important, **I disagree** that it deserves more funding than the education of young people."*

**技巧**：用 Although 引导让步从句，先承认成人识字教育的重要性，再在主句中用 I disagree 明确反对其应获得更多资金，使立场更加审慎有力。

**2. 因果机制句**
> *"This is **because** they often struggle to secure stable jobs and experience serious inconvenience in daily activities."*

**技巧**：用 This is because 解释文盲群体困境的原因，struggle to 和 experience 并列描述双重困难，因果逻辑清晰。

**3. 例证效果句**
> *"This is **particularly true for** migrant workers in developing countries who, **because** they cannot read road signs or use smartphone navigation applications, may find it difficult to navigate unfamiliar cities independently or ask others for help when necessary."*

**技巧**：用 This is particularly true for 引出具体例证，who 引导定语从句描述外来务工人员，because 引导原因从句解释具体困难，find it difficult to 描述后果，论证饱满。

**4. 反面论证句**
> *"**If** governments divert excessive resources away from schools, the consequences may persist for decades, including weaker innovation and a less skilled population."*

**技巧**：用 If 引导条件从句，主句用 may persist for decades 描述长期后果，including 现在分词短语补充具体表现，反面论证有力。

**5. 递进效果句**
> *"Such investment **not only prevents** illiteracy from emerging in the first place **but also exposes** young people to diverse perspectives, **which are indispensable for** addressing complex challenges in the information age and essential for both personal and societal development."*

**技巧**：用 not only... but also... 连接双重效果，which 引导非限制性定语从句补充说明多元视角的价值，递进论证有力。

**6. 综合总结句**
> *"**In conclusion, although** adult literacy programs can improve employment opportunities and living conditions for disadvantaged individuals, **prioritizing** children's education remains more important **because** it produces greater long-term social benefits, prevents illiteracy at its source, and prepares future generations to address increasingly complex challenges."*

**技巧**：用 although 引导让步从句，先承认成人识字教育的价值，再主句用 prioritizing... remains more important because... 明确立场并给出三个理由，总结全面有力。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 两段具体度悬殊：让步段细节密集（建筑工、工厂包装、路标、导航软件），反驳段却全是抽象论断，反驳段至少需要一个可感的例子来对等支撑。
- `young people are generally more receptive to learning than adults` 是反驳段的关键前提，却零支撑，可补一句认知发展窗口或教育回报率的理由。
- 全文未正面处理对方最有力的一点——成人文盲的困境是当下的，而儿童教育的回报要等二十年；补一句回应这一"时间差"，立场会更站得住。
