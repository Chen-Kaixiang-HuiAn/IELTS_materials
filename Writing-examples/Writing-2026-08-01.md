## Task 1

> **图表类型标注**：组合图（柱状图+饼图：海平面上升1米时六地区土地流失面积与受影响人口）

**The charts below show the land area lost (thousand km²) per one-meter rise and population (million) affected per one-meter rise in sea level in the predicted future. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2026-08-01](./Task1_img/2026-08-01.png)


The bar chart compares the projected amount of land that would be lost in six regions, namely South Africa, Europe, Central Africa, Asia, America and Oceania, if sea levels rose by one metre, while the pie chart shows the corresponding number of people affected. 

Overall, Asia would experience by far the greatest impact on both measures, whereas Oceania would be the least affected. The population ranking, however, is not the same as that for land-area loss, especially in Europe and Central Africa.

In detail, Asia is forecast to lose the largest area of land, at approximately 450,000 square kilometres, nearly three times America's predicted land loss of around 160,000 square kilometres. Europe follows with a figure similar to America's, at 150,000 square kilometres, compared with South Africa (120,000 square kilometres) and Central Africa (80,000 square kilometres). Meanwhile, the smallest amount of land, only around 10,000 square kilometres, would be submerged in Oceania. 

Regarding population, Asia again ranks first, with 250 million people expected to be affected, followed by South Africa, with 117 million, despite its comparatively modest land loss. Europe presents the clearest contrast: although its projected land loss is the third-largest, only 10 million people would be affected, ranking fifth and standing just above the figure for Oceania, where the lowest number would be recorded, at just 2 million.

### 精析

#### ① 结构分析（精简）

本题属于 **Combination chart（组合图：柱状图 + 饼图）** 类 Task 1，涉及海平面上升 1 米时，六个地区（South Africa, Europe, Central Africa, Asia, America, Oceania）的 **土地流失面积（柱状图）** 与 **受影响人口（饼图）** 两项指标。

本文采用 **"开头段 + 独立总览段 + 土地主体段 + 人口主体段"** 的 4 段式结构（开头段改写题目 → 独立总览段 → 主体段 1（土地流失面积）→ 主体段 2（受影响人口））：

- **Paragraph 1 — Introduction（开头段）**
  - 改写题目要素：`The bar chart compares the projected amount of land that would be lost in six regions... if sea levels rose by one metre, while the pie chart shows the corresponding number of people affected`（用 while 把两图一句话串起，点明"对应"关系）

- **Paragraph 2 — Overview（独立总览段）**
  - 全局总览：`Overall, Asia would experience by far the greatest impact on both measures, whereas Oceania would be the least affected`
  - 排名错位提示：`The population ranking, however, is not the same as that for land-area loss, especially in Europe and Central Africa`（为后文重点剖析埋下伏笔）

- **Paragraph 3 — Body 1（主体段：土地流失面积）**
  - Asia 最大（约 450,000 km²，近 America 160,000 的三倍）
  - Europe 与 America 相近（150,000），South Africa 120,000、Central Africa 80,000
  - Oceania 最小（仅约 10,000）

- **Paragraph 4 — Body 2（主体段：受影响人口）**
  - Asia 仍居首（250 million），South Africa 次之（117 million，尽管土地流失不大）
  - Europe 反差最鲜明：土地第三，但人口仅 10 million（排第五），仅高于 Oceania（2 million）

> **结构亮点**：作者把"双图但三大对比维度"（土地流失量排名、人口排名、二者错位）组织得极清楚。先以独立总览段（P2）给出全局结论（Asia 双第一、Oceania 双最后），再用 `The population ranking, however, is not the same...` 制造悬念，引出后文对 Europe / Central Africa 错位的重点剖析。这种"先总双维、再揭错位"的写法，把组合图最难的"两图排名不一致"变成了全文亮点，而非各自罗列。

#### ③ 四项能力维度分析（TA / CC / LR / GRA）

- **TA**：作者精准识别组合图的核心任务——比较两图而非分别描述。总览段（P2）即用 both measures 锁定双维对比框架，并以"排名错位"作为分析主轴，选取得当、特征突出。Asia 双第一、Oceania 双最后的极值，与 Europe 的错位，构成完整的主次结构。

**1. 双图总览 + 双维极值句**
> *"Overall, Asia would experience **by far the greatest impact on both measures**, **whereas** Oceania would be the least affected."*

**技巧**：by far 强化程度，both measures 点明双图对照，whereas 把首尾极值并置，一句完成组合图的全局判断。

**2. 倍数对比句**
> *"Asia is forecast to lose the largest area of land, **at approximately 450,000 square kilometres**, **nearly three times** America's predicted land loss of around 160,000 square kilometres."*

**技巧**：at 介词短语补充具体数据，nearly three times 倍数直接量化 Asia 的领先幅度，避免另起一句写比较。

**3. 错位反差句**
> *"Europe presents **the clearest contrast**: **although** its projected land loss is the third-largest, only 10 million people would be affected, **ranking fifth** and standing just above the figure for Oceania..."*

**技巧**：although 让步承认土地第三，主句用 only 10 million 制造反差，ranking fifth 分词补充人口排名，把"土地多却人少"压实在一句。

**4. 维度切换 + 让步句**
> *"**Regarding population**, Asia again ranks first, with 250 million people expected to be affected, followed by South Africa, with 117 million, **despite** its comparatively modest land loss."*

**技巧**：Regarding 切换至人口维度，with 复合结构补数据，followed by 串联次序，despite 让步点出 South Africa "土地小却人口第二"的错位，呼应总览段主线。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：教育类 · Agree/Disagree

**Mathematical skills are more important in real life than communication skills. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

**一些人认为数学技能在现实生活中比沟通技能更重要。你在多大程度上同意或不同意？**


Some argue that mathematical skills have greater practical value than communication skills in ordinary life. Although numeracy is useful in certain aspects of life and indispensable in some occupations, I disagree that it is more important.

有人认为，在日常生活中，数学技能比沟通技能具有更大的实用价值。尽管计算能力在生活的某些方面颇有用处，并且在部分职业中不可或缺，但我不同意数学技能更为重要这一观点。

Admittedly, mathematical literacy plays an important role in both personal and professional life. A sound knowledge of mathematics cultivates logical thinking and problem-solving skills, while fields such as accounting and economics rely heavily on numerical operations to address real-world problems. Proficiency in mathematics helps people manage household budgets, compare prices and understand interest rates, while giving those entering numerically demanding professions a clear advantage in today s competitive job market. Nevertheless, the mathematics used by most people in daily life rarely extends beyond basic arithmetic. This is particularly true now that calculators, financial applications and automated software can perform more complicated calculations quickly and accurately. Consequently, although advanced mathematics remains valuable for intellectual development and is essential in specialised fields such as engineering, finance and data analysis, its direct relevance to the everyday lives of most people is comparatively limited.

诚然，数学素养在个人生活和职业生活中都发挥着重要作用。扎实的数学知识能够培养逻辑思维和解决问题的能力，而会计、经济学等领域高度依赖数值运算来处理现实问题。熟练掌握数学有助于人们管理家庭预算、比较价格并理解利率，同时也能让准备进入数理要求较高行业的人在当今竞争激烈的就业市场中获得明显优势。然而，大多数人在日常生活中使用的数学通常不会超出基本算术的范围。尤其是在如今，计算器、金融应用程序和自动化软件都能迅速而准确地完成更复杂的计算。因此，尽管高等数学仍有益于智力发展，并且在工程、金融和数据分析等专业领域必不可少，但它与大多数人日常生活的直接关联相对有限。

By comparison, communication skills have become even more important in professional and social settings, where technology cannot replace human judgement and interpersonal sensitivity. Most contemporary projects, whether in technology, healthcare or academia, require multidisciplinary collaboration. Professionals must therefore communicate effectively, understand diverse perspectives and translate specialist knowledge into language accessible to non-specialists, particularly when working across departments. For example, doctors have to explain diagnoses clearly to patients, and engineers may need to present technical risks to non-specialist managers. This exchange of information allows responsibilities to be coordinated, misunderstandings to be resolved and common goals to be achieved more efficiently. Beyond the workplace, face-to-face interactions strengthen personal relationships. Facial expressions, tone of voice and body language help people convey emotions, build trust and respond appropriately to others. While someone who lacks advanced mathematical knowledge can use a digital tool or consult a specialist, a person who cannot communicate effectively may be unable even to explain the problem or obtain appropriate assistance.

相比之下，沟通技能在职业和社交场合中变得更加重要，因为技术无法取代人的判断力和人际交往中的敏感度。当代大多数项目，无论属于科技、医疗还是学术领域，都需要跨学科合作。因此，专业人士必须有效沟通、理解不同观点，并把专业知识转化为非专业人士能够理解的语言，尤其是在跨部门合作时。例如，医生需要向患者清楚解释诊断结果，工程师也可能需要向非专业管理人员说明技术风险。这种信息交流可以协调职责、化解误解，并使共同目标得到更高效的实现。在工作场所以外，面对面交流还能巩固个人关系。面部表情、语气和肢体语言有助于人们表达情感、建立信任，并对他人作出恰当回应。缺乏高等数学知识的人可以借助数字工具或咨询专业人士；但无法有效沟通的人甚至可能无法说明问题，也无法获得适当帮助。

In conclusion, although mathematical skills support logical thinking and remain indispensable in certain professions, everyday calculations are usually simple and increasingly technology-assisted. Communication skills are therefore more important, as they underpin effective collaboration, mutual understanding and successful problem-solving in almost every area of life.

总之，尽管数学技能有助于培养逻辑思维，并且在某些职业中仍不可或缺，但日常计算通常较为简单，而且越来越多地由技术辅助完成。因此，沟通技能更为重要，因为它们几乎在生活的每个领域都是有效协作、相互理解和成功解决问题的基础。

### 精析

#### ① 结构分析（精简）

本题属于 **Agree/Disagree** 类题型。此类题型的核心策略是：明确表明立场，再展开论证（通常先让步理解对方，再反驳立论）。

本文采用 **"先退后进"** 的四段式结构：

- **Paragraph 1 — Introduction（开头段）**
  - 呈现对立观点（paraphrase）：`Some argue that mathematical skills have greater practical value than communication skills in ordinary life`（替换原题 `Mathematical skills are more important in real life than communication skills`）
  - 表明立场：`Although numeracy is useful in certain aspects of life and indispensable in some occupations, I disagree that it is more important`（让步承认数学有用，但反对"数学更重要"）

- **Paragraph 2 — Body 1（让步段 Admittedly：数学的价值）**
  - 主题句：`Admittedly, mathematical literacy plays an important role in both personal and professional life`
  - 个人层面：培养逻辑思维与解决问题能力
  - 职业层面：会计/经济依赖数值运算；管理预算、比价、理解利率 → 数理行业就业优势

- **Paragraph 3 — Body 2（主论反驳段 Nevertheless：沟通更重要）**
  - 限缩数学：`Nevertheless, the mathematics used by most people in daily life rarely extends beyond basic arithmetic` → 技术替代 → 高等数学仅限专业领域，日常关联有限
  - 升起沟通：`By comparison, communication skills have become even more important...`
  - 职业机制：当代项目需跨学科协作 → 专业人士须有效沟通、转化专业知识（医生/工程师例证）
  - 延伸：信息交流协调职责、化解误解；面对面巩固关系（表情/语气/肢体语言）
  - 对比收束：缺数学可用工具/咨询；缺沟通甚至无法说明问题

- **Paragraph 4 — Conclusion（结尾段）**
  - 重申：`although mathematical skills support logical thinking and remain indispensable in certain professions, everyday calculations are usually simple...`
  - 结论：`Communication skills are therefore more important, as they underpin effective collaboration, mutual understanding and successful problem-solving...`

> **结构亮点**：本文采用经典"先退后进"。开头先用 Some argue 呈现对立观点，再用 Although... I disagree 明确反对"数学更重要"；Body 1 充分承认数学在个人与职业生活中的价值（逻辑、预算、利率、就业优势），Body 2 才用 Nevertheless 转折，先限缩数学的日常相关性（基本算术 + 技术替代），再举起沟通技能的多场景价值（协作、共情、跨域），并以医生/工程师具名案例锚定。结尾用 therefore 收束到"沟通更重要"，形成"理解对方 → 建设己方"的闭环。

#### ② 论证逻辑链拆解（标注有效写法）

**Body 1（让步段：数学价值）的逻辑链条：**

```
数学有价值（前提A）
    ↓ [个人层面]
```

> **逻辑亮点**：Body 1 用"分类论证"从个人与职业两层面承认数学价值，体现对对立立场的充分理解；Body 2 则先以"限缩 + 技术替代"削弱数学的日常权重，再用"沟通不可替代"上升，并以医生/工程师双案例把抽象论点具象化。两段"承认 → 限缩 → 超越"的递进，使 Agree/Disagree 的反方立场立得稳、破得巧，逻辑闭环。

#### ③ 四项能力维度分析（TA / CC / LR / GRA）

- **TA**：本文采用经典"先退后进"结构。开头用 Although 让步承认数学的实用性，再用 I disagree 明确反对"数学更重要"的命题。Body 1 充分承认对方合理之处（个人与职业生活中的数学价值），Body 2 才系统展开己方论证——先限缩数学日常相关性，再举起沟通技能的多场景价值，并以医生/工程师案例支撑，最后用 therefore 综合双方、回归"沟通更重要"的立场。这种"理解对方 → 建设己方"的推进，比一边倒更具说服力。
- **CC**：`Admittedly → Nevertheless → Consequently → By comparison` 串起"承认数学 → 限缩其日常 → 升起沟通"的论证弧线；`while` 在 Body 1 内并列"思维培养"与"职业应用"；`For example` 以医生/工程师双案例锚定抽象论述；结尾 `although... therefore` 让步 + 因果收束，张弛有度。
- **LR**：见模块⑤词汇表，含 `numeracy`、`indispensable`、`multidisciplinary collaboration` 等精准词。
- **GRA**：见模块⑤句式亮点。

#### ④ 可迁移句型骨架（直接套用）（题型：Agree/Disagree）

**骨架 1 — 先退后进亮立场（Intro）**
> Some argue that ______ have greater practical value than ______. Although ______, I disagree that ______.

**骨架 2 — 分类论证起手（Body 让步）**
> Admittedly, ______ plays an important role in both personal and professional life. A sound knowledge of ______ cultivates ______.

**骨架 3 — 限缩 + 技术替代（Body 反驳）**
> Nevertheless, the ______ used by most people in daily life rarely extends beyond ______. This is particularly true now that ______.

**骨架 4 — 对比升起 + 例证（Body 主论）**
> By comparison, ______ have become even more important in ______, where technology cannot replace ______. For example, ______.

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

| 衔接类型 | 原文表达 | 功能说明 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **立场预告** | `Some argue that... Although numeracy is useful..., I disagree that it is more important.` | 先呈对立，再明确反对 |
| **让步衔接** | `Admittedly, mathematical literacy plays an important role in both personal and professional life.` | Admittedly 引对方合理处 |
| **并列补充** | `A sound knowledge of mathematics cultivates..., while fields such as accounting... rely heavily on...` | while 并列思维与职业应用 |
| **转折限缩** | `Nevertheless, the mathematics used by most people... rarely extends beyond basic arithmetic.` | Nevertheless 转入限缩 |
| **因果推进** | `Consequently, although advanced mathematics remains valuable..., its direct relevance... is comparatively limited.` | Consequently 推导日常相关性有限 |
| **对比提升** | `By comparison, communication skills have become even more important...` | By comparison 引出己方主论 |
| **举例支撑** | `For example, doctors have to explain diagnoses... and engineers may need to present...` | For example 双案例 |
| **总结重申** | `In conclusion, although... Communication skills are therefore more important...` | therefore 收束立场 |

> **衔接亮点**：`Admittedly → Nevertheless → Consequently → By comparison` 串起"承认数学 → 限缩其日常 → 升起沟通"的论证弧线；`For example` 以医生/工程师双案例把"沟通不可替代"落到职业实景；结尾 `although... therefore` 让步 + 因果收束，立场推进有力、不显复读。

| 词汇/短语 | 中文含义 | 词性/用法 | 替代普通表达 |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **numeracy** | 计算能力 | 名词 | ability to do basic maths |
| **indispensable** | 不可或缺的 | 形容词 | essential / necessary |
| **logical thinking** | 逻辑思维 | 名词短语 | rational reasoning |
| **problem-solving skills** | 解决问题的能力 | 名词短语 | ability to solve problems |
| **numerically demanding** | 数理要求高的 | 形容词短语 | requiring heavy maths |
| **multidisciplinary collaboration** | 跨学科协作 | 名词短语 | cooperation across fields |
| **interpersonal sensitivity** | 人际交往中的敏感度 | 名词短语 | awareness of others' feelings |
| **underpin** | 构成……的基础；支撑 | 动词 | support / form the basis of |

**1. 让步转折复合句（开头段）**
> *"**Although** numeracy is useful in certain aspects of life and indispensable in some occupations, **I disagree that** it is more important."*

**技巧**：Although 让步承认数学的实用与职业价值，主句用 I disagree that... 明确反对"数学更重要"的命题，立场审慎而鲜明，避免一边倒。

**2. 并列实用收益句（让步段）**
> *"Proficiency in mathematics helps people manage household budgets, compare prices and understand interest rates, **while giving** those entering numerically demanding professions a clear advantage in today's competitive job market."*

**技巧**：helps... 三动词并列列举日常功能，while giving... 补充职业收益，一句覆盖"生活 + 职业"双层面，论证密度高。

**3. 限缩 + 技术替代句（反驳段）**
> *"**Consequently, although** advanced mathematics remains valuable for intellectual development..., **its direct relevance to the everyday lives of most people is comparatively limited**."*

**技巧**：Consequently 承接前句的技术替代，although 嵌套让步保留高等数学价值，主句用 comparatively limited 限缩其日常权重，为升起沟通做铺垫。

**4. 对比收束句（反驳段）**
> *"**While** someone who lacks advanced mathematical knowledge can use a digital tool or consult a specialist, a person who cannot communicate effectively may be unable even to explain the problem..."*

**技巧**：While 对比"缺数学"与"缺沟通"的后果——前者有替代方案，后者连问题都无法说明，凸显沟通不可替代，是反驳的收束重锤。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 benefit / relevance）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 核心对比失衡：题目是"数学 vs 沟通"二者孰重，Body 1 给数学三句充分展开（思维、职业、预算利率就业），Body 2 却在限缩数学后，把沟通技能也集中写"职业协作"（医生/工程师），鲜少触及"日常沟通"（仅结尾一句 face-to-face 巩固关系），两话题在"日常生活"层面的正面交锋不足。
- "technology cannot replace human judgement and interpersonal sensitivity" 是 Body 2 的关键支点，但全文未给一例"技术失败/误判"的反面情境来佐证，论点略显断言。
- 结尾 `Communication skills are therefore more important, as they underpin effective collaboration, mutual understanding and successful problem-solving` 与 Body 2 的 "allow responsibilities to be coordinated, misunderstandings to be resolved and common goals to be achieved" 几乎同义复读，推进感弱。

**仿写任务**：用「先退后进」骨架重写：*Some think memorising facts is more useful than learning how to think.*
