## Task 1

> **图表类型标注**：折线图（五国女性就业）

**The graph below shows the employment of women between 16 and 65 years old in five countries from 2003 to 2009.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-12-13](./Task1_img/2025-12-13.png)

The graph illustrates changes in the employment rates of women aged 16 to 65 in the UK, Canada, Germany, Chile, and Turkey between 2003 and 2009. 

As shown, Canada, Germany, and Chile experienced overall increases in female employment rates during the period. In detail, Germany’s rate rose steadily from 50% in 2003 to 65% in 2009, while Chile’s increased gradually from a lower base, climbing from 40% to 50%. The figure for Canada followed a generally upward but more variable pattern, declining from 70% to 65% between 2003 and 2005 before recovering steadily to approximately 75% by the end of the period.

By contrast, although the proportion of women in employment in the UK fluctuated, from 80% in 2003 to a peak of around 84% in 2007 before easing back to 80% by 2009, it maintained the leading position throughout. An opposite trend was observed in Turkey, where female employment fell from 20% in 2003 to a low of 10% in 2007 but rebounded modestly to around 15% in 2009, remaining by far the lowest among the countries shown.

Overall, the UK consistently recorded the highest levels of female employment, while Turkey accounted for the lowest throughout the period.

### 精析

#### ① 结构分析（精简）

本题属于 **Line Graph** 类 Task 1，涉及 5 个国家 1 个数据维度（女性就业率）在 2003 至 2009 年间的变化。

本文采用 **"总-分-总"** 三段式结构：

- **Paragraph 1 — Introduction（开头段）**
  - 改写题目要素：`changes in the employment rates of women aged 16 to 65`（替换原题 `the employment of women between 16 and 65 years old`）
  - 明确对象：UK, Canada, Germany, Chile, Turkey + 时间范围 2003–2009

- **Paragraph 2 — Body Details（主体段：按趋势分组）**
  - **上升趋势组**：Canada, Germany, Chile 的共同特征 + 各自细节
    - Germany：`rose steadily from 50% in 2003 to 65% in 2009`
    - Chile：`increased gradually from a lower base, climbing from 40% to 50%`
    - Canada：`declining from 70% to 65% between 2003 and 2005 before recovering steadily to approximately 75%`
  - **非上升趋势组**：UK 和 Turkey 的不同模式

- **Paragraph 3 — Overview（总结段）**
  - UK 始终保持最高
  - Turkey 始终保持最低

**1. 复杂状语前置句**
> *"By contrast, although the proportion of women in employment in the UK fluctuated, from 80% in 2003 to a peak of around 84% in 2007 before easing back to 80% by 2009, it maintained the leading position throughout."*

**技巧**：将 although 让步状语从句置于句首，主句后置，使句子结构复杂而富有层次，同时容纳了起点、峰值、回落三个数据点，信息密度极高。

**2. 分词短语作伴随状语**
> *"Chile's increased gradually from a lower base, **climbing** from 40% to 50%."*

**技巧**：用现在分词 climbing 补充说明变化过程，避免另起一句，使行文更加紧凑流畅。

**3. there be 句型的变体运用**
> *"An opposite trend was observed in Turkey, where female employment fell from 20% in 2003 to a low of 10% in 2007 but rebounded modestly to around 15% in 2009..."*

**技巧**：用被动语态 "was observed" 引出例外情况，再用 where 引导定语从句详细说明，既客观又具体。

**4. 对比并列结构**
> *"Overall, the UK consistently recorded the highest levels of female employment, while Turkey accounted for the lowest throughout the period."*

**技巧**：用 while 连接两个对比句，最高与最低形成鲜明对照，简洁有力地完成总结。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：环境类 · Advantages/Disadvantages

**Today, more and more people prefer travelling to untouched and unexplored natural areas. What are the advantages and disadvantages for individuals and the country?**

**如今，越来越多的人喜欢前往尚未开发、未被充分探索的自然地区旅行。这一趋势对个人和国家而言分别有哪些优势和劣势？**

It is true that journeys to untouched and exotic regions of the world have gained popularity in recent years. Travelling to remote areas brings notable benefits to both individuals and countries; nevertheless, it also poses several challenges that should not be overlooked.

不可否认，近年来，前往世界各地原始而异域地区进行探险式旅行的热度不断上升。前往偏远地区旅游不仅为个人和国家带来显著益处，但同时也伴随着一些不容忽视的挑战。

On the one hand, travelling to pristine natural areas offers unique advantages for individuals and stimulates economic development opportunities for host countries. From an individual perspective, exploring unspoiled landscapes often fosters a deeper appreciation of nature, and such environments allow visitors to escape the pressures of modern urban life and enjoy tranquillity, thereby encouraging more responsible attitudes toward sustainable living and significantly enhancing mental well-being. From a national perspective, the influx of tourists increases demand for local products such as handicrafts and souvenirs; more importantly, in pursuit of booming tourism to unexplored natural destinations, tourism development creates jobs in hospitality, food service, and transportation, all of which help alleviate unemployment and further strengthen the local economy, especially in remote areas. This, in turn, contributes to more balanced regional development by narrowing the economic gap between urban centres and remote communities.

一方面，前往原始自然区域旅行为个人提供了独特的体验，也为所在国创造了经济发展机遇。从个人角度来看，探索未经破坏的自然景观往往能够培养人们对自然更深层次的敬畏与欣赏。这类环境使游客得以暂时摆脱现代都市生活的压力，享受宁静，从而促使其形成更加负责任的可持续生活态度，并显著提升心理健康水平。从国家层面来看，游客数量的增加推动了对当地产品（如手工艺品和纪念品）的需求；更为重要的是，为了发展前往未开发自然目的地的旅游业，相关旅游开发在住宿、餐饮和交通等领域创造了大量就业机会，这不仅有助于缓解失业问题，也进一步增强了当地经济实力，尤其是在偏远地区。通过缩减市区和偏远地区的经济差距，这有助于均衡的地区发展。

On the other hand, it is essential to emphasize the critical negative impacts that tourism to far-flung places can have on natural environments and local communities living in sensitive areas. Firstly, the biodiversity of fragile ecosystems is severely threatened by human activities, particularly those stemming from excessive or poorly managed tourism practices. Specifically, the careless disposal of waste by tourists in rivers and forests contaminates natural habitats and places countless wildlife species at risk. Once these natural assets are destroyed, they are often impossible to restore, giving rise not only to the loss of flora and fauna but also to a disruption of the delicate ecological balance. Secondly, the relentless expansion of tourism may lead to hostility or conflict between residents and tourists who hold different values and lifestyles. For example, in many religious tribes, inappropriate behaviour arising from tourists' lack of cultural awareness may offend local people, thereby undermining the social cohesion of these communities.

另一方面，必须强调的是，前往偏远地区的旅游活动可能对自然环境以及生活在生态敏感区域的当地社区造成严重的负面影响。首先，脆弱生态系统的生物多样性正受到人类活动的严重威胁，尤其是源于过度或管理不善的旅游行为。具体而言，游客在河流和森林中随意丢弃垃圾会污染自然栖息地，使无数野生动物物种面临风险。一旦这些自然资源遭到破坏，往往难以恢复，不仅会导致动植物资源的流失，还会破坏原本脆弱而精细的生态平衡。其次，旅游业的持续扩张可能引发当地居民与游客之间的敌意甚至冲突，尤其是在双方价值观和生活方式存在明显差异的情况下。例如，在许多 religious 部落中，游客由于缺乏文化意识而表现出的不当行为可能冒犯当地居民，从而削弱社区内部的社会凝聚力。

In conclusion, while travel to untouched natural areas enhances individual well-being and stimulates economic growth in remote regions, it simultaneously threatens fragile ecosystems and local cultures. Therefore, sustainable tourism management is essential to reconcile economic development with long-term environmental protection and social harmony.

总而言之，尽管前往原始自然地区旅行有助于提升个人福祉并促进偏远地区的经济增长，但这一行为同时也对脆弱的生态系统和地方文化构成威胁。因此，实施可持续的旅游管理对于在经济发展、环境保护与社会和谐之间实现长期平衡至关重要。

| **far-flung places** | 遥远偏僻之地 | 名词短语 | remote areas |
| **delicate ecological balance** | 脆弱微妙的生态平衡 | 名词短语 | nature balance |
| **undermining the social cohesion** | 侵蚀社会凝聚力 | 动词短语 | breaking the community |
| **reconcile... with** | 使…与…相调和、兼顾 | 动词短语 | balance... with |

**1. 复杂因果链句式**
> *"...such environments allow visitors to escape the pressures of modern urban life and enjoy tranquillity, **thereby encouraging** more responsible attitudes toward sustainable living and **significantly enhancing** mental well-being."*

**技巧**：用 thereby encouraging 和 significantly enhancing 两个现在分词短语并列作结果状语，将"逃离压力→培养态度→提升健康"的因果链浓缩于一句，信息密度极高。

**2. 让步转折复合句**
> *"Although other resources are rated at a slightly lower level of 91%, the figure is still much higher than those recorded for the other two subjects."*（注：此为Task 1句式，Task 2中可借鉴类似结构）

**技巧**：Task 2 中类似结构如 `Although maintaining architectural uniformity can protect cultural heritage to some extent, I disagree...`，先让步再反驳，使立场更加审慎有力。

**3. 条件后果推演句**
> *"Once these natural assets are destroyed, they are often impossible to restore, giving rise not only to the loss of flora and fauna but also to a disruption of the delicate ecological balance."*

**技巧**：用 Once 引导条件从句，主句用 giving rise not only to... but also to... 连接双重后果，既展示了逻辑推导，又通过并列结构增强了节奏感。

**4. 综合总结句**
> *"Therefore, sustainable tourism management is essential to reconcile economic development with long-term environmental protection and social harmony."*

**技巧**：用 essential to reconcile A with B 的结构将三个核心概念（经济发展、环境保护、社会和谐）融于一句，简洁有力地收束全文并升华主题。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 任务响应不对称：题目要求就 individuals **和** country 各谈利弊，但优势段"个人＋国家"两条都写了，劣势段只写了环境与社区，个人层面的风险（医疗与救援匮乏、成本高、安全隐患）完全缺席。补一条即可补齐 TA 的缺口。
- `in many religious tribes, inappropriate behaviour arising from tourists' lack of cultural awareness may offend local people` 太笼统：religious tribes 不是准确说法，也没有具体的冒犯行为。换成"进入寺庙不脱鞋、未经允许拍摄仪式"这类可感知的细节，例子才有分量。
- 优势段第二句从 `From an individual perspective` 一路串到 `mental well-being`，一句塞进四层因果；国家层面的 `in pursuit of booming tourism to unexplored natural destinations, tourism development creates jobs` 又让 tourism 一句内出现两次且状语悬垂。两处都建议断句并换词。
