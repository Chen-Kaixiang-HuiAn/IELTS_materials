> **图表类型标注**：组合图（折线图 + 饼图（年轻人口占比 + 地区分布））

**The graph and pie charts below show the percentage of population aged between 18 and 34 years old in a country and the percentage of this age group of people living in different regions from 1978 to 2011.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-10-18](./Task1_img/2025-10-18.png)

The graph illustrates how the proportion of people aged 18 to 34 changed in a particular country between 1978 and 2011, while the pie charts compare where this age group lived (in cities, towns, or rural areas) in those two years. 

As shown, the share of young adults in the total population generally declined over the period, despite a modest rise in the early years. Specifically, it accounted for just over 35% in 1978 and increased slightly to a peak of nearly 38% in 1992 before falling gradually to about 30% by the end of the period. 

In terms of residential distribution, the majority of 18–34-year-olds lived in cities throughout the period. In 1978, 55% of them resided in urban areas, and this proportion rose further to 65% in 2011, roughly twice the share of those living in towns (30%), which also recorded a notable increase of 10%. In contrast, although 25% of this age group lived in rural areas in 1978, slightly higher than the figure for towns, the proportion dropped sharply to just 5% in 2011, making rural areas the least common type of residence.

Overall, while the proportion of young adults declined consistently between 1978 and 2011, their living patterns became significantly more urbanized, reflecting a pronounced movement away from rural areas toward cities and towns.

**1. 让步+趋势描述**
> *"the share of young adults in the total population **generally declined** over the period, **despite a modest rise in the early years**"*

**技巧**：用"despite a modest rise in the early years"让步承认早期上升，但强调"generally declined"的总体趋势。"modest"一词精准描述了早期上升的幅度有限。

**2. 数据全生命周期描述**
> *"it **accounted for just over** 35% in 1978 **and increased slightly to a peak of** nearly 38% in 1992 **before falling gradually to** about 30% by the end of the period"*

**技巧**：用"accounted for→increased slightly to a peak of→before falling gradually to"三个动词短语，按时间顺序完整描述数据的起点、峰值和终点。"just over"和"nearly"等模糊表达与精确年份搭配，使数据描述既准确又自然。

**3. 倍数+对比**
> *"this proportion **rose further to** 65% in 2011, **roughly twice the share of** those living in towns (30%), **which also recorded a notable increase of** 10%"*

**技巧**：用"rose further to"描述城市进一步增长，"roughly twice the share of"用倍数关系突出城市主导地位，"which also recorded..."非限制性定语从句补充城镇的增长。一句完成"城市增长→城市vs城镇→城镇增长"的三重信息。

**4. 总结段综合句**
> *"**while** the proportion of young adults **declined consistently** between 1978 and 2011, their living patterns **became significantly more urbanized**, **reflecting a pronounced movement** away from rural areas toward cities and towns"*

**技巧**：用"while"连接两个同步趋势（比例下降+城市化），"reflecting"现在分词解释城市化趋势背后的迁移动力。总结句将两图信息交叉整合，展现了高阶分析能力。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：犯罪与法律类 · Problem-Solution

**Certain laws have little effect now because it is difficult for the police to enforce them. Why is that? How can this problem be solved?**

**某些法律如今几乎无效果，因警方难以执行。为什么如此？如何解决这一问题？**

In many countries, some laws seem to have little real-world impact because law enforcement agencies struggle to implement them effectively. This situation arises from a combination of technological challenges and a shortage of law enforcement personnel. Addressing these weaknesses requires a comprehensive approach that modernizes both legal frameworks and policing practices.

在许多国家，一些法律似乎几乎没有现实影响，因为执法机构难以有效地落实它们。这种情况源于技术挑战与执法人员短缺的共同作用。要解决这些问题，需要一种能够同时使法律框架和警务实践现代化的综合性方法。

As observed, the growing complexity of contemporary cybercrimes and the shortage of enforcement personnel have limited the effectiveness of some laws. From a technological perspective, the rapid development of social media platforms enables the swift dissemination of criminal methods across geographical boundaries, providing opportunities for cybercriminals to commit fraud and identity theft on a transnational scale. For example, several fraud rings in northern Myanmar use popular short-video sharing applications to recruit members and post deceptive content targeting greedy or unsuspecting victims, many of whom are Chinese citizens. Such crimes make it extremely challenging for Chinese police forces to identify and prosecute offenders. From the perspective of law enforcement resources, many developing countries face severe budget constraints, leaving officers without adequate training or technology to monitor and investigate cases. Under these conditions, the enforcement of less visible regulations, such as those related to environmental protection, consumer safety, or minor traffic violations, tends to be neglected.

可以看到，当代网络犯罪日益复杂，而执法人员的短缺限制了某些法律的有效性。从技术角度来看，社交媒体平台的迅速发展使犯罪手法能够跨越地理边界快速传播，为网络犯罪分子在跨国范围内实施诈骗和身份盗用提供了机会。例如，缅甸北部的一些诈骗团伙利用流行的短视频分享应用招募成员，并发布欺骗性内容以诱骗贪婪或毫无戒心的受害者，其中许多人是中国公民。这类犯罪使中国警方在识别和起诉犯罪者时面临极大困难。从执法资源的角度来看，许多发展中国家面临严重的预算限制，使警员缺乏足够的培训和技术来监控与调查案件。在这种情况下，与环境保护、消费者安全或轻微交通违规相关的、不太显性的法规往往被忽视。

To address these problems, establishing a multilateral cooperation framework and investing in institutional capacity building are essential. For transnational crimes, international cooperation through information sharing and joint investigations is indispensable. For instance, with regards to the case mentioned above, Chinese and Myanmar governments can collaborate through mechanisms that enable real-time intelligence exchange between countries. Meanwhile, cooperation with social media companies should be institutionalized, so that they are required to take legal responsibility by monitoring fraudulent content, verifying user identities, and promptly removing malicious accounts. In response to resource limitations, governments should increase funding, expand recruitment, and introduce professional development programs, particularly in automated surveillance and AI-driven data analysis, all of which would greatly enhance police efficiency. At the same time, legal systems should be continuously updated to address emerging forms of misconduct. Integrating advanced technologies and raising public awareness of common illegal practices can make law enforcement less dependent on manpower.

为了解决这些问题，建立多边合作框架和加强机构能力建设至关重要。对于跨国犯罪，国际合作——包括信息共享与联合调查——是不可或缺的。例如，在上述案例中，中缅两国政府可以通过机制实现国家间实时情报交流。同时，应将与社交媒体公司的合作制度化，使其承担法律责任，包括监控欺诈内容、核实用户身份，并及时删除恶意账户。针对资源有限，各国政府应增加资金投入、扩大招聘，并推行职业发展计划，特别是在自动化监控与人工智能驱动的数据分析等领域，这些措施都将显著提升警务效率。与此同时，法律体系应不断更新，以应对新兴的不当行为形式。通过整合先进技术并提高公众对常见违法行为的认识，可以减少执法对人力的依赖。

In conclusion, ineffective law enforcement largely stems from technological advances that outpace regulation and from resource shortages within police institutions. By enhancing international collaboration, strengthening institutional capabilities, and modernizing legal systems, governments can ensure that laws remain both enforceable and impactful.

总之，执法不力主要源于技术进步超越了监管能力，以及警察机构内部的资源短缺。通过加强国际合作、强化机构能力和使法律体系现代化，各国政府能够确保法律既可执行，又能发挥实效。

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

| 衔接类型 | 原文表达 | 功能说明 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **因果衔接** | `arises from a combination of...` | 引出原因分析 |
| **角度切换** | `From a technological perspective... From the perspective of law enforcement resources...` | 在同一分析框架内切换角度 |
| **例证衔接** | `For example...` | 用具体案例支撑论点 |
| **条件衔接** | `Under these conditions...` | 说明资源短缺导致的后果 |
| **过渡衔接** | `To address these problems...` | 从原因分析转向解决方案 |
| **分层论述** | `For transnational crimes... In response to resource limitations... At the same time...` | 清晰划分三个解决方案层面 |
| **总结衔接** | `In conclusion...` | 收束全文，重申核心观点 |

> **衔接亮点**：衔接手段功能分明——"From a technological perspective→From the perspective of law enforcement resources"在原因段内部切换角度，"For transnational crimes→In response to resource limitations→At the same time"在方案段内部分层推进。"To address these problems"实现从问题到方案的自然过渡。

**1. 多重修饰+因果说明**
> *"the rapid development of social media platforms **enables** the swift dissemination of criminal methods across geographical boundaries, **providing opportunities for** cybercriminals to commit fraud and identity theft on a transnational scale"*

**技巧**：用"enables... providing opportunities for..."构建因果链——平台发展→快速传播→提供机会。"across geographical boundaries"和"on a transnational scale"两个短语强调犯罪的跨国性质。

**2. 例证+影响说明**
> *"several fraud rings in northern Myanmar **use** popular short-video sharing applications to recruit members and post deceptive content targeting greedy or unsuspecting victims, **many of whom are** Chinese citizens. **Such crimes make it extremely challenging for** Chinese police forces to identify and prosecute offenders"*

**技巧**：用"use... to recruit... and post..."描述诈骗团伙的操作，"many of whom are..."非限制性定语从句补充受害者身份，"Such crimes make it extremely challenging for..."说明对中国警方的挑战。例证与影响紧密相连。

**3. 目的-措施复合句**
> *"cooperation with social media companies **should be institutionalized**, **so that they are required to** take legal responsibility by monitoring fraudulent content, verifying user identities, and promptly removing malicious accounts"*

**技巧**：用"should be institutionalized"提出制度建议，"so that they are required to..."目的状语从句说明具体要求。"monitoring... verifying... and promptly removing..."三个并列动名词短语详细说明合作内容。

**4. 总结段概括句**
> *"**By enhancing** international collaboration, **strengthening** institutional capabilities, and **modernizing** legal systems, governments **can ensure that** laws remain both enforceable and impactful"*

**技巧**：用三个并列动名词短语（enhancing, strengthening, modernizing）概括解决方案的三个维度，"can ensure that"引出最终结果，"both enforceable and impactful"并列两个积极属性。总结句简洁有力，覆盖了全文核心建议。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- `targeting greedy or unsuspecting victims` 中的 greedy 带有归咎受害者的意味，学术写作宜保持中立，删去或换成 `financially vulnerable`；例证里点名具体国别的写法也建议中性化为"跨境电信诈骗团伙"。
- 原因段第二条落在"环保、消费者安全、轻微交通违法等低可见度法规被忽视"，但方案段几乎只谈跨国网络犯罪，对这类日常法规如何执行没有对应措施，问题与方案不对称。
- 末句 `raising public awareness of common illegal practices` 是一个全新的解决方向，却压在段尾一笔带过；要么删掉，要么单列一句展开机制。
