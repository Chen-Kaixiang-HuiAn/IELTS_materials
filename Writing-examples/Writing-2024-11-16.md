**The graph below shows the number of enquiries received by the tourist information office in one city over a six-month period in 2011.**

![2024-11-16](./Task1_img/2024-11-16.png)

The graph illustrates changes in the volume of inquiries handled by a tourist information office in a particular city through in-person visits, letters/emails, and telephone calls during the first half of 2011.

As seen, enquiries made by telephone and in person both increased considerably in general. Notably, the former started at 900 in January, accounting for the highest number among the three methods in that month, and it experienced slight fluctuations in the first three months before rising steadily to 1,600 in June. Meanwhile, despite the latter being by far the lowest (around 400), the figure for it grew steadily each month, surpassing that for letter or email enquiries and telephone enquiries in March and April, respectively. By June, in-person enquiries reached approximately 1,900, the highest volume recorded.

On the other hand, queries made by letter/email registered at around 750 in January and decreased gradually throughout the six months, dropping to just under 400 by May and stabilizing onwards. This method became the least preferred after March, coinciding with the rising popularity of in-person and telephone communication.

Overall, questions made in person and by telephone surged over the period, while those made by letter or email saw a steady decline. Among the three methods, in-person enquiries experienced the most rapid growth, eventually becoming the most common means of communication.

#### ① 结构分析（精简）

本题属于 **Line Graph** 类 Task 1，涉及 3 种咨询方式在 6 个月内的数量变化。

本文采用 **"总-分-总"** 三段式结构：

- **Paragraph 1 — Introduction（开头段）**

  - 明确对象：in-person visits, letters/emails, telephone calls + 时间范围（first half of 2011）

- **Paragraph 2 — Body Details（主体段：按趋势分组）**
  - **上升组**：telephone 和 in-person 的共同特征 + 各自细节

**2. 让步状语从句+主句转折**
> *"Meanwhile, **despite the latter being by far the lowest** (around 400), the figure for it grew steadily each month..."*

**技巧**：despite 引导的让步结构先强调 in-person 的起点最低（by far the lowest），主句再转折描述其稳步增长。这种"先抑后扬"的结构突出了 in-person 的增长幅度，为后文的超越描述做铺垫。

**3. 分词短语链描述连续变化**
> *"...decreased gradually throughout the six months, **dropping to** just under 400 by May **and stabilizing onwards**."*

**技巧**：用 dropping to 和 stabilizing onwards 两个现在分词短语，依次描述 letter/email 的下降终点和后续稳定状态。这种"动作1+动作2"的链式结构，在一句内完整呈现了数据的后半段变化。

**技巧**：用 coinciding with 现在分词短语解释 letter/email 下降的背景原因，将两种现象的关联性紧凑地表达在一个句子中。这种"现象+背景"的结构，既描述了数据，又揭示了趋势背后的替代逻辑。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：科技类 · Positive/Negative

**People today can shop, work, and communicate with others via the internet instead of face to face communication. Is it a positive or negative development?**

**如今，人们可以通过互联网购物、工作以及与他人交流，而无需面对面沟通。这是一种积极还是消极的发展？**

In today's digital age, the internet has transformed how we shop, work, and communicate, often replacing physical malls, offices, and face-to-face interactions. This revolution has positively reshaped our lives by offering unparalleled convenience and efficiency, though its overuse also presents significant challenges.

在当今数字时代，互联网改变了我们购物、工作和沟通的方式，取代了传统的实体商场、办公室和面对面互动。尽管这一变革通过提供前所未有的便利和效率积极重塑了我们的生活，但过度使用互联网也带来了显著的挑战。

It is widely acknowledged that the internet allows people to perform tasks like shopping, working, and communicating, which provides convenience and enhances connectivity for those who embrace the technology. To begin with, e-commerce has created a global marketplace and provides 24/7 access to domestic services, as well as a wider variety of international merchandise and services for consumers worldwide. This is particularly beneficial for individuals with busy schedules or limited mobility. Aside from that, communication technology strongly shapes how society interacts, with digital gadgets powered by the latest advancements enabling young people to connect with others through social media. Given that many young people study abroad or in remote domestic cities to pursue advanced degrees, they rely heavily on technology to maintain close contact with friends and relatives. This eliminates geographical barriers and fosters a sense of closeness and community despite physical distances, providing crucial emotional support and alleviating feelings of homesickness and loneliness.

众所周知，互联网使人们能够完成诸如购物、工作和沟通等任务，为拥抱技术的人带来了便利并增强了连接。首先，电子商务创造了一个全球市场，不仅提供全天候的本地服务，还为消费者带来了更多种类的国际商品和服务。这对于日程繁忙或行动不便的人来说尤为有利。此外，通信技术深刻地塑造了社会互动的方式。通过最新技术支持的数字设备，年轻人可以通过社交媒体与他人保持联系。考虑到许多年轻人为了追求更高的学位会选择在国外或偏远的国内城市学习，他们严重依赖技术与朋友和家人保持密切联系。这种方式消除了地理障碍，在物理距离存在的情况下依然能够营造亲密感和社区感，从而提供重要的情感支持，缓解思乡和孤独感。

Nevertheless, excessive reliance on digital devices and platforms negatively affects career advancement and interpersonal skills, particularly for individuals heavily dependent on virtual setups. Remote workers often face fewer promotion opportunities due to limited visibility with managers as physical presence fosters stronger impressions through face-to-face interactions and spontaneous conversations. In contrast, on-site employees benefit from informal networking opportunities, such as lunch meetings and office events, where meaningful relationships with influential colleagues or leaders are cultivated. Even worse, reliance on digital communication diminishes interpersonal skills because it lacks the emotional depth inherent in face-to-face interactions. Messaging platforms and social media encourage concise, simplistic exchanges, leading to reduced exposure to complex vocabulary and sentence structures. This, combined with the absence of nonverbal cues and advanced verbal interaction, hampers the development of effective communication skills. As a result, both personal and professional relationships weaken, and this demonstrates the long-term consequences of prioritizing digital interactions over in-person engagement.

尽管如此，过度依赖数字设备和平台会对职业发展和人际交往技能产生负面影响，尤其是对那些严重依赖虚拟环境的人来说尤为明显。面对面的互动和即兴对话能够留下更深刻的印象，远程工作者由于缺乏与管理者的直接接触而通常面临较少的晋升机会。相比之下，现场工作的员工受益于非正式的社交机会，比如午餐会和办公室活动，在这些场合，他们可以与有影响力的同事或领导建立有意义的关系。更糟糕的是，对数字交流的依赖削弱了人际交往能力，因为这种交流缺乏面对面互动所固有的情感深度。信息平台和社交媒体鼓励简短、简单的交流，导致人们减少接触复杂词汇和句式的机会。这种现象再加上缺乏非语言线索和高级语言互动的环境，阻碍了有效沟通能力的发展。因此，个人和职业关系都会因此而削弱，这也体现了优先数字互动而非面对面交流的长期负面影响。

In conclusion, the internet's transformative impact on shopping, communication, and work has brought immense convenience, global connectivity, and emotional support across distances. However, it also diminishes interpersonal skills and limits career advancement for some. Despite these drawbacks, the overall benefits of increased accessibility and efficiency make this a positive development.

总之，互联网在购物、沟通和工作方面的变革性影响带来了极大的便利、全球连通性和跨越距离的情感支持。然而，它也削弱了人际交往能力，并限制了部分人的职业发展。尽管存在这些缺点，互联网所带来的可及性和效率的提升使其总体上是一种积极的发展。

#### ④ 可迁移句型骨架（直接套用）（题型：Positive/Negative）

**骨架 1 — 积极影响**
> One positive effect of ______ is ______, which contributes to ______.

**骨架 2 — 消极影响**
> On the negative side, ______ could lead to ______.

**骨架 3 — 总体判断**
> Weighing both, the development is more ______ than ______.

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

| 衔接类型 | 原文表达 | 功能说明 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **让步转折** | `though its overuse also presents significant challenges` | 在开头段末尾引入反面视角，为后文讨论做铺垫 |
| **分类展开** | `To begin with... Aside from that...` | 在 Body 1 内部分论点1和论点2，层次清晰 |
| **强烈转折** | `Nevertheless...` | 从积极影响转向消极影响，标志论证方向的重大转换 |
| **递进深化** | `Even worse...` | 在 Body 2 内部从职业影响推进到人际技能影响，论证层次加深 |

> **衔接亮点**：作者的衔接手段功能明确且层次丰富。"To begin with" 和 "Aside from that" 实现了同一方向内的论点展开，"Nevertheless" 实现了方向转换，"Even worse" 实现了同方向内的递进。这种"展开-转换-递进"的衔接层次，使文章结构严谨且富有变化。

| 词汇/短语 | 中文含义 | 词性/用法 | 替代普通表达 |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **transformed** | 彻底改变；重塑 | 动词（过去分词） | changed |
| **unparalleled** | 无与伦比的 | 形容词 | unmatched / unequaled |
| **enhances** | 提升；增强 | 动词 | improves / increases |
| **eliminates** | 消除；排除 | 动词 | removes / gets rid of |
| **fosters** | 培育；促进 | 动词 | encourages / promotes |
| **diminishes** | 削弱；减少 | 动词 | reduces / weakens |
| **hamper** | 阻碍；妨碍 | 动词 | hinder / obstruct |

**1. 定语从句+分词短语**
> *"...communication technology strongly shapes how society interacts, **with digital gadgets powered by the latest advancements enabling** young people to connect with others through social media."*

**技巧**：用 with + 名词 + 过去分词 + 现在分词 的独立主格结构，描述通信技术的具体实现方式。这种结构将技术背景（powered by the latest advancements）和行为结果（enabling young people to connect）紧凑地结合，信息密度高。

**2. 原因状语从句+主句推导**
> *"**Given that** many young people study abroad or in remote domestic cities to pursue advanced degrees, they rely heavily on technology to maintain close contact with friends and relatives."*

**技巧**：用 Given that 引导原因状语从句，说明留学生依赖技术的原因。这种结构比 Because 更正式，适合学术写作。从句中 "study abroad or in remote domestic cities" 的并列结构，涵盖了留学的两种主要形式，论证全面。

**3. 对比结构+结果推导**
> *"**In contrast, on-site employees benefit from** informal networking opportunities, such as lunch meetings and office events, **where** meaningful relationships with influential colleagues or leaders are cultivated."*

**技巧**：用 In contrast 明确标示对比关系，where 引导的定语从句补充说明非正式社交场合的作用。这种"对比+具体场景+结果"的结构，使论证从抽象观点推进到具体证据，增强了说服力。

**4. 因果链式推导**
> *"This, **combined with** the absence of nonverbal cues and advanced verbal interaction, **hampers** the development of effective communication skills. **As a result**, both personal and professional relationships weaken..."*

**技巧**：用 combined with 补充并列原因，hamper 描述核心影响，As a result 推导最终结果。这种"多因→核心影响→最终结果"的链式结构，使因果逻辑清晰且层次分明。特别是 hamper 一词，准确描述了阻碍而非完全阻止的微妙含义。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 题目三要素是 shop / work / communicate，Body 1 主题句也照抄了这三项，但正文只展开了购物与沟通，**work 的积极面（远程办公省通勤、弹性时间）完全缺位**；而 Body 2 却大篇幅论述远程工作的弊端，正反两侧覆盖面不对称。
- 结论判定为 positive，但 Body 2 给出的两条弊端（晋升受阻、沟通能力退化）分量并不轻，仅用 `Despite these drawbacks` 一带而过，缺少"为什么利大于弊"的权衡理由；补一句判断依据（如受益人群更广、弊端可通过混合办公缓解）会更有说服力。
- Body 1 内部失衡：电商论点只有两句，留学生一例却铺了四句；把留学生段压缩一句、给电商补一个具体场景，段落节奏更均匀。
- `Even worse` 语域偏口语且带情绪，学术议论文中改为 `More seriously` / `A further concern is that` 更合适。
