**The graphs below give information about the performance of a train company in October and November of 2008.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-07-26](./Task1_img/2025-07-26.png)

The two graphs illustrate changes in the percentage of trains that were late and that were cancelled over a seven-week period from early October to late November in 2008. 

As shown, the train delay rate fluctuated significantly during the given timeframe. In the first week of October, approximately 20% of trains ran late. That figure rose slightly in the second week, reaching around 30%, before gradually dropping to the lowest point of about 5% in week 4. However, it increased again in November, with a dramatic spike to 55% in the second week, but saw a decline to 40% in the third week. 

By contrast, the percentage of train cancellations experienced a pattern of sharp upturns and downturns. Starting at about 1% in the first week of October, it surged to 7% in week 2, then fell back to the initial level in week 3. Although the rate tripled to 3% in week 4, it dropped again to 1% in the first week of November. Over the next two weeks, the cancellation rate climbed notably, peaking at just under 5% in the second week, but decreased slightly to 4% in the final week.

Overall, train delays worsened markedly in November, especially during the second week, while cancellations peaked in mid-October and remained relatively moderate for the remainder of the period.

**1. 时间序列+变化描述**
> *"In the first week of October, approximately 20% of trains ran late. **That figure rose slightly** in the second week, **reaching** around 30%, **before gradually dropping** to the lowest point of about 5% in week 4"*

**技巧**：用 "That figure" 回指前文数据，避免重复；用 "reaching" 作结果状语，"before gradually dropping" 作时间状语。一句完成 "数据→微升→结果→持续下降" 的完整叙述。

**2. 转折+剧烈变化**
> *"**However**, it increased again in November, **with a dramatic spike to** 55% in the second week, **but saw a decline to** 40% in the third week"*

**技巧**：用 "However" 标志趋势反转，"with a dramatic spike to" 描述剧烈变化，"but saw a decline to" 再次转折。三个衔接成分（However/with/but）使一句之内完成 "反转→峰值→回落" 的复杂变化。

**3. 分词短语+时间推进**
> *"**Starting at about 1%** in the first week of October, it **surged to** 7% in week 2, **then fell back to** the initial level in week 3"*

**技巧**：用 "Starting at" 分词短语引出起点，再用 "surged to... then fell back to..." 描述变化过程。分词短语作状语使句式更加紧凑，避免了多个简单句的堆砌。

**4. 总结段对比结构**
> *"train delays **worsened markedly** in November, especially during the second week, **while** cancellations **peaked in mid-October** and **remained relatively moderate** for the remainder of the period"*

**技巧**：用 "while" 连接两图的不同模式，"especially" 强调关键时间点，"remained relatively moderate" 描述总体状态。总结句对比鲜明，覆盖了两图的核心特征。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：社会与政府类 · Advantages/Disadvantages

**In some countries, people's lifestyles are changing rapidly, and this affects family relationships. Do the advantages of this trend outweigh the disadvantages?**

**在一些国家，人们的生活方式正在迅速发生变化，而这种变化正对家庭关系产生影响。这个趋势的利大于弊吗？**

In recent years, many countries have witnessed rapid lifestyle changes, and these changes have inevitably impacted family connections. While such developments offer notable benefits, I believe the disadvantages related to overall social well-being and the erosion of family bonds ultimately outweigh those advantages.

近年来，许多国家见证了生活方式的快速变迁，而这些变化无可避免地影响到了家庭联系。尽管这一趋势带来了一些显著的好处，但我认为，其对社会整体福祉及家庭纽带的侵蚀所造成的弊端最终超过了这些好处。

Admittedly, evolving lifestyles bring certain benefits such as a more colorful life and the elimination of traditional boundaries. Scientific breakthroughs in frontier areas have enabled people to enjoy more free time in this digital age by releasing them from the drudgery of housework and from the confines of conventional domestic roles. Under these circumstances, the younger generation can participate in recreational activities to relieve stress or spend time with family. In addition to that, lifestyle transformations directly affect family relationships, redefining how individuals connect and support each other. Nowadays, modern communication technology provides high-quality remote communication by enabling instant global connectivity, which also encourages migration by reducing geographical barriers and enhancing the ability to maintain family ties. For example, as more young people study abroad to pursue advanced degrees or live independently, social media and portable devices help reinforce human connections by making long-distance communication more accessible, especially during moments of loneliness.

诚然，生活方式的演变在丰富人们生活和打破传统界限方面确实带来了一些益处。前沿领域的科技突破使人们摆脱了家务劳动和数字时代的繁琐任务，从而拥有了更多的自由时间。在这种情况下，年轻一代可以参与娱乐活动以缓解压力，或者与家人共度时光，而不再被局限于传统的家庭角色。此外，生活方式的转变也直接影响着家庭关系，重新定义了个体之间的联结方式和彼此支持的方式。当今，现代通讯技术通过实现全球即时互联，提供了高质量的远程沟通体验，这也通过减少地理障碍，增强了维系家庭联系的能力，从而鼓励人们迁移。例如，越来越多的年轻人选择出国深造或独立生活，而社交媒体和便携设备则在孤独时刻让人们能够更轻松地进行远程交流，从而增强了人际联系。

Nevertheless, the above-mentioned advantages are often overshadowed by the broader negative consequences of rapidly changing lifestyles borne by society. As lifestyle changes continue to reshape personal priorities and digital literacy becomes more increasingly crucial to personal employability than before, young people are compelled to attend occupational training schools on weekends to meet the demanding admission requirements of industry giants. An excessive number of youths competing for limited career advancement opportunities intensifies the problem of meaningless involution, especially in Northeast Asian countries, leading many to immerse themselves in overwork, often with little meaningful progress, while quality time with loved ones is sacrificed. Worse still, traditional family structures are gradually weakening due to a lack of intergenerational support and a diminished sense of responsibility for older generations. These shifts have led to increased feelings of isolation, particularly among the elderly, and a decline in the emotional resilience that once stemmed from close-knit family systems.

然而，上述优势往往被快速变化的生活方式在社会层面带来的更广泛负面影响所掩盖。随着生活方式的变化不断重塑个人的优先事项，数字素养比以往任何时候都更关键于个人的就业竞争力，年轻人不得不在周末参加职业培训，以满足大型企业苛刻的录取标准。在求职激烈竞争的背景下，尤其是在东北亚国家，越来越多的年轻人陷入了“内卷”的泥潭，在几乎没有实际进展的情况下长期过度劳累，而与亲人共度的高质量时光则因此被牺牲。更糟糕的是，由于代际支持的缺失和对长辈责任感的减弱，传统家庭结构正在逐步瓦解。这些转变导致了老年人孤独感的加剧，以及源自紧密家庭关系的情感韧性的衰退。

In conclusion, while changed modern lifestyles offer personal freedom and enhanced connectivity, they also lead to excessive competition, weakened family structures, and emotional isolation. These adverse effects on social cohesion and intergenerational support ultimately outweigh the benefits, making it essential to strike a balance between embracing progress and preserving meaningful family bonds.

总之，尽管现代生活方式的变化为人们带来了更大的自由和更强的联通能力，但它们也导致了过度竞争、家庭结构弱化以及情感孤立。这些对社会凝聚力和代际支持的负面影响最终超过了所带来的好处，因此，在拥抱进步的同时，寻求与维系有意义家庭关系之间的平衡显得尤为重要。

**骨架 1 — 优势起手**
> The main advantage of ______ is that it enables ______.

**骨架 2 — 劣势对照**
> However, a notable disadvantage is ______, which may ______.

**骨架 3 — 平衡立场**
> On balance, the benefits outweigh the drawbacks provided that ______.

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

| 衔接类型 | 原文表达 | 功能说明 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **让步引入** | `Admittedly...` | 承认优点存在 |
| **因果衔接** | `by releasing them from... Under these circumstances...` | 从原因推导到结果 |
| **递进补充** | `In addition to that...` | 在同一立场内添加新论点 |
| **转折反驳** | `Nevertheless...` | 从优点转向缺点 |
| **程度升级** | `Worse still...` | 在缺点论证中升级严重性 |
| **总结衔接** | `In conclusion...` | 收束全文，重申立场 |

> **衔接亮点**：衔接手段层次分明——"Admittedly→In addition to that"在优点段内部推进，"Nevertheless→Worse still"在缺点段内部升级。"Worse still"的使用尤为精妙，将论证从"个人竞争"推进到"家庭结构瓦解"，形成层层递进的批判力度。

| 词汇/短语 | 中文含义 | 词性/用法 | 替代普通表达 |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **witnessed** | 经历；见证 | 动词，见证 | seen |
| **inevitably impacted** | 不可避免地冲击（影响） | 动词短语，不可避免地影响 | certainly affected |
| **evolving lifestyles** | 不断演变的生活方式 | 名词短语，演变中的生活方式 | changing lifestyles |
| **drudgery** | 单调乏味的苦活 | 名词，苦差事 | hard work |
| **confines of conventional domestic roles** | 传统家庭角色的桎梏 | 名词短语，传统家庭角色的束缚 | limits of traditional roles |
| **redefining** | 重新定义 | 动词，重新定义 | changing |
| **overshadowed by** | 被……盖过；相形见绌 | 动词短语，被...掩盖 | hidden by |
| **involution** | 内卷（此为中式借用义） | 名词，内卷 | excessive competition |
| **immerse themselves in overwork** | 让自己深陷超负荷工作 | 动词短语，沉浸于过度工作 | work too much |
| **close-knit family systems** | 关系紧密的家庭体系 | 名词短语，紧密的家庭体系 | tight family relationships |

**1. 方式状语+结果推导**
> *"Scientific breakthroughs in frontier areas have enabled people to enjoy more free time in this digital age **by releasing them from the drudgery of housework and from the confines of conventional domestic roles**"*

**技巧**：用 "by releasing them from... and from..." 并列两个方式状语，说明科技突破如何带来自由时间。"drudgery" 和 "confines" 两个词的选用使论述更具批判性和学术性。

**2. 多重目的状语**
> *"modern communication technology provides high-quality remote communication **by enabling** instant global connectivity, **which also encourages** migration **by reducing** geographical barriers and **enhancing** the ability to maintain family ties"*

**技巧**：用 "by enabling... which also encourages... by reducing... and enhancing..." 构建多重目的和结果层次。一句完成 "技术→功能→行为→效果" 的完整推导，信息密度极高。

**3. 结果连锁句**
> *"An excessive number of youths competing for limited career advancement opportunities intensifies the problem of meaningless involution, especially in Northeast Asian countries, **leading many to immerse themselves in overwork**, often with little meaningful progress, **while** quality time with loved ones is sacrificed"*

**技巧**：用 "leading many to..." 引出直接结果，再用 "while" 并置另一个被牺牲的结果（与家人共处时间）。"especially in Northeast Asian countries" 作为插入语提供了地域聚焦，使抽象论述具象化。

**4. 因果总结句**
> *"These shifts have led to increased feelings of isolation, particularly among the elderly, and a decline in the emotional resilience **that once stemmed from close-knit family systems**"*

**技巧**：用 "These shifts" 回指前文的家庭结构变化，"particularly among the elderly" 强调最受影响群体，再用 "that once stemmed from..." 定语从句追溯情感韧性的来源。这种"现状→受影响群体→历史对比"的句式使结尾极具深度。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 第三段 `digital literacy becomes more increasingly crucial` 出现双重比较级（more + increasingly），语法有误，删掉 `more` 即可。
- 同段 `meaningless involution` 中的 `involution` 是中式英语（英语里意为"退化、卷曲"，无"内卷"义），而⑤词汇表还把它当作亮点收录；建议改为 `a self-defeating rat race` 或 `zero-sum competition`。
- 优点段的第一个论点（科技把人从家务中解放出来→有更多闲暇）几乎没有落回"家庭关系"，而这正是本题的限定焦点；应直接接上"因此有更多亲子、夫妻相处时间"，否则前半段有偏题风险。
