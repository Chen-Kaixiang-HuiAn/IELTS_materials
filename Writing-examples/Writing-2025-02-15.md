> **图表类型标注**：组合图（多扇形图（英国音乐产业分部门））

**The charts below give information about the UK music industry by sector, in three years 2013, 2014 and 2015. Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-02-15](./Task1_img/2025-02-15.png)

The bar charts illustrate changes in the total income from live music, music publishing, and recorded music, as well as the number of workers employed in those sectors in the UK from 2013 to 2015. 

As seen, the income generated from the three music sectors followed different trends. Live music consistently generated the highest income, which rose steadily from approximately£240 million in 2013 to around £270 million in 2015. In contrast, the net earnings from recorded music experienced a significant decline, with income dropping from £170 million in 2013 to about half that of live music by 2015. Music publishing, bringing in the second highest revenue initially, saw a decrease in its profits from £180 million in 2013 to £160 million in 2014 before recovering to approximately £185 million in 2015. 

Regarding employment, the number of workers live music employed was the highest each year, nearly tripling from 3,800 in 2013 to almost 9,000 by 2015. Recorded music, while employing around 3,500 workers in 2013 and 2,250 in 2014, witnessed its workforce drop to the lowest level at just 1,900 in 2015. Meanwhile, despite its income fluctuations, music publishing maintained a relatively stable labor force at approximately 2,200.

Overall, live music consistently led in both income and employment, while recorded music declined moderately in both areas. Music publishing, despite its fluctuating revenue, showed a steady increase in its workforce over the period.

**1. 分词伴随句**
> *"Live music consistently generated the highest income, **which rose steadily** from approximately £240 million in 2013 to around £270 million in 2015."*

**技巧**：主句陈述排名地位，定语从句"which rose steadily"补充具体变化。这种"地位+变化"的句式结构是描述数据的经典模式，既给出了"是什么"又说明了"怎么变"。

**2. 倍数表达句**
> *"the number of workers live music employed was the highest each year, **nearly tripling** from 3,800 in 2013 to almost 9,000 by 2015."*

**技巧**：用"nearly tripling"这一现在分词短语概括变化幅度，避免了"increased by about 137%"的枯燥表达。倍数表达是雅思写作中描述大幅变化的优质策略。

**3. 让步对比句**
> *"**Meanwhile, despite its income fluctuations**, music publishing maintained a relatively stable labor force at approximately 2,200."*

**技巧**："despite + 名词短语"标注与主句趋势相反的前置条件，使读者注意到收入与就业之间的背离关系。这种句式能够体现作者对数据间复杂关系的洞察力。

**4. 综合概述句**
> *"Overall, live music consistently led in both income and employment, while recorded music declined moderately in both areas."*

**技巧**：概述段用"while"将两个部门的对比并置，"in both income and employment"和"in both areas"的平行结构强化了对比的对称性。"consistently led"和"declined moderately"的动词选择也体现了对变化幅度的精准把握。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：环境类 · Agree/Disagree

**The best way to protect the environment is to use local resources, such as food or building materials rather than transporting resources form other places. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

**保护环境的最佳方式是使用本地资源，如食物或建筑材料，而不是从其他地方运输资源。你在多大程度上同意或不同意这一观点？**

Some people argue that the most effective way to reduce environmental impact is to use local resources like food and construction materials, instead of importing them from distant regions. Although using local resources can help lower pollution from transportation, I disagree with the view that prioritizing local resources is the best approach to protecting the environment.

一些人认为，减少环境影响的最有效方法是使用本地资源，如食物和建筑材料，而不是从遥远地区进口这些资源。尽管使用本地资源可以帮助减少运输带来的污染，我不同意优先使用本地资源是保护环境的最佳方法这一观点。

Admittedly, prioritizing local resources can help reduce pollution, particularly that caused by transportation. Since sourcing goods, including but not limited to food, raw materials, and manufactured products, from nearby locations significantly decreases the need for long-distance transport. This reduction results in fewer vehicles on the road, lower fuel consumption, and ultimately, lower emissions of greenhouse gases and pollutants like carbon dioxide, nitrogen oxides, and particulate matter. In effect, air quality improves, and the overall carbon footprint of the supply chain is minimized.

诚然，优先使用本地资源可以帮助减少污染，尤其是由交通运输引起的污染。由于从附近地区采购商品，包括但不限于食物、原材料和制造产品，显著减少了长途运输的需求。这一减少导致了道路上车辆数量的减少、燃料消耗的降低，最终减少了温室气体和污染物（如二氧化碳、氮氧化物和颗粒物）的排放。实际上，空气质量得到了改善，供应链的整体碳足迹也得到了最小化。

However, while using local resources contributes to environmental protection, it is not the best or only solution. First, not all regions have the capacity to produce resources sustainably. In some areas, especially those with barren land, local agriculture may require excessive use of water, fertilizers, and pesticides to secure food supply, which leads to soil degradation, water pollution, and loss of biodiversity. In contrast, importing goods from regions with more efficient and eco-friendly production methods can have a lower overall environmental impact, despite the emissions from transportation. Second, relying solely on local resources can bring about resource depletion, particularly in areas with limited natural resources. This strain on local ecosystems may cause long-term environmental harm through deforestation, overfishing, and soil nutrient depletion, outweighing the short-term benefits of reduced pollution from transportation.

然而，虽然使用本地资源有助于环境保护，但它并不是最佳或唯一的解决方案。首先，并非所有地区都有能力可持续地生产资源。在一些地区，特别是那些土地贫瘠的地方，当地农业可能需要过度使用水、肥料和杀虫剂来确保食物供应，从而导致土壤退化、水污染和生物多样性的丧失。相比之下，从那些采用更高效、环保的生产方式的地区进口商品，尽管有运输带来的排放，但总体环境影响可能更低。其次，单纯依赖本地资源可能导致资源枯竭，尤其是在自然资源有限的地区。这种对当地生态系统的压力可能通过森林砍伐、过度捕捞和土壤营养耗竭等方式造成长期的环境伤害，超过了减少交通污染带来的短期利益。

A holistic approach that combines sustainable production methods, efficient resource management, and environmentally friendly transportation technologies is more effective in addressing the complex challenges of environmental protection. Advancements in green technology, as demonstrated by electric vehicles and renewable energy-powered transport systems, are continuously reducing the environmental impact of transportation and offer a balanced solution for protecting the environment. As these technologies become more widespread, the environmental cost of transporting goods from distant regions will decrease, so it less justifiable to prioritize local resources at the expense of other sustainable practices.

一种综合的方式，结合可持续的生产方式、高效的资源管理和环保的运输技术，更能有效地应对环境保护这一复杂挑战。绿色技术的进步，如电动汽车和可再生能源驱动的交通系统，正持续减少交通运输的环境影响，为保护环境提供了平衡的解决方案。随着这些技术的普及，运输远距离商品的环境成本将减少，因此，优先考虑本地资源而忽视其他可持续做法的合理性将下降。

In conclusion, while prioritizing local resources can reduce pollution from transportation, it often overlooks other critical environmental factors like production efficiency, resource availability, and ecological sustainability. Thus, effective environmental protection requires a comprehensive approach that is beyond merely minimizing transportation emissions.

总之，虽然优先考虑本地资源可以减少交通运输带来的污染，但它常常忽视了生产效率、资源可用性和生态可持续性等其他重要的环境因素。因此，有效的环境保护需要采取一种全面的方式，远远不止于减少交通排放。

### 精析

#### ① 结构分析（精简）

本题属于 **Agree/Disagree** 类题型。此类题型的核心策略是：先承认对方观点的部分合理性，再通过多维度反驳论证自己的立场，最后提出替代方案。

本文采用 **"让步-反驳-替代方案-综合" 四段式** 结构：

- **Paragraph 1 — Introduction（开头段）**
  - 改写题目（paraphrase）：`Some people argue that the most effective way to reduce environmental impact is to use local resources like food and construction materials, instead of importing them from distant regions`（替换原题 `The best way to protect the environment is to use local resources`）
  - 表明立场：`I disagree with the view that prioritizing local resources is the best approach to protecting the environment`（明确不同意）
  - 预告框架：先承认本地资源减少运输污染，再论证其局限性，最后提出综合方案

- **Paragraph 2 — Body 1（让步段）**
  - 主题句：`Admittedly, prioritizing local resources can help reduce pollution, particularly that caused by transportation`
  - 论点：本地采购减少长途运输→车辆减少→燃料消耗降低→温室气体排放减少
  - 效果：承认本地资源在运输污染方面的实际价值

- **Paragraph 3 — Body 2（反驳段）**
  - 主题句：`However, while using local resources contributes to environmental protection, it is not the best or only solution`
  - 论点1：并非所有地区都能可持续生产（贫瘠土地需要过度用水、化肥、农药）
  - 论点2：单纯依赖本地资源导致资源枯竭（森林砍伐、过度捕捞、土壤营养耗竭）
  - 效果：从生产端和资源端两个维度反驳

- **Paragraph 4 — Body 3（替代方案段）**
  - 主题句：`A holistic approach that combines sustainable production methods, efficient resource management, and environmentally friendly transportation technologies is more effective`
  - 论点：绿色技术进步（电动汽车、可再生能源交通）正在降低运输环境影响
  - 效果：提出比"本地优先"更优的替代方案

- **Paragraph 5 — Conclusion（结尾段）**
  - 重申立场：本地资源减少运输污染但忽视其他因素
  - 总结升华：需要超越减少交通排放的综合方案

> **结构亮点**：本文采用了"让步→反驳→替代方案"的三段式主体论证，比传统的"让步→反驳"结构更加完整。特别是第三主体段提出"holistic approach"，不仅否定了原观点，还提供了建设性的替代方案，体现了批判性思维的最高境界——不是简单否定，而是提出更好的选择。

- **CC**：本文在让步段中使用了"Since... This reduction results in... ultimately..."的因果链，将"本地采购"到"排放减少"的逻辑推导得非常严密。这种"因为→所以→最终"的链条式衔接使让步论证本身就显得非常扎实，为后文的反驳奠定了可信的基础。
- **LR**：见模块⑤词汇表，含 `prioritizing`、`greenhouse gases` 等精准词
- **GRA**：见模块⑤句式亮点

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

| 衔接类型 | 原文表达 | 功能说明 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **让步衔接** | `Admittedly... Although...` | 先承认对方合理性 |
| **转折反驳** | `However... In contrast...` | 转向反驳论证 |
| **因果推导** | `This reduction results in... In effect...` | 逐层推导环境效果 |
| **递进扩展** | `First... Second...` | 在反驳段内分层论证 |

> **衔接亮点**：本文在让步段中使用了"Since... This reduction results in... ultimately..."的因果链，将"本地采购"到"排放减少"的逻辑推导得非常严密。这种"因为→所以→最终"的链条式衔接使让步论证本身就显得非常扎实，为后文的反驳奠定了可信的基础。

| 词汇/短语 | 中文含义 | 词性/用法 | 替代普通表达 |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **prioritizing** | 优先考虑；把…放在首位 | 动词 | putting first |
| **greenhouse gases** | 温室气体 | 名词短语 | harmful gases |
| **carbon footprint** | 碳足迹 | 名词短语 | environmental impact |
| **barren land** | 贫瘠土地 | 名词短语 | poor land |
| **soil degradation** | 土壤退化 | 名词短语 | soil damage |
| **holistic approach** | 整体性（综合）方案 | 名词短语 | complete solution |

**1. 因果链式长句**
> *"Since sourcing goods, including but not limited to food, raw materials, and manufactured products, from nearby locations significantly decreases the need for long-distance transport. This reduction results in fewer vehicles on the road, lower fuel consumption, and ultimately, lower emissions of greenhouse gases and pollutants like carbon dioxide, nitrogen oxides, and particulate matter."*

**技巧**：第一句用"Since"引导原因状语从句，"including but not limited to"列举具体商品类型；第二句用"This reduction results in..."推导三层后果。这种"原因→结果1→结果2→最终结果"的链条式写法使论证逻辑严密、层次分明。

**2. 条件+后果复合句**
> *"**In some areas, especially those with barren land**, local agriculture may require excessive use of water, fertilizers, and pesticides to secure food supply, **which leads to** soil degradation, water pollution, and loss of biodiversity."*

**技巧**：插入语"especially those with barren land"精确限定适用范围，避免了论证的绝对化。"which leads to"引导的非限制性定语从句将过度生产的三个后果并列呈现，使论证既有针对性又有广度。

**3. 比较对比句**
> *"**In contrast**, importing goods from regions with more efficient and eco-friendly production methods can have a lower overall environmental impact, **despite the emissions from transportation**."*

**技巧**："In contrast"标记对比关系，"despite"引导的让步状语承认运输排放的存在，但主句强调"overall environmental impact"可能更低。这种"承认局部劣势但强调整体优势"的句式是反驳论证的优质策略。

**4. 综合总结句**
> *"Thus, effective environmental protection requires a comprehensive approach that is **beyond merely minimizing transportation emissions**."*

**技巧**：结尾句用"Thus"总结全文，"beyond merely minimizing transportation emissions"用介词短语精准概括了文章的核心论点——环境保护不能仅仅停留在减少交通排放这一单一维度。"comprehensive approach"与开头的"best way"形成呼应，完成了从否定到建构的论证闭环。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 让步段的 "Since sourcing goods... significantly decreases the need for long-distance transport." 是一个只有从句、没有主句的残句，应与后一句合并（如 Since..., there are fewer vehicles...）或直接删去 Since。
- 第 4 段结尾 "so it less justifiable to prioritize local resources" 漏了系动词（应为 it is less justifiable），位置又在段末，较为显眼。
- 让步段通篇是"减少运输→少烧油→少排放"的抽象推演，没有任何行业或场景实例，而反驳段却有 barren land、deforestation、overfishing 等具体情形，两段的实证密度不对称。

**仿写任务**：用「先退后进」骨架重写：*Some think space exploration is a waste of money.*
