## Task 1

> **图表类型标注**：柱状图（多目的出行方式占比）

**The chart below shows the percentage of people choosing different ways of different purposes in the UK in 2013.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-11-15](./Task1_img/2025-11-15.png)

The bar chart illustrates the proportions of people in the UK who walked, utilized buses, trains, cars, and other means for education, work, leisure, and shopping in 2013.

As seen, walking, cars, and other methods exhibited comparable levels of usages for both education and shopping: around 25% walked and 30% used cars, while approximately 10% relied on other means for those two purposes. However, the pattern diverged for work and leisure, as car utilization peaked at about 40% for commuting, far exceeding the figures for walking and other forms, which accounted for roughly 5% each. In contrast, leisure activities saw the highest proportion of walking (35%), while the rates of those riding cars and other transport remained noticeably lower at just 10%. 

Regarding public transport, the percentage of people using buses was higher than that of those using trains, except for work, for which 25% of people rode those two modes. For education, bus usage (30%) was significantly higher than train use (5%). Their roles appeared similar in leisure and shopping: buses accounted for 25% of leisure travel, whereas trains were relied on by only 10%; for shopping, the gap narrowed, with 20% using buses and 15% travelling by train.

Overall, cars were the most widely used mode of transport for all purposes except leisure, while walking and buses were also commonly chosen. Meanwhile, trains and other modes were used by comparatively fewer people.

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：工作类 · Discuss Both Views

**Some people think that the ability to compete with others for a successful career is important. Others believe that the ability to work with others as a team is more important. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.**

**有些人认为，与他人竞争的能力对职业成功至关重要；另一些人则认为，与他人合作开展团队工作更为重要。讨论这两种观点，并给出你自己的看法。**

Some claim that competition is a decisive factor in professional growth, while others argue that teamwork is more crucial in determining professional success. While the capacity to compete can drive personal career advancement, I believe that the ability to collaborate effectively with others is ultimately more essential in today's workplace.

一些人认为，竞争是职业成长的决定性因素，而另一些人则主张，团队合作对职业成功更为关键。尽管竞争能力能够推动个人职业发展，但我认为，在当今的职场环境中，有效地与他人协作的能力最终更为重要。

Competition in modern workplaces is complicated and varied, making it a double-edged sword. Positively, constructive competition motivates individuals to refine their expertise and pursue innovation to stay ahead. In demanding fields, such as sales or professional sports, enhanced skills and newly acquired techniques enable employees to outperform their peers and secure better positions and financial rewards. By contrast, in labor markets that are already saturated with qualified applicants, those who cultivate an excessive competitive edge may push teams into irrational rivalry and meaningless involution, thereby worsening social problems. For instance, to remain employable and earn decent salaries in an era marked by a surplus of degree holders, many young people feel compelled to work or study far beyond regular hours rather than start families, which contributes to declining birth rates and intensifies ageing-population pressures.

现代职场中的竞争复杂多样，是一把双刃剑。从积极的一面来看，建设性的竞争能够激励个人提升专业技能、追求创新，以保持领先。在要求极高的领域，如销售或专业体育，增强的技能和新掌握的技术使员工能够超越同侪，从而获得更好的职位和更丰厚的报酬。相反，在已经被合格求职者高度饱和的劳动力市场中，那些过度培养竞争优势的人可能会将团队推向不理性的对立和无意义的“内卷”，从而加剧社会问题。例如，在高学历人才过剩的时代，为了保持就业竞争力并获得体面的薪资，许多年轻人不得不在正常工作或学习时间之外投入大量精力，而不是组建家庭，这也导致出生率下降并加剧人口老龄化压力。

On the other hand, modern workplaces increasingly emphasize collective achievement, elevating the value of teamwork over individual competition. This shift is driven by the reality that most contemporary projects, whether in technology, healthcare, or academia, require multidisciplinary collaboration. In supportive environments, cooperation accelerates progress toward shared goals and contributes to project success. This heightened efficiency and productivity are often recognized by employers, potentially leading to performance-related bonuses or salary increments. Beyond financial rewards, collective accomplishment enhances job satisfaction. Shared laughter, mutual support, and positive interactions create a more enjoyable and less stressful workplace, fostering a nurturing atmosphere where collaboration often facilitates conflict resolution. As professional networks expand and trust deepens among colleagues, conflicts tend to be approached more constructively, and this process strengthens group cohesion.

另一方面，现代职场越来越强调集体成就，使团队合作的价值高于个人竞争。这一转变源于一个事实，即当今大多数项目——无论是科技、医疗还是学术领域——都需要多学科协作。在支持性的环境中，合作能够加速实现共同目标的进程，并推动项目取得成功。这种效率和生产力的提升往往会得到雇主的认可，从而带来绩效奖金或薪资晋升。除了经济上的回报外，集体成就还能提高工作满意度。共同的欢笑、彼此的支持与积极的互动，使工作场所更加轻松愉快、压力更小，营造出一种有利于合作并促进冲突解决的良好氛围。随着职业网络的扩大以及同事之间信任的加深，人们往往会以更具建设性的方式处理矛盾，而这一过程也进一步增强了团队凝聚力。

In my view, a highly competitive employee who cannot cooperate may hinder collective productivity by creating conflict or withholding information. By contrast, individuals with strong teamwork abilities build trust, reinforce cohesion, and create supportive conditions for peak collective performance.

在我看来，一个高度好胜却缺乏合作能力的员工，可能会因制造冲突或隐瞒信息而阻碍集体生产力。相比之下，具备强大团队合作能力的个人能够建立信任、强化凝聚力，并为团队实现最佳表现创造有利条件。

In conclusion, while competition can motivate individuals to excel, it may also generate unnecessary pressure and harmful rivalry. Teamwork, however, promotes multidisciplinary cooperation, boosts efficiency, enhances job satisfaction, and strengthens workplace relationships. Therefore, collaborative ability is ultimately more essential for sustained career success in modern professional environments.

总之，尽管竞争能够激励个人追求卓越，但它也可能带来不必要的压力和有害的对立。而团队合作则促进多学科协作、提升效率、增强工作满意度，并强化职场关系。因此，在现代职场环境中，协作能力最终对持续的职业成功更为重要。

### 精析

#### ① 结构分析（精简）

本题属于 **Discuss Both Views** 类题型。此类题型的核心策略是：双方观点各一段分析，最后给出自己的综合立场。

本文采用 **"平衡-立场"** 四段式结构：

- **Paragraph 1 — Introduction（开头段）**

  - 表明立场：`While the capacity to compete can drive personal career advancement, I believe that the ability to collaborate effectively with others is ultimately more essential`（竞争有价值，但协作更重要）
  - 预告框架：先讨论竞争的双刃剑效应，再论证团队合作的优越性，最后给出立场

- **Paragraph 2 — Body 1（竞争论）**
  - 主题句：`Competition in modern workplaces is complicated and varied, making it a double-edged sword`
  - 论点1（积极面）：建设性竞争激励个人提升专业技能、追求创新 → 在销售或专业体育等要求极高的领域，增强的技能使员工超越同侪 → 获得更好职位和更丰厚报酬
  - 论点2（消极面）：在劳动力市场已饱和的情况下，过度竞争优势可能将团队推向不理性对立和无意义内卷 → 加剧社会问题
  - 举例：高学历人才过剩时代，年轻人为保持竞争力不得不加班 → 导致出生率下降和人口老龄化压力

- **Paragraph 4 — Conclusion（结尾段）**
  - 重申立场：`collaborative ability is ultimately more essential for sustained career success in modern professional environments`
  - 总结升华：团队合作促进多学科协作、提升效率、增强工作满意度、强化职场关系

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

| 衔接类型 | 原文表达 | 功能说明 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **观点引入** | `On the one hand... On the other hand...` | 清晰划分双方立场 |

| **例证衔接** | `For instance...` | 用具体案例支撑论点 |

| **递进补充** | `Beyond financial rewards...` | 在团队合作论中超越经济层面 |
| **总结衔接** | `In conclusion...` | 收束全文，重申立场 |

**1. 双面论证句**
> *"**Positively**, constructive competition motivates individuals to refine their expertise and pursue innovation to stay ahead. **By contrast**, in labor markets that are already saturated with qualified applicants, those who cultivate an excessive competitive edge may push teams into irrational rivalry and meaningless involution, **thereby worsening** social problems"*

**技巧**：用"Positively"引出积极面，"By contrast"转折引出消极面，"thereby worsening"现在分词短语说明最终后果。"double-edged sword"的框架使论证更加 balanced。

**2. 因果链式长句**
> *"**As a result of** unsustainable buying habits, millions of electronic devices... **end up in** landfills each year, **which not only depletes** natural resources **but also damages** ecosystems"*

**技巧**：用"As a result of"引导原因，"end up in"描述结果，"which not only... but also..."定语从句并列两个后果。因果链完整而清晰。

**3. 递进+氛围描述**
> *"**Beyond financial rewards**, collective accomplishment enhances job satisfaction. Shared laughter, mutual support, and positive interactions create a more enjoyable and less stressful workplace, **fostering a nurturing atmosphere where** collaboration often facilitates conflict resolution"*

**技巧**：用"Beyond financial rewards"超越经济层面，"Shared laughter, mutual support, and positive interactions"三个并列名词短语营造氛围，"fostering a nurturing atmosphere where..."现在分词短语说明氛围的作用。"nurturing atmosphere"的表述极具感染力。

**4. 总结段综合句**
> *"**Teamwork, however, promotes** multidisciplinary cooperation, **boosts** efficiency, **enhances** job satisfaction, and **strengthens** workplace relationships"*

**技巧**：用四个并列动词（promotes, boosts, enhances, strengthens）概括团队合作的多重价值，从"协作"到"效率"到"满意度"到"关系"层层递进。总结句简洁有力，覆盖了全文核心论点。
