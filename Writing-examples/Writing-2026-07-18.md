## Task 1

> **图表类型标注**：折线图（中国公路/海运/铁路货运周转量 2001–2015，单位 mtk）

**The graph shows the difference of methods in china to transport goods between 2001 and 2015.**
**Summise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2026-07-18](./Task1_img/2026-07-18.png)


The graph illustrates changes in freight turnover in China by road, sea and rail from 2001 to 2015, measured in million tonne-kilometres (mtk). 

As shown, sharp rises were seen in both road and sea freight turnover, despite some fluctuations after both peaked in 2011. In detail, road and sea transport stood at almost the same level in 2001, at around 0.5 mtk. Both figures then rose to about 3 mtk in 2009, before reaching approximately 5.3 mtk and 4.6 mtk, respectively, in 2011. They then fell to around 4.5 mtk and 4.1 mtk in 2013, before recovering slightly to 5 mtk and 4.4 mtk, respectively, by the end of the period.

In contrast, rail showed a slower overall increase, with its freight turnover rising from 1.2 mtk in 2001, more than double the figures for road and sea, to around 3 mtk in 2011. It then stabilised at this level until 2013. However, its freight turnover subsequently fell to just 2.4 mtk in 2015, moving in the opposite direction to road and sea in the final period.

Overall, all three modes recorded growth, but road became the dominant mode from 2003 onwards and remained the highest by the end of the period, while rail showed the least growth and became the lowest of the three modes in the final year.

### 精析

#### ① 结构分析（精简）

本题属于 **Line Graph（折线图）** 类 Task 1，涉及 3 种运输方式（road, sea, rail）在 2001–2015 年间的货运周转量（单位 mtk），需处理**多线 + 长时间跨度 + 趋势反转**三重难点。

本文采用 **"总-分-总"** 思路下的 4 段式结构（开头段改写题目 → 主体段 1（走势相近的公路与海运，段首以 As shown 领起）→ 主体段 2（走势相异的铁路）→ 收尾概述段（Overall））：

- **Paragraph 1 — Introduction（开头段）**
  - 改写题目要素：`The graph illustrates changes in freight turnover in China by road, sea and rail from 2001 to 2015, measured in million tonne-kilometres (mtk)`（替换原题 `The graph shows the difference of methods in china to transport goods between 2001 and 2015`），并补出原题未给的计量单位

- **Paragraph 2 — Body 1（主体段：走势相近的公路与海运）**
  - 段首话题句：`As shown, sharp rises were seen in both road and sea freight turnover, despite some fluctuations after both peaked in 2011`（先给主趋势 + 让步承认波动，是主体段 1 的话题句，并非独立总览段）
  - 起点合写：`road and sea transport stood at almost the same level in 2001, at around 0.5 mtk`
  - 中段与峰值：2009 年双双升至约 3 mtk，2011 年分别达约 5.3 与 4.6 mtk
  - 回落与回升：2013 年跌至约 4.5 与 4.1 mtk，期末小幅回升至 5 与 4.4 mtk

- **Paragraph 3 — Body 2（主体段：走势相异的铁路）**
  - 对比起手：`In contrast, rail showed a slower overall increase`
  - 起点优势：2001 年 1.2 mtk，`more than double the figures for road and sea`
  - 平台期与逆转：2011 年约 3 mtk → 2013 年前维持该水平 → 2015 年降至仅 2.4 mtk，`moving in the opposite direction to road and sea in the final period`

- **Paragraph 4 — Overview（收尾概述段）**
  - 三种方式均实现增长
  - 公路自 2003 年起成为主导并在期末保持最高；铁路增幅最小且在末年成为三者中最低

> **结构亮点**：三线折线图最难处理的是"哪几条线该合写、哪条该单写"，本文的分组依据不是运输方式的属性，而是**走势的相似度**——公路与海运起点几乎重合（均约 0.5 mtk）、同年见顶（2011）、同步回落又同步回升，因此被压进同一段用 `Both figures` / `They` 平行处理；铁路则起点最高（1.2 mtk）、增速最慢、末段独自下行，单独成段并以 `In contrast` 领起。这一分组让"两条线并行、一条线背离"的图形特征直接由段落划分表达出来。主体段 1 以 `As shown` 起手给出主趋势，收尾段（P4）以 `Overall` 给出全局排名结论（公路主导、铁路垫底），前后呼应、不重复。

#### ② 数据组织与对比策略（标注有效写法）

| 写法 | 原文示例 | 技巧说明 |
|------|---------|---------|
| **同起点合并处理** | `road and sea transport stood at almost the same level in 2001, at around 0.5 mtk` | 两线起点接近时用 almost the same level 合写，省下一整句，也顺势说明为何二者可放同一段 |
| **双线并行+respectively** | `Both figures then rose to about 3 mtk in 2009, before reaching approximately 5.3 mtk and 4.6 mtk, respectively, in 2011` | Both 合并主语，before + V-ing 串起第二个时间点，respectively 完成一对一映射，一句写下 2 线 × 2 年共 4 个数据 |

| **反向走势收束** | `moving in the opposite direction to road and sea in the final period` | 分词短语点明趋势相反，把本段结论直接挂回上一段，完成跨段对比 |

> **数据亮点**：15 年 × 3 条线可读出的数据点极多，作者只锚定 **2001 / 2009 / 2011 / 2013 / 2015** 五个节点，且每个节点都有选取理由——起点、中途持平点、峰值、谷值、终点，正是折线图必写的五类位置。约数处理也很讲究：`at around 0.5`、`about 3`、`approximately 5.3`、`just 2.4` 四种模糊限定轮换使用，既符合读图的估值性质，又避免同一个 about 反复出现。最见功力的是 `more than double the figures for road and sea` 这一插入语——它让铁路"起点最高却终点最低"的完整故事在一句之内埋下前提，使末段"铁路增幅最小、末年最低"的结论显得顺理成章。

**1. 被动+让步起手句（主体段 1 段首）**
> *"As shown, **sharp rises were seen in** both road and sea freight turnover, **despite some fluctuations after both peaked in 2011**."*

**技巧**：被动结构 sharp rises were seen in 把"上升"这一趋势名词化并置于主语位置，句子重心落在变化本身而非"图表显示"；despite 让步先行承认后续的下跌，使"急剧上升"的总判断不会被第二段的 2013 年回落打脸——主题句与细节因此不矛盾，这是波动型折线图起手的关键。

**2. 双线并行+respectively 压缩句**
> *"**Both figures** then rose to about 3 mtk in 2009, **before reaching** approximately 5.3 mtk and 4.6 mtk, **respectively**, in 2011."*

**技巧**：Both figures 把两条线合并为一个主语；before + 动名词把 2011 年的峰值挂在同一句上，避免另起一句写"到 2011 年时"；respectively 完成"公路 5.3 / 海运 4.6"的一对一映射。一句之内交代 2 条线 × 2 个年份共 4 个数据，是多线折线图最经济的写法。

**3. 独立主格+插入同位语句（对比段）**
> *"In contrast, rail showed a slower overall increase, **with its freight turnover rising from 1.2 mtk in 2001, more than double the figures for road and sea, to around 3 mtk in 2011**."*

**技巧**：with + 名词 + 现在分词构成独立主格，把具体数据挂在"增速较慢"这一定性判断之后；更巧的是 more than double the figures for road and sea 作为同位语被**插在 from... to... 结构中间**，纵向变化（1.2 → 3）与横向对比（是公路/海运的两倍多）在同一句内同时完成，且不打断主干。

**4. 分词短语跨段收束句（对比段）**
> *"However, its freight turnover subsequently fell to **just** 2.4 mtk in 2015, **moving in the opposite direction to road and sea in the final period**."*

**技巧**：just 一词强化"仅"2.4 的低位感；句末现在分词短语不是补充信息，而是把本段数据直接接回上一段的公路/海运走势，形成"我方下行、彼方上行"的显性对照。用一个分词完成跨段对比，比另起一句 "This was different from..." 紧凑得多。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- Overview 中的 `road became the dominant mode from 2003 onwards` 首次出现 2003 这一时间点，而两个主体段从 2001 直接跳到 2009，完全没有交代公路何时、以何数值超越铁路；总结里最关键的排名转折点在正文缺少数据支撑。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：社会类 · Causes + Solutions

**Everyday activities cause stress for many people in the modern world. What are the causes of this problem, and what can be done to address the problem?**

**日常活动给现代社会中的许多人带来压力。这是什么原因造成的？可以采取什么措施来减少人们所承受的压力？**


In contemporary society, many people find ordinary tasks increasingly stressful. This is largely because intense workplace competition creates severe time pressure, while technological advancement has produced unrealistic expectations of speed and constant availability. Although this problem is widespread, it can be mitigated through workplace reform and a more responsible use of technology.

在当代社会，许多人发现普通的日常事务正变得越来越有压力。这主要是因为激烈的职场竞争造成了严重的时间压力，而技术进步也带来了对速度和持续在线的不现实期待。尽管这一问题十分普遍，但可以通过工作场所改革以及更加负责任地使用技术来缓解。

As observed, intense competition and technological advancement are two major reasons why people increasingly find everyday activities stressful. In many developing economies, limited access to well-paid employment, combined with rising living costs, forces young adults to compete relentlessly for career advancement. This problem is particularly evident in some Northeast Asian societies, where long working hours and compulsory overtime remain common. Under these conditions, employees have to compress commuting, household duties and family responsibilities into the little time left outside work, meaning that even simple daily tasks can become additional sources of pressure. Alongside this intensified competition, scientific breakthroughs have substantially improved productivity, enabling individuals to complete tedious tasks and manage several responsibilities simultaneously. However, such convenience has also conditioned people to expect similar speed and efficiency in every aspect of life. The resulting culture of immediacy can make even minor delays psychologically stressful while placing people under constant pressure to remain available.

显而易见，激烈的竞争和技术进步是人们越来越觉得日常活动有压力的两个主要原因。在许多发展中经济体中，高薪工作的机会有限，再加上生活成本不断上升，迫使年轻人为了职业发展而持续竞争。这一问题在一些东北亚社会尤其明显，因为长时间工作和强制加班仍然较为普遍。在这种情况下，员工不得不把通勤、家务和家庭责任压缩到工作之外所剩无几的时间里，这意味着即使是简单的日常事务也会成为额外的压力来源。除了这种加剧的竞争之外，科学突破也显著提高了生产力，使个人能够完成繁琐任务并同时处理多项责任。然而，这种便利也使人们逐渐习惯于期待生活各个方面都具备同样的速度和效率。由此形成的即时文化会让哪怕轻微的延迟也带来心理压力，同时使人们长期处于必须随时在线的压力之下。

To address these causes, governments and employers should cooperate to reduce excessive workplace pressure and establish healthier digital boundaries. Governments could strengthen labour regulations by limiting compulsory overtime and requiring companies to provide sufficient paid leave, thereby giving employees adequate time to manage household responsibilities without constant urgency. Meanwhile, employers should evaluate staff according to the quality of their work rather than the number of hours for which they remain available. Flexible schedules and remote-working arrangements could also reduce commuting time, allowing workers to complete everyday tasks at a more manageable pace. Regarding technological pressure, companies should establish clear rules prohibiting non-urgent messages outside working hours. Employees should also disable unnecessary notifications so that they can recover mentally without fearing that a delayed response will harm their careers. These measures would gradually weaken the expectation of constant efficiency and make daily responsibilities considerably less stressful.

为了解决这些原因，政府和雇主应当合作，减少过度的职场压力，并建立更健康的数字边界。政府可以通过限制强制加班、要求企业提供充足带薪休假来加强劳动法规，从而让员工有足够时间处理家庭责任，而不必一直处于紧迫状态。与此同时，雇主应当根据员工的工作质量，而不是他们保持在线的时长来评价员工。弹性工作制和远程办公安排也可以减少通勤时间，使劳动者能够以更可控的节奏完成日常事务。针对技术带来的压力，企业应制定明确规则，禁止在非工作时间发送非紧急信息。员工也应关闭不必要的通知，这样他们就可以在心理上得到恢复，而不必担心回复延迟会损害自己的职业发展。这些措施将逐渐削弱人们对持续高效的期待，并使日常责任明显减少压力。

In conclusion, everyday activities become stressful because fierce workplace competition deprives people of personal time, and technological convenience encourages expectations of instant responses. Stronger labour protections, flexible working arrangements and clear limits on after-hours communication can restore personal control and make daily responsibilities more manageable.

总之，日常活动之所以会造成压力，是因为激烈的职场竞争剥夺了人们的个人时间，而技术便利又强化了对即时回复的期待。更有力的劳动保护、弹性工作安排以及对下班后沟通的明确限制，可以恢复个人对生活的掌控感，并使日常责任更容易应对。

### 精析

#### ① 结构分析（精简）

本题属于 **Causes + Solutions（原因 + 对策）** 类双问题型。此类题型的核心策略是：原因与对策必须**一一对应**，且每条对策要交代责任主体与作用机制，不能停留在措施名称。

本文采用 **"预告-归因-对治-汇总"** 四段式结构：

- **Paragraph 1 — Introduction（开头段）**
  - 改写题目（paraphrase）：`In contemporary society, many people find ordinary tasks increasingly stressful`（替换原题 `Everyday activities cause stress for many people in the modern world`）
  - 双原因预告：`This is largely because intense workplace competition creates severe time pressure, while technological advancement has produced unrealistic expectations of speed and constant availability`
  - 双对策预告：`it can be mitigated through workplace reform and a more responsible use of technology`——两个词组分别对应后文两条对策链

- **Paragraph 2 — Body 1（原因段）**
  - 主题句：`intense competition and technological advancement are two major reasons why people increasingly find everyday activities stressful`
  - 原因一（职场竞争）：发展中经济体高薪岗位有限 + 生活成本上升 → 年轻人 `compete relentlessly for career advancement` → 东北亚社会 `long working hours and compulsory overtime` → 通勤/家务/家庭责任被压缩进 `the little time left outside work` → 简单日常事务成为额外压力源
  - 原因二（技术）：科学突破提升生产力 → 但 `such convenience has also conditioned people to expect similar speed and efficiency in every aspect of life` → 形成 `culture of immediacy` → 轻微延迟即造成心理压力 + 必须随时在线

- **Paragraph 3 — Body 2（对策段）**
  - 过渡句：`To address these causes, governments and employers should cooperate to reduce excessive workplace pressure and establish healthier digital boundaries`
  - 对治原因一：**政府**加强劳动法规（限制强制加班 + 要求充足带薪休假）；**雇主**按工作质量而非在线时长评价员工；弹性工作与远程办公减少通勤
  - 对治原因二：`Regarding technological pressure`，**企业**禁止非工作时间的非紧急信息；**员工**关闭不必要通知
  - 效果句：`These measures would gradually weaken the expectation of constant efficiency and make daily responsibilities considerably less stressful`

- **Paragraph 4 — Conclusion（结尾段）**
  - 因果重述（换词表达）：`fierce workplace competition deprives people of personal time, and technological convenience encourages expectations of instant responses`
  - 措施汇总：`Stronger labour protections, flexible working arrangements and clear limits on after-hours communication can restore personal control`

> **结构亮点**：本文的两段主体是**严格镜像**的。开头段 `workplace reform and a more responsible use of technology` 两个词组已经把提纲写死；Body 1 按"竞争 → 技术"的顺序展开，Body 2 用 `To address these causes` 明示对应关系并沿**完全相同的顺序**给出对策，中间以 `Regarding technological pressure` 切换到第二条链，读者可以逐条对表检查。更值得学的是对策段的**主体分层**：政府（labour regulations）→ 雇主（evaluation criteria、flexible schedules）→ 企业（communication rules）→ 员工（disable notifications），四类主体各领一项职责，避免了"政府应该加强管理"式的一锅端；结论段的三项措施则分别来自这四个层级，汇总而不新增。

#### ② 论证逻辑链拆解（标注有效写法）

**Body 1（原因段）的双链条：**

```
日常事务越来越令人有压力（问题）
    ↓ [原因一：职场竞争]
→ 发展中经济体高薪岗位机会有限 + 生活成本上升
    ↓ [被迫反应]
→ 年轻人为职业发展持续竞争（compete relentlessly）
    ↓ [区域证据]
→ 东北亚社会：长工时 + 强制加班仍普遍
    ↓ [时间挤压机制]
→ 通勤 / 家务 / 家庭责任被压进工作之外所剩无几的时间
    ↓ [接回题干]
→ 即使简单日常事务也成为额外压力来源

    ↓ [原因二：技术进步]
→ 科学突破大幅提升生产力（可完成繁琐任务、同时处理多项责任）
    ↓ [心理迁移]
→ 便利使人期待生活各方面都具备同等速度与效率
    ↓ [文化成型]
→ 形成即时文化（culture of immediacy）
    ↓ [双重后果]
→ 轻微延迟即产生心理压力 + 必须随时在线的持续压力
```

**Body 2（对策段）的双链条（与上段严格对应）：**

```
两条原因需分别对治（To address these causes）
    ↓ [对治竞争 · 制度层—政府]
→ 加强劳动法规：限制强制加班 + 要求企业提供充足带薪休假
    ↓ [机制 thereby]
→ 员工获得处理家庭责任的时间，不必长期处于紧迫状态
    ↓ [对治竞争 · 管理层—雇主]
→ 以工作质量而非在线时长评价员工
    ↓
→ 弹性工作制 + 远程办公 → 减少通勤 → 以更可控节奏完成日常事务
    ↓ [对治技术压力 · 组织层—企业]
→ 明确规则：禁止非工作时间发送非紧急信息
    ↓ [对治技术压力 · 个人层—员工]
→ 关闭不必要通知 → 心理得以恢复，且不必担心延迟回复损害职业
    ↓ [汇总效果]
→ 逐渐削弱"持续高效"的社会期待 → 日常责任压力明显下降
```

> **逻辑亮点**：两条原因链都不止步于"因为竞争激烈所以有压力"这种同义反复，而是各自补出**中间机制**——竞争链的关键一步是"时间挤压"（`compress commuting, household duties and family responsibilities into the little time left outside work`），技术链的关键一步是"心理迁移"（生产力提升 → 期待迁移到生活各方面 → 形成即时文化），正因为有这两步，"日常事务变成压力源"才被真正解释。对策链同样每条都带作用机制：`thereby giving employees adequate time...`、`allowing workers to complete everyday tasks at a more manageable pace`、`so that they can recover mentally without fearing that a delayed response will harm their careers`——三处分别用 thereby / 现在分词 / so that 三种不同结构交代"为什么这条措施能起效"，这是 Solutions 段避免退化成措施清单的核心手法。

#### ③ 四项能力维度分析（TA / CC / LR / GRA）

- **TA**：双问全部作答且比重均衡——原因段两条链、对策段两条链，无"有原因无对策"或"有对策无原因"的错位。开头段 `workplace reform and a more responsible use of technology` 已把两条主线写死，Body 2 以 `To address these causes` 明示对应并沿相同顺序推进，评分要求的"回应题目全部部分"得到结构性保障。对策还按政府—雇主—企业—员工四层主体分配责任，且每条都带作用机制，具体度高于泛泛而谈；结论段用 `deprives people of personal time`、`encourages expectations of instant responses` 换词重述两条原因，未引入新论点。
- **CC**：段落入口清晰（`As observed` 进入归因、`To address these causes` 进入对策、`In conclusion` 收束），段内则用 `Alongside this intensified competition` 与 `Regarding technological pressure` 两处切换第二条链，且两段的切换位置一一对齐。回指与结果衔接同样密集：`Under these conditions` 回指长工时与加班、`such convenience` 回指生产力提升带来的便利、`The resulting culture of immediacy` 用 resulting 把上句直接转成本句主语、`These measures` 打包整段对策，衔接几乎全部由回指与逻辑词承担，不靠 firstly / secondly 这类机械序号。
- **LR**：见模块⑤词汇表，含 `compete relentlessly`、`compulsory overtime`、`culture of immediacy`、`digital boundaries` 等精准词
- **GRA**：见模块⑤句式亮点

#### ④ 可迁移句型骨架（直接套用）（题型：Causes + Solutions）

**骨架 1 — 双原因预告（Intro，本题型必备）**
> This is largely because ______ creates ______, while ______ has produced unrealistic expectations of ______.

**骨架 2 — 让步+可缓解声明（Intro 收尾，同时预告两条对策）**
> Although this problem is widespread, it can be mitigated through ______ and ______.

**骨架 3 — 对策+手段+机制三合一（Solutions 核心句）**
> Governments could strengthen ______ by limiting ______ and requiring ______, thereby giving ______ adequate ______ without ______.

**骨架 4 — 评价标准替换（切中病根式对策）**
> Employers should evaluate ______ according to ______ rather than ______.

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

| 衔接类型 | 原文表达 | 功能说明 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **背景引入** | `In contemporary society, many people find ordinary tasks increasingly stressful` | 开头段改写题目，把 everyday activities 换为 ordinary tasks |
| **原因预告** | `This is largely because intense workplace competition creates severe time pressure, while technological advancement has produced...` | because + while 一句给出两条原因，largely 限定强度 |
| **让步+可解性** | `Although this problem is widespread, it can be mitigated through workplace reform and a more responsible use of technology` | 承认普遍性的同时预告两条对策方向 |
| **归因起手** | `As observed, intense competition and technological advancement are two major reasons why...` | As observed 领起原因段并复述两条主线 |
| **例证收窄** | `This problem is particularly evident in some Northeast Asian societies, where long working hours and compulsory overtime remain common` | particularly evident in + where 从句把泛论落到具体区域 |
| **情境回指** | `Under these conditions, employees have to compress commuting, household duties and family responsibilities...` | Under these conditions 回指长工时与加班，避免复述 |
| **追加并列** | `Alongside this intensified competition, scientific breakthroughs have substantially improved productivity` | Alongside 引出第二条原因，同时回指第一条 |
| **利转弊** | `However, such convenience has also conditioned people to expect similar speed and efficiency in every aspect of life` | However 把"技术是好事"扭转为"技术生成压力" |
| **对策过渡** | `To address these causes, governments and employers should cooperate to...` | 明示对策与原因的对应关系 |
| **主体/分项切换** | `Meanwhile, employers should evaluate staff...` / `Regarding technological pressure, companies should establish clear rules...` | Meanwhile 换主体、Regarding 换对治对象 |

> **衔接亮点**：本文最值得学的是**用回指代替复述**。`Under these conditions`（回指长工时与强制加班）、`such convenience`（回指生产力提升带来的便利）、`The resulting culture of immediacy`（用 resulting 把上一句的结果直接变成本句主语）、`These measures`（打包整段对策）、`To address these causes`（打包整段原因）——五处回指分布在两个主体段的关键接缝上，使段落之间、句子之间无需任何重复即可衔接。同时 `Alongside this intensified competition` 一处兼具"回指前文 + 引出新点"双重功能，比单写 Moreover 信息量更大；全篇没有出现 firstly / secondly / last but not least 这类机械序号，层次却始终清楚。

|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **intense workplace competition** | 激烈的职场竞争 | 名词短语 | strong competition at work |
| **constant availability** | 随时在线／随时可被联系 | 名词短语 | always being reachable |

| **digital boundaries** | 数字边界 | 名词短语 | limits on the use of devices |

**1. 双原因预告句（开头段）**
> *"This is **largely because** intense workplace competition creates severe time pressure, **while** technological advancement has produced unrealistic expectations of speed and constant availability."*

**技巧**：because 从句内部用 while 并列两条原因，一句话就把全篇提纲交出去；largely 限定"主要而非唯一"，避免绝对化。Causes + Solutions 题型最忌开头段只写背景不给方向，这一句同时完成"归因预告"与"篇章导航"两件事。

**2. 情境回指+机制推导句（原因段）**
> *"**Under these conditions**, employees have to **compress** commuting, household duties and family responsibilities **into the little time left outside work**, **meaning that** even simple daily tasks can become additional sources of pressure."*

**技巧**：Under these conditions 回指上句的长工时与强制加班，省去复述；compress... into... 一个动词把"时间被挤压"这一抽象机制写得可感；句末 meaning that 分词结构直接推出与题干措辞高度贴合的结论（`even simple daily tasks... sources of pressure`），使原因论证精确落在题目问的点上。

**3. 对策+手段+机制三合一句（对策段）**
> *"Governments could strengthen labour regulations **by** limiting compulsory overtime and requiring companies to provide sufficient paid leave, **thereby giving** employees adequate time to manage household responsibilities **without constant urgency**."*

**技巧**：主句给措施方向（strengthen labour regulations），by + 动名词给具体做法（限制加班、要求带薪休假），thereby + 动名词给作用机制（员工获得时间），末尾 without constant urgency 再回扣"压力"这一核心。一句话三层递进，是 Solutions 段最实用的句型——阅卷人既看到"做什么"也看到"为什么有效"。

**4. 标准替换+目的从句句（对策段）**
> *"employers should evaluate staff **according to the quality of their work rather than the number of hours for which they remain available**." … "Employees should also disable unnecessary notifications **so that** they can recover mentally **without fearing that a delayed response will harm their careers**."*

**技巧**：前句用 according to A rather than B 直接替换评价标准，精准打击"随时在线"这一病根，比"雇主应该关心员工"具体得多；后句用 so that 交代目的、再以 without fearing that... 补出员工真正的心理顾虑（怕回复慢影响前途），说明对策为何能落地。两句一制度一个人，层级互补。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段应「推进」而非复述，可补一句实施优先级或落地难点。
- Causes 与 Solutions 必须一一对应，避免"无对策的原因"或"无原因的对策"。
- 每条对策都应交代责任主体与作用机制，避免"政府应加强管理"式空话。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 两条原因链的证据密度不对等：竞争链有 `some Northeast Asian societies, where long working hours and compulsory overtime remain common` 这样的具体区域证据，技术链却只有 `The resulting culture of immediacy` 这一抽象概括，缺少可感场景（如即时通讯的已读回执、外卖与打车的分钟级预期）。
- 对策的适用范围未加限定：`Flexible schedules and remote-working arrangements could also reduce commuting time` 对制造业、零售、医疗等必须到岗的职业并不适用，若补一句限定（如仅限知识型岗位）会更严谨；同理 `disable unnecessary notifications` 对需值班的岗位也有难度。
- 结论段基本是原样点名：`Stronger labour protections, flexible working arrangements and clear limits on after-hours communication` 三项均为 Body 2 措施的复述，未做任何推进——例如未指出立法执行的难度，也未交代若只能做一件事应优先做哪一项。

**仿写任务**：用「对策+手段+机制三合一」骨架写一句：描述『城市可通过限制私家车进入中心区来缓解交通拥堵』。
