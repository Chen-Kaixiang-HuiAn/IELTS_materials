> **图表类型标注**：表格（五类机动车占比变化）

**The table shows five types of registered motor vehicle in Australia in 2010 and 2015, with the change of percentage over these five years.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-04-19](./Task1_img/2025-04-19.png)

The table illustrates the changes in the number of registered cars, light business vehicles, motorcycles, trucks, and camper vans in Australia between 2010 and 2015.

As seen, vehicle registrations increased substantially from 3.73 million in 2010 to 5.35 million in 2015, having a total growth of 43%. Among the vehicles, cars remained the most common type of vehicle, with ownership rising by 58% (from 1.58 million to 2.5 million), significantly contributing to the overall increase in vehicle numbers. 

Meanwhile, the most dramatic percentage change was observed in light business vehicles. Although this category accounted for the smallest share in 2010 (just 150,000), their registrations tripled to 450,000 in 2015. The quantity of registered motorcycles also experienced a considerable increase from 250,000 to 350,000, witnessing a 40% increase. However, by 2015, motorcycles had accounted for the smallest registration rate. Truck registrations showed a more modest climb of 20%, from 1 million to 1.2 million. In contrast, the least significant change was seen in camper vans, which increased by just 13% (from 750,000 to 850,000).

Overall, while the registration of all vehicle categories saw growth over the five years, the rate of increase varied considerably for each. Light business vehicles saw the steepest proportional rise, whereas camper vans recorded the smallest growth.

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：社会与政府类 · Positive/Negative

**In many parts of the world, children are given more freedom than in the past. Is this a positive or negative development?**

**在世界许多地区，儿童如今相比过去拥有了更多的自由。这种变化是积极的进步，还是带来了负面影响？**

In recent decades, societal changes have granted children significantly more freedom than previous generations in some regions. Although this shift has raised concerns about declining discipline and poor decision-making, I believe it is largely a positive change, for increased personal freedom encourages individual growth and supports the full development of a child's potential.

近几十年来，社会的变迁使得某些地区的孩子相比过往几代人享有了显著更多的自由。尽管这一趋势引发了人们对纪律松散和决策能力不足的担忧，但我认为这在很大程度上是一种积极的发展，因为更高的个人自由能够促进个体成长，有助于孩子全面挖掘自身潜力。

Admittedly, greater independence among children can lead to serious negative consequences, including diminished discipline and short-sighted planning. In the past, strict parental and educational authority helped instill obedience and respect for rules. In contrast, modern permissive parenting often leaves children without clear boundaries, making it difficult for them to understand the consequences of their actions or conform to social expectations. This erosion of discipline is evident in classroom misconduct and even antisocial behavior in public. Even worse, in the absence of adult intervention, children may miss the valuable window of time needed to develop sound judgment. When children are allowed to make important choices on their own, such as how to spend their time, what to eat, or when to study, they often prioritize short-term satisfaction over long-term benefits. As a result, they may struggle in adulthood and lack the resilience to face challenges and overcome setbacks.

不可否认，儿童自主性增强确实可能带来一些严重的负面后果，比如纪律下降和目光短浅的选择。在过去，家长和学校的权威往往能够有效地培养孩子的服从意识和对规则的尊重。而在当代，较为宽松的育儿方式常常缺乏明确界限，使孩子难以理解行为的后果，或者难以适应社会规范。这种纪律的弱化在课堂上的不良行为，甚至公共场合中的反社会行为中都有所体现。更严重的是，在缺乏成年人的及时引导下，孩子可能错过培养健全判断力的关键阶段。当他们被允许自行决定如何安排时间、吃什么或何时学习时，往往倾向于追求即时满足，而忽视长期利益。因此，他们在成年后可能会面临更多困难，缺乏应对挑战和克服挫折的能力。

Nevertheless, allowing children more freedom yields numerous benefits, particularly in terms of personal growth and the nurturing of individual talents. If children are permitted to choose activities independently, they cultivate independent thinking and creativity. This is because learning through freely chosen, self-directed experiences is aprogressive educational approach that promotes autonomy and practical skill development. For instance, when students are free to choose their own projects, such as building a robot or starting a blog, they often demonstrate greater motivation and creativity that extend beyond the classroom. Moreover, when children explore new experiences like volunteer work without constraints, they may discover hidden talents and interests. Those who choose to participate regularly in volunteering often improve their communication skills and learn how to collaborate effectively with others to solve problems. In doing so, they gain exposure to the world beyond school and home, while also building meaningful friendships and a sense of social responsibility.

然而，适度的自由也带来了诸多积极影响，尤其是在个人成长和能力培养方面。如果孩子能自主选择活动，他们将更容易培养独立思考和创造力。这是因为通过自主参与和自由探索获得的学习经验，符合现代教育中强调自主性和实践能力的理念。例如，当学生可以自由选择项目——比如制作机器人或开设博客时——他们通常会表现出更高的积极性和创造力，而且这种动机往往能延伸至课堂之外。此外，当孩子可以不受限制地尝试志愿服务等新鲜经历时，他们可能会发现自己潜藏的兴趣和才能。那些经常参与志愿活动的孩子，往往在沟通能力和团队合作方面有显著提升。他们不仅接触到了课本之外的真实世界，还建立了深厚的友谊，增强了社会责任感。

In conclusion, while granting children greater self-determination causes reduced discipline and poor decision-making if left unchecked, it also nurtures independence, creativity, and personal growth. With proper guidance, this freedom empowers children to explore their potential, develop essential life skills, and grow into resilient individuals.

总之，尽管在缺乏引导的情况下，让孩子拥有更多自主权可能导致纪律松懈和错误决策，但合理的自由同样能够激发他们的独立性、创造力和成长动力。在恰当的引导下，这种自由能够帮助孩子发掘潜力，掌握关键的生活技能，最终成长为具备韧性和责任感的独立个体。

| **因果推导** | `This is because... As a result...` | 解释原因并推导结果 |
| **例证衔接** | `For example... For instance...` | 用具体例子支撑论点 |

> **衔接亮点**：本文在益处段中使用了"This is because learning through freely chosen, self-directed experiences is a progressive educational approach that promotes autonomy and practical skill development"的因果解释，将"自由选择"与"进步教育理念"关联，使论证具有理论深度。

**1. 因果链式长句**
> *"In contrast, modern permissive parenting often leaves children without clear boundaries, making it difficult for them to understand the consequences of their actions or conform to social expectations."*

**技巧**："In contrast"与过去的严格教育对比，主句陈述现代育儿的问题，现在分词短语"making it difficult..."推导后果，"or"连接两个并列的困难（理解后果+适应社会期望）。

**2. 递进后果句**
> *"**Even worse**, in the absence of adult intervention, children may miss the valuable window of time needed to develop sound judgment."*

**技巧**："Even worse"将论证推向更严重的后果，"in the absence of"用介词短语替代"without"更加正式，"the valuable window of time"用比喻表达强调关键期的珍贵。

**3. 条件+效果句**
> *"**When students are free to choose their own projects**, such as building a robot or starting a blog, they often demonstrate greater motivation and creativity that extend beyond the classroom."*

**技巧**："When"引导条件从句，"such as"列举具体例子，主句陈述效果，"that extend beyond the classroom"用定语从句说明效果的延展性。

**4. 综合总结句**
> *"With proper guidance, this freedom empowers children to explore their potential, develop essential life skills, and grow into resilient individuals."*

**技巧**：结尾句用"With proper guidance"回应前文提到的"缺乏引导"问题，主句用"empowers... to explore... develop... and grow into..."三个并列不定式说明自由的积极作用。这种"问题解决→积极成果"的结构使结尾既有回应又有升华。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 益处段出现 `is aprogressive educational approach` 的粘连拼写（应为 `a progressive`），属硬伤。
- 立场是"利大于弊"，但弊端段的因果链更长、措辞更狠（`Even worse`、`struggle in adulthood`），益处段反而偏温和，论证强度与最终立场倒挂；建议在益处段补一层"自由如何反过来培养自律"的回应，直接接住弊端段的指控。
- 结论句 `granting children greater self-determination causes reduced discipline and poor decision-making` 用 `causes` 把可能性说成必然，与正文一贯的 `can lead to / may` 口径不符，改为 `can cause` 更稳。
