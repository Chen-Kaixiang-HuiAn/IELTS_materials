## Task 1

> **图表类型标注**：柱状图（双图对比（男女闲暇活动占比 + 场所访问））

**The bar charts below show the percentage of males and females who enjoy leisure time from 1996 to 2006, as well as the percentage of males and females visiting different places in 2006.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-01-18](./Task1_img/2025-01-18.png)

The bar charts illustrate changes in the percentages of males and females participating in leisure activities from 1996 to 2006 and their preferences for various leisure destinations (cinema, pop center, museum, KTV, and beach) in 2006. 

As shown in the first chart, the proportion of males enjoying leisure time remained consistently high, despite experiencing a slight decline. Specifically, it decreased marginally from approximately 80% in 1996 to around 78% in 2006. In contrast, the percentage of females engaging in recreational activities started lower but demonstrated a steady increase over the decade, rising from about 38% to 60%, thereby reducing the gender gap in leisure participation by the end of the period. 

Regarding leisure destinations in 2006, nearly 30% of females went to beaches, while slightly fewer males, just under 25%, did so. A similar pattern was observed in cinemas, where roughly 25% of women participated, a proportion twice as high as that of men. Conversely, more males visited pop centers (30%), double the rate of females. Museums had the smallest gender gap, with 20% of males and 18% of females visiting such, while KTV was the least preferred destination, attracting under 15% of both genders.

Overall, although males constantly had higher levels of leisure participation, the gap between genders narrowed over time. As for destination preferences, more males frequented pop centers, whereas females favored beaches and cinemas.

**1. 让步+趋势复合句**
> *"The proportion of males enjoying leisure time remained consistently high, **despite experiencing a slight decline**."*

**技巧**：主句陈述总体特征（始终较高），让步状语补充细节（略有下降）。这种"主-次"结构避免了信息的平铺直叙，使描述更有层次感。"despite + 动名词"是描述"总体稳定但有小幅波动"的经典句型。

**2. 因果链式句**
> *"The percentage of females engaging in recreational activities started lower but demonstrated a steady increase over the decade, rising from about 38% to 60%, **thereby reducing the gender gap** in leisure participation by the end of the period."*

**技巧**：句子主干描述趋势，现在分词短语"rising from..."提供具体数据，"thereby reducing..."提炼数据意义。三个层次层层推进，从"发生了什么"到"具体数字"再到"意味着什么"，是数据描述的高阶写法。

**3. 倍数对比句**
> *"A similar pattern was observed in cinemas, where roughly 25% of women participated, **a proportion twice as high as that of men**."*

**技巧**：用同位语结构"a proportion twice as high as that of men"补充说明前文数据，避免了另起一句的断裂感。"twice as high as"是表达倍数关系的地道用法，比"25% vs 12.5%"更加简洁有力。

**4. 综合概述句**
> *"Overall, although males constantly had higher levels of leisure participation, the gap between genders narrowed over time."*

**技巧**：概述段用"although"引导让步从句，承认男性始终领先的事实，同时用主句强调"差距缩小"这一核心趋势。这种"承认A但强调B"的句式是Overview段的经典写法，能够体现对数据的深度解读。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：教育类 · Agree/Disagree

**Some people think that there could be more benefits to society if more people studied business than history. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

**有人认为，如果更多人学习商业而非历史，社会可能会受益更多。你在多大程度上同意或不同意？**

Some argue that having more people major in business-related subjects rather than history brings societal advantages. Although it is true that business education supports socio-economic development, I disagree with the view that more people pursuing business studies offers greater advantages than studying history.

有些人认为，让更多人主修商业相关学科而非历史会为社会带来更多好处。虽然商业教育确实促进了社会经济的发展，但我不同意他们认为更多人学习商业比学习历史带来更大好处的观点。

Admittedly, an increasing number of students choosing to study business management at university enables them to secure well-paying jobs and empowers society by creating a talent pool to tackle financial challenges. That is primarily because a business degree provides students with in-depth academic knowledge and skills to pursue global career opportunities. It also helps them develop a broad understanding of the core elements of businesses and related areas, including but limited to finance and human resources, which are highly sought after by potential employers, especially in a rapidly growing economy. Thus, graduating with transferable skills and strong business knowledge increases candidates’ likelihood of securing a decent job. Secondly, business skills can be utilized to address challenges and developments in both the business world and society, such as the listing of a company or debt restructuring. These skills assist entrepreneurs and administrators in making managerial decisions that consider ethical, economic, and social implications.

不可否认，越来越多的学生选择在大学学习商业管理，这不仅能帮助他们获得高薪工作，还能通过培养应对金融挑战的人才库来赋能社会。首先，商业学位为学生提供了深入的学术知识和技能，使他们能够追求全球职业机会。同时，商科知识还帮助学生广泛了解企业的核心要素及其具体领域，例如金融和人力资源等，这些领域在快速增长的经济中受到潜在雇主的高度青睐。拥有这些可转换技能和扎实商业知识的毕业生，更有可能找到一份体面的工作。其次，商业技能可以用来应对商业和社会中的挑战和发展，例如公司上市或债务重组。这些技能帮助企业家和管理者做出兼顾伦理、经济和社会影响的管理决策。

However, the aforementioned advantages are not worth it if an excessive number of candidates enroll in business subjects while few major in history. The main argument is that an increased "involution" generation focusing on a specific program undoubtedly causes greater competition among them. Even if they earn a degree from a renowned university, an oversupply of graduates makes it difficult for many to find suitable occupations. In contrast, studying history offers distinct benefits that go beyond immediate career prospects, helping learners address complex questions and dilemmas by examining how the past has shaped and continues to shape global and local relationships between societies and individuals. Moreover, history provides the public with critical perspectives to analyze problems related to both the past and the present. For example, a course on the history of public health might highlight how environmental pollution and poor decision-making have disproportionately affected less affluent communities – two significant factors in the water crisis in Flint, Michigan.

然而，如果过多的学生选择商业学科，而选择历史的学生减少，上述优势就不足为道了。不断增加的专注于某一特定专业的“内卷一代”不可避免地会导致更激烈的竞争。即使他们从知名大学获得学位，毕业生过剩也会使许多人难以找到合适的职业。相比之下，学习历史带来的独特好处超越了直接职业前景。它帮助学习者通过研究过去如何塑造并继续影响全球和本土社会之间的关系，来解决复杂的问题和困境。此外，历史为公众提供了分析与过去和现在相关问题的重要视角。例如，一门关于公共卫生史的课程可能会揭示环境污染和糟糕决策如何对较贫困的社区造成不成比例的影响——这两者是密歇根州弗林特水危机的关键因素。

In conclusion, while a business management degree equips students with valuable knowledge and contributes to society's economic challenges, an overabundance of business graduates may exacerbate competition and limit career opportunities. On the other hand, disciplines such as history offer unique benefits, fostering critical thinking and a deeper understanding of societal dynamics.

总之，尽管商业管理学位为学生提供了宝贵的知识，并对社会的经济挑战做出了贡献，但过多的商业毕业生可能会加剧竞争并限制就业机会。另一方面，像历史这样的学科则提供了独特的益处，能够培养批判性思维，并加深对社会动态的理解。

### 精析

#### ① 结构分析（精简）

本题属于 **Agree/Disagree** 类题型。此类题型的核心策略是：先承认对方观点的部分合理性，再通过反驳论证自己的立场，最后总结。

本文采用 **"让步-反驳-综合" 四段式** 结构：

- **Paragraph 1 — Introduction（开头段）**
  - 改写题目（paraphrase）：`Some argue that having more people major in business-related subjects rather than history brings societal advantages`（替换原题 `more benefits to society if more people studied business than history`）
  - 表明立场：`I disagree with the view that more people pursuing business studies offers greater advantages than studying history`（明确不同意）
  - 预告框架：先承认商业教育的价值，再论证过度集中商业的弊端和历史学科的独特价值

- **Paragraph 2 — Body 1（让步段）**
  - 主题句：`Admittedly, an increasing number of students choosing to study business management... enables them to secure well-paying jobs and empowers society`
  - 论点1：商业学位提供深入知识和技能，帮助获得全球职业机会
  - 论点2：商业技能帮助解决实际经济挑战（如公司上市、债务重组）
  - 效果：承认商业教育的实际价值

- **Paragraph 3 — Body 2（反驳段）**
  - 主题句：`However, the aforementioned advantages are not worth it if an excessive number of candidates enroll in business subjects while few major in history`
  - 论点1：过度集中导致"内卷"和就业竞争加剧
  - 论点2：历史学科提供超越职业前景的独特价值——批判性思维和分析能力
  - 论点3：以弗林特水危机为例，说明历史课程如何帮助理解当代问题
  - 效果：从竞争和思维培养两个维度反驳原观点

- **Paragraph 4 — Conclusion（结尾段）**
  - 重申立场：商业学位有价值，但过度饱和会适得其反；历史学科培养批判性思维
  - 总结升华：平衡发展才是最佳路径

> **结构亮点**：本文的反驳策略非常巧妙——作者没有直接否定商业教育的价值，而是采用"以子之矛攻子之盾"的策略：既然商业教育的目的是帮助就业，那么当所有人都学商业时，就业优势就不存在了。这种归谬式论证比直接否定更有说服力。

| **转折反驳** | `However... In contrast...` | 转向反驳论证 |
| **因果推导** | `That is primarily because... Thus...` | 解释原因并推导结果 |
| **举例衔接** | `For example...` | 用具体案例支撑论点 |

> **衔接亮点**：本文在让步段和反驳段之间使用了"However, the aforementioned advantages are not worth it..."这一强力转折，直接否定前文的"优势"，形成鲜明的逻辑断裂。这种"先立后破"的衔接方式使反驳更加有力。

| 词汇/短语 | 中文含义 | 词性/用法 | 替代普通表达 |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **major in** | 主修（某专业） | 动词短语 | study |
| **empowers society** | 赋能社会；增强社会能力 | 动词短语 | helps society |
| **sought after** | 抢手的；备受青睐的 | 形容词短语 | wanted |
| **exacerbate** | 加剧；使恶化 | 动词 | make worse |
| **oversupply** | 供给过剩 | 名词 | too many |
| **dilemmas** | 两难困境 | 名词 | problems |

**1. 复杂让步句**
> *"**Admittedly**, an increasing number of students choosing to study business management at university enables them to secure well-paying jobs and **empowers society** by creating a talent pool to tackle financial challenges."*

**技巧**："Admittedly"作为段落开头，明确标记让步立场。句子主干用"enables... and empowers..."双重谓语，同时从个人和社会两个层面论证商业教育的价值，使让步更加充分。

**2. 条件+后果复合句**
> *"**Even if** they earn a degree from a renowned university, **an oversupply of graduates makes it difficult** for many to find suitable occupations."*

**技巧**：使用"Even if..."引导的让步条件从句，先假设最有利情况（名校毕业），再用主句否定其结果（仍然难就业）。这种"极端情况也不成立"的论证方式极具说服力，是反驳段的优质句型。

**3. 比较对比句**
> *"**In contrast**, studying history offers distinct benefits that go beyond immediate career prospects, helping learners address complex questions and dilemmas by examining how the past has shaped and continues to shape global and local relationships between societies and individuals."*

**技巧**："In contrast"明确标记对比关系，"that go beyond"将历史学科的价值提升到超越商业教育的维度。现在分词短语"helping learners..."和"by examining..."层层展开历史学科的具体价值，使论证既有高度又有细节。

**4. 综合总结句**
> *"In conclusion, while a business management degree equips students with valuable knowledge and contributes to society's economic challenges, **an overabundance of business graduates may exacerbate competition** and limit career opportunities."*

**技巧**：结尾句再次使用"while"让步结构，承认商业学位的价值，然后用主句强调"overabundance"的核心问题。"exacerbate"一词精准表达了"使本已存在的问题更加严重"的含义，体现了词汇的精确运用。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 让步段序数词不成对：先写 "That is primarily because..."，后面却接 "Secondly"，读者找不到对应的 Firstly，建议统一为 First / Second。
- `including but limited to finance and human resources` 漏掉 not，字面意思与本意相反；另 "the aforementioned advantages are not worth it" 语域偏口语，可换 are largely offset。
- 结论段把商业与历史两方好处并列陈述，却没有再明确回扣"I disagree"的立场（如点明"因此更多人转向商业未必更有利于社会"），收尾力度弱于开头段。

**仿写任务**：用「先退后进」骨架重写：*Some think space exploration is a waste of money.*
