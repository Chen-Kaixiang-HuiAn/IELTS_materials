![2025-08-16](./Task1_img/2025-08-16.png)

The bar charts illustrate changes in the total population of a UK town between 1951 and 2011, along with the distribution of residents across five age cohorts: 0-14, 15-29, 30-39, 40-64, and 65 and above. 

As shown, the town’s population saw a significant upturn in the most part of the 60-year period. Specifically, it stood at about 100,000 in 1951, rose steadily to nearly 150,000 in 1971, and peaked at 200,000 in 1991 before it fell to around 170,000 in 2011. 

Regarding age distribution, the 40-64 group dominated in 1951, comprising almost 45% of the town’s inhabitants, but their proportion declined markedly to just above one-fifth in 2011. A similar downward trend was observed among those aged 30-39, whose share dropped from roughly 18% to below 12%, despite some fluctuations. By contrast, the population of people aged 65 and over increased consistently, more than doubling from about 10% to one-quarter, becoming one of the largest age groups. Meanwhile, the figures for both the 0-14 and 15-29-year-olds grew steadily, with that for the former rising from around 14% to 22%, and that of the latter expanding from a similar base (14%) to approximately 20%.

Overall, the town witnessed an initial sustained population growth followed by a modest contraction, and its demographic composition shifted markedly from a predominantly middle-aged population to an ageing society.

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：健康类 · Problem-Solution

**Nowadays, people spend a great deal of time sitting, which leads to health problems. Why does this happen? What are the best ways to encourage people to sit less?**

**如今，人们花费大量时间坐着，这会导致健康问题。为什么会出现这种现象？鼓励人们减少久坐的最佳方法是什么？**

In contemporary society, sedentary lifestyles, which are associated with a variety of health problems, have become increasingly prevalent. The underlying causes of this behavioural pattern are multifaceted, yet effective strategies can be implemented to mitigate its detrimental consequences.

在当代社会，久坐的生活方式日益普遍，这种生活方式与多种健康问题密切相关，许多人每天都长时间保持坐姿。这种行为模式的根本原因是多方面的，但可以采取有效策略来减轻其不利后果。

As observed, the nature of modern work and study, combined with rapid technological advancements in entertainment and transportation, has significantly contributed to many individuals spending prolonged periods seated each day. That is, a considerable proportion of both work and education is now computer-based, requiring individuals to remain at their desks for extended hours. In addition, shortages of adequate employment opportunities and higher education institutions in certain regions, particularly in Northeast Asia, compel many people to engage in sedentary study and work in order to compete for scarce professional or academic prospects. This, in turn, blurs the boundary between work and personal life. Beyond excessive working and studying hours, technological innovations in leisure and transport further reinforce sedentary habits. In today's digital era, online streaming and social media have displaced physically demanding recreational activities, whereas the rapid development of modern transportation has reduced opportunities for active ways of traveling, such as walking or cycling.

正如所观察到的，现代工作和学习的性质，以及娱乐和交通领域的快速技术进步，都显著促成了长时间久坐。目前，相当大比例的就业和教育依赖计算机，这要求个人长时间坐在办公桌前。此外，某些地区（尤其是东北亚）就业机会和高等教育机构的不足，使许多人为了争夺有限的职业或学术机会而从事久坐的学习和工作。这进一步模糊了工作与个人生活的界限。除了过度的工作和学习时间之外，休闲和通勤领域的技术创新也加剧了久坐习惯。在当今数字时代，在线视频和社交媒体取代了需要体力的休闲活动，而现代交通的快速发展减少了主动通勤（如步行或骑行）的机会。

With these challenges in mind, a combination of societal and individual interventions is indispensable to counteract sedentary lifestyles. On a broader scale, governments should promote healthier work-life patterns and encourage companies to pursue constructive competition rather than meaningless involution with little tangible progress, so as to liberate the younger generation from excessive screen-based work. Furthermore, increased public investment in higher education could alleviate institutional shortages, ensuring that students and professionals do not have to endure disproportionate sedentary hours competing for limited opportunities, but instead have more time to devote to physical well-being. At the individual level, people should be encouraged to incorporate active routines into their daily lives to counteract prolonged sitting. These seemingly minor adjustments, such as cycling to nearby destinations or taking the stairs, can cumulatively reduce sedentary behaviour. As these practices become habitual, they help individuals break the cycle of inactivity, and reduce the risk of lifestyle-related diseases.

面对这些挑战，社会层面和个人层面的干预相结合是抵消久坐生活方式的必要措施。在更广泛的层面上，政府应推动更健康的工作与生活模式，并鼓励企业追求建设性的竞争，而非缺乏实质进展的无意义内卷，从而解放年轻一代，避免他们过度从事屏幕工作。此外，增加对高等教育的公共投资可以缓解机构短缺，确保学生和专业人士不必为有限的机会忍受过度久坐，而有更多时间投入身体健康。在个人层面，应鼓励人们将积极的日常习惯融入生活，以对抗长时间久坐。这些看似微小的调整，例如骑车前往附近目的地或走楼梯，累积起来可以显著减少久坐行为。当这些习惯逐渐形成，它们有助于个人打破不活动的循环，最终带来长期健康益处，并降低与生活方式相关疾病的风险。

In conclusion, sedentary lifestyles largely stem from computer-based work and study, heightened academic and professional competition, and the far-reaching impact of technological developments in leisure and transport. Nevertheless, with governments promoting healthier structures and individuals adopting active routines, it is possible to reduce prolonged sitting and its associated health hazards.

总之，久坐的生活方式主要源于基于计算机的工作和学习、加剧的学业与职业竞争，以及休闲和交通技术发展带来的深远影响。然而，在政府支持推动更健康的结构和个人采取积极日常习惯的情况下，有可能减少长时间久坐及其相关健康风险。

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

| **因果衔接** | `has significantly contributed to...` | 引出原因分析 |
| **解释说明** | `That is...` | 对前文进行具体化解释 |
| **递进补充** | `In addition... Beyond...` | 在同一分析框架内添加新原因 |

| **分层论述** | `On a broader scale... At the individual level...` | 清晰划分社会和个人层面 |
| **总结衔接** | `In conclusion...` | 收束全文，重申核心观点 |

**1. 多重因素因果句**
> *"**the nature of modern work and study, combined with rapid technological advancements in entertainment and transportation**, has significantly contributed to many individuals spending prolonged periods seated each day"*

**技巧**：用 "combined with" 将三个因素（工作学习性质、娱乐技术、交通技术）并列，再用 "has significantly contributed to" 引出结果。一句涵盖多重原因，信息密度极高。

**2. 条件-机制-结果链**
> *"shortages of adequate employment opportunities and higher education institutions in certain regions, particularly in Northeast Asia, **compel** many people to engage in sedentary study and work in order to compete for scarce professional or academic prospects. **This, in turn, blurs** the boundary between work and personal life"*

**技巧**：用 "compel... to..." 描述机制，"in order to" 说明目的，"This, in turn, blurs..." 引出深层后果。"in turn" 强调了因果的连锁性，使论证更具深度。

**3. 对比并列句**
> *"online streaming and social media **have displaced** physically demanding recreational activities, **whereas** the rapid development of modern transportation **has reduced** opportunities for active ways of traveling, such as walking or cycling"*

**技巧**：用 "whereas" 对比两种技术（娱乐技术 vs 交通技术）的不同但相似的影响（都减少了体力活动）。"have displaced" 和 "has reduced" 使用不同动词表达相似效果，避免了重复。

**4. 目的-结果复合句**
> *"increased public investment in higher education **could alleviate** institutional shortages, **ensuring that** students and professionals do not have to endure disproportionate sedentary hours competing for limited opportunities, **but instead have** more time to devote to physical well-being"*

**技巧**：用 "ensuring that" 引导目的状语从句，再用 "but instead" 转折引出积极结果。"do not have to endure... but instead have..." 的对比结构使政策建议的效果更加鲜明。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- `sedentary` 全篇出现五次以上（lifestyles / study and work / habits / hours / behaviour），建议换用 `desk-bound`、`physically inactive`、`screen-tethered` 等做部分替换。
- 原因二（东北亚就业与高校名额不足）到"久坐"的因果跨度偏大，中间的 `This, in turn, blurs the boundary between work and personal life` 又岔向工作—生活边界，偏离久坐主线；可删或改写为直接指向"坐着的时长被拉长"。
- 方案段的政府建议（增加高等教育投资）同样迂回；题目问的是"鼓励少坐"的最佳方式，建议至少补一条直接措施，如强制工间活动、站立式办公、城市步行与自行车道建设。
- 首段中文译文多出"许多人每天都长时间保持坐姿"一句，英文原文并无对应内容，建议对齐。
