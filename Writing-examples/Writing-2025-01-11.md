**The plans below show a student accommodation building 2010 and now.**

![2025-01-11](./Task1_img/2025-01-11.png)

The two floor diagrams compare the layout of a student accommodation building in 2010 with its current state. 

In 2010, the accommodation featured three student bedrooms, shared facilities, and gardens to both the north and south of the building. The main entrance, located at the left corner, led into a hallway connecting the bedrooms and communal areas. Immediately to the left of the entrance was the first bedroom, while the centrally located bathroom was adjacent to it. The second bedroom was positioned on the lower right corner, while the third bedroom was set up in the upper right corner, with the kitchen between them. The living room occupied the center of the northern part of the building, to the left of which was a portion of the garden. 

Currently, the student accommodation has undergone a redesign to increase the number of bedrooms. Specifically, the living room and part of the garden have been transformed into two additional bedrooms, resulting in a new layout with five bedrooms, one of which features an en-suite near its entrance. As for the shared kitchen, it now serves as both a practical facility and a social hub. Meanwhile, the southern garden has been converted into a car parking area, but the northern garden has been left unchanged.

Overall, the building has experienced several changes including the addition of student bedrooms and new facilities, such as en-suite rooms and car parking, at the expense of leisure spaces.

> *"The main entrance, **located at the left corner**, led into a hallway connecting the bedrooms and communal areas."*

**2. 关系从句串联空间**
> *"The living room occupied the center of the northern part of the building, **to the left of which was** a portion of the garden."*

**技巧**：使用"介词+关系代词"结构（to the left of which）将两个空间元素连接在一个复合句中，体现了高超的句式驾驭能力。这种结构比"The living room was in the center. A portion of the garden was to the left of it"更加紧凑和学术化。

**3. 现在完成时的被动语态**
> *"the living room and part of the garden **have been transformed into** two additional bedrooms"*

**技巧**：现在完成时的被动语态（have been transformed）强调变化从过去持续到现在的结果，是地图题描述变化的经典句式。相比一般过去时的"were transformed"，现在完成时更能体现"当前状态"这一核心信息。

**4. 结果分词短语**
> *"resulting in a new layout with five bedrooms, **one of which features** an en-suite near its entrance"*

**技巧**："resulting in"作为结果状语，承接前文变化并引出后续细节；"one of which"引导非限制性定语从句，对"five bedrooms"进行补充说明。这种"总-分"句式结构在描述复杂变化时非常高效。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：环境类 · Agree/Disagree

**Some people say that the fashion industry is bad for the environment as it encourages consumers to by all the time. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

**有人说时尚行业对环境有害，因为它鼓励消费者不断购买。你在多大程度上同意或不同意这一观点？**

The fashion industry has been increasingly criticized for its negative environmental impacts because it often encourages overconsumption and wastefulness through practices like fast fashion. Although the industry may not directly enforce overconsumption, I agree that it is highly detrimental to the environment due to its unsustainable practices and significant ecological footprint.

时尚行业因其对环境的负面影响而受到越来越多的批评，因为它通过快时尚等方式鼓励过度消费和浪费。虽然该行业并未直接强制消费者过度消费，但我同意其对环境的高度破坏性，这是由于其不可持续的运作方式和显著的生态足迹所致。

It cannot be denied that the fashion industry creates opportunities for frequent purchases by introducing new trends and affordable products, thereby influencing consumer behavior. However, the decision to overconsume ultimately depends on individual choices. While the industry provides options, it does not mandate excessive consumption. Instead, it offers a spectrum of choices, including many brands and designers that focus on creating durable pieces designed to last for years rather than disposable items in order to cater to varying consumer values and priorities. For example, those who prioritize sustainability or minimalism can engage with brands that align with their principles. Consequently, critics of the overconsumption argument emphasize personal accountability, arguing that not all shoppers are influenced by marketing tactics or the allure of fast fashion.

不可否认的是，时尚行业通过推出新趋势和实惠的产品，为频繁购买创造了条件，从而影响了消费者的行为。然而，过度消费的决定最终取决于个人选择。尽管时尚行业提供了各种选择，但它并未强制要求消费者过度消费。相反，它提供了一个广泛的选择范围，其中包括许多专注于制造耐用产品的品牌和设计师，这些产品旨在使用多年，而不是作为一次性商品，以迎合不同消费者的价值观和优先事项。例如，那些优先考虑可持续性或极简主义的人可以选择与其原则相符的品牌。因此，批评过度消费论点的人强调个人责任，认为并非所有消费者都会受到市场营销策略或快时尚吸引力的影响。

Nevertheless, the environmental impact of the fashion industry remains severe due to its manufacturing practices and disposable culture, which directly contribute to environmental degradation. To begin with, the production of textiles demands extensive water usage and pesticides. This not only depletes local water resources but also disrupts ecosystems, linking production methods to widespread ecological harm. Additionally, the use of synthetic fibers and chemical-intensive dyeing processes releases pollutants into rivers and, thus, contaminate water sources. This, in turn, adversely affects aquatic life and endangers nearby communities that rely on these resources for survival. Even worse, fast fashion amplifies these problems by encouraging unsustainable consumer behavior, particularly as brands release new collections multiple times a year, fostering a culture of frequent purchases and disposability. This leads to the generation of vast amounts of textile waste, much of which is non-biodegradable. These materials persist in landfills or require incineration that result in long-term environmental damage.

尽管如此，由于其生产方式和一次性文化，时尚行业对环境的影响仍然十分严重，这直接导致了环境退化。首先，纺织品的生产需要大量的水和农药。这不仅耗尽了当地的水资源，还破坏了生态系统，使生产方式与广泛的生态破坏联系在一起。此外，合成纤维的使用以及化学密集型的染色过程会向河流中排放污染物，从而污染水源。这反过来对水生生物产生不利影响，并危及依赖这些资源生存的周边社区。更糟糕的是，快时尚通过频繁发布新系列，鼓励了不可持续的消费行为，尤其是随着品牌一年多次推出新款，进一步助长了频繁购买和一次性使用的文化。这导致了大量纺织废弃物的产生，其中许多是不可降解的。这些材料要么长期存在于垃圾填埋场中，要么需要焚烧处理，从而造成长期的环境破坏。

In conclusion, while the fashion industry offers diverse options that accommodate varying consumer preferences, its manufacturing processes and the rise of fast fashion have led to significant environmental challenges from resource depletion to textile waste. These issues highlight the need for systemic reforms and more conscious consumer choices to reduce the industry's ecological impact.

综上所述，尽管时尚行业提供了多样化的选择以满足不同的消费者偏好，其生产过程和快时尚的兴起却导致了从资源耗竭到纺织废弃物等一系列严重的环境问题。这些问题凸显了系统性改革的必要性，以及消费者在减少该行业生态影响方面做出更加自觉选择的需求。

#### ① 结构分析（精简）

本题属于 **Agree/Disagree** 类题型。此类题型的核心策略是：先承认对方观点的合理之处，再提出自己的反驳论据，最后总结立场。

  - 改写题目（paraphrase）：`The fashion industry has been increasingly criticized for its negative environmental impacts because it often encourages overconsumption and wastefulness through practices like fast fashion`（替换原题 `the fashion industry is bad for the environment`）

| **因果递进** | `This not only... but also... This, in turn...` | 逐层推导环境后果 |
| **递进加剧** | `Additionally... Even worse...` | 从一般问题推向更严重问题 |

> **衔接亮点**：本文的衔接词使用极具层次感。让步段用"However""Consequently"保持逻辑连贯；主论段用"To begin with""Additionally""Even worse"形成三级递进，使论证力度逐步升级。特别是"Even worse"的使用，将快时尚问题提升到比生产污染更严重的位置，体现了作者对问题严重性的精准判断。

| 词汇/短语 | 中文含义 | 词性/用法 | 替代普通表达 |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **detrimental** | 有害的；不利的 | 形容词 | bad |
| **overconsumption** | 过度消费 | 名词 | buying too much |
| **unsustainable practices** | 不可持续的做法 | 名词短语 | bad ways of working |
| **ecological footprint** | 生态足迹 | 名词短语 | environmental impact |
| **depletes** | 耗尽；使枯竭 | 动词 | uses up |
| **non-biodegradable** | 不可生物降解的 | 形容词 | cannot break down naturally |

**1. 复杂让步句**
> *"**Although** the industry may not directly enforce overconsumption, **I agree that** it is highly detrimental to the environment due to its unsustainable practices and significant ecological footprint."*

**技巧**：使用"Although... I agree that..."结构，在开头段即完成让步+立场的双重任务。"due to"引导的原因状语将"unsustainable practices"和"ecological footprint"并列，使立场声明既有深度又有广度。

**2. 因果链式长句**
> *"This not only **depletes** local water resources but also **disrupts** ecosystems, linking production methods to widespread ecological harm."*

**技巧**："not only... but also..."结构连接两个并列后果，随后的现在分词短语"linking..."将前文与更宏观的"ecological harm"关联。这种"具体→抽象"的推进方式使论证既有细节又有高度。

**3. 递进式后果推导**
> *"This, in turn, **adversely affects** aquatic life and **endangers** nearby communities that rely on these resources for survival."*

**技巧**："This, in turn"表示因果传导，将河流污染的影响从"水生生物"传递到"依赖资源的社区"，体现了论证的延展性。"adversely affects"和"endangers"的动词升级也使语气逐步加重。

**4. 综合总结句**
> *"These issues highlight the need for systemic reforms and more conscious consumer choices to reduce the industry's ecological impact."*

**技巧**：结尾句使用"highlight the need for"将问题指向解决方案，"systemic reforms"和"conscious consumer choices"分别从制度和个人层面提出对策，体现了全面思考。不定式"to reduce"明确行动目标，使结尾既有总结又有呼吁。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 让步段（Body 1）以 "critics of the overconsumption argument emphasize personal accountability" 收尾，整段停在对方立场上而未回扣自己的 agree，读者读完会短暂失去方向；末尾补半句"然而个人选择仍受行业供给结构塑造"即可把逻辑拉回。
- 两处主谓一致问题：`releases pollutants into rivers and, thus, contaminate water sources`（应为 contaminates）与 `require incineration that result in`（应为 results）。GRA 项上属于可避免的低级失分。
- 让步段的例证 "those who prioritize sustainability or minimalism can engage with brands that align with their principles" 完全抽象，没有任何品牌类型或做法佐证，与主论段里"一年多次上新"这样的具体细节反差明显。
