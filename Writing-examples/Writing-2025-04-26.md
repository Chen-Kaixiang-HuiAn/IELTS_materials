> **图表类型标注**：柱状图（双城通勤方式占比）

**The chart below shows the percentage of people who chose different kinds of transport to get to work in Melbourne and Sydney in 2006 and 2011.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant .**

![2025-04-26](./Task1_img/2025-04-26.png)

The bar chart illustrates the changes in the percentage of people choosing public transport, private transport, and cycling or walking in Melbourne and Sydney between 2006 and 2011.

As seen, private transport remained the most popular mode of travel in both cities throughout the period, although its usage declined slightly. In detail, the proportion of people relying on private vehicles in Melbourne dropped marginally, falling from roughly 84% in 2006 to around 79% in 2011. A similar pattern was observed in Sydney, where the figure decreased from approximately 77% to 72%. 

In contrast, public transport usage experienced a gradual rise. In Melbourne, the percentage of residents who chose to commute grew from about 14% in 2006 to nearly 16% in 2011. Sydney followed a comparable trend, with the figure there increasing from approximately 17% to just under 20%. Meanwhile, cycling or walking was the least favored means of transport in both locations. In Melbourne, the share of people opting to cycle or walk was steady at around 5% over the five years, while Sydney saw a slight drop in its corresponding statistics (from about 7% to around 4%).

Overall, while private transport continued to be the dominant choice in both cities, there was a clear shift toward greater public transport usage. Moreover, cycling and walking consistently attracted the smallest proportion of travelers.

**1. 让步趋势句**
> *"As seen, private transport remained the most popular mode of travel in both cities throughout the period, **although its usage declined slightly**."*

**技巧**：主句陈述总体地位（最受欢迎），让步从句补充细节（略有下降）。这种"主-次"结构避免了信息的平铺直叙，使描述更有层次感。

**2. 同步对比句**
> *"In Melbourne, the percentage of residents who chose to commute grew from about 14% in 2006 to nearly 16% in 2011. **A similar pattern was observed in Sydney**, with the figure there increasing from approximately 17% to just under 20%."*

**技巧**：第一句描述墨尔本的变化，第二句用"A similar pattern was observed in Sydney"标注相似趋势，"with + 名词 + 现在分词"补充悉尼的具体数据。这种"具体→概括→具体"的结构使对比清晰而高效。

**3. 稳定描述句**
> *"In Melbourne, the share of people opting to cycle or walk was steady at around 5% over the five years, while Sydney saw a slight drop in its corresponding statistics (from about 7% to around 4%)."*

**技巧**：用"while"将两个城市的不同稳定性并置对比，"corresponding statistics"避免了重复"percentage of people opting to cycle or walk"，"steady at"和"saw a slight drop"的对比突出了两个城市的差异。

**4. 综合概述句**
> *"Overall, while private transport continued to be the dominant choice in both cities, there was a clear shift toward greater public transport usage."*

**技巧**：概述段用"while"引导让步从句承认私人交通的主导地位，主句强调"向公共交通转变"的核心趋势。"clear shift"精准概括了数据背后的社会变化。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：教育类 · Discuss Both Views

**Some people think that teachers should be responsible for teaching students to judge what is right and wrong and to behave well. Others say that teachers should only teach students academic subjects. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.**

**一些人认为，教师应当负责教导学生判断是非并培养良好行为；另一些人则认为，教师只需教授学术知识。讨论双方观点，并给出你的看法。**

While some argue that teachers should focus on instilling moral values and promoting appropriate behavior, others believe that their primary role should be limited to delivering academic content. In my view, although teachers are responsible for teaching academic subjects, their thoughts and behaviors inevitably influence students’ moral development.

虽然有人主张教师应专注于灌输道德价值观和促进恰当行为；但是，亦有人认为，教师的主要职责应仅限于传授学术内容。依我之见，虽然教师的核心任务是教授学科知识，但他们的思想与行为不可避免地会影响学生的道德发展。

Teachers prioritizing students’ acquisition of cutting-edge knowledge at school plays a crucial role in enhancing students’ capabilities and helping them achieve long-term success, particularly in today’s digital age in which many competency-based jobs require candidates to have a strong foundation in interdisciplinary subjects, which are typically acquired through formal education. Without extensive professional knowledge, individuals may struggle to secure decent employment in a highly competitive labor market. This is particularly evident in fields like frontierscience, where research demands expertise across multiple disciplines. However, an exclusive focus on academic achievement can result in students who excel in exams but lack practical skills and a strong work ethic. In such cases, upon graduation, these students may have difficulty in tackling complex tasks that require humanistic understanding, making it difficult for them to establish success in their chose field.

教师在学校中优先促进学生掌握前沿知识，对于提升学生能力并助力其长期成功起着至关重要的作用，尤其是在当今这个数字化时代，许多以能力为基础的岗位要求求职者具备坚实的跨学科知识基础，而这些通常需通过正规教育获得。缺乏扎实的专业知识，个人可能难以在竞争激烈的就业市场中找到体面的工作。这种现象在前沿科学等领域尤为明显，这些领域的研究往往要求具备多学科的专业技能。然而，若过于偏重学术成就，可能导致学生虽然考试成绩优异，却缺乏实际操作能力和良好的职业素养。在这种情况下，这些学生在毕业后可能难以应对需要人文理解的复杂任务，进而难以在所选领域中取得成功。

Given that instilling moral principles and fostering collaboration skills are as equally essential as academic instruction, it is understandable why some argue that teachers should assume a more comprehensive role in students’ development by teaching them to distinguish between right and wrong and encouraging positive behavior. In this view, education extends beyond academic achievement to include the cultivation of character and moral values. Since students spend a significant amount of time at school, teachers are uniquely positioned to influence their personal growth and guide them toward becoming responsible and ethical individuals. In some Asian cultures, for example, teachers are regarded as parental figures, and society places considerable expectations on them, especially during compulsory education. Beyond cultural factors, in an increasingly complex and fast-changing society, young people need a clear moral framework to make sound decisions. When parental guidance is inconsistent or lacking, teachers’ guidance can serve as a crucial compass, helping to fill that gap and support students' overall development for long-term success.

鉴于灌输道德观念和培养协作能力与学术教学同等重要，有人主张教师应在学生发展中承担更为综合的角色，教导他们辨别是非并鼓励积极行为。从这一观点来看，教育不仅限于学业成就，还包括品格和道德价值观的培养。由于学生在学校度过大量时间，教师因而在影响学生个人成长、引导其成为负责任、有道德感的人方面具有独特优势。在一些亚洲文化中，教师被视作家长的延伸，社会对他们寄予厚望，尤其是在义务教育阶段。除文化因素外，在日益复杂、变化迅速的社会中，年轻人需要清晰的道德框架以作出正确决策。当家庭指导不一致或缺失时，教师的引导可作为关键补充，帮助学生实现全面发展，为长期成功打下基础。

In conclusion, teachers should not prioritize academic instruction at the expense of moral and holistic development. They have a significant influence on shaping students' moral character, helping them recognize right from wrong, and promoting universal values that contribute to their long-term personal and professional success.

综上所述，教师不应以牺牲学生道德和整体发展的代价来片面强调学术教学。他们在塑造学生道德品格、帮助学生识别是非及推广普遍价值观方面具有重要影响，这对于学生未来个人与职业的长期成功至关重要。

| **递进扩展** | `Beyond cultural factors...` | 从文化因素扩展到社会需求 |

> **衔接亮点**：本文在道德段中使用了"Given that instilling moral principles and fostering collaboration skills are as equally essential as academic instruction, it is understandable why some argue that..."的复杂句式——用"Given that"引导原因状语，"it is understandable why"表达理解，使论证既有逻辑又有同理心。

| 词汇/短语 | 中文含义 | 词性/用法 | 替代普通表达 |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **instilling moral values** | 灌输道德价值观 | 动词短语 | teaching moral values |
| **delivering academic content** | 传授学科知识内容 | 动词短语 | teaching academic subjects |
| **cutting-edge knowledge** | 前沿知识 | 名词短语 | latest knowledge |
| **interdisciplinary subjects** | 跨学科科目 | 名词短语 | subjects from different fields |
| **holistic development** | 整体（全人）发展 | 名词短语 | all-round development |
| **universal values** | 普世价值 | 名词短语 | common values |

**1. 复杂因果句**
> *"**Given that** instilling moral principles and fostering collaboration skills are as equally essential as academic instruction, **it is understandable why** some argue that teachers should assume a more comprehensive role in students' development by teaching them to distinguish between right and wrong and encouraging positive behavior."*

**技巧**："Given that"引导原因状语从句，"it is understandable why"表达理解，主句说明具体观点。"by teaching... and encouraging..."用两个动名词短语说明教师的综合角色。这种"原因→理解→具体角色"的结构使论证既有逻辑又有温度。

**2. 转折局限句**
> *"**However**, an exclusive focus on academic achievement can result in students who excel in exams but lack practical skills and a strong work ethic."*

**技巧**："However"标记转折，"an exclusive focus on"精准指出问题所在，"result in"推导后果。"who excel in exams but lack..."用定语从句对比学生的两种状态，使局限性一目了然。

**3. 条件+方案句**
> *"**When parental guidance is inconsistent or lacking**, teachers' guidance can serve as a crucial compass, helping to fill that gap and support students' overall development for long-term success."*

**技巧**："When"引导条件从句，主句用"serve as a crucial compass"比喻教师引导的重要性，现在分词短语"helping to fill that gap..."说明具体作用。"compass"的比喻使论证更加生动。

**4. 综合总结句**
> *"In conclusion, teachers should not prioritize academic instruction at the expense of moral and holistic development."*

**技巧**：结尾句用"at the expense of"精准表达"以牺牲...为代价"的含义，"moral and holistic development"两个形容词并列修饰"development"，从道德和整体两个维度说明不应被牺牲的内容。这种"否定+精确说明"的结构使立场表达清晰有力。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 三处硬伤：`fields like frontierscience` 粘连（应为 `frontier science`）；`in their chose field` 应为 `chosen field`；`establish success` 搭配不自然，宜作 `achieve success`。
- Body 1 主题句一句到底近 60 词，连套 `particularly in... in which... which are typically...` 三层修饰，建议在 `formal education` 处断句，主题句只保留"学科知识为长期发展打底"这一层。
- 开头段的立场是描述性的（教师"不可避免地影响"学生道德发展），结论却转为规范性的"不应以牺牲道德发展为代价"，两处口径不完全一致；可在开头即写明"教师应自觉承担这一影响"。
