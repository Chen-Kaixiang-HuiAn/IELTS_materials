## Task 1

> **图表类型标注**：柱状图（多国交通燃料使用）

**The chart below shows information about fuel used in the transport sector in different countries in Europe, compared to the EU average, in 2009 and 2010.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-04-12](./Task1_img/2025-04-12.png)

The bar chart illustrates the fuel consumption in the transport sector in six European countries, namely Slovakia, Italy, France, Austria, Spain, and Greece, compared to the EU average in the years 2009 and 2010.

As seen, the proportion of fuel used for transportation in Slovakia and Spain declined significantly over the two-year period. In 2009, Slovakia was the only country whose percentage of fuel consumed in the transport sector (8.5%) exceeded the EU average (6%). By 2010, that figure had dropped sharply to around 6.8%, though it still remained the highest. In Spain, the percentage fell from roughly 3.8% in 2009 to about 3% in 2010. The EU’s average followed the same downward trend, decreasing slightly to 5%.

In contrast, the other countries (Italy, France, Austria, and Greece) either maintained or increased their ratios of fuel usage in transport. To be specific, Austria’s rate remained constant at 5.5% across both years, while Italy’s and France’s both experienced a modest rise of 0.6%, climbing from 4.5% to 5.1% and from 3.6% to 4.2%, respectively. Greece, despite having the lowest proportion in both years, recorded the largest growth (by 0.8%), with its consumption growing from 1.2% to 2%.

Overall, Slovakia consistently recorded the highest proportion of fuel used for transportation, despite a notable downturn in it, while Greece had the lowest data in both years, even though it experienced the most significant upturn.

> *"In 2009, Slovakia was **the only country whose percentage of fuel consumed in the transport sector (8.5%) exceeded the EU average (6%)**."*

**2. 同步对比句**
> *"To be specific, Austria's rate remained constant at 5.5% across both years, while Italy's and France's both experienced a modest rise of 0.6%, climbing from 4.5% to 5.1% and from 3.6% to 4.2%, **respectively**."*

**技巧**："To be specific"从概括转向细节，"while"对比Austria的持平与Italy和France的上升。"respectively"精准对应两个国家与两组数据，避免了信息混淆。

**3. 让步极值句**
> *"Greece, **despite having the lowest proportion in both years**, recorded the largest growth (by 0.8%), with its consumption growing from 1.2% to 2%."*

**技巧**："despite + 动名词短语"标注与主句趋势相反的前置条件，主句强调"最大增幅"。这种"低基数+高增长"的对比使数据描述更加立体和有趣。

**4. 综合概述句**
> *"Overall, Slovakia consistently recorded the highest proportion of fuel used for transportation, **despite a notable downturn in it**, while Greece had the lowest data in both years, **even though it experienced the most significant upturn**."*

**技巧**：概述段用"while"将两个极端对象并置，每个对象都用"despite/even though"引导的让步从句标注与排名相反的趋势变化。这种"排名+相反趋势"的双重对比使概述信息极其丰富。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：工作类 · Agree/Disagree

**High-level computer skills are a necessary factor for a successful career. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

**高水平计算机技能是职业成功的必要因素。你在多大程度上同意或不同意？**

Some argue that in today’s increasingly digital world, possessing high-level computer literacy is essential for achieving career success. While computer skills can provide a significant advantage in many professions, I disagree that they are universally necessary for success across all career paths.

一些人认为，在当今这个日益数字化的世界里，具备高水平的计算机素养对于实现职业成功至关重要。尽管计算机技能在许多职业中确实具有显著优势，但我并不认为它在所有职业道路上都是成功的必要条件。

Admittedly, mastering extensive knowledge plays an essential role in achieving career milestones, particularly in fields like technology, finance, engineering, and design, where advanced computer skills are not only beneficial but often indispensable. This is largely due to the growing prevalence of automation and digital methodologies in the modern workplace, which means that employees with strong technical skills are typically more efficient and adaptable, thereby becoming more valuable to employers. Meanwhile, professionals in information technology fields, including software developers, data analysts, and digital marketers rely heavily on complex software, programming languages, and data management tools. In these fields, therefore, mastery of high-level computer applications is a key determinant of both performance and upward mobility. Without these skills, professionals may lack the insight required to solve complex problems, potentially missing out on promotion opportunities or even facing reduced employability in an increasingly competitive job market.

诚然，掌握广泛的知识在实现职业里程碑方面起着重要作用，尤其是在技术、金融、工程和设计等领域，高级计算机技能不仅有益，而且往往是不可或缺的。这主要是因为现代职场中自动化和数字化工作流程日益普及，具备扎实技术能力的员工通常更加高效且具备适应性，因此对雇主而言更具价值。包括软件开发人员、数据分析师和数字营销人员在内的专业人士严重依赖复杂的软件、编程语言和数据管理工具。因此，在这些领域，高水平计算机应用的掌握是工作表现和职业晋升的重要决定因素。缺乏这些技能的专业人士可能无法具备解决复杂问题所需的洞察力，进而可能错失晋升机会，甚至在日益激烈的就业市场中失去竞争力。

Nevertheless, it is important to recognize that recent technological innovations have significantly reduced the importance of computer literacy in some professional fields, and that career success is multifaceted. To begin with, scientific breakthroughs in artificial intelligence (AI) have enabled many complex tasks to be completed efficiently by automated software. This means that even individuals without technical knowledge of hardware or software can still accomplish professional tasks effectively by interacting with AI systems using natural language. For example, AI models like ChatGPT can assist users in completing various assignments without requiring them to understand the underlying technology. Moreover, a well-rounded employee who can build relationships, adapt to change, and lead others may thrive even in roles that do not demand advanced computer skills. In today’s interconnected world, which increasingly values empathy and collaboration, soft skills such as effective communication, cross-cultural understanding, and teamwork play a critical role. These qualities are essential for professionals who must navigate complex challenges and contribute meaningfully across diverse fields.

尽管如此，我们也应认识到，近来的技术创新已经在某些专业领域大大降低了计算机素养的重要性，同时，职业成功本身具有多维属性。首先，人工智能（AI）领域的科学突破使许多复杂任务能够通过自动化软件高效完成。这意味着，即使是缺乏硬件或软件技术知识的人，也可以通过使用自然语言与 AI 系统互动，有效完成职业任务。例如，像 ChatGPT 这样的 AI 模型可以协助用户完成各种工作任务，而无需其理解底层技术。此外，一个全面发展的员工，如果能够建立良好的人际关系、适应变化并具备领导力，即使在不需要高级计算机技能的岗位上，也可能取得出色的表现。在当今这个日益重视同理心与合作精神的互联世界中，诸如有效沟通、跨文化理解和团队合作等软技能发挥着至关重要的作用。这些素质对于需要应对复杂挑战并在多元化领域中做出有意义贡献的专业人士而言，是不可或缺的。

In conclusion, while high-level computer skills are crucial in technology-driven sectors, they are not universally required for career success. With the rise of AI simplifying complex tasks and the growing emphasis on soft skills, success increasingly depends on a multifaceted range of competencies beyond technical expertise.

总之，尽管高水平计算机技能在以技术为主导的行业中至关重要，但它并不是实现职业成功的普遍前提。随着 AI 技术简化复杂任务，以及软技能日益受到重视，职业成功越来越依赖于超越技术专长的多元能力。

#### ④ 可迁移句型骨架（直接套用）（题型：Agree/Disagree）

**骨架 1 — 先退后进亮立场（Intro）**
> Although ______ deserves a central place because ______, I disagree with the view that ______.

**骨架 2 — 分类论证起手（Body）**
> From the perspective of ______, doing X helps ______ and ______. Meanwhile, X also offers ______ that go beyond ______.

**骨架 3 — 抽象→具体例证（Body）**
> For example, a course on ______ may show how ______ have harmed ______, as illustrated by ______.

#### ⑤ 衔接 / 词汇 / 句式亮点（去水版）

| 衔接类型 | 原文表达 | 功能说明 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **让步衔接** | `Admittedly... Although...` | 先承认对方合理性 |
| **转折反驳** | `Nevertheless... Moreover...` | 转向反驳并扩展论证 |
| **因果推导** | `This is largely due to... This means that...` | 解释原因并推导结果 |
| **例证衔接** | `For example...` | 用具体例子支撑论点 |

> **衔接亮点**：本文在反驳段中使用了"This means that even individuals without technical knowledge of hardware or software can still accomplish professional tasks effectively by interacting with AI systems using natural language"的因果推导，将"AI技术进步"与"非技术人员也能完成专业任务"直接关联，使反驳论证非常有力。

| 词汇/短语 | 中文含义 | 词性/用法 | 替代普通表达 |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **computer literacy** | 计算机素养 | 名词短语 | computer skills |
| **career milestones** | 职业发展中的重要节点 | 名词短语 | career achievements |
| **upward mobility** | 向上流动（晋升空间） | 名词短语 | promotion |
| **multifaceted** | 多层面的；多维的 | 形容词 | having many sides |
| **competencies** | 胜任能力 | 名词 | skills |
| **technical expertise** | 技术专长 | 名词短语 | technical skills |

**1. 复杂让步句**
> *"**Admittedly**, mastering extensive knowledge plays an essential role in achieving career milestones, particularly in fields like technology, finance, engineering, and design, where advanced computer skills are not only beneficial but often indispensable."*

**技巧**："Admittedly"标记让步立场，主句陈述价值，"particularly in fields like..."精确限定范围，"where"引导的定语从句进一步描述这些领域的特征。"not only beneficial but often indispensable"用递进结构强调计算机技能的必要性。

**2. 因果推导句**
> *"This is largely due to the growing prevalence of automation and digital methodologies in the modern workplace, which means that employees with strong technical skills are typically more efficient and adaptable, thereby becoming more valuable to employers."*

**技巧**："This is largely due to"解释原因，"which means that"推导第一层后果，"thereby becoming..."推导第二层后果。这种"原因→后果1→后果2"的链条式写法使论证逻辑严密。

**3. 技术反驳句**
> *"This means that **even individuals without technical knowledge of hardware or software can still accomplish professional tasks effectively** by interacting with AI systems using natural language."*

**技巧**："This means that"承接前文的技术突破，主句用"even individuals without..."强调最不利情况也能受益，"by interacting with..."说明具体方式。这种"技术突破→普遍受益→具体操作"的结构使反驳论证非常扎实。

**4. 综合总结句**
> *"With the rise of AI simplifying complex tasks and the growing emphasis on soft skills, success increasingly depends on a multifaceted range of competencies beyond technical expertise."*

**技巧**：结尾句用"With"引导的介词短语设定背景（AI兴起+软技能受重视），主句强调"multifaceted range of competencies"。"beyond technical expertise"用介词短语明确超越技术专长的论点，使结尾既有总结又有升华。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 让步段主题句 `mastering extensive knowledge plays an essential role in achieving career milestones` 过于笼统，没有对准本题的 `high-level computer skills`，建议直接写成"掌握高阶计算机技能"，让让步的靶心与题目一致。
- `Meanwhile, professionals in information technology fields, including software developers, data analysts, and digital marketers rely heavily on...` 中 `Meanwhile` 表并列略松（此处是举例细化，宜用 `Indeed` 或 `In IT in particular`），且 `digital marketers` 之后漏了一个逗号。
- 反驳段从 ChatGPT 的例子直接跳到 soft skills，中间缺一句"AI 拉低技术门槛→用人标准重心转移"的过渡，两条论证线之间的衔接略显生硬。

**仿写任务**：用「先退后进」骨架重写：*Some think space exploration is a waste of money.*
