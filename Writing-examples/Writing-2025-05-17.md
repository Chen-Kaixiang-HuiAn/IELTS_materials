## Task 1

> **图表类型标注**：折线图（三地区汽车保有量）

**The graph below shows the number of cars in three regions in the UK from 1998 to 2008.**
**Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

![2025-05-17](./Task1_img/2025-05-17.png)

The graph illustrates changes in car ownership across three regions of the UK, namely the UK mainland, Ireland Island, and the Isle of Man, from 1998 to 2008.

As seen, while the number of registered cars increased in both the UK mainland and Ireland Island, that in the UK mainland experienced the most substantial growth. Specifically, in 1998, over 23 million cars were registered in the UK mainland, and that figure rose gradually, reaching nearly 27 million by 2008, with a more noticeable upsurge occurring between 2003 and 2008 compared to the previous five-year span. 

Despite Ireland Island having the second highest car ownership, which stood at around 14 million in 1998, its statistics followed a similar upward trend to that in the UK mainland, witnessing a moderate yet consistent rise to just under 16 million by 2008. In contrast, the Isle of Man consistently had the fewest cars among the three regions, and it experienced a decline in car ownership, which fell from approximately 6 million in 1998 to about 5 million in 2008.

Overall, the UK mainland maintained the highest number of cars and showed the most significant growth over the period, while the Isle of Man recorded the lowest figures and was the only region to see a decrease in car ownership.

**1. 阶段对比句**

**2. 让步+趋势句**
> *"**Despite Ireland Island having the second highest car ownership**, which stood at around 14 million in 1998, its statistics followed a similar upward trend to that in the UK mainland, **witnessing a moderate yet consistent rise** to just under 16 million by 2008."*

**技巧**："Despite + 动名词短语"承认排名地位，"which"引导的定语从句提供具体数据，主句描述趋势，"witnessing"补充说明变化特征。这种"地位→数据→趋势→特征"的四层结构使信息传递非常完整。

**3. 对比下降句**
> *"**In contrast**, the Isle of Man consistently had the fewest cars among the three regions, and it experienced a decline in car ownership, which fell from approximately 6 million in 1998 to about 5 million in 2008."*

**技巧**："In contrast"标记与前两个地区的对比，主句陈述排名和趋势，"which"引导的定语从句提供具体数据。"consistently had the fewest"强调了最低地位的持续性。

**4. 综合概述句**
> *"Overall, the UK mainland maintained the highest number of cars and showed the most significant growth over the period, **while** the Isle of Man recorded the lowest figures and **was the only region to see a decrease** in car ownership."*

**技巧**：概述段用"while"将两个极端地区并置对比，"was the only region to see a decrease"精准标注了Isle of Man的独特性。这种"最高+增长最大 vs 最低+唯一下降"的对比使概述信息极其丰富。

## Task 2

> **话题与题型标注**：媒体与广告类 · Agree/Disagree

**The media should include more stories that report good news. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

**媒体应当报道更多的好消息。你在多大程度上同意或不同意这一观点？**

In an age where sensationalism often dominates headlines, some claim that media outlets should focus more on positive news. Although the media featuring more uplifting stories could enhance public morale and encourage pro-social behavior, I disagree with their opinion.

在一个耸人听闻的新闻常常占据头条的时代，一些人认为媒体应当更多关注正面新闻。尽管报道更多令人振奋的故事可能有助于提升公众士气并鼓励亲社会行为，但我并不同意这种观点。

Admittedly, a media landscape that places greater emphasis on good news fosters optimism and inspires constructive action in an era already marked by global challenges. When people read about individuals or communities making a difference through human achievements or scientific breakthroughs, they are more likely to feel empowered to contribute to society and develop a sense of national pride. For example, the emergence of DeepSeek, an advanced artificial intelligence system capable of processing vast amounts of digital information and generating accurate, human-like responses, highlights China’s growing strength in technological innovation. By prominently featuring this breakthrough in the media instead of focusing solely on reports of crime, natural disasters, or armed conflicts, public attention shifts toward progress and possibility. This not only showcases China's ability to overcome reliance on or restrictions imposed by Western countries but also boosts national morale and fosters confidence, especially in high-tech fields where global competition is intense.

诚然，在全球挑战不断的当下，一个更加注重好消息的媒体环境确实能激发人们的乐观情绪，并鼓励建设性的行动。当人们看到个体或社会通过人类成就或科技突破在改变世界时，他们更有可能产生参与社会建设的动力，培养民族自豪感。例如，DeepSeek 的出现——这是一种能够处理海量数字信息并生成准确且类似人类回应的先进人工智能系统——突显了中国在科技创新方面日益增强的实力。相比于只关注犯罪、自然灾害或武装冲突等负面事件，媒体若将这项突破性进展予以突出报道，便能将公众注意力引向进步与可能性。这不仅展示了中国克服对西方国家依赖或突破其限制的能力，也增强了国民士气，尤其是在全球竞争激烈的高科技领域，进一步激发了信心。

Nevertheless, a helpful media narrative should be grounded in reality rather than shaped by an artificial preference for positive news under the guise of achieving a more“balanced” or proportionate approach, where upbeat stories are given equal visibility. While promoting positivity is not inherently harmful, the media, as the fourth estate, should avoid distorting the actual state of affairs by overrepresenting optimistic accounts. This practice can undermine public trust, obscure critical issues, and hinder informed decision-making. A truly constructive narrative acknowledges both progress and problems, offering a more accurate reflection of the world and allowing audiences to engage with reality rather than with an idealized version of it. For example, ongoing geopolitical conflicts in the Middle East have resulted in severe humanitarian crises, food shortages, and the destruction of essential infrastructure, so residents in that regions struggle to meet even their basic living needs. Reporting these tragic yet true events can encourage the international community to cooperate and provide much-needed disaster relief.

然而，有效的媒体叙事应立足现实，而不是被人为的“正面新闻偏好”所主导，借“平衡报道”之名来人为地提高乐观内容的曝光度。尽管传递正能量本身并无害处，但作为“第四权力”的媒体，应避免通过过度呈现正面信息来歪曲现实。这种做法可能会削弱公众信任、掩盖关键问题，并阻碍理性决策的形成。真正具有建设性的媒体叙事应同时展现进步与问题，从而更准确地反映世界，帮助受众面对真实而非理想化的现实。例如，中东地区持续的地缘政治冲突已造成严重的人道主义危机、粮食短缺和关键基础设施的破坏，当地居民甚至难以满足最基本的生活需求。报道这些虽令人痛心但确实存在的事件，有助于促使国际社会展开合作，提供急需的灾难救助。

In conclusion, while the inclusion of positive news can uplift public spirit and foster a sense of collective progress, the media's foremost responsibility lies in presenting an honest, comprehensive account of reality. By confronting both achievements and adversities societies can make informed decisions and cultivate meaningful change.

总之，虽然正面新闻的加入有助于提振公众情绪、营造集体进步的氛围，但媒体的首要责任应是呈现一个真实、全面的世界图景。唯有直面成就与困境，社会才能做出明智决策，并推动真正有意义的变革。

| **转折反驳** | `Nevertheless...` | 转向反驳论证 |
| **因果推导** | `This not only... but also...` | 推导正面新闻的多重效果 |
| **例证衔接** | `For example...` | 用具体例子支撑论点 |

> **衔接亮点**：本文在让步段中使用了"This not only showcases China's ability to overcome reliance on or restrictions imposed by Western countries but also boosts national morale and fosters confidence, especially in high-tech fields where global competition is intense"的递进结构，将"展示能力"与"提升士气"两个效果并列，并用"especially"精确限定范围。

| 词汇/短语 | 中文含义 | 词性/用法 | 替代普通表达 |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **sensationalism** | 哗众取宠的报道风格 | 名词 | shocking news |
| **fosters optimism** | 培育乐观情绪 | 动词短语 | makes people optimistic |
| **the fourth estate** | 第四权力（指新闻界） | 名词短语 | the media |
| **grounded in reality** | 立足于现实 | 形容词短语 | based on reality |
| **artificial preference** | 人为制造的偏好 | 名词短语 | unnatural preference |
| **humanitarian crises** | 人道主义危机 | 名词短语 | human crises |

**1. 因果递进句**
> *"This **not only showcases** China's ability to overcome reliance on or restrictions imposed by Western countries **but also boosts** national morale and fosters confidence, **especially in high-tech fields where global competition is intense**."*

**技巧**："not only... but also..."结构连接两个并列效果，"especially"插入语精确限定范围，"where"引导的定语从句进一步限定"high-tech fields"。这种"效果1+效果2+范围限定"的结构使论证既有广度又有深度。

**2. 条件+后果句**
> *"**If stricter regulations on access to violent media were implemented**, a safer and more nurturing environment for young people could be fostered, ultimately helping them grow into empathetic and morally grounded individuals."*

**技巧**："If"引导条件从句，主句推导后果，"ultimately helping..."用现在分词短语说明最终目标。这种"条件→直接后果→最终目标"的链条式写法使建议非常具体和有说服力。

**3. 对比论证句**
> *"**A truly constructive narrative acknowledges both progress and problems**, offering a more accurate reflection of the world and allowing audiences to engage with reality rather than with an idealized version of it."*

**技巧**：主句提出真正建设性叙事的标准，现在分词短语"offering..."和"allowing..."说明这一标准的两个效果，"rather than"将"reality"与"idealized version"对比，突出真实报道的价值。

**4. 综合总结句**
> *"In conclusion, while the inclusion of positive news can uplift public spirit and foster a sense of collective progress, **the media's foremost responsibility lies in presenting an honest, comprehensive account of reality**."*

**技巧**：结尾句用"while"引导让步从句承认正面新闻的价值，主句用"foremost responsibility lies in"强调媒体的核心责任。"honest, comprehensive account of reality"三个形容词并列修饰"account"，从诚实性、全面性和现实性三个维度定义媒体责任。

#### ⑥ 可提升空间 + 仿写任务

**可提升空间（通用要点，按篇酌情采纳）：**
- 结论段避免复读开头，应「推进」而非「重复」立场。
- 让步段衔接词（this perspective 等）回指别太远，可加限定词收紧。
- 同一话题词（如 career / benefit）避免三现，用近义替换。

**本文针对性可改进点（按篇分析，点到即止）：**
- 两处硬伤：`a more“balanced” or proportionate approach` 引号前缺空格；`residents in that regions struggle` 单复数不一致，应为 `those regions`。
- 开头段直接写 `I disagree with their opinion`，但全文真正的主张是"多报正面新闻可以，但不能为求正面而失真"，属有条件的反对；把立场句写成 `I disagree that positive stories should be prioritised at the expense of accuracy` 会更贴合正文。
- 让步段用整段篇幅铺陈单一例证，其中对系统能力与行业竞争的描述超出论点所需；压缩两句，把空间让给反驳段的第二条理由（削弱公众信任、掩盖关键问题），后者目前只有概括、没有例证。

**仿写任务**：用「先退后进」骨架重写：*Some think space exploration is a waste of money.*
