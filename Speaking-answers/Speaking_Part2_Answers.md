### 1. 为家人骄傲 🔥

> **Cue Card**: Describe a time when you felt proud of a family member

**You should say**:
- When it happened
- Who the person is
- What the person did
- And explain why you felt proud of him/her
**【分段拆解与词数】**

**Paragraph 1 — 开场定位（20-25 词）**
> I'd like to talk about my father, who runs a hardware store in my hometown Quanzhou. The moment that made me truly proud of him happened about three years ago when I was in my second year of university.

**Paragraph 2 — 背景铺垫（35-40 词）**
> My father started his business from scratch about twenty years ago. He began as an apprentice in a small workshop, learning everything about metalwork and machinery. Over the years, he built up his own hardware store through sheer hard work and persistence.

**Paragraph 3 — 核心事件（50-60 词）**
> Three years ago, our city launched a new environmental policy requiring all businesses to upgrade their waste management systems. Many shop owners complained and tried to find loopholes, but my father took it seriously. He invested his own savings to install a proper recycling system in his store and even started educating his customers about sustainable practices. Within six months, his store became a model example in our district.

**Paragraph 4 — 收尾解释（40-50 词）**
> I felt incredibly proud because he didn't just follow the rules—he embraced the spirit behind them. It showed me that success isn't just about making money; it's about taking responsibility for your community. His actions taught me what integrity really means, and that's something I carry with me every day.

**【总词数】约 180-200 词**

**【核心素材】故事线 A：父亲 —— 创业成功 + 环保意识**

**【切换提示】**
- 考「爱护自然之人」→ 侧重父亲的环保行为细节，减少创业背景
- 考「遇到困难终成功的人」→ 侧重创业初期的资金困难和坚持
